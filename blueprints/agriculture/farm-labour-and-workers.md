                Once you pay anyone regularly, most Caribbean countries expect you to be registered as an employer with the tax authority and the national insurance or social security scheme, such as NIS in Trinidad and Tobago, Barbados or Guyana, or NIS, NHT and HEART in Jamaica.                 ## Notes

            - name: Heat stress plan for the dry season
              description: |-

                Long hours in full sun at 32 degrees and above cause dizziness, cramps and heat collapse, and new or older workers are most at risk. A plan for water, shade, break times and shifting heavy work to the cool of the morning protects people and keeps output up through the dry season.

                ## Milestones

# Farm Labour and Workers

Farm Labour and Workers is for the farmer who employs people, from a smallholder taking on a first helper to a farm manager running permanent staff and a harvest crew of twenty. The projects start with foundations: a seasonal labour map, employer registration, pay structures, written terms and a safety induction. Then come the routines that keep a team paid, safe and turning up, the skills of supervising and training workers, decisions on piece rates, contractors and transport, dated events such as the harvest recruitment drive and year-end returns, situations from family labour to foreign workers, and specialist work on productivity, labour sharing and buyer social audits.

What repeats is the weekly pay run, a daily morning briefing, the monthly contributions remittance, a monthly toolbox talk, housing and welfare checks, quarterly one-to-ones and a yearly pay review. The Onboarding plan, Operational checklist, Skills matrix, 1:1, Risk register and Person templates pair with the projects that point to them. Employment law, tax and social security rules differ by country, so every legal and statutory step here is about preparing and asking the labour office, the tax authority or an adviser, not a ruling from this list.
