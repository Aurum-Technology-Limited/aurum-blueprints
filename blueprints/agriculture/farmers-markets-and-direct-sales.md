# Farmers Markets and Direct Sales

Direct sales mean you keep the margin a vendor or supermarket would take, but you also take on the selling: the stall, the price board, the change, the WhatsApp messages at night and the customer who wants to know what to do with christophene. This area is for farmers who sell, or want to sell, some or all of their crop straight to households. It starts with the foundations (choosing channels, permits, a verified scale, payments, a price list and a WhatsApp catalogue), then the weekly machinery of orders, picking, packing, delivery and cash-up, the selling skills, the decisions that grow a regular trade such as box schemes and farm stands, the dated peaks of the year, variants for part-time, family, group and tourist-area sellers, and finally the specialist work of organising a market and planning supply around your buyers.

The rhythm is the market week: a Tuesday order cut-off, a Wednesday pick list, a Thursday availability broadcast, Friday route planning, a Saturday sales log and a Sunday cash-up, with monthly price, customer and leftover reviews, quarterly market surveys and a yearly round of licence and scale checks. The Operational checklist, Metrics log, Purchase decision and Marketing campaign templates pair with the packing list, the market day log, packaging choices and the first market day launch.
