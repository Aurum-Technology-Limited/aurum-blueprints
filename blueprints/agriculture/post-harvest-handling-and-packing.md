                ## Purpose
                Most growers know they lose produce after picking but not where, so money goes on fixes that miss the real leak. Following a single harvest of your main crop from the plant to the buyer, and counting what is thrown out or downgraded at each step, shows whether the damage happens in the field, the shed, the store or the truck.

                ## Milestones
                1. One full harvest of the main crop followed from picking to delivery, with the date and weather noted.
                2. Rejects and downgrades counted or weighed at each step: field, receiving, packing, storage and transport.
                3. The step with the largest loss named, with a likely cause written beside it.
                4. Two changes chosen to try on the next harvest of the same crop.

# Post-Harvest Handling and Packing

A large share of Caribbean produce never reaches a buyer in saleable condition, and most of that loss happens in the hours after picking: fruit cut in the midday sun, crates left on the headland, greens packed warm, mangoes stored beside the bananas. This area follows the produce from the field edge to the buyer's door, starting with foundations (a loss walk-through, maturity signs, storage needs, crates, a shaded receiving spot and written grades), then the operating routines of harvest day, cleaning, cold room logs, culls and stock rotation, the skills of cooling, curing, ripening and gentle handling, decisions about cold rooms, power, packing lines and transport, the dated peaks and storms of the year, variants for smallholders, part-time growers and sheds that take in neighbours' crops, and finally shelf-life trials, hot water treatment and cold chain mapping.

The rhythm is the harvest week: picking planned on Sunday, culls counted on Friday, crates and the shed cleaned at the end of the week, the cold room read every day, stock rotated on Monday, a monthly loss review, generator test and packaging count, and a yearly cold room service before the busy season. The Purchase decision, Operational checklist, Metrics log and Training program templates pair with the equipment choices, the harvest-day routine, the cold room log and the handling course for your pickers and packers.
