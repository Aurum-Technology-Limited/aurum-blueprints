# Wholesale Market Selling

Wholesale markets move most of the Caribbean's fresh produce, through vendors, higglers, hucksters and traffickers who buy by the crate or sack and sell on, and they reward the farmer who arrives with the right crop, graded properly, at a price already known. This area starts with the foundations (which markets, which crops, the rules, transport, a price baseline and a sales ledger), then the weekly machinery of market day, price checks, credit and cash, the skills of grading, weighing, negotiating and reading the market, the decisions about middlemen, consignment and transport, the dated peaks and shocks of the market year, variants for part-time, young, group and family sellers, and finally aggregating for neighbours and standing orders with the big vendors.

The rhythm is the market week: prices checked twice a week, the kit loaded the night before, credit chased every Tuesday, takings banked and reconciled, a monthly buyer check-in and crate count, and a quarterly ranking of who pays and who does not. The Metrics log, Operational checklist, Person and Purchase decision templates pair with the sales ledger, the market-day routine, your buyer records and the truck decision.
