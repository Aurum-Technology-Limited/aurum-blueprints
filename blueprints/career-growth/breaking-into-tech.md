# Breaking Into Tech

This area is for anyone moving into a technology career from somewhere else: a student on a non-computing degree, a career changer in their thirties or forties, a parent studying in the gaps, or someone in a market with few tech employers. It starts with foundations (choosing a track from real job ads, an honest skills audit, a study budget in hours and money, and the self-study, bootcamp or degree decision), then the routines that keep learning moving, the core skills every junior role assumes, the portfolio and application work that turns study into evidence, the dated moments such as hackathons and technical interviews, variants for different lives, and finally what to do once the first job is won.

What repeats is a short daily practice session, a Sunday learning log, weekly coding problems, build sessions and code review with a study partner, a Monday application block, monthly checks against the job ads and the community calendar, and a quarterly look at whether the track still fits. The Skills matrix, Purchase decision, Course, Portfolio piece and Job application templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
