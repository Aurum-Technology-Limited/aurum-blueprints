              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                A break without a reason tends to dissolve into admin and guilt by week three, and it is also harder to ask for. Writing one page on why you want this time, what you hope is different at the end and what you are deliberately not trying to do gives you the spine of the proposal to your employer and a test for every later choice about length, money and plans.

                ## Milestones
                1. A one-page statement naming the main purpose (rest, learning, a project, family, travel or exploring a change) and at most two secondary ones.
                2. Three outcomes written so a friend could check them at the end, such as a finished course or a decision made.
                3. A short list of things the sabbatical is explicitly not for.
                4. The statement read aloud to one trusted person and revised once.

                ## Notes
                Rest is a legitimate main purpose. Writing it down honestly stops you filling the break with projects to justify it.

            - name: Sabbatical income gap and cost budget
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Most of the money risk is not the travel or the course, it is the months of fixed costs that keep running while pay stops or drops. A budget that sets lost income, ongoing bills, the plans themselves and a re-entry buffer side by side tells you how much you need saved before you go and how long you can afford to be away.

                ## Milestones
                1. Net monthly income during the break worked out, whether full, partial or zero.
                2. Fixed monthly costs that continue listed with amounts, including rent or mortgage, insurance and subscriptions.
                3. Planned sabbatical costs estimated: travel, courses, equipment, childcare changes.
                4. A total target including a buffer of at least one month of costs after you return.
                5. The number of months you can afford compared with the length you chose.

                ## Notes
                Remember costs that start on return, such as a pension catch-up or a deposit for new childcare. A financial adviser can check the plan if large sums or investments are involved.

# Career Sabbatical Planning

Career sabbatical planning covers the whole arc of a deliberate break from work, from the first written reason for taking it to the conversation that decides what you do once you are back. The projects start with foundations (purpose, policy, leave or resign, length, budget, benefits, tax questions and the household agreement), move through the proposal, cover plan and signed agreement, the handover and the operating rhythms that keep money, home and network steady while you are away, then the learning, travel and rest you take the break for, the dated moments of leaving and returning, versions for families, burnout recovery, freelancers, managers and people nearing retirement, and finish with fellowships, policy advocacy and planning the next one.

What repeats is a Sunday countdown review in the months before you leave, a Monday plan and Friday reflection while you are off, monthly checks on spending, bills, network and industry news on varied days, daily language and wellbeing habits where you choose them, and a monthly transfer into the fund for the next break. The Savings goal template suits the fund, Meeting notes suits the request and return meetings, Operational checklist suits the final fortnight, Course suits study on the break and Trip suits the travel route. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
