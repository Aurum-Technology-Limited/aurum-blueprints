# Career Sponsors & Advocates

This area is for knowledge workers and managers who have noticed that the best roles are often filled before they are advertised, by people whose names were mentioned in a meeting they never attended. It starts with the foundations (telling a sponsor from a mentor, mapping where careers are decided, auditing who already speaks for you and choosing what you want sponsored), moves through the monthly and quarterly machinery that keeps sponsors informed and willing, then the skills of asking and briefing, the decisions about which sponsors to keep and which offers to accept, the dated moments such as nominations and talent reviews, versions for remote, returning and reorganised working lives and, last, the work of sponsoring other people well.

What repeats is small but steady: a monthly two-line update to each sponsor, a monthly look at roles coming open, monthly time with senior leaders outside your chain and with peer advocates, quarterly sponsor conversations and portfolio reviews, and a yearly reset around the talent review calendar.
