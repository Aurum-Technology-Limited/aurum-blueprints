                ## Purpose
                Most people can name a dozen things they want from work, but choices only get easier once those wants are ranked. # Career Values & Purpose Clarity

Career values and purpose clarity is about knowing what you actually want from work, such as autonomy, impact, money, mastery, security or belonging, and ranking those wants honestly enough that they settle real choices. It suits anyone weighing a job, a course or a change, and students choosing a first direction. The projects start with foundations (a values sort, your best and worst moments at work, an energy audit, deal-breakers, a money floor and a first purpose statement), then the routines that keep values in view, the frameworks worth knowing, decisions about employers, ethics and trade-offs, dated events such as a solo values day, situations from student to mid-career and tight budgets, and finish with helping others do the same work.

The rhythms are a Friday values scorecard, a Sunday reflective note, a monthly energy and meaning review, a values item on your one-to-one agenda, a quarterly purpose statement revision and a yearly re-sort of your values. The **Habit tracker** template suits the weekly scorecard, **Metrics log** suits the monthly review, **1:1** suits manager conversations, **Feedback themes** suits what other people tell you and **Reading queue** suits the reading list. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
