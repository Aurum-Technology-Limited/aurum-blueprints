              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Rewriting a CV without a target produces a polite list of duties that fits no job in particular. Writing down the one or two job titles you are aiming for, the seniority, the sector and the locations gives every later edit a test: does this line help a reader hiring for that role? Do it first, in under an hour, before touching the document itself.

                ## Milestones
                1. One primary and at most one secondary target job title written down, with seniority level.
                2. Sector, location or remote preference and salary band noted for each target.
                3. Three things a hiring manager for that role would most want to see listed in plain words.
                4. The brief saved where every CV version can reference it.

                ## Notes
                Two very different targets usually need two different CVs. If your list has three or more, pick the one you would accept tomorrow and write for that first.

                Recruiters and applicant tracking systems search for the words the job ad uses, not the words your current employer uses.                 ## Purpose
                A master inventory is the long, private document every tailored CV is cut from: every role, project, tool, award and result with dates and numbers, no length limit. Building it once ends the scramble of remembering what you did in 2019 each time an application is due, and stops good achievements falling off the page simply because you forgot them.

            - name: Finding numbers for every achievement
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Hiring managers believe numbers more than adjectives, but most people under-count what they can measure. Going back through the inventory to attach a figure to each achievement, such as money saved, time cut, volume handled, people trained or error rates reduced, turns vague claims into evidence, and the figures you can defend in an interview are the only ones worth printing.

                ## Milestones
                1. Each achievement in the inventory tagged with a type of measure: money, time, volume, quality, people or scale.
                2. A defensible figure or honest estimate attached to at least two thirds of them.
                3. Estimates labelled as approximate in the inventory, with how you reached them.
                4. Any figure covered by confidentiality rephrased as a percentage or range.

                ## Notes
                Scale counts even without results: budgets held, customers served, team size, number of sites. Never invent a figure; an interviewer will ask how you calculated it.
              priority: high
              deadlineOffsetDays: 30
              frontmatter:
                mode: building
                output_kind: artifact
                success_criteria: "At least two thirds of inventory achievements carry a number, each with a recorded source or estimation method."
                cadence: one-shot
                effort_hours_estimate: "4"
              tasks:
                - "Tag each achievement in the inventory with money, time, volume, quality, people or scale"
                - "Find a figure or reasoned estimate for the ten strongest achievements"
                - "Write how each estimate was reached beside it in the inventory"
                - "Convert confidential figures into percentages or ranges"

            - name: Achievement bullets rewritten as action and result
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Duty lists such as responsible for managing the team tell a reader what the job was, not how well you did it. Rewriting each kept bullet as a strong verb, what you did and the measurable result changes the document from a job description into evidence, and it is the single largest improvement most CVs can make.

                ## Milestones

                2. At least two thirds of bullets in recent roles end with a result or scale.
                3. No bullet runs past two lines on the page.
                4. Three to six bullets per recent role, ordered strongest first.

                ## Notes

              priority: high

# CV & Resume Rewrite

This area takes a tired CV or resume and rebuilds it around the roles you actually want and the results you can prove. It starts with foundations (a target role brief, a keyword map from real job ads, the right regional format, a master inventory of everything you have done), moves through the routines that keep the document sharp, then the writing skills and the hard decisions about gaps, length and structure, and ends with versions for students, career changers, experienced professionals and specialist fields such as engineering, creative work, executive roles and the public sector.

What repeats is light: a weekly tailoring and send-log habit while you are applying, monthly parse tests, proofreading and link checks, a quarterly top-up of the master CV and a yearly refresh after your review. The Resume, Cover letter, Job application, Metrics log, Feedback themes and Purchase decision templates pair with the projects that point at them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
