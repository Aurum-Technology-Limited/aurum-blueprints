              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Most early friction in a first job comes from unspoken expectations: how fast to reply, when to ask for help, whether to work late, how your manager likes to hear bad news. One short conversation in the first fortnight, written up afterwards, replaces months of guessing and gives you something to point back to when things get busy.

                ## Milestones

                4. A date set to revisit the norms after your first three months.

                ## Notes
                Start from the **1:1** template. Ask questions rather than proposing rules: "How do you like to hear that something is going wrong?" gets a more honest answer than "I'll tell you on Fridays".

# Early-Career Foundations

This area is for the first five years of a working life, when nobody hands you a syllabus and the habits you form quietly become your reputation. It starts with the basics of a first job (agreeing working norms, a task system, your contract and payslip, a map of who does what), then builds the weekly machinery of updates, reviews and follow-through, the core skills of writing, meetings and owning mistakes, the early decisions about qualifications, boundaries and when to move, and finally the situations of students, apprentices, first-generation professionals and international graduates, ending with leading your first piece of work and a five-year look back.

The rhythms that repeat are a short daily shutdown, a Monday plan and a Friday update to your manager, a monthly coffee with a peer, a monthly payslip check and feedback request, and a quarterly look at whether your habits are holding. The 1:1, Meeting notes, Feedback themes, Trip and Development plan templates pair with the projects that need them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
