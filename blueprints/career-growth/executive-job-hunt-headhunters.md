            - name: Deciding between an open and a confidential search
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Senior people rarely search in the open while employed, because word of a CEO or divisional director looking around travels fast through investors, boards and the search community. Deciding up front whether your search is confidential, discreetly known to a few, or fully open sets every later choice: who you call, what you post and what you tell your current chair.

                ## Milestones
                1. Your reason for moving written in two sentences you would be comfortable repeating to a chair.
                2. A choice made between a confidential, selectively known or open search, with the reason noted.
                3. The people who may know about the search named, and everyone else treated as out of the loop.
                4. A trigger agreed with yourself for when the search would become open, such as a restructuring announcement.

                ## Notes
                If your contract has a duty of fidelity or notice clauses that touch on seeking other work, read them before speaking to anyone outside the business.
              priority: high
              deadlineOffsetDays: 10
              frontmatter:
                mode: building
                output_kind: decision
                success_criteria: "A written note records the search type chosen, the named people who may know, and the trigger for going open."
                cadence: one-shot
                effort_hours_estimate: "2"
              tasks:
                - "Write two sentences on why you are ready to move now"
                - "Reread your employment contract for fidelity and confidentiality clauses"
                - "Choose a confidential, selective or open search and note why"
                - "List the few people who may be told, and nobody else"

# Executive Job Hunt & Headhunters

Executive job hunting works differently from ordinary job searching: most senior roles are filled through retained search firms, chairs and investors, and the process runs for months under confidentiality. The projects start with foundations (the decision to search, a target brief, your narrative, track record and a map of the search firms), then the operating routines that keep partners and networks warm, the skills of each selection stage, decisions about routes and employer types, the dated events from final rounds to contract and announcement, situations such as searching after an exit or stepping up to the C-suite, and finish with specialist routes like a management buy-in or an external CEO succession candidacy.

The rhythms are a weekly search review and leadership-change scan, monthly network reconnections and a funnel check, and quarterly calls with your key search partners. The **Person** template suits each search partner, **Meeting notes** suits consultant briefings, **Job application** suits each live mandate, and **Metrics log** suits the monthly funnel. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
