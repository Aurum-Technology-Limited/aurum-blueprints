# Graduate Job Hunt

Graduate Job Hunt is for students in their final year, and recent graduates, trying to turn a degree into a first proper job. It starts with foundations (a timeline, target sectors, a weekly hours budget, entry requirements and a long list of employers), then the routines that keep a search moving, the skills that graduate selection actually tests, from numerical reasoning to group exercises, the decisions about further study, location and smaller employers, the dated events from careers fairs to results day, situations such as international, mature or first-generation students, and finally a few projects for those going after the most competitive schemes.

What repeats is a weekly vacancy scan, twice-weekly test practice, a weekly alumni message and a Sunday rest check, with monthly reviews of scheme opening dates, your application funnel, your careers adviser meeting and your evidence bank. The **Job application**, **Metrics log**, **Meeting notes**, **Cover letter** and **Feedback themes** templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
