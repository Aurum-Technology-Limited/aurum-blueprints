# Industry Networking Events

Industry networking events covers conferences, meetups, trade shows, receptions and association evenings, and how to come away from them with more than a tote bag. The projects start with foundations (clear goals, a shortlist of events, a budget, an introduction and a way to capture contacts), then the routines that turn meetings into relationships, the conversation skills themselves, decisions about which events to keep, specific events to prepare for, situations such as job hunting, changing industry or attending with limited evenings, and finally the work of people who host and shape events rather than only attend them.

What repeats is a Monday clear-out of event follow-ups, a weekly scan for upcoming events, a monthly meetup and keep-in-touch round, a quarterly pipeline review and a yearly look at which events earned their place. The **Trip** template suits a major conference, **Metrics log** suits the event return log, **Person** suits your key contacts, **Meeting notes** suits session notes and **Purchase decision** suits a membership choice. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
