# International Job Relocation

Moving abroad for work is three projects at once: landing the job, getting the legal right to do it, and rebuilding a working and home life in a place where the unwritten rules are different. The projects start with foundations (your reasons and red lines, a scored country shortlist, the visa route map, documents, qualification recognition, a budget and the family conversation), then the routines that carry an overseas search and a move, the knowledge of a new work culture, contracts and tax, the decisions about packages, housing and money, the dated events from the recce trip to arrival week, variants for partners, children, pets and parents left at home, and finish with assignments, licensed professions, permanent residence and the move home.

The rhythms are a weekly overseas role search, a weekly contact in the destination city, daily language practice, a weekly visa and countdown check, monthly money, home-ties and settling-in reviews, and quarterly checks on permit dates and days spent abroad. The **Resume** template suits the country-format CV, **Monthly budget and bills** carries the relocation budget, **Risk register** suits the visa evidence file, **Trip** suits the recce visit and **Purchase decision** suits choosing health cover. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
