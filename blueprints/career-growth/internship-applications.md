# Internship Applications

Internship applications covers the whole run from deciding what kind of placement you want to walking out of it with a graduate offer or a strong reference. It starts with the foundations (goals, the recruiting calendar for your field, a one-page student CV, a target list and a tracker), then the weekly machinery of applying, following up and networking, the skills that get you through forms, online tests and video interviews, the choices between paid and unpaid, summer and year-long, and the events from careers fairs to your final presentation. Later projects cover particular situations, such as studying abroad on a visa, being the first in your family at university or needing adjustments, and finish with research, technical, creative and policy internships.

What repeats is a weekly application sprint, a weekly closing date check, follow-ups and commercial awareness reading, a monthly sweep of careers portals and a monthly coffee chat, plus a termly review with your careers adviser. The **Resume**, **Cover letter**, **Job application**, **Meeting notes**, **Onboarding plan** and **Portfolio piece** templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
