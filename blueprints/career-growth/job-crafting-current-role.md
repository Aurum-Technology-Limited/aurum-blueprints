              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Most people can say they dislike parts of their job but not which parts, or how many hours those parts take. Logging every task for two working weeks with the time spent and a simple energy score turns a vague sense of drift into a list you can actually reshape, and every later project in this area builds on it.

                ## Milestones
                1. Ten working days logged in blocks of 30 minutes or longer, each with a task label.
                2. Every block scored from minus two (draining) to plus two (energising).
                3. Tasks grouped into eight to fifteen categories with total hours for each.
                4. The three most draining and three most energising categories named, with their share of the fortnight.

                ## Notes
                Log at lunch and at the end of the day, not once a week: memory flatters the interesting work and forgets the admin. Start from the **Metrics log** template.

# Job Crafting Your Current Role

Job crafting is the quiet work of reshaping the job you already have: which tasks you do more or less of, who you work with, and how you think about what the work is for. This area starts with foundations (an energy and task audit, a strengths map, the gap between your written and real job, and a clear sense of what you are free to change), moves into the weekly and monthly machinery that keeps a crafted role from sliding back, then the skills of proposing, renegotiating and reframing, specific changes and decisions, the dated moments such as goal setting and reorganisations that open a window, versions for frontline, remote, long-tenure and caring lives, and finally the advanced work of crafting a hybrid role or helping a whole team do it.

What repeats is small and deliberate: a Friday check-in on what you shifted that week, protected focus and learning slots, monthly energy scores, a task drift review and contact with the people your work serves, and a quarterly crafting conversation with your manager. The Metrics log, 1:1 and Development plan templates pair with the projects that keep scores, conversations and skill plans. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
