              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Before the first offer lands, decide the three to five conditions you will not trade, such as a pay floor, a maximum commute or no weekend on-call. Written down in a calm week, they stop a flattering offer call from talking you out of what you already knew mattered. They also make declining a poor fit quick and guilt-free.

                ## Milestones
                1. A list of no more than five non-negotiables, each stated as a measurable limit.
                2. A separate list of strong preferences that could be traded.
                3. Each non-negotiable tested against one past job to check it is real.
                4. The list saved where you will see it during every offer conversation.

                ## Notes
                If everything is a non-negotiable, nothing is. More than five usually means some are preferences in disguise.
              priority: high
              deadlineOffsetDays: 7
              frontmatter:
                mode: building

                cadence: one-shot
                effort_hours_estimate: "2"
              tasks:
                - "Write down the three worst things about your last two jobs"
                - "Turn each into a measurable limit, such as a commute in minutes"

            - name: Weighted criteria for comparing offers
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Pay, growth, culture, commute and stability rarely point the same way, and without weights the most recent or loudest factor wins. Giving each criterion a share of 100 points before you see the offers turns a tangle of impressions into a score you can argue with. Weights set in advance are also harder to bend toward the answer you secretly want.

                ## Milestones
                1. Eight to twelve criteria listed, covering pay, growth, people, work pattern and risk.
                2. Weights adding up to 100 assigned across the criteria.
                3. A one to five scoring scale described in words for each criterion.
                4. The weights sense-checked with one trusted person.

                ## Notes
                Keep pay to one or two rows. Splitting it into base, bonus, pension and equity rows quietly gives money four votes.
              priority: high
              deadlineOffsetDays: 10
              frontmatter:
                mode: building
                output_kind: decision
                success_criteria: "A criteria sheet with weights totalling 100 and a written one to five scale for every criterion is dated before any offer is scored."
                cadence: one-shot
                effort_hours_estimate: "3"
              tasks:
                - "List every factor that has made you happy or unhappy at work"
                - "Group the factors into eight to twelve criteria"
                - "Spread 100 points across the criteria"
                - "Describe what a low, middle and top score looks like for each one"
                - "Revisit the weights after any big change at home or work @recurring(yearly)"

# Job Offer Comparison

Job Offer Comparison is about choosing well between offers, including the option of staying where you are, on pay, equity, growth, culture, commute and stability rather than on the biggest headline figure. The projects start with foundations (non-negotiables, weighted criteria, a baseline score for your current job and one comparison sheet), then the routines that keep you ready between searches, the research skills for judging equity, contracts, culture and managers, the decisions and events of an offer week, situations such as relocating, caring or a layoff, and finally specialist comparisons for startups, executive roles and long-range scenarios.

What repeats is a Monday review of late-stage processes during a search, monthly checks on recruiter approaches, insider contacts and employer health, a quarterly stay-or-go score and vesting update, and a yearly refresh of your criteria, take-home figures and decision lessons. The **Job application** template pairs with the status board, **Metrics log** with the quarterly scorecard, **Risk register** with the employer health watchlist and **Meeting notes** with offer call write-ups. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
