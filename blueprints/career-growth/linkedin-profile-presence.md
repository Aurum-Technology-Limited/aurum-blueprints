# LinkedIn Profile & Presence

LinkedIn profile and presence covers the page people land on when they look you up and the habit of showing up in their feed often enough to be remembered. The projects start with foundations (an audit, your audience and keywords, photo, headline, About section, experience, settings and proof), then the weekly machinery of posting, commenting and connecting, the writing and format skills behind good posts, decisions such as content themes and Premium, the announcements that matter most, versions for career changers, returners, parents, freelancers and managers, and finally a newsletter and a yearly review.

The rhythms are a weekly post, two short commenting slots and a connection round, a monthly analytics check and congratulations round, a quarterly profile refresh and feed clean-up, and a yearly review of the whole presence. The **Content pipeline** template suits the posting rhythm, **Metrics log** suits the analytics check, **Portfolio piece** suits the Featured section and **Purchase decision** suits the Premium question.
