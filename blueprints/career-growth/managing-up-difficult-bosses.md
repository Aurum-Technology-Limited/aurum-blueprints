# Managing Up & Difficult Bosses

This area is for anyone whose working week depends on one person they did not choose: the manager. It starts with the foundations (what your boss is measured on, how they like to work, priorities and decision rights agreed in writing, an honest look at what kind of difficult you are dealing with and your own part in it), then the weekly machinery of one-to-ones, status notes and early warnings, the skills of disagreeing, saying no and giving feedback upwards, tactics for specific bosses such as the micromanager, the absent boss and the credit taker, set pieces like a new manager arriving, versions for remote workers, parents, graduates and matrix roles, and finally the habits of people who make every boss more effective.

What repeats is the rhythm that keeps a boss informed and a relationship honest: a weekly one-to-one agenda you own, a Friday status note, a midweek risk scan and a private log, monthly checks on priorities, open asks, key dates and whether you still want to stay, and quarterly feedback and relationship reviews. The Person, 1:1 and Feedback themes templates pair with the projects that use them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
