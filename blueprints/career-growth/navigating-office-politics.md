# Navigating Office Politics

Office politics is simply how an organisation decides things when the formal process runs out, and opting out of it usually means other people decide for you. This area is for knowledge workers and managers who want to read that system accurately and take part in it cleanly. It starts with foundations (the real organisation chart, how decisions get made, your stakeholders, their incentives and your own integrity lines), moves through the routines that keep your map current, then the skills of influence and disagreement, the hard calls about battles, repair and factions, the dated moments such as reorganisations, new executives and budget season, versions for remote workers, matrix roles, small firms and public bodies and, last, the work of senior people who shape the political culture itself.

What repeats is light but steady: a weekly look at what has shifted, groundwork before the meetings that matter, a monthly cross-functional coffee, monthly updates to your stakeholder log and favours ledger, and a quarterly redraw of the power map. The Person template suits the stakeholder log, Meeting notes suits groundwork conversations and the Risk register suits reorganisations and mergers.
