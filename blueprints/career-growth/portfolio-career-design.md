# Portfolio Career Design

Portfolio career design is for anyone whose working week is made of several pieces: a part-time role plus two clients, a creative practice funded by teaching and contract work, or two or three fractional senior jobs at once. The projects start with foundations (a map of your strands, an honest time and energy audit, an income floor, the shape you want and the contracts, accounts and conflict checks that make it legal and calm), then the operating routines that keep money and time flowing, the skills of pricing, scoping and switching between roles, and the decisions about which strands to grow, change or drop. Later projects cover dated moments such as your first year end and a fully covered holiday, situations like parenting, a creative practice or a fractional executive mix, and finish with specialist work on associate networks, intellectual property and five-year scenarios.

The rhythms are a Monday planning session and a daily shutdown note, weekly invoicing and pipeline checks, a monthly income ledger, tax set-aside and admin block, and a quarterly look at whether the mix still matches the shape you chose. The **Metrics log** template suits the income ledger, **Purchase decision** suits choosing your tools, and the **Risk register** suits concentration and lean-month planning. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
