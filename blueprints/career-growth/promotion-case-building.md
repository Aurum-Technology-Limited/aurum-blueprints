              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Most promotion decisions are made against a document, a career ladder or level guide, that many candidates have skimmed once and never quoted. Getting the current version for both your level and the next one, and reading them side by side, turns a vague ambition into a list of specific behaviours a committee will look for.

                ## Milestones
                1. The current level guide or career ladder for your role family found, with its version date.
                2. The descriptions for your current level and the target level copied into one document, side by side.
                3. Every phrase that changes between the two levels highlighted.
                4. Any criterion you cannot interpret written down as a question for your manager.

                ## Notes
                If no written ladder exists, see the project on promotion in a company without a formal ladder. Ask HR or your manager whether the published guide is the one calibration actually uses; some firms have a newer internal version.

# Promotion Case Building

This area is for anyone who wants the next title and would rather earn it with a case than wait to be noticed: knowledge workers, engineers on a technical ladder and managers stepping up to run bigger teams. It starts with the foundations (the written criteria, the process, an honest gap score and an agreed plan), moves through the monthly machinery that keeps evidence and visibility building, then the writing and presenting skills, the decisions about what to put in the case, the cycle itself from submission to answer, versions for particular situations and, last, the work of people who write and judge cases for others.

What repeats is deliberately small: a monthly evidence-to-criteria mapping, a promotion slot in your 1:1s, a monthly impact number for flagship work, a monthly update to your skip-level, and quarterly gap reviews, peer feedback and sponsor briefings, with a yearly reset when the cycle calendar comes out. The Skills matrix, Development plan, 1:1, Feedback themes and Metrics log templates pair with the projects that use them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
