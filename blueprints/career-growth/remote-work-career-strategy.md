                ## Purpose
                Remote covers everything from a fully distributed company with no office to a hybrid team that expects you in three days a week, and job adverts blur the difference. Writing down what you actually need, such as the number of office days you will accept, the earliest and latest meeting hours you can work and whether you want async or meeting-heavy culture, gives you a filter for every role and every conversation with your current employer.

                ## Milestones

                2. The maximum office days per month and maximum travel days per year you will accept written down.
                3. Your workable meeting window stated in your own time zone.
                4. The list shared with anyone who helps your search, such as a partner or a recruiter.

                ## Notes
                Be honest about why you want remote work. Commute, caring duties, focus and location each lead to different roles, and the reason shapes which compromises you can live with.

# Remote Work Career Strategy

This area is for knowledge workers and engineers who want to work away from the office without falling off the radar: people hunting for a genuinely remote role and people already remote who want to keep growing. It starts with foundations (what kind of remote you want, where you can legally be hired, a set-up and connection that hold up, and a working agreement with your manager), then the weekly and monthly machinery of visibility and relationships, the written and on-camera skills remote work runs on, the decisions about offers, pay and return-to-office mandates, the dated moments such as remote interviews and offsites, versions for parents, hybrid minorities, new managers and workers employed across borders, and finally the work of senior people shaping how a distributed organisation operates.

What repeats is a weekly written update and work log, a weekly look at remote job boards while you are searching, monthly virtual coffees and demos, a quarterly plan for in-person time and a quarterly remote career check, with a yearly reset of your non-negotiables and event plan. The Purchase decision, 1:1, Job application, Operational checklist, Onboarding 30/60/90 and Development plan templates pair with the projects that use them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
