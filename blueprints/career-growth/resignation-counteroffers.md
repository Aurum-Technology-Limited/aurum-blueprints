            - name: Contract notice and leaving terms check
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Before you say a word to your manager, read what you signed. Your contract, any later variation letters and the staff handbook set your notice period, whether the employer can put you on garden leave or pay in lieu, how unused holiday is treated and which restrictions follow you out. Knowing these in advance stops a surprise in the resignation meeting from changing your start date.

                ## Milestones
                1. The latest signed contract, any variation letters and the current handbook gathered in one folder.
                2. Your notice period, how it must be given (in writing, to whom) and when it starts counting written on one page.
                3. Garden leave, pay in lieu, holiday on leaving and any restrictive covenant clauses quoted word for word.
                4. A list of anything unclear, with a decision on whether to ask HR neutrally or an employment adviser.

                ## Notes
                If the contract and the handbook disagree, note both versions and ask an adviser which applies. Do not ask HR pointed questions about notice the week before you resign unless you are comfortable that it signals your plans.

            - name: Unconditional offer confirmed before resigning
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                A verbal yes from the new employer is not a job. Offers are often conditional on references, background or credit checks, right to work, medical clearance or a signed contract, and people who resign on a conditional offer occasionally find themselves with neither job. Confirming in writing that every condition is cleared is the single most protective step in this area.

                ## Milestones
                1. A written offer and contract received, with salary, title, start date and conditions stated.
                2. Each condition listed with its status: cleared, pending or waived, confirmed in writing by the new employer.
                3. A signed contract returned and a countersigned copy or written acceptance received.
                4. A dated note recording the moment the offer became unconditional.

                ## Notes
                If the new employer wants your current manager as a referee, ask whether that reference can be taken after you resign and whether the offer is unconditional apart from it.
              priority: high

              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Resigning a few weeks too early can cost a year's bonus, a vesting tranche or a pension match, and some contracts ask you to repay a sign-on bonus, relocation costs or training fees if you leave within a set period. Putting a figure and a date against each item shows what leaving costs and what a new employer may be asked to cover.

                ## Milestones

                4. A total at risk calculated for three possible resignation dates.

                ## Notes

            - name: Counteroffer walk-away terms set in advance
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Counteroffers arrive fast and land on the emotions: flattery, guilt and a bigger number in the same five minutes. Deciding beforehand what you would need to stay, if anything, and what you will say while you think, keeps the decision yours rather than the meeting's.

                ## Milestones
                1. A clear position recorded: not open to a counteroffer, or open only if specific conditions are met.
                2. If open, the conditions written as concrete terms such as title, pay, team or start date for a change.
                3. A holding sentence ready that buys time without signalling interest, for example asking to reply within two working days.
                4. Your position shared with one trusted person who will ask you about it after the meeting.

                ## Notes
                Using a new offer only to force a pay rise is a known move, but the new employer has invested in you and the market is small. If a raise is all you want, a direct conversation about pay usually serves you better.
              priority: high
              frontmatter:
                mode: building
                output_kind: decision
                success_criteria: "A written counteroffer position, with any stay conditions and a holding sentence, exists before the resignation meeting."
                cadence: one-shot

              tasks:

                4. A signed or emailed version ready to send straight after the conversation with your manager.

                ## Notes

# Resignation & Counteroffers

This area is for the stretch between deciding to leave and walking out on the last day with your relationships intact. It starts with the checks that belong before you resign (notice terms, a truly unconditional offer, the bonus and equity at stake, your reasons written down), moves through the notice period machinery of handover, knowledge transfer and stakeholder updates, then the skills of reading covenants and declining a counteroffer, the decisions and events around resignation day, exit interview and last day, and ends with situations such as resigning on a visa or after parental leave, managing a resignation from your own team, and senior, competitor and shareholder exits.

What repeats is mostly short-lived: weekly handover, transfer and stakeholder reviews during notice, a daily shutdown note in the final fortnight, monthly payslip checks after you leave and a monthly review if you accepted a counteroffer. Two habits last longer: a quarterly refresh of your exit-ready file and a living role handbook, plus a quarterly keep-in-touch rhythm with former colleagues. The Meeting notes, Onboarding plan, Operational checklist and 1:1 templates pair with the projects that point at them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
