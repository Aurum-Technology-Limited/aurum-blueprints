            - name: Readiness decision for going back to work
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Going back because a date arrived, rather than because the household, your health and your energy are ready, is the most common reason a return stalls in the first three months. Answering a short set of honest questions about why, when and how many hours, and talking them through with the people at home, turns a vague intention into a decision you can plan around.

                ## Milestones
                1. Your main reasons for returning written in three or four lines, ranked.
                2. A target window for starting work agreed, such as a season or a month range.
                3. The hours you can realistically work stated as a weekly number.
                4. The decision talked through with a partner, family member or close friend and their concerns noted.

                ## Notes
                Readiness is rarely total. The aim is a decision with known gaps, not waiting until every worry has gone.
              priority: high
              deadlineOffsetDays: 14

# Returning After a Career Break

Returning after a career break covers the months before and after going back to paid work, whether you stepped away to raise children, care for a relative, recover from illness or travel. The projects start with foundations (readiness, the household money picture, care cover, the gap story and a list of people to reconnect with), then the weekly and monthly routines of a return search, the skills refresh, decisions about hours, routes and references, and the dated moments from a returnship application to the first day back. Later projects cover specific returns for parents, carers, people recovering from illness and long-term travellers, and finish with return-to-practice routes in regulated professions and helping other returners.

The rhythms are a weekly search session and wins note, a Sunday look at industry news, a weekly household reset once you are working, a monthly reconnection and returner programme check, a monthly budget look and a quarterly progress review. The **Resume** template suits the gap-friendly CV, **Job application** suits returnship rounds, **Purchase decision** suits the work kit, **Onboarding plan** suits the first month back and **1:1** suits the check-ins with your new manager. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
