                ## Purpose
                Most side projects are chosen because the idea is fun, then sit in a portfolio that no hiring manager connects to the role you want. Writing down the specific door you want opened in the next 18 months, a move to data engineering, a design lead role, a first client in a new sector, gives every later choice of idea, stack and audience a test to pass.

                ## Milestones

                2. Three gaps between your current record and that target listed, such as no shipped mobile work or no public writing.
                3. The kind of evidence that would close each gap named, for example a live app with users or a published dataset.
                4. The goal pinned at the top of your side project notes.

                ## Notes

            - name: Employment contract and IP clause check
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Many employment contracts claim ownership of anything you create that relates to the employer's business, and some claim anything made on their equipment or in working hours. Reading the intellectual property, confidentiality and outside work clauses before you build means you know what you own, rather than discovering it when a project gets traction or you try to sell it.

                ## Milestones
                1. The intellectual property, inventions, confidentiality and outside activities clauses located in your contract and staff handbook.
                2. A plain-language summary written of what the employer claims and under what conditions.
                3. Any unclear wording listed as questions for HR or an employment lawyer.
                4. A decision recorded on which project ideas are safe, need permission or are off limits.

                ## Notes
                This is about reading and asking, not legal conclusions. Rules on employee inventions differ by country and by contract; if a project could become valuable, get the answer from HR in writing or from an employment lawyer.
              priority: high
              deadlineOffsetDays: 10

                output_kind: knowledge

# Side Projects for Career Leverage

This area is for engineers, creatives and knowledge workers who build things outside the day job and want those evenings to count for the career, not just fill a folder of half-finished repos. It starts with foundations (the goal a project should serve, the contract and conflict checks, a time budget, a scored idea list and a one-page brief), then the weekly and monthly routines that keep a project moving, the skills that make a project legible to a hiring manager, decisions about scope, licences, collaborators and endings, the dated moments when a project pays out, variants for parents, job seekers, managers and different crafts, and finally the work of maintainers and people with a decade of projects behind them.

What repeats is a protected Saturday build session, a Monday backlog trim, a midweek build log, a twice-monthly changelog, monthly reviews of progress, energy, evidence and user feedback, and a quarterly keep, pivot or archive call. The Product spec, Portfolio piece, Metrics log and Risk register templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
