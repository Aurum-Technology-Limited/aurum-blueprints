            - name: Naming what is wrong at work
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                A bad quarter, a demanding manager and a genuinely damaging workplace feel similar at 11pm, but they call for different responses. Writing down the specific patterns you see (shouting, blame shifting, unpaid overtime expected, colleagues leaving in a steady stream, being cut out of meetings) turns a fog of dread into something you can judge, and tells you whether an exit plan or a fix is the right project.

                ## Milestones
                1. A one-page list of the specific behaviours and conditions that are causing harm, each with a rough start date.
                2. Each item marked as a person problem, a team problem or an organisation-wide problem.
                3. A note of what has already been tried to change things, and what happened.
                4. A written conclusion: plan an exit, try one more fix with a date, or wait and watch for a set period.

                ## Notes
                Keep the list factual. "Was told in front of the team that my work was useless" is more useful later than "my manager hates me".
              priority: high
              deadlineOffsetDays: 14
              frontmatter:
                mode: research
                output_kind: decision
                success_criteria: "A dated page listing the harmful patterns, their scope and past attempts to fix them, ending in a written conclusion about what to do next."
                cadence: one-shot
                effort_hours_estimate: "2"
              tasks:
                - "Write ten specific things about the job that make you dread going in"
                - "Mark each one as a person, team or organisation problem"
                - "Note what you have already tried and how it went"
                - "Write one sentence deciding whether to plan an exit, try one fix or watch"
            - name: Target exit date and fallback date
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Without a date, an exit plan drifts into another year of "after the busy season". Choosing a target date, plus a later fallback date by which you will leave even without a new job if things have not improved, gives the search a deadline and gives you a light at the end of the week when it gets bad.

                ## Milestones
                1. A target date for starting a new role, chosen with your notice period and hiring timelines in mind.
                2. A fallback date written down, with the conditions under which you would leave without a job lined up.
                3. Two or three tripwires noted that would bring the date forward, such as a health warning from your doctor.
                4. Both dates shared with one person you trust, who will ask you about them.

                ## Notes
                Most professional searches take three to six months from first application to start date, and senior roles longer. Work backwards from that, not from how you feel today.
              priority: high
              deadlineOffsetDays: 30
              frontmatter:
                mode: building
                output_kind: decision
                success_criteria: "A target date, a fallback date and at least two tripwires are written down and have been shared with one trusted person."
                cadence: one-shot
                effort_hours_estimate: "2"
              tasks:
                - "Look up your notice period and count back from a realistic start date"
                - "Pick a target date and a later fallback date"
                - "Write the tripwires that would make you leave sooner"
                - "Tell one trusted person both dates and ask them to check in"
            - name: Private incident record kept off work systems
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Memories of hostile meetings blur within weeks, and if you later need to explain why you left, negotiate an exit or raise a formal concern, a dated factual record is worth far more than a strong feeling. Keeping it on a personal device, not a work laptop or work email, means it stays yours if access is cut suddenly.

                ## Milestones
                1. A private document or notebook set up on a personal device or on paper at home.
                2. Past incidents written up with date, time, who was present, what was said and any witnesses.
                3. A simple format chosen so each new entry takes under five minutes.
                4. A rule written at the top: facts, quotes and effects only, no commentary.

                ## Notes
                Do not forward confidential company documents to personal accounts to support your record; that can breach your contract. Note that a document exists and where it is instead.
              priority: high
              deadlineOffsetDays: 7
              frontmatter:
                mode: building
                output_kind: artifact
                success_criteria: "A private, dated incident record exists outside work systems, with past incidents written up in a consistent factual format."
                cadence: one-shot

              tasks:
                - "Create a private document on a personal device for the record"
                - "Write up the three worst incidents you remember with dates and witnesses"
                - "Add a fixed entry format with date, people, words used and effect on you"
                - "Check that nothing in the record is copied from confidential work files"

            - name: Exit cash runway calculation
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Money is what keeps people in damaging jobs long after their health says leave. Knowing exactly how many months your savings would cover essentials, and what that number could become in six months of saving, turns a vague fear into a figure you can plan around and grow.

                ## Notes
                Three to six months of essentials is a common target for leaving without a job lined up. Decide your own number with your household, not from a rule.
              priority: high

            - name: Protective boundaries while you plan

                ## Purpose

Setting a few firm limits now (a log-off time, no work chat on the phone at weekends, a lunch break away from the desk) protects the hours your search depends on without giving anyone grounds to complain about your work.

# Toxic Workplace Exit Plan

Toxic workplace exit plan is for anyone whose job is wearing down their health, confidence or finances and who wants to leave on their own terms rather than in a crisis. The projects start with foundations (naming what is happening, setting an exit date, a private record, cash runway, a health baseline and your contract terms), then the weekly routines that keep a search going while you are still at work, the skills for getting through hostile meetings and explaining your departure, the big decisions about staying, going with or without a job and references, and the dated events from the resignation meeting to your first month somewhere better. Later projects cover particular situations, such as being the main earner, holding a work visa, leaving a family business or a small job market, and end with harassment, regulated professions and the recovery after you have gone.

The rhythms are a weekly exit review and evidence note, twice-weekly search sessions, a weekly reconnection message, monthly savings transfers and health check-ins, and a short daily wind-down after work. The **Savings goal** template suits the exit fund, **Job application** suits each role you pursue, **Habit tracker** suits the recovery routine and **Sleep review** suits the monthly health check-in. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
