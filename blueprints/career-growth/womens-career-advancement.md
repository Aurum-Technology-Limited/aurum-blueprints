# Women's Career Advancement

Women's career advancement covers the patterns that slow women down at work and the moves that keep progress going: pay that drifts below peers, promotions judged on proof rather than potential, a calendar full of office housework, vague feedback, and the years of pregnancy, parenthood, caring and menopause. The projects start with foundations (a two-year direction, a pay check, the real promotion criteria, a calendar audit and your workplace policies), then the routines that keep you visible and sponsored, the skills of negotiating, saying no and holding the floor, decisions about titles, roles and employers, and dated moments such as calibration, parental leave and a leadership programme. Later projects cover life stages from pregnancy to menopause and finish with the work of a woman manager who sponsors, allocates and pays her own team fairly.

The rhythms are a weekly credit check and meeting preparation, a Sunday household reset, monthly touchpoints with sponsors and a peer amplification circle, a monthly scan for stretch work and a quarterly pay and progression check, with a yearly self-advocacy calendar. The **Metrics log** template suits the pay and allocation records, **Development plan** suits the two-year direction, **Onboarding plan** suits the first month back from leave, **1:1** suits sponsor and manager conversations and **Feedback themes** suits the review language audit. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
