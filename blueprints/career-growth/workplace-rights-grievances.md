              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Memory fades and gets challenged, while a note written the same day with dates, words used and witnesses present is hard to dismiss. Keeping one plain log, entered weekly at minimum and the same day for anything serious, turns a vague sense of unfairness into a record an adviser or investigator can work with.

                ## Milestones

                4. Entries kept factual, with feelings noted separately from events.

# Workplace Rights & Grievances

Workplace rights and grievances is for anyone who suspects they are underpaid, treated unfairly, discriminated against or put at risk at work, and wants to deal with it properly rather than stew or blow up. The projects start with foundations (your contract and policies, a dated incident log, your own documents, the time limits and independent advice), then the routines that keep evidence and pay records straight, the knowledge behind discrimination, equal pay, harassment and whistleblowing, the decisions from an informal word to a formal grievance, a settlement or an external claim, and the dated hearings, interviews and appeals. Later projects cover particular situations, such as pregnancy, disability, agency and migrant work, a first job or a collective complaint, and finish with representatives, managers and people who have had a grievance raised against them.

The rhythms are a weekly incident log entry and hours record, a weekly case file update while a grievance is open, a monthly payslip check, a quarterly look at leave balances and unresolved issues, and a yearly refresh of the rules and your policies. The **Metrics log** template suits the payslip and hours checks, **Meeting notes** suits every hearing, interview and informal conversation, and the **Risk register** template suits a watch for retaliation after you raise a concern. None of this is legal advice: it is the preparation that makes advice from a union, an advice service or an employment lawyer quicker and cheaper. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
