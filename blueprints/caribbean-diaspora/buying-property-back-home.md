# Buying Land or a House Back Home

This area is for anyone living abroad who wants to own land or a house back home, whether that is a first house lot in Clarendon, a retirement home in Grenada or a place for parents in Berbice. It starts with the foundations (a written brief, an all-in budget, the rules for your passport, your own attorney, a trusted person on the ground and the scams to watch for), then the routines of owning from a distance, what to learn about titles, surveys, planning and building, the checks and decisions of the purchase itself, completion and the build milestones, family situations, and finally letting, phased and off-plan builds and rescuing a stalled project.

What repeats is the paperwork of owning from abroad: a monthly savings transfer, a weekly call with your attorney while the sale is live, monthly payment reconciling, caretaker reports and drawdowns, quarterly boundary walks, and a yearly round of land tax, insurance and a title search. The Risk register, Vendor, Savings goal, Purchase decision, Trip and Remediation plan templates pair with the projects that name them.
