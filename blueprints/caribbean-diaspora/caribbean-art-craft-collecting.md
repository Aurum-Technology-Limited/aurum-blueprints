# Caribbean Art and Craft Collecting

This area is for anyone who wants Caribbean art and craft on their walls and shelves with some thought behind it, whether that means a first painting bought from a studio in Kingston or Port of Spain, Haitian metalwork carried home from Croix-des-Bouquets, or a growing collection built from London, New York or Toronto. It starts with foundations (a collecting focus, a budget, a catalogue, photographs, paperwork, insurance and safe display), then the routines that keep you looking, saving and checking condition, the knowledge that makes you a sharper buyer, decisions about first purchases, commissions, framing, shipping and fair dealing, the trips, fairs and auctions worth planning, variants for small budgets, working artists, inherited pieces and collectors abroad, and finally provenance, appraisal, patronage and handing the collection on.

What repeats is a monthly gallery or studio visit, a monthly look at your artist watchlist, a monthly transfer to the art pot and a paperwork catch-up, a quarterly condition check, a yearly rehang, valuation and collection review, and a few yearly calendar and support tasks. The Savings goal, Reading queue, Purchase decision, Vendor and Trip templates pair with the art pot, reading list, first purchase, framer and buying trip projects. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
