              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Divali, Eid, Phagwa, Hosay and Easter all move each year because they follow lunar, Hindu or church calendars, and Eid can shift by a day depending on the moon sighting your masjid follows. Knowing how each date is set, and who announces it, stops the scramble when a holiday lands a week earlier than last year. It also explains why your cousins back home sometimes celebrate a day apart from you.

                ## Milestones

                2. The body or calendar that announces each date noted, such as your mandir, masjid or church.
                3. The likely date range for each festival written down for the next three years.
                4. A rule agreed for the house on which announcement you follow for Eid.

                ## Notes
                In Trinidad and Tobago the government gazettes the Divali and Eid public holiday dates, which can differ from the date your local temple or masjid uses abroad. Check both.

# Caribbean Festivals and Holidays

This area is for anyone who wants the Caribbean calendar to mean something in their own house, whether that house is in San Fernando, Georgetown and Kingston or in Croydon, Queens and Scarborough. It starts with the foundations (one festival calendar, leave, a budget, the family's own traditions and where to find supplies and congregations), moves to the yearly routines for the Christmas, Divali, Ramadan and August seasons, then the history and skills behind Emancipation, Independence, Indian Arrival, Hosay, Phagwa, parang and Easter kites, the decisions about safety, work and mixed households, the nights you host, the harder situations such as a first festival alone or after a loss, and finally community flag raisings, public events and a parang group of your own.

What repeats is a monthly look-ahead at the coming festivals, a January reset of the year's dates, a monthly festival savings transfer, greetings checked before each month, the seasonal routines for Christmas, Divali, Ramadan and August, a quarterly look at your after-festival notes and a monthly photo sort. The Savings goal, Operational checklist and Risk register templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
