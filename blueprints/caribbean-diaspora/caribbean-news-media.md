# Caribbean News and Media

This area is for anyone who wants to follow home properly from abroad, or follow the whole region from one island, without drowning in forwarded rumours. It starts with foundations (a map of the papers, stations and official channels for your country, a trusted source list, subscriptions, alerts, home radio online, family group rules and a plan for breaking crises), moves through the routines that keep you informed, the verification and reading skills that keep you from being fooled, decisions about podcasts, follows and live TV, dated moments such as election night and budget day, plans for older relatives, children, students, newcomers and returning residents, and finally the specialist work of tipping off reporters, writing, publishing and researching old newspapers.

What repeats is a short daily headline check, a weekly digest, death notices and long reads, a weekly fact-check log and talk radio slot, a monthly look at the home economy, your clippings and your media diet, a quarterly source refresh and a yearly review of subscriptions and habits. The Purchase decision, Reading queue, Habit tracker and Content pipeline templates pair with subscriptions and streaming kit, the long reads queue, the fact-check habit and any newsletter or podcast you start. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
