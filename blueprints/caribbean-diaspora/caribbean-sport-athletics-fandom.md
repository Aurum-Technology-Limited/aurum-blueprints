# Caribbean Athletics and Sport Following

This area is for anyone who follows Caribbean athletes and teams, at home or across the water, and for those who still lace up and play. It starts with the foundations (the teams and athletes you follow, a championship calendar, legal ways to watch, a budget and a club back home worth backing), then the weekly and monthly routines of an organised fan, the rules and history that make every final richer, decisions about viewing, trips and scams, the events worth planning from a living-room watch party to Penn Relays and Champs, situations for children, returning players and older relatives, and finally the specialist work of supporters' clubs, officiating and backing young athletes.

What repeats is a Monday round-up of results, a monthly viewing plan and season log, a standing donation to a club back home, two training sessions a week if you play, and a yearly look at subscriptions and the memorabilia archive. The Savings goal, Metrics log, Training program, Reading queue, Purchase decision and Trip templates pair with the projects that name them.
