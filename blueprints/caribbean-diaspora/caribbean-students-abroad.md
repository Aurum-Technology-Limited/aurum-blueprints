# Caribbean Students Studying Abroad

This area is for Caribbean students heading abroad to study, and for the parents paying, worrying and cheering from home, whether the destination is London, Toronto, Miami or a scholarship in Havana. It runs from the first decisions (why, where, what it costs and how your CSEC and CAPE results count) through applications, scholarships and offers, the visa, housing and first days abroad, the routines of a working term, the skills and choices of settling in, the big dates, plans for parents, mature students, athletes and others, and finally postgraduate funding, research and the choice to stay on or come home.

What repeats is a weekly call home, study plan, spending log and wellbeing check, a monthly budget review, allowance transfer and Caribbean community night, termly checks on grades and visa dates, and a yearly sponsor report and Christmas flight search. The Trip, Onboarding 30/60/90, Monthly budget and bills, Job application and Remediation plan templates pair with the departure, first ninety days, budget, part-time work and failed module projects.
