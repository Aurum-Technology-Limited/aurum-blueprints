                4. The officiant's role confirmed: who may solemnise and who registers the marriage afterwards.

                ## Notes
                Write down the date you checked and the name of anyone you spoke to. Rules and fees change, and an email confirmation is worth keeping.

# Caribbean Wedding Back Home

Caribbean Wedding Back Home is for couples living in London, Toronto, New York or anywhere else who want to say their vows in Port of Spain, Kingston, Bridgetown, Georgetown, Castries or the village their grandmother came from, and for the parents and aunties who end up running half of it. It starts with the big choices (island, date, licence rules, documents, budget and guest list), moves through the weekly and monthly routines that keep a wedding organised from three thousand miles away, the traditions and paperwork worth learning properly, the vendor and venue decisions, the dated events from the planning visit to the wedding day, situations such as a second marriage, a yard wedding or joining two faiths, and finally the specialist work of large hotel contracts, small-island logistics and a full multi-day Indo-Caribbean wedding.

The rhythm is a weekly planning hour with your partner, a weekly call with your people on the island, a Monday RSVP check in the final months, and monthly rounds for the budget, deposits, receipts, the savings deposit and the countdown checklist, with black cake fed weekly once it is baked. The Savings goal, Operational checklist, Vendor, Purchase decision, Risk register and Trip templates pair with it. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
