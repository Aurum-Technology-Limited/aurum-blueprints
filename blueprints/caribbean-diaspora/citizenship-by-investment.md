# Citizenship by Investment Programmes

This area is for anyone weighing a Caribbean citizenship by investment programme, whether for a family plan B, easier travel or a link to the region, and for Caribbean people who want to understand a policy that funds much of their own country. It starts with foundations (your reasons, the five programmes, your own country's rules, the full cost, the family and the paperwork), builds knowledge of routes, due diligence, visa-free access and scams, then works through the decisions on programme, route, agent and money, the application from police certificates to the oath, life after the passport, family situations, and the specialist ground of refusals, revocation, resale and regional reform.

The rhythm is mostly quiet once the passport arrives: a weekly tracker update and monthly chase while an application is open, a monthly ledger reconciliation, monthly checks on programme notices and visa news, quarterly checks on visa-free access, expiry dates and any real estate income, and a yearly obligations review. The Vendor, Purchase decision, Operational checklist and Risk register templates pair with the projects that point at them.
