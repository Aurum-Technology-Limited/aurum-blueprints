# Crop Over and Junkanoo

Crop Over and Junkanoo is for anyone who wants to wuk up on the road to Spring Garden on Kadooment Day or rush down Bay Street before dawn on Boxing Day, whether you live in Bridgetown or Nassau or are flying in from London, Brooklyn or Toronto. It starts with the season map, the budget and choosing a Kadooment band or Junkanoo group, moves through the routines that carry you from band launch or the first shack night to the parade, the skills worth learning from cowbell to tuk drum to crepe pasting, the smaller decisions about sections, seats and getting around, the big nights themselves, situations such as bringing children or coming home for the first time, and the work seasoned members take on, from marshalling a section to building a lead piece.

The rhythm follows two calendars: a monthly saving deposit and costume instalment, weekly shack nights and practices from autumn for Junkanoo, a Wednesday fete check and Friday listening through the Crop Over summer, a Monday check-in while either season is live, and a yearly review after Kadooment and again after New Year's Day. The Savings goal, Purchase decision, Trip, Training program and Operational checklist templates pair with it. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
