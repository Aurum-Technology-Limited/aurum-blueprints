              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Most Caribbean people living abroad qualify for more groups than they realise: a past students' or old scholars association for their secondary school (many schools call their alumni old boys or old girls), a national association for their island, a village or parish group, church and professional networks, and university alumni chapters. Listing the ones active in your city, with what each actually does and how often it meets, lets you join the one or two that fit your time instead of drifting between half a dozen WhatsApp groups.

                ## Milestones
                1. A list of at least five groups you are eligible for, with city, contact and meeting pattern.
                2. Each group's main activity noted: social, fundraising for home, member welfare, networking or a mix.
                3. One or two groups chosen to join this year, with the reason in a sentence.
                4. A first contact made with the secretary or membership officer of each chosen group.

                ## Notes
                Schools in Trinidad and Tobago, Jamaica, Barbados and Guyana often have chapters in New York, Toronto and London; if yours has none nearby, ask the home association for members in your city. Consulates and high commissions usually keep a list of national associations they work with.

            - name: Reading and refreshing the constitution
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Plenty of associations run on a constitution typed decades ago that nobody has read since: it may name officer roles that no longer exist, require a quorum of 40 at meetings that now draw 15, or say nothing about online meetings and votes. Reading it against how the group really works, and putting amendments to members, prevents an election or a spending decision being challenged later.

                ## Milestones
                1. The signed constitution and every later amendment found and kept in one file.
                2. A list of clauses the group no longer follows, set beside how it actually works today.
                3. Amendments drafted for online meetings, quorum, voting and how officers are removed.
                4. Amendments voted on at a general meeting under the constitution's own rules for change, and the new version filed.

                ## Notes
                If the association is a registered charity or nonprofit, the regulator may need to approve or be told about changes to certain clauses. Check before the vote, not after.
              priority: high
              deadlineOffsetDays: 90
              frontmatter:
                mode: building
                output_kind: artifact
                success_criteria: "An amended constitution covering online meetings, quorum and officer removal has been approved at a general meeting and filed with any regulator that needs it."
                cadence: phased
                effort_hours_estimate: "12"
              tasks:
                - "Ask past secretaries for the signed constitution and every amendment"
                - "Mark each clause the association no longer follows in practice"
                - "Ask the agent to draft plain wording for online meeting and quorum clauses"
                - "Circulate the proposed amendments with the general meeting notice"

              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Membership records split between the treasurer's spreadsheet, the group chat and an old exercise book make it impossible to say who is a paid-up member with a vote. One register with name, contact, year group or home district, membership type, last payment and consent to be contacted is the base for renewals, elections and welfare, and data protection law in many countries expects it to be held with care.

                ## Milestones

                2. Each record showing membership type, date of last payment and how the member agreed to be contacted.

                ## Notes
                In the UK and the EU this falls under data protection law, and other countries have their own rules. Collect only what you use, and ask members to confirm their details once a year.

            - name: Association bank account with two signatories
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Dues and ticket money paid into a member's personal account is the most common start of a falling out, even when everyone is honest. An account in the association's name, with two signatories from different households and a rule that every payment needs both, protects the money and the people handling it.

                ## Milestones
                1. A bank or credit union account opened in the association's name.
                2. Two or three signatories from different households named in committee minutes.
                3. All dues, ticket sales and donations routed to that account only.
                4. Signatories changed at the bank within a month of any officer change.

                ## Notes
                Banks usually ask for the constitution, minutes appointing the signatories and identity documents for each. Some offer community accounts free of monthly fees, so compare two before choosing.

            - name: Charity or nonprofit registration decision
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Registering as a charity or nonprofit can let donors claim tax relief, opens doors to grants and adds weight with the school or community back home, but it also brings annual returns, rules on spending and duties for trustees. Comparing the options using the regulator's own guidance and one adviser, before a big appeal, means the choice is made on purpose and not after a donor asks for a receipt the group cannot give.

                ## Milestones
                1. The regulator's thresholds and requirements written down, for example the Charity Commission in England and Wales, 501(c)(3) status with the IRS in the US or the CRA in Canada.
                2. Costs, yearly duties and benefits compared with staying an unregistered members' club.
                3. Advice taken from a charity adviser, an accountant or a larger association that has registered.
                4. A decision minuted and, if registering, the application submitted.

                ## Notes
                Registration abroad does not make the association a charity in the Caribbean country, and gifts to a school there may need their own paperwork. Ask your adviser about both ends.
              priority: high
              deadlineOffsetDays: 120
              frontmatter:
                mode: research
                output_kind: decision
                success_criteria: "The committee has minuted a decision on registration, based on the regulator's guidance and one adviser's view, and submitted the application if it chose to register."

# Diaspora Associations and Alumni Groups

This area is for anyone who belongs to, or wants to run, a group that keeps Caribbean people abroad connected to each other and to home: a past students' or old scholars association for a secondary school, an island or national association, or a village, parish or district group. It starts with the foundations (finding the right group, the constitution, officer roles, the register, a proper bank account and the registration question), then the routines that keep a group healthy, the skills of chairing, minuting, treasury and fundraising, decisions about dues, projects and money sent home, the big events from the dinner and dance to a reunion back home, plans for newcomers, young alumni, retirees and returnees, and finally grants, restricted funds and succession.

What repeats is the monthly committee meeting, the treasurer's reconciliation, register updates, welcome calls and filing, a weekly check for welfare news, quarterly calls with the school or home community, the newsletter and event rotas, and the yearly AGM, renewal drive, insurance and charity return. The Meeting notes, Purchase decision, Remediation plan, Operational checklist, Trip and Marketing campaign templates pair with the committee meeting, payments platform, dispute, dinner and dance, reunion and online appeal projects.
