                ## Purpose
                Notting Hill on the August bank holiday, the Labor Day parade on Brooklyn's Eastern Parkway, Toronto's Caribana weekend in early August and Miami Broward in October each have a different feel, cost and crowd. # Diaspora Carnivals Abroad

Diaspora carnivals in London, Brooklyn, Toronto, Miami, Leeds and beyond bring the Caribbean road to the cities where the family now lives, and they reward planning as much as any carnival back home. This area starts with the foundations, choosing a carnival, a budget, leave, a bed and a safety plan, then builds the yearly machinery of savings, band launches, crew roles and deposits, the knowledge behind the road, band and costume decisions, the big events from J'ouvert to parade day, plans for children, elders, first-timers and solo masqueraders, and finally volunteering, section leading and starting a band of your own.

The rhythm is monthly through the year: savings on payday, costume instalments, crew check-ins and band news, with weekly walks and a Sunday countdown in the final weeks and a yearly review once the costume is put away. The Savings goal, Operational checklist, Training program, Purchase decision, Trip and Risk register templates pair with the savings pot, road kit, stamina plan, band shortlist, overseas trip and new band projects.
