# Immigration Status and Windrush Claims

This area is for Caribbean families abroad who need their immigration paperwork in order, from the Windrush generation (Caribbean people who settled in Britain between 1948 and 1973, named after the first ship that brought them) to green card and permanent residence holders in the US and Canada. It starts with foundations (a household status table, the arrival timeline, a master folder, records requests, a regulated adviser and year-by-year residence proof), then the routines that keep status current, what to learn about the Windrush schemes, absences abroad and status checks, the applications and claims themselves, appointment and ceremony days, situations from elderly parents to separated partners, and finally escalation, family-wide planning and helping others in your community.

The rhythm is steady rather than heavy: a monthly look at expiry dates, the travel log and rule changes, a weekly check while any application is open, quarterly checks of the document folder, online account and contact details, and a yearly evidence top-up and family review. The Meeting notes, Operational checklist and Person templates pair with the adviser, appointment day and elderly parent projects.
