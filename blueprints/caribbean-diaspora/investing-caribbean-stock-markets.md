# Investing in Caribbean Stock Markets

Owning shares in Caribbean companies, the banks, insurers, conglomerates and manufacturers listed on exchanges such as the TTSE in Port of Spain, the JSE in Kingston and the Barbados exchange, is a way of keeping a stake in home that pays dividends. This area starts with the foundations, a map of the exchanges, a licensed broker, the account paperwork and a way to fund it, then builds the routines that keep holdings in order, the knowledge that makes thin regional markets less mysterious, the decisions and dated events such as IPOs, AGMs and selling, situations from a child's share account to an investment club, and the specialist work of cross-listings and total return.

The rhythm is monthly and yearly: a contribution day, a month-end value log, an exchange rate check and dividend notices each month, a weekly scan of exchange notices, quarterly results, and a yearly statement reconciliation, tax pack, fee audit and portfolio review.
