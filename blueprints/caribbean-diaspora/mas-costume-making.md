              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Hot glue burns, solvent and spray paint fumes, and cut wire ends that catch an eye are the everyday injuries of mas camps, and they rise sharply in the tired final weeks. A short safety set-up with ventilation, eye protection, a burn kit and a rule for wire ends costs little and takes one afternoon.

                ## Milestones
                1. Safety glasses and cut-resistant gloves within reach of every bench.
                2. Solvent glues and spray paint used only outdoors or with a window open and a fan running.
                3. A first aid kit with burn gel and a fire extinguisher by the door.
                4. A camp rule that every cut wire end is turned in or taped.

                ## Notes

# Mas Costume Making and Wire Bending

Mas, short for masquerade, is the costumed heart of carnival, and this area is for the people who make it: wire benders, designers, sewers, stoners and the volunteers who fill a mas camp, the workshop where a band builds its costumes. It starts with tools, safety, materials, measurements and a first headpiece, moves through the routines that keep a camp producing and the hand skills of wire, covering, stones, feathers and fitting, then costing, materials decisions and selling a section. After that come the dated moments (launch night, the final fortnight, collection day), situations such as a child's costume, a first section or a private commission, and specialist work on kings, queens, giant puppets and apprentices.

What repeats follows the season: a weekly camp session, volunteer roster and practice hour, monthly stock counts, tool checks, calendar reviews and photo backups, and yearly storage, supplier, competition and portfolio updates. The Purchase decision, Operational checklist, Vendor, Portfolio piece and Development plan templates pair with the toolkit, safety, supplier, portfolio and apprentice projects.
