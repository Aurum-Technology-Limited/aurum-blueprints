# Nine Nights and Funerals Back Home

This area is for the phone call every family abroad dreads: a death back home in Kingston, Port of Spain, Georgetown or Castries, and the rush of travel, money, ritual and grief that follows. It starts with what you can put in place now, the phone tree, passports, a travel fund, leave and the family conversations about wishes and costs, then the funds, grave upkeep and remembrance routines, knowledge of the first 48 hours, nine nights and the rites of each faith, decisions about funeral homes, plots and repatriation, and the funeral week itself from flights to thank-yous. It ends with situations such as being unable to travel or bringing children raised abroad, and the specialist work of repatriation, ashes, tombing and planning your own funeral across two countries.

The repeating work is light: monthly payments into a travel fund, a sibling funeral fund and a burial society, a quarterly check of the phone tree, a weekly grief notebook through the first year, and yearly reviews of passports, wishes, cover, grave upkeep and anniversaries. The Savings goal, Meeting notes, Vendor, Purchase decision, Trip and Operational checklist templates pair with the travel fund, the cost conversation, the funeral home shortlist, repatriation cover, the flight home and the nine night.
