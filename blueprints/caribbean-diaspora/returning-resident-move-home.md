            - name: Returning resident eligibility check
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Several Caribbean governments offer returning nationals a once-only concession on duty for household effects and often a vehicle, run through units such as Jamaica's Returning Residents Facilitation Unit, the Barbados Facilitation Unit for returning nationals or Guyana's remigrant scheme. The rules differ by country: minimum years abroad, citizenship, the age of a vehicle, how long you must have owned goods and how soon after arrival you must claim. Confirming the current rules from the official source before you buy or ship anything stops expensive mistakes.

                ## Milestones
                1. The official returning resident or returning national page for your country found and saved.
                2. The current eligibility rules written in your own words, with the date you checked.
                3. Each adult's eligibility confirmed or questions listed for the unit.
                4. A reply from the unit or customs department on any point that was unclear.

                ## Notes
                Rules change, and advice from relatives who came home ten years ago may be out of date. Trust the customs department or facilitation unit in writing.

# Returning Resident Move Home

Moving back home after years in London, Toronto, New York or Miami is one of the biggest decisions a Caribbean family makes, and the paperwork, the container and the expectations at both ends all go better with a plan. This area starts with the foundations: the decision and arrival date, an eligibility check for the returning resident duty concession, the document folder, the timeline and the moving fund. It then covers the routines that run through the move, shipping and customs, closing down life abroad, the big days of packing and clearance, settling in with local registrations, work and healthcare, situations for retirees, families with children and non-national partners, and the specialist work of keeping a route back open and helping the next person home.

The rhythm is a weekly countdown before the move, monthly bills, prescriptions, mail and settling-in reviews after it, and yearly checks on documents, concession conditions, pension life certificates, insurance and your status abroad. The Savings goal, Trip, Vendor, Purchase decision and Operational checklist templates pair with the moving fund, the reconnaissance trip, the shipper, the vehicle decision and the close-down abroad.
