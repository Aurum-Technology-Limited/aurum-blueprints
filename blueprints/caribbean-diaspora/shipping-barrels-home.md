              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Each Caribbean country sets its own rules for barrels: what counts as personal effects or gifts, any duty-free allowance, which items attract extra taxes and what the consignee must bring to clear them. Reading your destination's rules on the customs authority's own website, not a social media post, before you buy a single item means the barrel is packed for the rules that actually apply. Rules change, so the check is worth repeating each year.

                ## Milestones
                1. The customs authority's official page on personal shipments or barrels found and saved.
                2. Any duty-free or concession arrangement for barrels noted, with its conditions and dates.
                3. The documents the consignee needs to clear a barrel listed, such as photo ID and a taxpayer number.
                4. Questions the website does not answer sent to the customs authority or a licensed broker.

                ## Notes
                Concessions for barrels, where they exist, often have strict dates and conditions. Check the current year's notice rather than relying on last year's.

# Shipping Barrels Home

This area is for anyone who packs a barrel, the cardboard or plastic drum Caribbean families fill with groceries, clothes and gifts and ship home by sea, whether from London, New York, Toronto or Miami. It starts with the foundations (customs rules, a trustworthy shipper, a briefed consignee and an honest packing list), then the monthly routines that fill and track a barrel, the packing skills, the decisions about cost, insurance and alternatives, the big shipping dates, the family situations barrels serve, and the work of whoever organises barrels for the whole family.

What repeats is a monthly buy for the barrel and a monthly set-aside, quarterly checks on expiry dates, storage and sale prices, weekly tracking while a barrel is at sea, and a yearly round of cut-off dates, wish lists and Christmas and back-to-school planning. The Vendor, Purchase decision, Savings goal, Metrics log and Operational checklist templates pair with the projects that name them.
