# Skills Volunteering Back Home

This area is for doctors, nurses, dentists, teachers, engineers, accountants, developers and other professionals abroad who want to put their skills to work back home, whether in a clinic in Haiti, a teachers' college in Guyana or a small business in St Vincent. It moves from the foundations (a skills inventory, a vetted host, an agreed need, registration, insurance and a written agreement), through the routines that keep a partnership alive, the skills of working as a guest in another health or school system, decisions on supplies, equipment and software, the mission weeks, training days and business clinics themselves, plans for retirees, busy workers, students and families, and finally volunteer rosters, disaster deployment, research partnerships and handing the work to local trainers.

What repeats is a monthly call with the host, monthly mentoring and IT support sessions, a monthly hours log, quarterly impact notes, CPD entries and handover tidying, and a yearly check of credentials, insurance, safeguarding and the volunteering calendar. The Skills matrix, Vendor, Meeting notes, Metrics log, Trip and Operational checklist templates pair with the skills inventory, the sending group check, the host calls, the hours log, the pre-departure list and the mission week. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
