# Soca, Calypso and Dancehall Music

This area is for anyone who takes Caribbean music seriously, whether you grew up with calypso on the radio in Port of Spain, dancehall at a Kingston street dance or soca on the road at Notting Hill, and for the creatives who want to make it too.
