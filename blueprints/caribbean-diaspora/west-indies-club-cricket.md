# West Indies Cricket and Club Cricket

This area is for anyone who loves West Indies cricket and wants to follow it properly or play it themselves, whether at a club in Port of Spain or Bridgetown or in a Saturday league in Leeds, Brooklyn or Brampton. It runs from the foundations (choosing and joining a club, the Laws, first kit, helmet safety, fitness and a fixture calendar), through the weekly routines of nets, availability, stats and kit care, the batting, bowling, fielding, keeping and scoring skills, the decisions about bats, coaching and formats, Test, tour and CPL trips and club events, plans for parents, returners, women, newcomers and veterans, and finally coaching, umpiring, captaincy and committee work.

What repeats is a weekly nets night, availability reply, stats entry and kit wash, a weekly West Indies round-up, pre-season sessions twice a week, monthly checks on fixtures, goals, subs, helmet and bat, and a yearly season review. The Purchase decision, Habit tracker, Metrics log, Training program, Remediation plan and Trip templates pair with the kit, nets, stats, winter training, no-ball and match trip projects.
