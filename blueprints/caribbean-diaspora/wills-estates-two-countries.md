# Wills and Estates Across Two Countries

Owning a house in Mandeville and a pension in Manchester, or a credit union account in San Fernando and a condo in Brooklyn, means your estate will pass through two legal systems, two sets of lawyers and two probate registries, and one will rarely covers both cleanly. This area starts with the foundations (an inventory of what you own where, domicile, any old will, intestacy, a lawyer and an executor on each side and safe originals), then the knowledge that avoids expensive mistakes, the wills, nominations and powers of attorney themselves, the routines that keep the plan current, the signing days and family meeting, situations from a new marriage to a retiree who has moved back home, and the specialist work of trusts, legacies and serving as someone else's executor.

The rhythm is light but steady: a monthly scan of new papers, payment into an estate costs fund and record of rent on property back home, a quarterly refresh of the asset register, digital accounts and dormant accounts, and a yearly will review, nominations check, executor pack and note to the family.
