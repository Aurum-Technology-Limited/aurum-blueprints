# Barbados Common Entrance (BSSEE)

This area takes a Barbadian family through the Class 4 year of the Secondary Schools' Entrance Examination, better known as the Common Entrance or the 11-Plus, from checking this year's format to the first week of First Form. It starts with the set-up a parent can do in a few weeks (a format and reform check, a diagnostic, a study corner, past papers and a revision calendar), builds the weekly routines, then works topic by topic through English and Mathematics before school choices, the mock, the final weeks, exam day and placement. The last group covers particular situations, including a parent working abroad and a family moving home, and the projects a Class 4 teacher or lessons tutor would run.

The rhythm is four after-school sessions a week, a daily mental arithmetic warm-up and bedtime reading, a weekly word list, a weekly composition, a Friday mistake review, a Saturday timed paper and a monthly parent check-in. The Meeting notes, Habit tracker, Reading queue, Metrics log, Remediation plan and Purchase decision templates pair with the projects that point at them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
