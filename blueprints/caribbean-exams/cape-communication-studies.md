# CAPE Communication Studies

Everything a sixth former, a parent or a teacher needs for CAPE Communication Studies, the single-unit course most students take in their first year of sixth form. It opens with set-up (the format check, a diagnostic, a timetable, past papers, a glossary and agreed targets), moves through the weekly routines, then follows the three modules in syllabus order, Gathering and Processing Information, Language and Community, and Speaking and Writing, before the internal assessment portfolio and oral, the timed papers, the mock, exam day, and projects for private candidates, teachers and candidates aiming for the top grade.

The rhythm is mostly weekly: editorials on Monday and Thursday, language notebook entries on Tuesday, a Paper 01 drill on Wednesday, essay swaps on Thursday, new glossary terms on Friday, a timed essay and error log on Saturday and a parent check-in on Sunday, with a monthly key terms quiz, a listening exercise and a score review, and one timed section a day in the final four weeks. The Meeting notes, Metrics log, Habit tracker, Reading queue and Remediation plan templates pair with the projects that point at them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
