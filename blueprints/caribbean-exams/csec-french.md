# CSEC French

Everything a Form Four or Form Five student, a parent or a French teacher needs for CSEC French, from checking the current paper format to walking into the oral with a warm-up planned. It opens with set-up (the format check, a diagnostic, a timetable, a past-paper and audio pack, a vocabulary notebook and an error log), runs the weekly routines for vocabulary, listening, reading, speaking and verbs, then works through the grammar and the syllabus vocabulary themes before the paper skills for listening, reading, writing and the oral, any SBA, the mock, the final weeks and exam days, finishing with projects for Creole speakers, private candidates, parents, teachers and students looking beyond CSEC.

The rhythm is short and frequent: ten minutes of vocabulary every day, shadowing on Monday and Thursday, verb drills on Tuesday and Friday, listening on Wednesday, a numbers dictation on Thursday, a reading text on Saturday and a parent check-in on Sunday, with a timed letter, a comprehension passage, an error quiz and a score review each month. The Habit tracker, Metrics log, Reading queue, Remediation plan, Purchase decision and Trip templates pair with the projects that point at them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
