# CSEC Geography

This area takes a CSEC Geography student from the first syllabus check to results day, in roughly the order the subject is taught and examined. It opens with the foundations (the current syllabus, a diagnostic paper, a two-year plan, command words, diagrams and a Caribbean case study bank), sets up the weekly routines, then works through map reading and interpretation, the natural systems from plate tectonics, rivers, limestone and coasts to weather, hurricanes and the rainforest, the human systems from population and migration to farming, mining, industry and tourism, and the hazards and environmental issues that tie them together. After that come the field study SBA, exam technique, the mock, the final weeks and exam day, with projects for parents, teachers, private candidates and results at the end.

The rhythm is two weekly study sessions, a weekly mapwork drill, daily key term recall, a weekly hazards log, a monthly timed Paper 01 and Paper 02 question, and a termly talk with your teacher. The Skills matrix, Habit tracker, Metrics log, Meeting notes and Operational checklist templates pair with it. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
