# CSEC Music

This area is for students sitting CSEC Music, for the parents paying for lessons and listening through the bedroom door, and for self-taught pannists, church musicians and bedroom producers who want the qualification. It starts with the foundations (the current syllabus, your instrument, a baseline and a timetable), then the weekly routines that build a musician, the content of the listening paper in syllabus order from notation to Caribbean and world styles, exam technique, performing, composing, the SBA, the mock, the final weeks and exam day, plans for particular students and teachers, and finally work for students aiming at the top grade and beyond.

What repeats is daily practice on your instrument, ear training on Mondays and Thursdays, sight-reading on Tuesdays and Fridays, a listening diary on Wednesdays, theory on Saturdays, a composition idea and a parent check-in on Sundays, a recorded run-through on the 9th and a timed listening paper on the 18th of each month. The Purchase decision, Habit tracker, Metrics log, Feedback themes and Portfolio piece templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
