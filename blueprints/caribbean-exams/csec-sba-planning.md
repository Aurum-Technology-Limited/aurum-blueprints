# CSEC SBA Planning Across Subjects

This area is for the parent or guardian of a Fourth or Fifth Form student juggling several CSEC School-Based Assessments at once, with projects for the teachers behind them too. It starts with the foundations (an inventory of every SBA, the syllabus rules, a master calendar, the honesty rules and a first conversation with each teacher), sets up the planning, backup, feedback and check-in routines, builds the research, data, charting, referencing and formatting skills every SBA shares, then moves through decisions, drafts, sign-off and submission to projects for parents, resitting and transferring students, SBA coordinators and subject teachers.

The rhythm is a Sunday planning session, a Saturday backup, weekly write-up and feedback checks, a daily work block in heavy terms, monthly progress, risk and parent check-ins, and dates reconfirmed each term. The Meeting notes, Skills matrix, Risk register, Remediation plan and Operational checklist templates pair with it, while the detail of each subject's SBA lives in that subject's own area. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
