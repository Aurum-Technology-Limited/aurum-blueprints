              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Craft knives, lino cutters, turpentine, fixative sprays, dyes, hot wax and kilns all cause real injuries in school art rooms. Learning the safe way to use the tools in your own expressive forms, and writing the rules into the front of your journal, keeps you working through the year instead of nursing a cut hand in the month before submission.

                ## Milestones
                1. The art room rules read, and signed if the school requires it.
                2. Safe handling written for each tool and material in your forms, such as cutting away from the body, gloves for dyes and ventilation for solvents.
                3. The first aid kit, eyewash and fire extinguisher located in the art room.
                4. A one-page safety sheet for working at home kept in the journal.

                ## Notes
                Never fire a kiln or use hot wax without your teacher present, and ask before using any solvent at home.

# CSEC Visual Arts

This area is for fourth and fifth form students taking CSEC Visual Arts, and for the parents and art teachers behind them. It starts with the foundations (the current syllabus, your expressive forms, a kit, the reflective journal and a studio schedule to the submission date), then the weekly studio and sketchbook routines, the elements and principles of design, each expressive form in the order the syllabus sets out two-dimensional and three-dimensional work, Caribbean and world art history, the production pieces, journal and submission, the mock and exam day, plans for particular students and teachers, and a portfolio for what comes next.

What repeats is a daily sketch, a Monday progress photo, a Wednesday artist study, written-paper practice on Thursdays, a parent check-in on Fridays, a Saturday studio block, a Sunday journal entry and a monthly self-critique against the criteria. The Purchase decision, Habit tracker, Feedback themes, Reading queue and Portfolio piece templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
