# CXC CPEA Primary Exit Assessment

This area takes a Grade 6 child through the Caribbean Primary Exit Assessment the way it is really run, with coursework through the year and an external test at the end. It starts with the current CXC format, a baseline and a year plan, sets up the weekly routines, then works subject by subject through Mathematics, Language, Science and Social Studies before the investigation, the writing portfolio, the mock, exam day and results, with projects for carers, transfers, teachers and tutors at the end.

The rhythm is daily reading and mental maths, four short school-night sessions, a Sunday check-in, weekly spelling and mistakes reviews, and a monthly timed practice set and note to the teacher. The Reading queue, Habit tracker, Metrics log, Meeting notes and Course templates pair with it. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
