# Jamaica PEP Grade Six Exams

This area walks a family through the grade six year of Jamaica's Primary Exit Profile, from the first check of the current format to placement in a high school. It starts with the set-up a parent can do in a week (the format check, a starting diagnostic, a study corner and a revision calendar), builds the after-school routines, then works subject by subject through the ability test, Mathematics, Language Arts, Science, Social Studies and the performance tasks, before the mock, the final weeks, the test days and the school choices. The last group is for particular situations and for the grade six teacher and the extra lessons tutor.

The rhythm is an after-school session four days a week, a short daily maths warm-up and bedtime reading, a weekly word bank, a Saturday timed set and a monthly parent check-in. The Habit tracker, Reading queue, Metrics log, Meeting notes, Remediation plan and Purchase decision templates pair with the projects that point at them.
