# SEA Creative Writing

This area is for Standard 5 pupils preparing for the Creative Writing paper of Trinidad and Tobago's Secondary Entrance Assessment, and for the parents and teachers helping them. It starts with the foundations (the current format and rubric, a baseline composition, a checklist your child can actually use and a week-by-week plan to SEA day), then the weekly routines, the craft skills from five-minute planning and openings to endings, paragraphing and description, the decisions about timing, person and prompt types, the mocks and exam morning, plans for particular situations, and finally projects for class teachers and lessons tutors.

What repeats is a timed composition every Saturday, two short quick-writes a week, a Friday read-aloud check-in, weekly reading like a writer, a redraft on the 14th and a rubric review on the 28th of each month. The Metrics log, Habit tracker, Feedback themes, Reading queue, Purchase decision and Course templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
