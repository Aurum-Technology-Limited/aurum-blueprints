# SEA English Language Arts

Everything a Standard Five student, a parent or a teacher needs to prepare for the SEA English Language Arts paper, from finding out exactly what the current paper looks like to walking into the exam room calm. It starts with set-up (the format check, a diagnostic paper, a timetable and an error notebook), moves through the daily and weekly routines, then works topic by topic through grammar, spelling, vocabulary and comprehension, and finishes with timed papers, the mock, the final weeks, projects for parents and teachers, and stretch work for confident students.

The rhythm is small and steady: twenty minutes of reading and one new word every day, a spelling list on Monday and a test on Friday, a timed comprehension passage on Saturday, a short parent check-in on Sunday and a mixed grammar quiz each month. The Habit tracker, Metrics log, Reading queue, Meeting notes, Purchase decision and Remediation plan templates pair with the projects that point at them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
