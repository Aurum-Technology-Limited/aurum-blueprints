# SEA Mathematics

This area is for a Standard 4 or Standard 5 child preparing for the Mathematics paper of the Secondary Entrance Assessment, and for the parents, class teachers and lessons tutors helping them. It opens with the foundations (the current framework, a diagnostic paper, number facts, a study corner and a timetable counted back from exam day), then the weekly practice routines, then one project per topic through the four strands of number, measurement, geometry and statistics, each finishing on past-paper questions. After those come the fixes and decisions, the mock and the final weeks, projects for particular situations such as an anxious child or a family newly arrived from abroad, and work for teachers and strong students.

What repeats is a ten-minute daily warm-up, two topic drills and a timed mixed set each week, a Sunday mistake-log review, a Friday parent check-in and a full timed paper every month. The Habit tracker, Metrics log and Purchase decision templates pair with the projects that name them. Deadlines assume roughly seven months to the exam, so shift them once the Ministry publishes the date. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
