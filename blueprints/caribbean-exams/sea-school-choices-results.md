            - name: How SEA placement really works
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Placement is not just a score: it combines the mark, the order of your choices, the places each school has, and for many denominational schools a share of places the board selects itself. Parents who understand the mechanics write choice lists that protect the child, while parents who do not sometimes waste a top choice or leave no realistic option at the bottom.

                ## Milestones
                1. A one-page summary, in your own words, of how scores, choices and available places combine.
                2. The role of denominational boards in selecting part of their intake understood, with the current rules checked.
                3. The meaning of each choice position on the form clear to both parents.
                4. Any question the summary leaves open taken to the class teacher or the Ministry.

                ## Notes
                The Ministry's own guidance overrules anything in your summary. Rules on denominational selection and on the number of choices have changed before, so check the current year rather than relying on an older sibling's experience.

# SEA School Choices and Results

This area covers the other half of the SEA year in Trinidad and Tobago: not the papers themselves, but choosing secondary schools, understanding how placement works, getting through results day and settling a child into Form One. It is written for the parent first and the child second, and runs from foundations (official dates, placement rules, family priorities, a documents folder) through the weekly routines of the SEA year and Form One, the skills and decisions behind a good choice list, and the dated events from open days to orientation. It ends with harder situations, such as an unexpected placement, a transfer request, a family returning from abroad, and passing on what you learned to the next cohort.

The rhythms are weekly during the SEA year (a Monday deadline check, a Saturday entry in the school research log, a Sunday wellbeing chat), then weekly and nightly in Form One for school messages, a Friday check-in and packing the bag, with a monthly savings transfer and transport payment alongside. The Meeting notes, Savings goal and Habit tracker templates pair with the teacher conversation, the Form One savings pot and the night-before routine.
