            - name: Group map across islands and sectors
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Few families can say from memory which companies the group owns in which island, at what percentage, through which holding entity, and who runs each one day to day. A single map of every operating company, its island, sector, ownership route, listed or private status and chief executive is the page every later governance decision starts from, and it usually turns up a dormant company or a forgotten minority stake.

                ## Milestones
                1. Every operating and holding company in the group listed with its island of incorporation and where it trades.
                2. Each company tagged by sector, ownership percentage and the entity that holds it.
                3. Listed companies marked with their exchange and the family's combined voting stake.
                4. The chief executive or general manager of each company named.
                5. The map checked by the group company secretary and dated.

                ## Notes
                Draw it on one page if you can. A group that needs six pages to explain has usually grown by accident, and the map is where that conversation begins.
              priority: high
              deadlineOffsetDays: 30
              frontmatter:
                mode: building

# Caribbean Family Business Groups

This area is for the family that owns a group of businesses across the Caribbean: distribution and retail, manufacturing, insurance, property, hotels or shipping, spread over two or more islands, some listed on a regional exchange and some private. It starts with foundations (a group map, who from each branch sits where, the group's purpose, island risk profiles, licences, agencies and banking), moves through the routines that keep a multi-island group governed, the knowledge owners need about listed companies, foreign exchange and the single market, decisions on the portfolio, executives and partners, events such as a hurricane strike, an acquisition or a takeover bid, situations for founders, parents and family abroad, and ends with specialist work on dividing a group, climate reporting and taking a company private.

What repeats is a monthly cash and foreign currency position and licence check, a quarterly group performance review, related party review, closed period calendar, risk register update and island visit, a May hurricane readiness check, and a yearly strategy round, agency review, executive review and listed company AGM. The Board meeting, Compliance control, Meeting notes and Risk register templates pair with the quarterly review, the licence register, island visits and group risks. Securities, tax, competition and employment questions in each island belong with the group's lawyers, auditors and advisers there.
