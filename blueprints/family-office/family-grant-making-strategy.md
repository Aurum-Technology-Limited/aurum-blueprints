              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Most families who decide to give strategically discover they already give a great deal, scattered across dozens of causes nobody chose on purpose. Listing every gift of the last five years, by giver, recipient, amount and reason, shows what the family actually cares about and where the money really goes before anyone writes a new strategy.

                ## Milestones
                1. A single list of every gift above a set threshold from the last five years, by family member and vehicle.
                2. Each gift tagged with a cause category and whether it was solicited, personal or planned.
                3. Totals by cause, by giver and by year set out on one page.
                4. Three observations about patterns written up for the next family discussion.

                ## Notes
                Include gifts made personally, through companies and through any foundation or donor-advised fund. Leaving out personal giving usually hides half the picture.

            - name: Family giving values conversation
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Grant strategies built on the founder's preferences alone tend to lose the next generation within a decade. One facilitated conversation, in which each adult says what they want the family's giving to stand for and what they would never fund, gives the strategy roots the whole family recognises.

                ## Milestones
                1. Every adult family member invited, with a short pre-reading and the five-year look back attached.
                2. Each person's top three causes and any red lines recorded in their own words.
                3. Common ground and genuine differences listed separately, without forcing agreement.
                4. A one-page statement of giving values circulated for comment within two weeks.

                ## Notes
                Use an outside facilitator if one generation tends to dominate. Recording differences honestly matters more than producing a tidy consensus.

                output_kind: artifact

            - name: Choosing two or three focus areas
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Spreading a giving budget across forty causes produces forty thank-you letters and very little change. Narrowing to two or three focus areas, each defined tightly enough that a grant officer can say yes or no to an enquiry, is the single decision that turns giving into grant-making.

                ## Milestones
                1. A long list of candidate focus areas drawn from the values statement and past gifts.
                2. Each candidate scored for family interest, funding gap and the family's ability to add something beyond money.
                3. Two or three focus areas chosen, each with a two-sentence definition and a geography.
                4. A short list of what is explicitly out of scope agreed alongside them.

                ## Notes
                Define geography early. A focus on literacy means something very different in one town than across a region.
              priority: high
              deadlineOffsetDays: 60
              frontmatter:
                mode: research
                output_kind: decision
                success_criteria: "Two or three focus areas, each with a written definition, geography and out-of-scope list, are approved by the family's giving forum."
                cadence: one-shot
                effort_hours_estimate: "12"
              tasks:
                - "List every candidate cause named in the values conversation"
                - "Score each candidate on interest, funding gap and what the family brings"
                - "Write a two-sentence definition and geography for each finalist"
                - "Put the chosen focus areas to the giving forum for approval"
            - name: Annual giving budget and grant size bands
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Without a set budget, grant decisions become a series of separate negotiations about whether the family can afford each one. Agreeing an annual giving figure, with the share for each focus area and bands for small, medium and large grants, lets the committee say yes quickly and say no without awkwardness.

                ## Milestones
                1. An annual giving figure agreed, with the basis noted, such as a fixed sum or a share of income.
                2. The budget split across focus areas, discretionary grants and a small reserve.
                3. Three grant size bands defined with typical amounts and approval routes.
                4. Any required minimum payout for the family's vehicle confirmed with the adviser.

                ## Notes
                Ask the family's tax adviser or the foundation's accountant about any legal payout rule. The budget is a family choice; the minimum may not be.

# Family Grant-Making Strategy

This area is for the parents, grandparents and family staff who decide what the family gives to, and for the nonprofit professionals who run that giving with them. It starts with the foundations (a look back at past gifts, shared values, focus areas, a budget, a written policy and clear decision rights), builds the machinery of intake, dockets, payments and grantee reporting, then covers the skills of reading charity accounts and measuring results, the bigger choices about unrestricted and multi-year funding, dated events such as a first open round and a site visit trip, situations for each generation and the specialist work of cross-border grants, advocacy and a ten-year review.

The rhythm is a weekly triage of enquiries, monthly checks on the register, payments, reports and budget, a quarterly grant committee and grantee check-ins, and a yearly cycle of policy review, family giving report, next-generation docket and grantee convening. The Board meeting template suits the quarterly docket, Trip suits site visits, Vendor suits choosing a philanthropy adviser and Metrics log suits tracking results across the portfolio.
