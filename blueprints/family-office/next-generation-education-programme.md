              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Most families start educating their children about wealth with a single seminar and a good intention, then stop when the speaker leaves. A two-page charter that names the programme's purpose, who it serves, who sponsors it on the family council and how success will be judged turns a gesture into something the family will still be running in fifteen years.

                ## Milestones
                1. A charter of two pages or fewer stating purpose, the age range served, the sponsor and the yearly budget owner.
                2. Three to five outcomes the family wants every graduate of the programme to have, written in plain words.
                3. The charter discussed with the family council or senior generation and any changes recorded.
                4. The adopted charter filed with the family's governance documents.

                ## Notes
                Keep the outcomes about capability and character (can read a statement, knows how the trust works, has held an outside job) rather than about wealth figures. Link the charter to the family constitution if one exists.

# Next-Generation Education Programme

This area is for the parents and family office staff who want the rising generation to arrive at adulthood ready for what they will one day own, rather than surprised by it. It starts with the foundations (a charter, a map of every child and young adult by age, learning goals, safeguarding and a policy on what to tell children when), moves into the quarterly session cycle and the records behind it, then the money, governance and trust lessons themselves, the decisions about providers, incentives and outside work, the dated occasions such as the eighteenth birthday briefing, the variants for toddlers, students and family abroad, and the specialist work of measuring results over a decade.

The rhythm is quarterly sessions and a quarterly report to the family council, monthly parent check-ins and allowance days, and a yearly cycle of calendar, budget, development plans, the summer retreat and the programme review. The Metrics log, Reading queue, Vendor, Development plan, Feedback themes and Course templates pair with the records, the library, the educator roster, individual plans, the survey and the investing course.
