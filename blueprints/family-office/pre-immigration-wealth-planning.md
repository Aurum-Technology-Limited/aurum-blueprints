                ## Purpose
                Planning goes wrong when advisers in two countries each assume a different move date, a different set of people moving and a different reason for going. A one-page brief stating who is moving, from where, to where, roughly when, for how long and why gives every adviser the same starting facts and stops expensive work on the wrong scenario.

                4. The family's main reasons for moving and its non-negotiables listed in plain words.

              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Becoming tax resident in the new country and ceasing to be resident in the old one are separate tests, and many families discover they are resident in both for a year. A short summary of how each country decides residence, written with an adviser in each, shows the real dates and facts that switch residence on and off.

                ## Milestones
                1. The residence tests used by the origin country set out in a page, including any day counts, home tests and ties.
                2. The same summary written for the destination country, including when residence starts in the year of arrival.

                4. Points where the family could be resident in both countries at once identified.
                5. Both summaries reviewed and initialled by the relevant adviser.

                ## Notes

# Pre-Immigration Wealth Planning

This area is for the family where someone is about to change country: parents moving with children for school or safety, a founder relocating with company shares, an adult child taking a job abroad, or a retired parent joining the family overseas. It starts with foundations (a move brief, residency rules for both countries, an adviser team on each side, a balance sheet, cost basis records and a source of wealth file), then the operating routines that keep the plan on track, the knowledge behind residency tests, exit charges and foreign income rules, the decisions on move dates, gains, gifts and restructuring, the dated events of departure, arrival and the first returns, situations for specific family members, and finally specialist work on investment routes, pre-arrival structures and a long-term plan for a family that keeps moving.

What repeats is a monthly day count, adviser call, timeline check, currency log and cost log, quarterly reviews of ties to the old country, account readiness and the risk register, and a yearly filing calendar in both countries. The Vendor, Risk register, Meeting notes, Reading queue and Operational checklist templates pair with the adviser team, the risk register, the joint adviser calls, the reading list and the departure month. Nothing here is tax, legal or immigration advice: every decision is agreed with qualified advisers in the country being left and the country being joined.
