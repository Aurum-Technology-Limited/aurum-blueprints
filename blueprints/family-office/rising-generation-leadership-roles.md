            - name: Rising generation roster and readiness map
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Most families can name their grandchildren but not say which of them are eighteen next year, which are at university abroad and which have asked about the family business. A single roster with age, location, studies, interests and any governance contact so far turns a vague hope about the next generation into a list of people the family can actually plan seats for.

                ## Milestones
                1. A roster of every member of the rising generation aged fourteen and over, by branch, with date of birth and current location.
                2. Each person's studies or work, stated interests and any previous governance contact noted in a column.
                3. A readiness grouping for each person: not yet eligible, ready to observe, ready for a committee role.
                4. The roster stored in the family office vault with an owner responsible for updating it.

                ## Notes
                Keep the readiness grouping descriptive, not a ranking. The roster will be seen by parents across branches, and a column that reads as a league table causes more harm than the missing information.

              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Arguments about who gets a seat are far easier to settle before any seat is offered than after a cousin has been passed over.             - name: Leadership pathway from observer to full member
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Young adults step up when they can see the next rung and what it takes to reach it. A one-page pathway showing the stages, typically observer, junior board member, committee member and full council or board member, with the time and evidence each stage expects, replaces the unspoken assumption that seats arrive with age.

                ## Milestones
                1. Four or five stages named, each with the forums it opens and the rights it carries.
                2. The minimum time and the evidence of readiness for moving between stages written for each step.
                3. Who decides each step, and how a young member can ask to be considered, stated in a sentence per stage.
                4. The pathway drawn as a single page and approved alongside the eligibility rules.

                ## Notes
                Evidence of readiness should be things a young member can do, such as attending a set number of meetings or presenting a paper, not personality judgements that leave them guessing.
              priority: high
              deadlineOffsetDays: 60

            - name: Post-meeting debriefs for observers
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Observers see a decision made but rarely see why the chair steered it the way she did or why two members disagreed. A short debrief within a week, with the chair or a mentor, explains the reasoning, answers questions and asks what the observer would have said, which is where judgement starts to form.

# Rising Generation Leadership Roles

This area is for parents and senior family members who want their children and nieces and nephews to take real seats in family governance, and for the students and young adults who are being offered those seats. It starts with the foundations (a roster of the rising generation, eligibility rules, a written pathway and role descriptions), moves into the machinery of observer seats, a junior board and mentoring, then the practical skills of reading a pack and speaking up in a meeting, the decisions about voting and pay, the dated events, the situations different young members find themselves in, and the specialist work of seats on operating boards and measuring whether the pathway works.

The rhythm follows the governance calendar: a quarterly cycle of briefings, meetings and debriefs, monthly mentor check-ins and a monthly update to the cohort, and a yearly round of readiness reviews, the retreat, the survey and the junior board chair election. The Board meeting template suits the junior board, Development plan suits each member's log, 1:1 suits mentor check-ins, Onboarding plan suits induction and Feedback themes suits the annual survey.
