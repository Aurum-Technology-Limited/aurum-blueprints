# Wealth Structuring Jurisdictions

This area is for families who hold, or are about to hold, companies, foundations or other vehicles in more than one country, and who want the choice of where to put them made on evidence rather than habit or a single adviser's preference. It starts with foundations (a map of what already exists, written objectives, a residence map of the family, a lead adviser, a weighted scorecard, home-country rules and a reputation screen), then the operating routines that keep each jurisdiction in good standing, the knowledge needed to read substance laws, vehicle types, treaties and court systems, the comparisons and decisions such as Cayman, Bahamas or BVI and onshore alternatives, dated events like a site visit and the decision meeting, situations for parents with children abroad, retirees simplifying and Caribbean-resident families, and finally specialist work on continuity, split administration, sanctions and an independent second opinion.

What repeats is a monthly scan of law and list changes, a quarterly structure chart refresh and beneficial ownership check, quarterly local board meetings, a monthly fee and document calendar, and a yearly cycle of substance filings, provider reviews, cost totals and a jurisdiction health review. The Meeting notes, Risk register, Compliance control, Vendor and Trip templates pair with adviser meetings, the health review, substance filings, provider choices and site visits. None of this is tax, legal or investment advice: every jurisdiction choice is confirmed in writing by qualified advisers in each country involved.
