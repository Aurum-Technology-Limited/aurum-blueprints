# Childcare & Nursery Arrangements

This area is for parents arranging care for a baby or preschool child, whether that means a nursery, a childminder, a nanny, family help or a mix of them. It starts with the foundations (a needs brief, the choice of care type, real costs, funding, waiting lists, visits and contracts), then the routines that keep childcare running, the skills of calm drop-offs, safeguarding questions and employing a carer, the decisions about days, location and alternatives, the dated moments from settling in to leaving for school, the situations that change the picture, and finally nanny employment, long-range cost planning and the parent committee.

What repeats is a Sunday rota check, a weekly bag restock and Friday diary read, a monthly invoice check, quarterly funding reconfirmations and key person catch-ups, and an annual review of the whole arrangement. The Monthly budget and bills, Operational checklist, Meeting notes, Person, Onboarding plan and 1:1 templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
