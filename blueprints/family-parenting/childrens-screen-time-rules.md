# Children's Screen Time Rules

This area is for parents who want screens, games and phones to have a settled place in family life instead of a nightly argument. It starts with the foundations (an honest audit, a device list, a written agreement, curfews, screen-free zones and the time controls that back them up), then the weekly and monthly routines that keep the rules working, the skills and knowledge behind good rules, the big decisions such as a first phone or social media, the dated moments, the variants for each age and family set-up, and finally the harder cases like compulsive gaming and handing control to an older teen.

What repeats is a nightly device handover, a weekly look at the screen time reports, a Friday weekend plan, a monthly family check-in and a termly refresh as children grow, with a yearly review on top. The Metrics log, Habit tracker, Meeting notes, Purchase decision and Trip templates pair with the projects that name them.
