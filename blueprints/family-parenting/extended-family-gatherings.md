# Extended Family Gatherings

This area is for anyone who hosts, travels to or simply survives the big extended family get-togethers, whether you are a parent herding small children through a three-day stay or an adult turning up on your own. It starts with foundations (a family list, a map of the year's gatherings, your own limits, a hosting rotation, cost sharing, dietary and access notes), then the operating machinery of look-aheads, countdowns, sign-up sheets and debriefs, the skills of cooking for a crowd and keeping the peace, decisions about where to spend the main festival and when to say no, the milestone occasions such as a grandparent's eightieth or a cousins' reunion, versions for new babies, new partners, divorced parents, dementia and relatives abroad, and finally specialist work on estrangement, accessible gatherings and handing the hosting role to the next generation.

What repeats is a quarterly look-ahead at the gatherings coming up, a monthly transfer into the travel fund, monthly contact with cousins and relatives abroad between gatherings, a yearly review of the hosting rotation and dates, the yearly gift draw and a debrief after every gathering. The Operational checklist, Savings goal, Meeting notes, Vendor and Trip templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
