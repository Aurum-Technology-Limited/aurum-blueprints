            - name: Home education rules where you live
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Rules for home education range from a simple right to educate at home with no registration, through annual notification and portfolio review, to required testing or approved curricula, and some places do not permit it at all. Reading the actual law and the local authority's published guidance before you start means you comply with what is required and do not hand over more than the law asks for.

                ## Milestones
                1. The law or regulation that governs home education where you live identified and read.
                2. A one-page summary of what you must do: registration, notification, subjects, hours, testing or reviews.
                3. The local authority's published policy compared with the law and any differences noted.
                4. A national or regional home education support organisation found for questions the summary cannot answer.

                ## Notes
                Local authority guidance sometimes asks for more than the law requires. Note which requests are legal duties and which are optional before you reply to anything.
              priority: high
              deadlineOffsetDays: 14
              frontmatter:
                mode: research
                output_kind: knowledge
                success_criteria: "A one-page summary of your legal duties, citing the governing law and the local policy, is saved where both parents can find it."
                cadence: one-shot
                effort_hours_estimate: "3"
              tasks:
                - "Find the law or regulation that governs home education where you live"
                - "Download the local authority's home education policy or guidance"
                - "Write a one-page summary of duties, deadlines and what is optional"
                - "Join one established home education support organisation or forum"
                - "Check whether the home education rules or local policy have changed @recurring(yearly)"
            - name: Formal withdrawal from school in writing
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Where a child is on a school roll, most systems require a written notice to the head teacher before the school removes them, and a few require consent first, for example for some special schools or children on a support plan. A dated letter and a confirmation of removal protect the family from attendance action and give a clear start date for home education.

                ## Milestones
                1. The correct withdrawal procedure for your child's school and situation confirmed.
                2. A short, dated letter of withdrawal sent to the head teacher with proof of delivery.
                3. Written confirmation received that the child is off the roll, with the date.
                4. Copies of the letter and confirmation filed in the home education records.

                ## Notes
                Keep the letter short and factual. You usually do not need to give reasons, and a meeting is not normally required before removal unless your local rules say so.
              priority: high
              deadlineOffsetDays: 21
              frontmatter:
                mode: building
                output_kind: deliverable

                cadence: one-shot
                effort_hours_estimate: "2"
              tasks:
                - "Check whether your child needs consent before removal from the roll"
                - "Draft a short withdrawal letter giving the date home education begins"

                - "File the letter and confirmation in the home education records folder"
            - name: Choosing your home education approach
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Families who pick a method in a rush often buy a full boxed curriculum, find it recreates the school day they left, and abandon it by spring. Reading about four or five approaches (structured curriculum, Charlotte Mason, classical, project-based, autonomous or unschooling, eclectic) and matching them to how your children learn and how much time you have gives a starting point you can defend and adjust.

                ## Notes

                Children who leave school often need weeks or months before they can learn in a new way, and parents need the same time to stop reproducing timetables at the kitchen table.                 ## Milestones

# Home Education

This area is for parents educating their children at home, and for the educators who teach, tutor or mentor home-educating families. It runs from the foundations (the rules where you live, leaving school properly, choosing an approach, a baseline and a written statement of provision) through the weekly and termly machinery of logs, portfolios and planning, the teaching skills worth learning, curriculum and co-op decisions, exam entries and trips, situations such as a child leaving school in distress or a parent who also works, and finally transcripts, group teaching and routes into college and university.

What repeats is a short daily log line, a Friday log summary, Sunday planning, a monthly trip and social calendar, a monthly portfolio pick and workload check, termly planning and progress conversations, and the yearly authority report and curriculum review. The Skills matrix, Purchase decision, Vendor, Trip and Development plan templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
