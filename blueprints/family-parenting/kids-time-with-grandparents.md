                ## Purpose
                Most families have more grandparents than they first count: two sets, perhaps step-grandparents, a great-grandmother, an honorary grandad next door.               description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Parents sometimes assume grandparents are longing to help, or politely refusing out of pride, when the truth is somewhere else: a grandmother still working three days a week, a grandad with a bad back who cannot lift a toddler into a car seat. # Kids' Time With Grandparents

Kids' time with grandparents covers everything that lets children and their grandparents see a lot of each other without the adults falling out over it, for parents juggling two sets of grandparents, a childcare deal and sometimes an ocean in between. It starts with the foundations of safety, house rules and a frank conversation about what everyone wants, moves through the routines of grandparent days, handovers, video calls and post, then the skills, decisions and big visits, and ends with separation, step-families, illness, loss and the rarer situations where contact needs limits.

The rhythms are a weekly grandparent day message and video call, monthly post, photos, expenses and shared activities, a quarterly check-in with the grandparents, and yearly updates to the safety pack, the holiday split, gift lists and the whole arrangement.
