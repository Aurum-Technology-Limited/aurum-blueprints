                ## Purpose
                Before anyone sits behind the wheel, find out exactly what your licensing authority requires: the minimum age for a learner permit, who may supervise, whether logged hours are compulsory, which roads learners may use and how long before a test can be taken. Rules differ sharply between countries and even between states, and the rules your own licence was issued under are probably out of date. A one-page summary from the official source settles arguments before they start.

                ## Milestones

                2. A one-page summary covering minimum age, supervisor rules, required hours, road restrictions and the order of tests.
                3. Any rule that has changed since you learned marked in the summary.
                4. The summary read by your teenager and every adult who might supervise.

                ## Notes
                Use only the licensing authority's own pages or printed guidance. Forums and driving school blogs often describe another country or an old version of the rules.

# Learner Driver Teen Practice

This area is for the parent in the passenger seat while a teenager learns to drive, from the first permit application to the first months on a full licence. It starts with the foundations (the rules where you live, supervisor eligibility, the permit, insurance, a legal practice car, a budget, an instructor and a logbook), then the weekly practice machinery, the skills to build road by road and condition by condition, the decisions along the way, the theory and practical tests, the family situations that change the plan, and finally the first year as a new driver.

What repeats is booking practice slots and adding up logged hours each week, a short debrief after drives, a weekly handover with the instructor, daily theory revision while it lasts, and monthly reviews of skills, costs, conditions covered and documents. The Purchase decision, Metrics log, Operational checklist, Savings goal and Trip templates pair with the projects that name them.
