# Newborn First Three Months

This area carries new parents through the twelve weeks after bringing a baby home. It starts with the foundations (a contacts sheet, a warning signs card, a safe sleep space, feeding support, the feed and nappy log, visitor rules, food and the birth registration), then the daily and weekly machinery of night rotas, totals, weigh-ins and recovery checks, the feeding and soothing skills, the decisions that tend to come up around week three, the visits and checks to prepare for, versions for families with a toddler, a neonatal stay, a caesarean, a single parent or no family nearby, and finally the specialist feeding situations.

What repeats is a daily feed and nappy total, daily bottle cleaning and tummy time, weekly weigh-in notes, a Sunday night rota review, midweek recovery and mood check-ins, twice-weekly rest windows for each parent, a Friday admin half hour, a Saturday house reset and a monthly nappy order. The Metrics log, Weekly meal plan, Household chores and Purchase decision templates pair with the projects that name them.
