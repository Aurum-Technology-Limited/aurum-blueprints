# Parental Leave Planning

This area is for parents planning maternity, paternity, adoption or shared leave, and especially for knowledge workers who need to hand over a role and come back to it in good shape. It starts with the foundations (entitlements, contract terms, notice dates, the split between parents and the money), then the routines that run before and during leave, the things worth learning, the decisions about cover, return dates and flexible work, the dated moments from the last week at work to the return meeting, the situations that change the rules, and finally the specialist work of checking pay fairness and resolving disputes.

What repeats is a weekly handover update and cover session before leave, a monthly budget check-in, pay slip check and keeping-in-touch log during it, a Sunday check-in between parents, quarterly plan reviews and a yearly look at how the return is working. The Savings goal, Monthly budget and bills, Course, Meeting notes and Onboarding 30/60/90 templates pair with the projects that name them.
