# Parenting Approach Agreement

This area is for two parents who love their children and still find themselves giving different answers to the same question. It starts with the foundations (how each of you was raised, shared values, the gaps between you, non-negotiables, discipline beliefs and a first written agreement), then the routines that keep you aligned week to week, the skills of disagreeing well and speaking with one voice, decisions on consequences, rewards, food, faith, independence, language and schooling, the dated moments worth agreeing ahead of, versions for default parents, shift workers, new babies and grandparent childcare, and finally the hard cases where you are truly stuck.

What repeats is a Sunday check-in and a Wednesday look at the flashpoint log, monthly reviews of the agreement, support and tag-outs on different days, a weekly handover note for default and away parents, a termly education check, quarterly carer briefings and yearly rewrites on each child's birthday and before the school year. The Meeting notes, Habit tracker and Course templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
