# Puberty & Growing Up Talks

This area is for parents who want their children to grow up knowing how their bodies work, what a period or a wet dream is before it happens, and what consent means, through many short, honest conversations rather than one dreaded talk. It starts with the foundations (your own comfort, who talks about what, the correct words, an age-by-age talk map, the school's lessons and body safety rules), then the routines that keep the conversation going, the knowledge and talking skills for each topic, practical decisions on products, bras, shaving and privacy, the first period and other milestones, versions for early and late developers, children with additional needs, single parents, two homes, faith families and fostered or adopted children, and finally plans for heavy periods, disclosures and sexual health scares.

What repeats is a weekly rhythm (Friday answers from the question box, a Sunday note of teachable moments, a Tuesday ask-first moment, a Thursday check on body comments and Saturday sports kit washing), monthly check-ins, adult debriefs, two-home updates, supplies restocks and period diary reviews on varied days, quarterly size, kit, school lesson and body safety checks, and a yearly birthday refresh of the talk map. The Reading queue, Habit tracker, Meeting notes, Purchase decision, Trip and Metrics log templates pair with the projects that name them.
