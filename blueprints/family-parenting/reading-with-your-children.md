                ## Purpose
                Before buying books or setting rules, spend one week noticing how reading actually happens in your home: who reads to whom, on which nights, for how long, and what gets skipped. # Reading With Your Children

This area is for parents who want reading to be a normal, enjoyable part of family life, from board books with a baby to swapping novels with a teenager. It starts with the foundations (a picture of how reading goes now, library cards, a bedtime story routine, a shelf and a reading corner, and knowing your child's reading stage), then the weekly machinery of story time, library trips and shelf rotation, the skills of phonics, prompting and reading aloud well, decisions about devices, comics and rewards, dated events like book day and the summer challenge, situations such as two homes, two languages or a reluctant reader, and finally book clubs, long read-alouds and helping at school.

What repeats is the nightly story and the short daily practice with the school reading book, a Saturday library trip, a Sunday family reading half hour, monthly shelf rotation and a books-finished log, and a termly chat with the class teacher. The Reading queue, Habit tracker, Metrics log, Meeting notes and Purchase decision templates pair with the projects that name them.
