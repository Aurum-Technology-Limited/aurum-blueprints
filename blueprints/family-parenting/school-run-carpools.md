# School Run & Carpools

This area is for parents running the twice-daily trip to and from school, whether that is a five-minute walk, a bus across town or a car shared with three other families. It starts with the foundations (a weekly run map, a leave-by time, the right way to travel, the school's collection rules, car seats, a walked route and a backup ladder), moves into the weekly and monthly machinery of rotas, carpools and car checks, then road and travel skills, decisions about clubs, cars and paid helpers, dated days like the practice run and school trips, variants for siblings at two schools, toddlers in tow, two households, shift workers and children with additional needs, and finally the specialist end of walking buses, school street campaigns and large carpool agreements.

What repeats is a Sunday rota check, a Friday reset of the door station, monthly carpool rotas, swap balances, car checks and cost totals, a termly review and a yearly winter plan.
