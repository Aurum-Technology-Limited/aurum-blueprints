            - name: Two-week sibling conflict log
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Most parents of two or more children feel the fighting is constant, yet when they write it down the bad patches cluster around the same few times, places and objects. Logging every argument, snatch or push for fourteen days, with the time, the trigger, who started and how it ended, replaces a general sense of chaos with evidence. Every later decision in this area is easier with that record in hand.

                ## Milestones
                1. A log set up on paper or a phone with columns for time, place, children involved, trigger, length and how it ended.
                2. Fourteen consecutive days logged, including the calm days, by whichever adult was present.
                3. The three most common triggers and the two worst times of day circled.
                4. A short written summary shared with the other parent or main carer.

                ## Notes
                Start from the **Metrics log** template. Record what actually happened rather than who you think was to blame; blame columns turn the log into a case file against one child.
              priority: high
              deadlineOffsetDays: 21
              frontmatter:
                mode: building
                output_kind: artifact
                success_criteria: "A completed fourteen-day log with the top three triggers and two peak times of day written down and shared with the other carer."
                cadence: one-shot
                effort_hours_estimate: "4"
              tasks:
                - "Set up a log with columns for time, place, children, trigger and outcome"
                - "Agree with every adult at home that each fight gets a line the same day"

                - "Write a five-line summary of the pattern for the other carer"

                The first minute of a sibling fight decides whether it fizzles out or escalates, and it is exactly when tired parents improvise, shout or take sides. # Sibling Rivalry & Sharing

This area is for parents of two or more children who are tired of refereeing, and who want brothers and sisters who can share a car, a bedroom and a parent without daily warfare. It starts with the foundations (a two-week fight log, a flashpoint map, a few house rules, a script both parents use and a clear line for when to step in), then the routines that keep things calmer, the skills children and parents practise to settle disputes, decisions on bedrooms, rewards and fairness, the events that test everyone, versions for a new baby, wide age gaps, step-siblings, single parents and a disabled sibling, and finally the specialist work on sibling bullying, restorative conversations and outside help.

What repeats is a short daily slot of one-to-one time with each child, a dinner-table habit of noticing kindness, a weekly turn rota, a Saturday team challenge, a Thursday check-in between parents, monthly one-child outings on varied days, and a yearly look at how the relationship between your children is changing. The Metrics log, Habit tracker, Operational checklist, Reading queue and Trip templates pair with the projects that name them.
