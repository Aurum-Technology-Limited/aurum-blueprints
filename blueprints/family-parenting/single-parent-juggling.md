# Single Parent Juggling

This area is for parents running a household as the only adult, whether newly separated, widowed or single by choice, and it treats support, routines and rest as things to build on purpose. It starts with the foundations (a load audit, a support map, a sick-day backup ladder, cover for your own illness, a handover file, a babysitter bench, a one-income budget and an entitlements check), moves through the daily and weekly machinery, the skills of asking for help and staying calm alone, decisions about paid help, swaps and moving, dated events such as a solo holiday, an operation or a house move, variants for babies, teenagers, shift workers, bereaved parents and carers, and ends with a yearly resilience review, a swap circle and mentoring.

What repeats is a nightly fifteen-minute reset, a Sunday week plan, a Wednesday recharge slot, a Thursday admin hour, a Saturday batch cook, monthly checks on the budget, favours, restocking and your own energy, quarterly checks that the backup ladder and handover file still hold, and a yearly review before the school year. The Operational checklist, Monthly budget and bills, Weekly meal plan, Sleep review, Purchase decision and Trip templates pair with the projects that name them.
