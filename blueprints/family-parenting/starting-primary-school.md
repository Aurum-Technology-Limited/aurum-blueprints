# Starting Primary School

This area takes a parent from the school offer letter to a settled child at the end of the first year. It starts with the foundations (the information folder, forms and consents, a medical plan, school apps, uniform, lunches, wraparound care, self-care skills and leave for the staggered start), then the bedtime, evening, morning, admin, kit and teacher routines, the skills to practise with your child, the decisions about start pattern, costs and shared jobs, the visits and first days to prepare for, versions for summer-born, anxious, late-joining, multilingual and two-home children and for your own feelings, and finally the work of an involved school parent.

What repeats is the nightly bag and uniform check, a Sunday admin half hour, a Friday settling-in note, midweek uniform washing, a Saturday packed lunch plan, twice-weekly phonics practice, termly date sweeps and monthly top-ups, budget logs and teacher notes. The Habit tracker, Household chores, Weekly meal plan, Purchase decision, Savings goal and Meeting notes templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
