              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                More than half of twins and almost all triplets are born before 37 weeks, so the usual advice to pack at 36 weeks leaves many multiple parents scrambling from a hospital bed. Having the bag, two car seats, the neonatal unit details and cover for work and older children in place by around 30 weeks turns an early labour into a plan rather than a crisis.

                ## Milestones
                1. The hospital bag packed with two of every baby item and kept by the door from about 30 weeks.
                2. Two car seats fitted in the car before the third trimester begins.
                3. The neonatal unit's visiting rules, parking and expressing room arrangements written down.
                4. Cover agreed for older children, pets and work if labour starts weeks early.
                5. The midwife team asked what signs of early labour mean you should call straight away.

                ## Notes
                Ask your maternity team when they would usually plan delivery for your type of twin or triplet pregnancy, and set your packing date well before that, not before the standard due date.

# Twins & Multiples Parenting

This area is for parents expecting or raising twins, triplets or more, where every routine has to work for two or three children of exactly the same age at once. It starts with the foundations (an early arrival plan, the multiples club, the kit and buggy decisions, telling identical babies apart, a two-baby feed log, the budget, a helper rota and the paperwork), then the weekly machinery of synchronised schedules, night shifts, stock and laundry, the feeding and settling skills, the decisions about classes, rooms and clubs, the dated firsts, versions for older siblings, split discharges, single parents, triplets, toddlers and teenagers, and finally the specialist work around prematurity, research registries, volunteering and loss.

What repeats is a daily feed total and evening bottle prep, a Sunday schedule review, Saturday night shift planning, Friday one-to-one time with each child, a Monday household reset, a Tuesday outing, a Thursday couple check-in, monthly stock orders, budget checks and milestone notes on different days, quarterly clothes rotation, and yearly class reviews, parents' evenings and the shared birthday. The Purchase decision, Metrics log, Monthly budget and bills, Household chores and Trip templates pair with the projects that name them.
