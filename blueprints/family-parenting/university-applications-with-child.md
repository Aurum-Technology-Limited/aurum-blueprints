            - name: Agreeing who does what on the application
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Applications go wrong in two opposite ways: a parent who quietly writes half of it, or a parent who only hears about a missed deadline afterwards. Agreeing at the start which parts your child owns, where you help and how you will check in avoids both, and keeps the application recognisably theirs, which is what admissions tutors want to read.

                ## Milestones
                1. A written list of application tasks, each marked as your child's or shared.
                2. An agreed way of raising worries without nagging, such as one weekly check-in.
                3. Portal logins held by your child, with a recovery contact agreed.
                4. The agreement revisited once after the first month.

                ## Notes
                Most admissions services ask applicants to confirm the statement is their own work. Help with ideas and proofreading is normal; drafting sentences for them is not.
              priority: high
              deadlineOffsetDays: 14
              frontmatter:
                mode: building
