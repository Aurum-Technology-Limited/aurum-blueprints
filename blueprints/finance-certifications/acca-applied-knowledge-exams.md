# ACCA Applied Knowledge Exams

This area is for students working through ACCA's three Applied Knowledge exams, Business and Technology, Management Accounting and Financial Accounting, whether straight from school, at university, already in an accounts job or changing career. It opens with the foundations (exemptions, registration, the current syllabus, the order of papers, a budget, materials and a dated calendar), then the study routines, the BT, MA and FA topics in syllabus order, exam technique and the decisions that rescue a slipping plan, booking, mocks, exam day and results, situations from school leaver to sitting in the Caribbean, and finally practical experience and the work of a candidate who wants more than a pass.

What repeats is a daily question set and flashcard review, weekly study blocks and an error log review, fortnightly timed practice, a monthly progress check against your booked date, monthly redo questions on variances and bank reconciliations, and the yearly subscription and syllabus checks. The Savings goal, Purchase decision, Habit tracker, Metrics log, Remediation plan and Operational checklist templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
