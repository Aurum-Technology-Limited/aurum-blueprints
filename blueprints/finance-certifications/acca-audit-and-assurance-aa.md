# ACCA Audit and Assurance (AA)

This area is for ACCA students preparing for Audit and Assurance, whether you already work on audits or have never seen a working paper. It starts with the foundations (the current syllabus and examinable documents, the session and centre, where AA sits in your exam order, materials, an hours budget, a calendar and the exam software), then the weekly practice machinery, a project for each block of the syllabus from the audit framework and ethics through planning and risk, internal control and evidence on specific balances to completion and the auditor's report, then answer technique, mocks, exam day and results, the situations that change the plan, and finally a few projects that take AA beyond the exam.

What repeats is a weekly rhythm of planned study blocks, a timed Section B question, objective test case sets and an error log review, alongside daily flashcards, monthly reading of examiner's reports and technical articles, a monthly progress review and a quarterly recheck of ACCA's AA pages. The Purchase decision, Habit tracker, Metrics log, Remediation plan and Operational checklist templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
