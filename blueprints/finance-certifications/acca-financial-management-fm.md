# ACCA Financial Management (FM)

This area is for ACCA students taking Financial Management, whether at university, in a first finance job or studying alone at the kitchen table. It starts with the foundations (exemptions, the current syllabus and exam format, the session, materials, a dated calendar, the formulae sheet and the exam software), moves through the weekly study routines and every syllabus area in ACCA's order, from financial objectives and working capital through investment appraisal, business finance and valuation to currency and interest rate risk, then covers the decisions that rescue a slipping plan, the mocks and exam day, situations such as resitting or studying around work, children or a Caribbean test centre, and finally projects for candidates who want to go beyond the pass.

What repeats is a daily objective test set, weekly study planning, a weekly error log review, formula recall and news log, a timed constructed-response question each week, a monthly progress check and examiner's report, and the quarterly and yearly ACCA account admin. The Purchase decision, Habit tracker, Metrics log, Remediation plan and Operational checklist templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
