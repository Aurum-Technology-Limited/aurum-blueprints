# ACCA Financial Reporting (FR)

This area is for an ACCA student sitting Financial Reporting, whether straight after the Applied Knowledge exams, from a degree with exemptions or from a job in an accounts team. It starts with the foundations (the current syllabus and format, a gap check on Financial Accounting, the exam session, materials and a dated calendar), then the weekly routines, the syllabus topics from the Conceptual Framework through single-entity standards, statements, groups and interpretation, the changes that rescue a slipping plan, the mocks, final weeks and exam day, situations such as working, caring or sitting from the Caribbean, and finally the work of a candidate taking FR beyond the pass.

What repeats is a daily question set and flashcard review, weekly study blocks, an error log tally and a partner call, monthly checks of the calendar, the score log, an examiner's report, a real annual report and reworked consolidation, cash flow and single-entity questions, plus a quarterly syllabus check and a yearly subscription renewal. The Purchase decision, Habit tracker, Metrics log, Remediation plan, Operational checklist and Portfolio piece templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
