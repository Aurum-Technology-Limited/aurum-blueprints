# ACCA Performance Management (PM)

This area is for an ACCA student sitting Performance Management, whether studying full time, training in a finance team or fitting it around a degree. It starts with the foundations (whether you need to sit PM, the current syllabus, the session, materials, a Management Accounting refresher, a baseline and a dated calendar), then the weekly study routines, the syllabus area by area from information systems to the behavioural side of performance, exam technique and recovery decisions, mocks, exam day and results, situations such as a resit, a finance job, a degree or sitting from the Caribbean, and finally the work of a candidate going beyond the pass.

What repeats is a daily objective test set and flashcard review, a weekly planning session, timed constructed-response question, error log review and partner call, a monthly plan check and examiner report reading, and the yearly subscription. The Savings goal, Purchase decision, Habit tracker, Metrics log, Remediation plan and Operational checklist templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
