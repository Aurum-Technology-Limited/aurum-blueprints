# ACCA Strategic Business Leader (SBL)

This area is for ACCA students and working finance people preparing for Strategic Business Leader, the integrated case exam that is one of the two Strategic Professional essentials. It runs from the foundations (current rules, your exam record, the ethics module, a budget, tuition, a dated plan, booking and a first look at the specimen exam), through the weekly practice machinery, the eight syllabus sections from leadership to change, the five professional skills and the decisions that shape a sitting, to two full mocks, exam day and results, the situations that change a plan, and three ways to use SBL at work and beyond.

What repeats is a weekly rhythm of planned study blocks, one timed requirement, a rewrite from the error log, typing practice and a peer-marked swap, alongside daily business reading, monthly plan reviews, score averages, skills ratings and examiner's report reading, and quarterly and yearly checks of ACCA's rules and your subscription. The Purchase decision, Skills matrix, Metrics log and Risk register templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
