# ACCA Strategic Business Reporting (SBR)

This area is for ACCA candidates preparing for Strategic Business Reporting, whether you are a student moving up from FR or an accountant who already prepares or audits group accounts. It starts with the foundations (the current syllabus and examinable documents, the session, your exam order, an FR gap check, materials, a calendar and the exam software), then the weekly practice machinery, a project for each part of the syllabus from ethics and the Conceptual Framework through specific standards and group accounts to interpretation, sustainability reporting and current issues, then answer technique, mocks, exam day and results, the situations that change the plan, and two projects that put SBR to work in your job.

What repeats is a weekly rhythm of study blocks, a timed written question, an ethics question, a partner answer swap and a marks lost review, alongside daily flashcards, monthly examiner's reports, technical articles, IASB and ISSB work plan checks and a progress review, and a quarterly recheck of ACCA's SBR pages. The Purchase decision, Habit tracker, Metrics log, Reading queue, Remediation plan and Operational checklist templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
