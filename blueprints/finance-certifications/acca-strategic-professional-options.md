# ACCA Strategic Professional Options

This area is for ACCA students and working accountants who have reached Strategic Professional and need to choose and pass two of the four options: Advanced Financial Management, Advanced Performance Management, Advanced Taxation and Advanced Audit and Assurance. It starts with the foundations (the current rules and syllabuses, the choice of options, variants, sequencing, booking, materials, an hours budget and a calendar), then the weekly practice machinery, a block of projects for each option so you can keep the two you chose and archive the rest, the exam technique that cuts across all four, the mock, final month, exam day and results, the situations that change the plan, and finally a few projects that carry the options into your work and experience record.

What repeats is a weekly rhythm of study blocks, a timed question and an error log review, a short daily formula or rates recall, monthly reading of examiner's reports and current developments, monthly topic rework for each option, a monthly progress review and a quarterly recheck of ACCA's options pages. The Purchase decision, Habit tracker, Metrics log, Remediation plan, Operational checklist and Development plan templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
