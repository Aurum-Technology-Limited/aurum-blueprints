# Actuarial Exams P and FM

This area is for students sitting the first two actuarial exams, Probability (P) and Financial Mathematics (FM), usually during a mathematics, statistics, economics or actuarial science degree. It starts with the foundations (the current syllabus, which exam to sit first, registration, an approved calculator, materials, costs, a calculus check and a week-by-week plan), then the study routines, the P and FM topics in syllabus order, the fixes and decisions that rescue a stalling plan, mock exams, exam day and results, an internship plan, situations such as a retake or sitting from the Caribbean, and finally the modelling, simulation and teaching that students who have already passed take on.

What repeats is a daily problem set and flashcard review, weekly timed sets, error log rework, formula rewrites and study group sessions, a monthly check of the plan against the exam date, and a quarterly look at the official exam pages. The Purchase decision, Metrics log, Habit tracker, Remediation plan, Job application and Resume templates pair with the projects that name them.
