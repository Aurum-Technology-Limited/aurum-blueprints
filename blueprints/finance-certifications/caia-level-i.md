# CAIA Level I

This area is for anyone studying for CAIA Level I, whether you work in fund operations, wealth management, consulting or an allocator's team, or you are a student aiming at a first job in alternatives. It starts with the foundations (your window and registration, eligibility, this window's curriculum, a diagnostic and a plan counted back from exam day), then the weekly study machinery, then the topics from professional standards and return measurement through real assets, hedge funds, private equity and structured products, followed by technique and window decisions, the mocks and exam day, the situations that change the plan, and two projects that put Level I to work.

What repeats is twice-weekly study blocks planned each Sunday, a Saturday timed set, daily flashcards, a Monday ethics warm-up, a Friday error log retest, a monthly progress and coverage review and a monthly check of the curriculum errata, with a quarterly look at work peaks. The Purchase decision, Metrics log, Habit tracker, Reading queue and Remediation plan templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
