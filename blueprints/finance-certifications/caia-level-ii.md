# CAIA Level II

CAIA Level II is the exam where alternatives stop being a list of asset classes and become an allocator's job: placing them in a portfolio, choosing and checking managers, and managing the risks that illiquid assets hide. This area is for students and working professionals sitting Level II, and it runs from the exam facts, a Level I gap audit and a dated study calendar, through the weekly study machinery and the topic readings, to the mocks and exam day, the situations that change the plan, and a few specialist exercises that carry the material into real work.

The rhythm is mostly weekly: study blocks on weekdays and a long Saturday session, a Friday timed topic set, two constructed-response answers, a reading summary and a Sunday error log review, with a daily flashcard session, a monthly progress review, formula rewrite, score trend and ethics case. The Purchase decision, Reading queue and Metrics log templates pair with the materials choice, the reading queue and the score log.
