# CAMS Anti-Money Laundering Exam

This area is for bank, credit union, money services and corporate services staff preparing for the ACAMS CAMS exam, from the first check of the eligibility rules to adding the letters after your name. It starts with the foundations (current exam rules, eligibility credits, the membership and exam package, a date, an hours budget and a calendar), then the weekly study machinery, a project for each part of the content from laundering and terrorist financing methods to sanctions, compliance programmes and investigations, the decisions and mocks that rescue a plan, exam day and results, situations for different roles across the Caribbean, and finally recertification, correspondent banking pressures and what to study next.

What repeats is a weekly rhythm of booked study blocks, mixed question sets, error log reviews, enforcement reading and a partner call, alongside daily flashcards, a monthly progress review, a monthly look at your FIU's and the sanctions authorities' updates, and a yearly recertification credit check. The Purchase decision, Skills matrix, Metrics log, Reading queue, Remediation plan and Operational checklist templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
