# Caribbean Accountancy Body Membership

This area is for accountants who have qualified through ACCA, the US CPA or another recognised body and now want membership of a national institute such as ICATT in Trinidad and Tobago, ICAJ in Jamaica, ICAB in Barbados, ICAG in Guyana, ICAEC in the Eastern Caribbean or BICA in the Bahamas. It starts with the foundations (choosing the institute, your admission route, the evidence pack and the application), then the yearly CPD and dues machinery, the local law, ethics and anti-money laundering knowledge members are expected to hold, decisions about dual membership and CPD spending, the practising certificate for public practice, the dated events, situations such as returning from abroad or a career break, and finally fellowship and service to the institute.

What repeats is a monthly CPD log and events check, a monthly ethics case, a quarterly technical update and evidence check, and the yearly cycle of subscription, CPD return, practising certificate renewal, indemnity cover, AML training and the AGM. The Person, Metrics log, Compliance control, Development plan, Meeting notes and Remediation plan templates pair with the projects that name them.
