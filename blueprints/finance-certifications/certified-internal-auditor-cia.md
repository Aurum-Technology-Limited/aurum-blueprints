# Certified Internal Auditor (CIA)

This area is for working auditors, risk and compliance staff and accountants studying for the IIA's three-part Certified Internal Auditor exam, from the first check of the syllabus to the year you report CPE as a certified auditor. It starts with the foundations (the current rules, your eligibility and application, the order of the parts, a review course, an hours budget and a first booked seat), then the daily and weekly study machinery, a project for each block of Part 1, Part 2 and Part 3 content, then mocks, remediation, the final fortnight, exam day, results and the experience sign-off, the situations that change the plan, and finally what an experienced internal auditor does with the credential.

What repeats is a daily question set and flashcard round, weekly study blocks, an error log review and a workplace examples habit, a monthly progress rating, mixed review and deadline check, and a quarterly recheck of the IIA's syllabus and rules, with monthly and yearly CPE logging once certified. The Purchase decision, Metrics log, Remediation plan, Operational checklist and Development plan templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
