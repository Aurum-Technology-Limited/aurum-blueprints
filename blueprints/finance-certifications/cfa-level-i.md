# CFA Level I

This area is for anyone preparing for CFA Level I, whether a final-year student or someone fitting study around a full-time job. It starts with the foundations (eligibility, choosing a window, fees, registration, the calculator and a week-by-week schedule), then the study routines, the ten topic areas in curriculum order with the practical skills module, the decisions that rescue a slipping plan, the mock exams and exam day, situations such as a retake or travelling to a test centre, and finally the ethics casework, real-company analysis and mentoring that experienced candidates take on.

What repeats is a daily question set and flashcard review, weekly sessions for logging hours, the error log, ethics and timed practice, a weekly call with a study partner and a monthly check of the plan against the exam date. The Savings goal, Purchase decision, Metrics log, Habit tracker, Remediation plan and Trip templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
