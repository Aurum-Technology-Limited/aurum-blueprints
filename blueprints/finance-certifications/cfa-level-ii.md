# CFA Level II

This area is for a candidate who has passed CFA Level I and now faces Level II, whether a university or master's student or an analyst studying around a full-time job. It starts with the foundations (the current topic outline and format, booking the appointment, any practical skills module, a Level I carry-over diagnostic, an hours budget, a topic-weighted plan, prep materials, the calculator, a formula sheet and an error log), then the weekly practice machinery, then one project per curriculum topic from quantitative methods through ethics, each ending on a logged practice set. After those come the halfway and final mocks, the final review weeks, exam week and results, then projects for full-time workers, sponsored candidates, students, candidates travelling to a test centre and retakers, and two that join the topics together.

What repeats is a daily flashcard review, weekday reading and question evenings, a Tuesday ethics set, a Wednesday spaced review vignette, a Thursday study group call, a Saturday timed vignette session and a Sunday error log review, with monthly plan checks, accuracy updates and integrated cases. The Purchase decision, Habit tracker, Metrics log and Trip templates pair with choosing materials, the daily cards, the accuracy log and travel to a test centre. Deadlines assume roughly six months to your exam appointment, so shift them once your window and date are booked. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
