# CFA Level III

This area is for candidates who have passed Level II and now face the level that asks them to write like a portfolio manager, whether they are a graduate student or already working in investments. It starts with the foundations (the current format, your pathway, registration and a realistic plan), then the weekly routines that keep study honest, the core readings and essay technique, the decisions that adjust the plan, the mocks and exam day, the situations that change the load, and finally the pathway plans and integrated practice of a well-prepared candidate.

What repeats is a daily study block, a Sunday plan reset, a weekly error log review, timed essay sets and ethics questions, flashcard reviews twice a week, a monthly progress review and a monthly integrated case. The Purchase decision, Habit tracker, Metrics log and Meeting notes templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
