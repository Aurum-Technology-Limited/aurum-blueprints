                ## Purpose
                The CFP marks are awarded by CFP Board in the United States and by member organisations of the Financial Planning Standards Board in other countries, and each sets its own education, examination, experience and ethics rules. Confirming which body covers the country where you will practise, before paying for any course, stops you studying for a route that will not certify you there.

                ## Milestones
                1. The certifying body for your country of practice identified, or the absence of one confirmed in writing.
                2. Its four requirements, education, examination, experience and ethics, summarised on one page from its own website.
                3. The current exam format, fees and windows noted with the date you checked them.
                4. A written decision on which body you will certify through.

                ## Notes
                Fees, question counts, hour totals and windows change. Take them only from the body's own pages, never from a forum post or an older study guide.

# CFP Certification

This area is for anyone working toward CFP certification: university students on a planning degree, professionals crossing over from accounting, banking or insurance, and freelance planners documenting their own client work. It starts with the foundations (the certifying body and its four requirements, the degree and education programme, disclosure readiness, a diagnostic, the exam window and a study plan), then the weekly study and experience routines, the exam's planning domains, the decisions about review courses, pathways and mocks, the exam and certification events, situations such as certifying from a country without a local body, and finally the renewal, practice and mentoring work of a certified planner.

What repeats is a weekly study block, coursework and error log checks, a daily question set, a monthly progress check and experience log entry, and, once certified, quarterly continuing education records with a yearly renewal and planning figures refresh. The Purchase decision, Metrics log, Course, Portfolio piece, Remediation plan and Development plan templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
