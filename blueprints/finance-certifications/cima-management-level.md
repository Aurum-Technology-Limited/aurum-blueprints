# CIMA Management Level

This area is for students and working finance people taking CIMA's Management level, from the first check of the current rules to the case study result. It opens with the foundations (rules, progression, tuition, an hours budget, a dated calendar and the first booking), then the weekly study machinery, a project for each block of Managing Performance, Advanced Management Accounting and Advanced Financial Reporting, the objective test endgame, the case study from competencies and pre-seen to exam day, the situations that change a plan, and two ways to make the level count at work.

What repeats is a weekly rhythm of booked study blocks, timed question sets, error log retests, learning outcome ratings and business news reading, alongside daily recall cards, a monthly progress review and score log, a monthly refresher on subjects already passed and a quarterly recheck of CIMA's rules. The Purchase decision, Skills matrix, Metrics log, Operational checklist and Remediation plan templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
