# CIMA Operational Level

This area is for anyone working through CIMA's Operational level, whether a university student, a finance assistant studying with employer support or a career changer starting from scratch. It begins with the foundations (exemptions, the current syllabus and blueprints, paper order, budget, materials and a dated calendar), then the weekly study routines, the E1, P1 and F1 topics in syllabus order, the decisions that rescue a slipping plan, the case study from booking to results, situations such as studying around a job, a degree, children or a Caribbean test centre, and finally the work of a candidate going beyond the pass.

What repeats is a daily question set and flashcard review, weekly study planning, an error log review, a partner call and a news log, a monthly plan check against your bookings and an annual subscription renewal. The Savings goal, Purchase decision, Habit tracker, Metrics log, Remediation plan and Operational checklist templates pair with the projects that name them.
