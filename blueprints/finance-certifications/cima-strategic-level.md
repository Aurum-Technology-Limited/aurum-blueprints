# CIMA Strategic Level

This area is for students and working finance people finishing CIMA at the Strategic level, from the first check of the current rules to using ACMA and CGMA after their name. It opens with the foundations (rules, eligibility, budget, tuition, hours, a dated route and the first booking), then the weekly study machinery, topic projects for E3 Strategic Management, P3 Risk Management and F3 Financial Strategy, the objective test endgame, the Strategic case study from competencies to results, the experience and membership steps, the situations that change a plan and three ways to use the level at work and beyond.

What repeats is a weekly rhythm of planned study blocks, timed question sets, error log entries, press reading and a work-peak check, alongside daily recall cards, monthly route reviews, score checks, refreshers on passed subjects and experience record entries, plus quarterly and yearly checks of CIMA's rules, your subscription, a team risk register and your CPD. The Purchase decision, Metrics log, Skills matrix, Development plan and Risk register templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
