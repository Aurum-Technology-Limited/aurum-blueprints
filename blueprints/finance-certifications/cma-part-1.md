# CMA Part 1

This area is for students and working accountants preparing for IMA's CMA Part 1, Financial Planning, Performance and Analytics, from the first check of the current rules to the day results arrive. It starts with the foundations (rules, membership, which part to sit first, the testing window, a review course, an hours budget, a calendar and a calculator), then the weekly study machinery, a project for each block of the six sections from external reporting through budgeting, performance and cost management to internal controls and technology and analytics, then essay technique, mocks and recovery, exam day and results, the situations that change the plan, and finally ways to put Part 1 to work.

What repeats is a weekly rhythm of booked study blocks, mixed question sets, error log reviews and a timed essay scenario, alongside daily flashcards, monthly outcome statement ratings, formula sheet rewrites and progress reviews, and a quarterly recheck of IMA's rules. The Purchase decision, Skills matrix, Metrics log, Remediation plan, Operational checklist and Trip templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
