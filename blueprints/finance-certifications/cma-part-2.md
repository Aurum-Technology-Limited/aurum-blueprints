# CMA Part 2

This area is for students and working finance professionals preparing for IMA's CMA Part 2, Strategic Financial Management, from the first check of the current outline to the certification steps after a pass. It starts with the foundations (rules, membership, part order, booking, a readiness check, calculator, course, hours and a calendar), then the weekly study machinery, a project for each block of financial statement analysis, corporate finance, decision analysis, risk management, investment decisions and professional ethics, the decisions and mocks that keep a plan honest, exam day and results, the situations that change the plan, and finally certification, continuing education and applying Part 2 at work.

What repeats is a weekly rhythm of planned study blocks, a timed essay scenario, an error log review, a capital budgeting rebuild, an ethics scenario, a weak section re-test and a partner check-in, alongside daily question sets, monthly formula rewrites, coverage ratings and score reviews, and a quarterly recheck of IMA's rules. The Purchase decision, Skills matrix, Metrics log, Remediation plan, Operational checklist and Trip templates pair with the projects that name them.
