# CPA Exam AUD Section

This area is for accounting students and working accountants preparing for the CPA Exam's Auditing and Attestation section, from the first check of the AICPA blueprint to the day the score posts. It starts with the foundations (the current exam rules, your application and Notice to Schedule, where AUD sits in your section order, a review course, an hours budget and a booked seat), then the daily and weekly study machinery, a project for each part of the four content areas from ethics and independence through risk assessment and evidence to reports and attestation work, then simulation technique, mocks, remediation, the final fortnight, exam day and results, the situations that change the plan, and finally what an experienced candidate or practitioner does with AUD.

What repeats is a daily multiple-choice set and flashcard round, weekly study blocks, simulation sessions and error log reviews, a monthly progress rating against the blueprint and a mixed review of finished topics, and a quarterly check of the blueprint and your credit window. The Purchase decision, Metrics log, Skills matrix, Remediation plan, Operational checklist and Trip templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
