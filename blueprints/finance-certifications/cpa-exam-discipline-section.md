# CPA Exam Discipline Section

This area is for accounting students and working accountants who have to pick one of the three CPA discipline sections, Business Analysis and Reporting (BAR), Information Systems and Controls (ISC) or Tax Compliance and Planning (TCP), and then pass it. It opens with the foundations (the current blueprints and testing windows, the choice itself, your Notice to Schedule, section order, a review course, an hours budget and a booked seat), then the daily and weekly study machinery, then topic projects for each of the three disciplines so you can archive the two you did not choose, followed by simulation technique, mocks, the final fortnight, exam day and results, the situations that bend the plan, and what an experienced candidate or practitioner does with the discipline afterwards.

What repeats is a daily question set and flashcard round, twice-weekly study blocks with a Sunday plan, a weekly error log review and simulation session, monthly progress ratings, mixed reviews and credit window checks, and a quarterly look at blueprint and testing window changes. The Purchase decision, Habit tracker, Skills matrix, Metrics log, Remediation plan, Operational checklist and Trip templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
