# CPA Exam FAR Section

This area is for anyone sitting the Financial Accounting and Reporting section of the CPA Exam, whether an accounting student finishing a degree, a staff accountant in public practice or a candidate applying from the Caribbean or elsewhere abroad. It starts with the foundations (the current Blueprint and its split from BAR, section order, the application and Notice to Schedule, a review course, a dated calendar and a booked seat), then the weekly routines, the US GAAP, governmental and not-for-profit topics, the changes that rescue a slipping plan, mocks, test day and score release, situations such as busy season, caring or a retake, and finally the work of a candidate taking FAR beyond the pass.

What repeats is a daily question set and journal entry deck, weekly study planning, simulation sessions and error log tallies, monthly checks of the calendar and score log, monthly spaced reviews of cash flows, revenue, leases, deferred tax, governmental and not-for-profit topics, and a quarterly Blueprint check. The Purchase decision, Habit tracker, Metrics log, Operational checklist, Trip and Remediation plan templates pair with the projects that name them.
