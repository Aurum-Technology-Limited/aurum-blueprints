# CPA Exam REG Section

This area is for accounting students and working accountants preparing for the CPA Exam's Taxation and Regulation section, from reading the current AICPA blueprint to the day the score arrives. It opens with the foundations (the blueprint, the board application and Notice to Schedule, section order, a review course, the tested tax year, a baseline, a calendar and the booking), then the weekly study machinery, a project for each part of the five blueprint areas from ethics and procedures through business law, property transactions and individuals to entities, then mocks, recovery and exam day, the situations that change the plan, and finally ways to use REG at work.

What repeats is a weekly rhythm of booked study blocks, error log retries, mixed question sets and a timed simulation drill, alongside daily flashcards, a monthly score review, coverage tracker update and computation rebuild, and a quarterly recheck of the AICPA's REG pages. The Purchase decision, Skills matrix, Metrics log, Remediation plan, Operational checklist and Trip templates pair with the projects that name them.
