# CPWA and Private Wealth Credentials

This area is for advisers, private bankers and independent planners working toward the CPWA from the Investments & Wealth Institute, or a similar private wealth credential, and for those who already hold one and want to use it well. It starts with the foundations (eligibility, choosing the right credential, the experience record, funding, enrolment and a calendar built back from the class dates), then the study routines, the subject skills from tax and estate concepts to family dynamics, the decisions about format, review providers and your service model, the executive education week and exam day, situations such as studying outside the United States or as a self-employed adviser, and finally the specialist work of advising wealthy families with other professionals.

What repeats is a twice-weekly pre-study session, a daily flashcard round, a weekly error log and client case note, a monthly progress review and tax rule check, and after certification the continuing education log and yearly renewal. The Metrics log, Reading queue, Purchase decision, Trip, Remediation plan and Savings goal templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
