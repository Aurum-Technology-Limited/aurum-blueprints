# Finance Technical Interview Prep

This area is for anyone who will be asked to walk through a DCF on the spot, whether a student heading into internship interviews or a professional switching into banking, private equity, equity research or corporate finance. It starts with the foundations (a scope map for your target roles, a question bank, a calendar counted back from your first rounds, a baseline mock and the accounting you need), then the daily and weekly practice machinery, the core technical topics from the linked statements through enterprise value, valuation, M&A and LBO basics, the improvements that make spoken answers sharp, the interview events themselves, plans for particular situations such as switching from audit or starting from an engineering degree, and finally the advanced material and coaching others.

What repeats is a daily flashcard review, recorded answer drills twice a week, a weekly peer mock, a weekly mistake log review, a Monday market numbers sheet and mental maths sessions, plus a monthly readiness score and a quarterly refresh of answers that depend on market conditions. The Skills matrix, Purchase decision, Habit tracker, Metrics log, Meeting notes and Remediation plan templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
