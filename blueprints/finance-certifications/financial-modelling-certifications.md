# Financial Modelling Certifications

This area is for students and working professionals who want a financial modelling course or certificate to mean something: the right programme chosen, the work finished and the skill visible to anyone who opens your files. It starts with the foundations (a clear goal, a provider comparison, a skills baseline, the assessment rules and a study schedule), then the routines that build speed and catch errors, the core builds from Excel functions to three statements, DCF and comparables, the decisions about tools and next levels, the assessment and capstone events, situations such as studying through term or moving from accounting into FP&A, and finally the specialist models an experienced modeller is asked for.

What repeats is a short daily keyboard drill, a weekly progress log and error review, a monthly rebuild from a blank sheet, a monthly annual report read and peer review swap, and a quarterly portfolio refresh and skills rescore. The Purchase decision, Skills matrix, Course, Metrics log, Portfolio piece and Resume templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
