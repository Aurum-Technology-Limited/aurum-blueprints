# Financial Modelling Tests

This area is for students and working professionals facing the modelling tests that sit between an interview and an offer in banking, private equity, credit and corporate development. It starts with the foundations (a map of which firms test and how, cold baseline attempts, a bare Excel set-up, a layout you can rebuild from memory and your own rules for take-home work), then the weekly and monthly drills, the core skills from the paper LBO to the three-hour case pack, the decisions about scope, output and the written recommendation, the real test days, situations such as testing around deal hours or across time zones, and finally the specialist cases set by property, credit and growth investors.

What repeats is a daily mental maths warm-up, a weekly paper LBO, keyboard drill, mistake review and deal-announcement prompt, a monthly three-hour mock, peer marking swap, blank-sheet rebuild and planted-error hunt, and a quarterly readiness review. The Skills matrix, Habit tracker, Metrics log, Purchase decision and Feedback themes templates pair with the projects that name them.
