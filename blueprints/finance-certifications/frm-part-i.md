# FRM Part I

This area is for students and working professionals preparing for GARP's FRM Part I, from the first check of the current rules to walking out of the test centre. It starts with the foundations (exam window, registration, calculator, materials, an hours budget and a calendar across the four books), then the weekly study machinery, a project for each block of Foundations of Risk Management, Quantitative Analysis, Financial Markets and Products, and Valuation and Risk Models, the mocks and decisions that rescue a plan, exam day and results, the situations that change the plan, and finally ways to make the knowledge last at work.

What repeats is a weekly rhythm of booked study blocks, mixed question sets, error log reviews, learning objective ratings and a partner check-in, alongside daily flashcards, a monthly progress review and formula sheet rewrite, and a quarterly recheck of GARP's rules. The Purchase decision, Skills matrix, Metrics log, Remediation plan, Operational checklist and Trip templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
