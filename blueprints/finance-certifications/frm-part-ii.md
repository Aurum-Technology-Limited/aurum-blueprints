# FRM Part II

This area is for candidates preparing for GARP's FRM Part II, whether you are a risk analyst fitting study around quarter-end or a master's student sitting it before your first job. It starts with the foundations (the exam window, your Part I standing, this year's readings, a six-topic diagnostic and a plan counted back from exam day), then the weekly study machinery, then the six Part II topics in the order GARP lists them, the practice exams and decisions of the final months, the situations that change the plan, and finally the work experience submission and life as a certified FRM.

What repeats is twice-weekly study blocks with a Sunday plan, a Saturday timed question set, daily flashcards, a monthly progress review and coverage update, and, once you have passed, a quarterly update of your experience record and monthly CPD logging. The Purchase decision, Metrics log, Reading queue, Remediation plan and Development plan templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
