# GMAT Focus Edition

This area is for anyone sitting the GMAT Focus Edition, whether a final-year student applying early or a professional fitting study around a demanding job. It starts with the foundations (the current format, a target score drawn from your schools, a baseline practice exam, the test date, budget, materials and an error log), then the weekly study machinery, the Quantitative Reasoning, Verbal Reasoning and Data Insights skills, the strategy decisions on section order, pacing and review, the practice exam series and exam day, situations such as a retake or a maths refresher, and finally top-percentile work and coaching someone else.

The Savings goal, Purchase decision, Metrics log, Habit tracker, Remediation plan and Trip templates pair with the projects that name them.
