# GRE General Test

This area is for anyone sitting the GRE General Test on the way to business school, a master's in finance or economics, or another graduate programme, whether a final-year student or a professional applying years after university. It starts with the foundations (the current format, which programmes accept the GRE, a target score, a diagnostic, materials, a calendar and a booked seat), then the weekly study machinery, the verbal, quantitative and writing skills, the pacing and planning decisions that rescue a stalled score, full practice tests, test day and sending scores, situations such as a full-time job, English as a second language or sitting from the Caribbean, and finally the work of a candidate chasing the top of the scale.

What repeats is a daily vocabulary review and quant set, weekly study planning, timed sections, essays, reading and error log tallies, monthly checks of the calendar and score log, and a quarterly look at the ETS rules. The Purchase decision, Habit tracker, Metrics log, Reading queue, Operational checklist and Trip templates pair with the projects that name them.
