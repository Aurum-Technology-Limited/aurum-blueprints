                ## Purpose
                Schools interview in very different ways: some invite only a share of applicants, some interview everyone who asks, some send an alumnus who has never seen your file and others send an admissions officer who has read every word. # MBA Admissions Interviews

This area is for anyone who has applied to business school and now has to talk their way through the interview, whether a final-year student applying to a deferred programme or a professional with a decade of work behind them. It starts with the foundations (each school's format, the invitation calendar, a story bank, your CV walk-through, why MBA and why this school, your goals and a working video set-up), then the weekly practice machinery, the answer skills from behavioural questions to team discussions and video essays, the decisions on coaching, format and weak spots, the interview events themselves, situations such as interviewing while working or as an international or returning applicant, and finally the specialist work of joint degrees and running mocks for others.

What repeats is a daily one-question speaking drill, a weekly partner mock, a weekly recorded solo session, a weekly tracker update and school news check, timed video prompts and group discussion practice, plus a monthly story bank refresh, a filler-word count and a format recheck on each school's website. The Purchase decision, Trip, Meeting notes and Habit tracker templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
