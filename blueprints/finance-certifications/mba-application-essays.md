            - name: Structuring the goals essay
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Goals essays that work usually move through what you have done, the moment that pointed you towards your goal, the goal itself and what the school adds, while ones that fail spend most of their words restating the CV. Practising a clear structure, then testing it with a word budget per section, keeps the essay about the future.

                ## Milestones
                1. An outline with four sections: past, turning point, goals and school, with a word budget for each.

# MBA Application Essays

This area is for anyone writing MBA applications, whether you are a final-year student aiming at a deferred programme or a professional with five years behind you and a round deadline in sight. It starts with the foundations (a round calendar, the current prompts, a story inventory, your goals, the admissions CV and the choice of recommenders), then the weekly machinery of writing, review and tracking, the writing skills each essay type needs, the decisions about optional essays, consultants and reuse, the dated submission sprints and what follows them, situations such as switching careers or applying as a reapplicant, and finally specialist essays for dual degrees, scholarships and helping the next applicant.

What repeats is a protected writing block twice a week, a weekly tracker and story-bank update, a monthly draft sent to a reader, a monthly recommender update, a monthly sweep of school sites for changed prompts and a quarterly refresh of your CV and career numbers. The Resume, Person, Feedback themes and Reading queue templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
