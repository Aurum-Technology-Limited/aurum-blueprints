# MBA School Shortlist

This area is for anyone deciding where to apply for an MBA, whether you are a few years into work and eyeing a full-time programme or a manager weighing a part-time or executive format around your job. It starts with the foundations (why you want the degree, the format, the region, your weighted criteria, a budget and a longlist), then the routines that keep research and deadlines current, the skills for reading employment reports, rankings and curricula, the decisions on cost, return, fit and the final cut, the visits and events worth planning, situations such as applying from the Caribbean, with a partner or as an early-career candidate, and finally the specialist questions of dual degrees, reapplying and choosing between admits.

What repeats is a weekly research hour, a weekly request for a call with a student or alumnus, a monthly shortlist review and scoring refresh, monthly checks of events, deadlines, scholarships and spending, quarterly rechecks of visa rules, test policies and exchange rates, and a yearly pass when new employment reports land. The Metrics log, Trip, Purchase decision, Meeting notes and Savings goal templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
