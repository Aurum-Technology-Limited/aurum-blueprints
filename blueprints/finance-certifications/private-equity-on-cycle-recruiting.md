# Private Equity On-Cycle Recruiting

This area is for analysts and associates in investment banking, consulting, transaction services or lending who want a private equity associate seat, whether through the compressed on-cycle process or the slower off-cycle market. It runs from the foundations (a readiness check, a fund map and target list, the headhunter map, a deal sheet and PE-style CV, confidentiality rules and a recruiting calendar), through the weekly machinery of headhunter contact, a pipeline tracker and deal rehearsal, the skills of talking about deals as an investor and answering fit questions, the decisions about routes, offers and resigning, the interview weeks themselves, variants for consultants, Europe, post-MBA, Caribbean and visa candidates, and finally the moves an associate makes once inside.

What repeats is a weekly headhunter touchpoint and pipeline update, a weekly market reading and deal rehearsal, a Friday off-cycle scan, a monthly deal sheet refresh, debrief review and mentor check-in, and a quarterly refresh of the fund list. The Resume, Person, Meeting notes and Onboarding 30/60/90 templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
