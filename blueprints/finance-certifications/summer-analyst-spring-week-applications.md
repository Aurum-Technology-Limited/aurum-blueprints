# Summer Analyst and Spring Week Applications

Finance internships for students run on their own calendar: insight days and spring weeks for first-years, summer analyst programmes for the penultimate year, and off-cycle or placement roles around them. This area starts with the foundations (which programmes exist for your year, which divisions and firms to aim at, a tracker and a one-page CV), then the weekly machinery of an application season, the written answers and online tests that screen most candidates out, the rounds themselves, the internship and the return offer, and finally variants for non-target, international, non-economics and working students.

The rhythm is a weekly application sprint while programmes are open, a short daily markets read, a midweek test practice block and a monthly sweep of careers pages and results, with a termly reset of the whole plan. The Resume, Cover letter, Job application and Onboarding plan templates pair with the CV, letters, each application round and the first weeks of the internship. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
