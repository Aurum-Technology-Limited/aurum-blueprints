            - name: Choosing which sustainable investing certificate to take
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Several credentials compete for the same CV line: CFA Institute's sustainable investing certificate (formerly the Certificate in ESG Investing), its Climate Risk, Valuation and Investing certificate, GARP's Sustainability and Climate Risk certificate, and courses from bodies such as the PRI Academy or national institutes. They differ in depth, cost, recognition and how much they assume you already know, so a written comparison against your actual role stops you paying for the wrong one.

                ## Milestones
                1. Three or four candidate credentials listed with their awarding body, format and stated prerequisites.
                2. Each one scored against the same five criteria: relevance to your role, employer recognition, cost, study time and exam format.
                3. Two colleagues or recruiters in your target field asked which credential they actually notice.
                4. One credential chosen, with the reason written in two sentences.

                ## Notes
                A broad ESG certificate suits most analysts and advisers; a climate-specific one suits risk, insurance and transition finance roles. Do not take both at once.
              priority: high

# Sustainable Investing Certificates

This area is for analysts, portfolio managers, advisers, risk and compliance staff and consultants studying for an ESG or sustainable investing certificate, such as CFA Institute's sustainable investing certificate, its climate certificate or GARP's Sustainability and Climate Risk certificate. It starts with the foundations (choosing the credential, checking the current rules, booking, an hours budget and a baseline), then the weekly and daily study machinery, a project for each block of the syllabus from ESG factors and stewardship to climate risk, disclosure rules and portfolio construction, then mocks, the final fortnight, exam day and results, the situations that change the plan, and finally what an experienced practitioner does with the credential.

What repeats is a daily question set and flashcard round, two weekly study blocks with a Sunday plan, a weekly error log review and news case file, a monthly progress rating, mixed review, deadline check and proxy vote reading, and a quarterly recheck of the awarding body's rules, with monthly and yearly learning logs once you have passed. The Purchase decision, Metrics log, Reading queue, Remediation plan, Operational checklist and Development plan templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
