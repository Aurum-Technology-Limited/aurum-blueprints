# Treasury Certification (CTP)

This area is for anyone preparing for the Certified Treasury Professional exam from AFP, whether you already sit in a corporate treasury team, work in accounting or FP&A and want to move across, or sell cash management services on the bank side. It starts with the foundations (eligibility, registration, the current content outline, materials, a diagnostic and a plan counted back from test day), then the weekly study machinery, then the treasury domains from the banking system and payment rails through cash positioning, forecasting, working capital, liquidity, capital markets, risk and technology, followed by technique decisions, the practice exams and test day, the situations that change the plan, and the work of keeping and using the credential.

What repeats is a Sunday planning slot with Tuesday and Thursday study evenings, a Saturday timed question set, daily flashcards on payment rails and terms, a Friday error log retest, a Monday note linking study to your day job, monthly reviews of progress, AFP candidate notices and your employer's bank fee statement, and a quarterly log of continuing education once you pass. The Purchase decision, Metrics log, Habit tracker, Reading queue and Remediation plan templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
