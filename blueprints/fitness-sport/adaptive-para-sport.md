# Adaptive & Para Sport

This area is for disabled athletes and anyone with an impairment who wants to train and compete, whether that means a first wheelchair basketball session after rehab or a push for a national para squad. It starts with foundations (a sport shortlist, accessible clubs, medical questions, an access profile, memberships and an equipment budget), moves through the routines that keep body and kit working, the skills of classification, push technique, transfers and guiding, the big equipment and funding decisions, and dated events from first competition to talent day. Then come situations such as acquired disability, progressive conditions, Deafness, learning disability and young athletes, and finally pathway planning, equipment tuning and sponsor work.

What repeats is a weekly training plan built around care routines, daily skin checks and symptom scores, weekly chair checks, shoulder sessions and helper rota, a monthly coach review, socket log and bearing clean, quarterly prosthetist, seating and funding checks, and a yearly recheck of medicines, memberships and your support profile. The Habit tracker, Metrics log, Meeting notes, Training program, Purchase decision and Trip templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
