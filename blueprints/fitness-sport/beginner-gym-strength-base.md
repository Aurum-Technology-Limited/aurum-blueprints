            - name: Finding starting weights in the first fortnight
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Nobody's first squat should be heavy. Starting every lift with the empty bar or a light dumbbell and adding a little each set until the bar slows, while two or three clean reps are still in reserve, finds a working weight that leaves weeks of room to add load.

                4. No session in the first fortnight ending with sore joints or failed reps.

                ## Notes

# Beginner Gym Strength Base

This area is for anyone walking into a gym to lift weights for the first time, whatever their age, size or starting fitness. It starts with the foundations (a readiness check, a gym you will actually use, an induction, a proven full-body programme and your first starting weights), then the weekly routines that keep sessions and progress moving, the technique of the main lifts, the decisions that shape your training, the milestones worth marking, plans for particular lives and bodies, and finally the step beyond the beginner phase.

What repeats is three sessions a week, a Sunday log review, a monthly progress check and a monthly technique video, a weekly meal plan, a quarterly look at your goal and programme, and a yearly anniversary review. The Purchase decision, Training program, Metrics log, Habit tracker and Weekly meal plan templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
