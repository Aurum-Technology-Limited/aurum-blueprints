            - name: Medical check before contact training
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Combat sport involves blows to the head and body, and some conditions, such as uncontrolled high blood pressure, certain eye problems or a past head injury, make that riskier. A conversation with your doctor before you start sparring, ahead of the paperwork amateur bodies ask for before a first bout, means any issue is found in a surgery rather than a ring.

                ## Milestones
                1. A list of past head injuries, knockouts, eye problems and current medicines written down.
                2. An appointment held with your doctor, with contact sport named as the reason.
                3. Any tests or restrictions the doctor suggests recorded.
                4. Your coach told about anything that affects training, in the detail you choose.

                ## Notes
                Mention any previous concussion even if it happened years ago in another sport. Your doctor decides what it means; your job is to report it accurately.
              priority: high

# Boxing & Combat Conditioning

This area is for anyone training boxing, kickboxing or Muay Thai, from people who have just left a fitness class for a proper club to amateurs preparing for registered bouts. It starts with the foundations (a gym, starter kit, a medical check, hand wrapping, stance and a twelve-month goal), then the weekly machinery of roadwork, skipping, shadow boxing, bag and strength work and an honest round log, the technical skills from the jab to the clinch, the decisions about sparring, head contact and kit, the events from a first interclub day to fight night, the situations that shape a fighter's week, and finally the work of experienced fighters and cornermen.

What repeats is daily skipping, roadwork on Tuesdays and Saturdays, shadow boxing three times a week, strength on Mondays and Thursdays, a Thursday bag session, a Friday weigh-in, a Saturday count of hard sparring rounds, monthly round totals before a coach check-in late in the month, a monthly sparring video, a quarterly goal review and a yearly registration renewal. The Purchase decision, Habit tracker, Metrics log, Training program, Operational checklist and Trip templates pair with the projects that name them.
