# Calisthenics & Bodyweight Strength

This area is for anyone who wants to get strong with their own bodyweight, from a first strict pull-up and wall handstand to muscle-ups, levers and planche work, using a bar, a pair of rings and little else. It runs from the foundations (a joint screen, a baseline test, the right kit, a proven routine and two target skills), through the weekly machinery of progression rules, retests and tendon checks, the skill ladders for pulling, pushing, legs and core, the fixes and decisions that break plateaus, dated events such as fitness tests and competitions, versions for heavier beginners, the over-fifties, travellers, parents and other athletes, and finally the specialist skills that take years.

What repeats is three fixed sessions a week, short daily pull-up and handstand practice, a weekly progression and joint check, two leg and two compression sessions, a monthly retest on the 12th, a monthly form video, a fresh four-week block each month and a quarterly skill review.
