                ## Purpose
                Clubs often run three or four Saturday sides plus Sunday, midweek T20 and social teams, and the XI you join decides how much you bat, bowl and enjoy the summer.             - name: Protective kit that fits and meets the safety standard
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Helmet, pads, gloves, thigh guard and box are the difference between a bruise and a broken finger or worse, and second-hand kit is often the wrong size or past its safe life. Checking each item for fit, and the helmet against the current national standard (BS 7928 in the UK, for example), before the first hard-ball net puts safety ahead of the season rather than behind an injury.

                ## Milestones
                1. Every protective item tried on and checked for fit, including the helmet grille gap and pad strap position.
                2. The helmet confirmed against the current safety standard, with its manufacture date noted.
                3. Any cracked, compressed or ill-fitting item replaced.
                4. A neck guard and inner thigh pad considered for facing quicker bowling.

                ## Notes
                Never adjust a helmet grille yourself; a gap that lets the ball through defeats the point. Replace a helmet after a significant ball strike if the manufacturer advises it.

# Club Cricket Season

This area is for club cricketers and the captains who get a side onto the field every weekend, from lower XI regulars to first-team players. It starts with the foundations (choosing a club, a bat, protective kit, shoes, registration, a fitness baseline, season availability and an emergency plan), moves through the weekly machinery of selection, nets, personal records, bowling workloads, rotas and fees, then the batting, bowling, fielding and Laws skills that win club games, decisions about formats and tactics, the season's events, versions for returners, parents, veterans, women's sides and new captains, and finally umpiring, multi-season analysis and fast bowling strength.
