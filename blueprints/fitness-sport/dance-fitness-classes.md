# Dance Fitness Classes

This area is for anyone using Zumba, barre, street dance or other dance-based classes to stay fit, whether you have two left feet or years of front-row experience. It starts with foundations (choosing a style, a health check, finding classes nearby, tasters, kit, payment and a twelve-week goal), then the weekly machinery of booking, tracking, home practice and term resets, the skills of counting music, base steps, barre alignment, grooves, low-impact options and turns, decisions about home practice and new styles, events such as dance-a-thons and showcases, versions for families, older adults, shift workers, students and people with health conditions, and finally intermediate classes, performance teams, choreography and instructor training.

What repeats is a Sunday booking slot, a Wednesday home practice of the week's trickiest combination, a Saturday attendance tally, a Monday message to your class buddy, a monthly review on the 26th, quarterly kit and timetable checks, and yearly reviews of your pass, your winter plan and any health plan. The Purchase decision, Metrics log, Habit tracker, Trip and Course templates pair with the projects that name them.
