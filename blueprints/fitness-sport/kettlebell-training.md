                Buying one bell that is too light for swings and too heavy for presses is the most common first mistake, because the hips can move far more than the shoulders.             - name: Safe swing space and floor at home or in the gym
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Any kettlebell that slips mid-swing travels a long way, so the space matters as much as the bell. Measuring clearance in front, behind and above you, choosing a floor that will not crack or slide, and agreeing a rule for children and pets keeps a heavy accident from ever being possible.

                ## Milestones
                1. A training spot with at least two metres clear in front and enough height for an overhead press.
                2. A floor surface chosen that grips the feet and survives a dropped bell, such as rubber matting.
                3. Flat, thin-soled shoes or bare feet decided on, with cushioned running shoes ruled out.
                4. A household rule written that nobody walks in front of a swinging bell.

                ## Notes
                Never swing towards a window, mirror, television or doorway. If the bell is going to leave your hands, let it go forward and down, never try to save it.
              priority: high

# Kettlebell Training

This area is for anyone training with kettlebells, from someone with one bell in the spare room to an athlete adding swings and get-ups to a sport, or a lifter heading for a kettlebell sport platform. It starts with the foundations (a readiness check, the right first bells, a safe space, the hinge and a first lesson), then the weekly machinery of sessions, logs and progression rules, the core skills one lift at a time, the decisions about equipment and style, the milestones worth marking, versions for busy parents, older starters, runners and team players, and finally doubles, long cycle, heavy get-ups and instructing.

What repeats is a three-session week, a short daily get-up practice, a Sunday log review, a monthly technique video and progression check, weekly hand care and carries, a quarterly bell inspection and block review, and a yearly look back at the whole year. The Purchase decision, Training program, Metrics log and Habit tracker templates pair with the projects that name them.
