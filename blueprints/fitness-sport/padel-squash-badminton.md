# Padel, Squash & Badminton

This area is for anyone who plays padel, squash or badminton, from the first pay-and-play booking to the competitive player chasing ranking points. It starts with the foundations (choosing a sport and venue, a booking account, the first racket, court shoes, eyewear, the rules and a coaching block), moves through the weekly machinery of bookings, partners, box leagues, match logs and restringing, then the core strokes and footwork of each sport, decisions about coaching and kit, events from americanos to inter-club leagues, versions for returners, tennis converts, office workers, couples and families, and ends with ranking seasons, opponent scouting and string experiments.

What repeats is a Sunday court booking, a Monday match log, a Wednesday solo session, Saturday ghosting and a twice-weekly arm routine, monthly partner, kit, grip, ball stock, box league and match reviews spread across the month, quarterly rating and string checks, and a yearly membership value check. The Purchase decision, Metrics log, Training program and Trip templates pair with the projects that name them.
