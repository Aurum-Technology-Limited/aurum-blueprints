# Pilates & Core Strength

This area is for anyone building core strength, posture and control through Pilates, whether on a mat at home or on a studio reformer, for general fitness, a grumbling back or another sport. It starts with the foundations (mat or reformer, health questions, a qualified instructor, a beginner course, a measured baseline, kit, a weekly schedule, a goal, a log and reformer safety), then the weekly machinery of home sessions, a morning primer, desk breaks, retests and progression blocks, the fundamentals of breathing, spinal placement, deep abdominal connection, rolling, side-lying, shoulder and rotation work, decisions about pricing, online classes and home equipment, milestones such as moving up a level, versions for backs, runners, golfers, older adults, parents and travellers, and finally the classical order, apparatus and instructor training.

What repeats is a Sunday booking for the week's classes, a Saturday home mat session, a ten-minute morning primer, desk breaks on Tuesdays and Thursdays, a monthly retest and credit check, quarterly posture photos and instructor check-ins, and an annual review. The Purchase decision, Course, Metrics log, Habit tracker, Training program and Trip templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
