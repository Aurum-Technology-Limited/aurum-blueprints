            - name: On-court conditioning baseline
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Recreational players usually fade in the last ten minutes, which is exactly when close games are decided. A simple timed baseline, such as a 17s shuttle across the court width and a set of full-court sprints with fixed rest, shows where your fitness really is and gives your conditioning work a target.

                ## Milestones
                1. A test protocol written: warm-up, 17s shuttle, full-court sprint set and rest times.
                2. The test run once with every time recorded.
                3. Heart rate at the end and how your legs felt the next morning noted.
                4. A retest date set six to eight weeks later.

                ## Notes
                A 17s is seventeen widths of the court, sideline to sideline, as fast as you can. If you have a heart condition, are returning from illness or have not trained hard in years, check with your doctor before any maximal test.

# Recreational Basketball

This area is for adults keeping basketball in their week, whether that means a Tuesday pick-up run, a Sunday league or a summer of outdoor 3x3. It opens with the foundations (finding a run, shoes, a warm-up and honest shooting and conditioning baselines), then the weekly machinery of shooting, free throws, conditioning, strength and logs, the skills that win recreational games, decisions about role, range and format, the games and tournaments worth preparing for, versions for returners, beginners, players over 40, busy parents and captains, and finally running your own group, film review, shot charts and refereeing.

What repeats is a Saturday solo shooting session, free throws twice a week, two conditioning and two strength sessions, short handling work at home, Monday availability replies, a month-end shooting total, and quarterly retests of your shooting and conditioning baselines. The Purchase decision, Operational checklist, Habit tracker, Training program and Metrics log templates pair with the projects that name them.
