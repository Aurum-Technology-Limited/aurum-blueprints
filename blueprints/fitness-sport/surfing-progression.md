                Most people who teach themselves on a hired board spend their first summer in the whitewater learning habits a coach would have fixed in a morning.               description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Rip currents are behind most rescues on surf beaches, and beginners are the people who drift into them without noticing. Learning to spot a rip from the beach, knowing what your local beach flags mean and how to escape a current turns the first months in the water from luck into judgement.

                ## Milestones

                2. Three rips spotted from the beach on different days and described in the session log.

                4. The coastal emergency number and the nearest lifeguarded beach saved in your phone.

                ## Notes
                Surf only at lifeguarded beaches, between the surf flags where they apply, until you can read a beach on your own. Ask lifeguards where the rips are running that day; they usually welcome the question.

# Surfing Progression

This area is for surfers at every stage, from a first lesson in the whitewater to someone chasing barrels and contest heats. It starts with the foundations (a good surf school, rip and flag knowledge, a swim check, the right foam board and wetsuit, a home break profile, etiquette, a session log and a goal for the year), then the routines that keep progress moving, the skills from catching green waves to cutbacks and wipeout composure, the board and coaching decisions, events from a first surf trip to a first contest, versions for inland, returning, working and parent surfers, and finally the specialist work of overhead waves, barrels, heat strategy and custom boards.

What repeats is a Thursday forecast check, twice-weekly paddle sessions, pop-up drills, shoulder care and a Saturday surfskate, a Sunday log catch-up and dawn patrol booking, a monthly board check on the 9th, filming on the 14th and a progress review on the 27th, plus quarterly swim and wetsuit checks and a yearly refresh of winter kit, safety cards and first aid. The Purchase decision, Metrics log, Training program, Operational checklist, Savings goal and Trip templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
