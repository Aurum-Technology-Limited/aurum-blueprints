# Training Recovery & Deloads

This area is for athletes in any sport who train hard enough that recovery, not effort, has become the limit on progress. It starts with the foundations (an honest audit of your load, your personal warning signs, readiness markers, rest day and deload rules, and a sleep baseline), then the weekly and monthly routines that keep fatigue in check, the knowledge that makes recovery decisions sensible, the experiments and choices worth making, the events that need their own recovery plan, versions for shift workers, new parents, team players and coaches, and finally the specialist work of reading overreaching and building a recovery playbook from your own data.

What repeats is a short daily readiness check and wind-down, a Sunday recovery review, a Wednesday active recovery session, a Friday life stress rating, a weekly recovery meal plan, a monthly fatigue check and deload reminder, a quarterly look at whether your deloads are working, and a yearly off-season break and season map. The Sleep review, Training program, Metrics log, Habit tracker, Weekly meal plan, Purchase decision and Trip templates pair with the projects that name them.
