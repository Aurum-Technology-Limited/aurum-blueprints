# Yoga Practice Progression

This area is for anyone who wants yoga to be a steady part of the week rather than an occasional class, whether you practise on a living-room mat, in a studio or both. It starts with the foundations (a health screen, kit, a first style and studio, a beginner course and baseline photos), moves through the weekly machinery of fixed sessions, a log and monthly progress checks, then the skills of sun salutations, alignment, balance, breath and arm balances, the decisions about styles, memberships and hot rooms, dated events such as workshops and retreats, versions for desk workers, older practitioners, athletes, families and travellers, and finally inversions, backbends, Mysore self-practice and teacher training.

What repeats is three practice slots planned every Sunday, a ten-minute daily minimum, a weekly yin session, a monthly pose check on the 18th, a fresh home sequence on the 3rd, mat care on the 9th and a quarterly review of where the practice is heading. The Purchase decision, Course, Habit tracker, Metrics log, Reading queue and Trip templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
