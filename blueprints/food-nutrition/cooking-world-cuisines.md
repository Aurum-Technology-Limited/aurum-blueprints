# Cooking Through World Cuisines

This area is for anyone who wants to cook a cuisine they love properly at home, one cuisine at a time, rather than collecting recipes that never quite taste right. It starts with the foundations (choosing the cuisine, trusted sources, a core pantry, the first grocer trip and three signature dishes), then the routines that keep you cooking it, the techniques that define particular cuisines, the decisions about substitutes, tools, heat and adaptation, the events that give the practice a goal, versions for partners, children, small kitchens and tight budgets, and finally the regional, scratch-made and teaching work of an experienced cook.

What repeats is a weekly cuisine night and technique slot, a monthly new dish, grocer restock, cookbook club and log review, a quarterly review that decides whether to stay or move to the next cuisine, and the festival dates each year. The Reading queue, Purchase decision, Course and Trip templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
