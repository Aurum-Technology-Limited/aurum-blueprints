            - name: Regular eating rhythm reset
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Hunger signals that have been ignored for years often go quiet, then arrive all at once as overwhelming hunger in the evening. Eating something every three to four hours for a few weeks, whether or not you feel hungry yet, is the usual first step dietitians use to bring cues back and take the edge off evening overeating.

                ## Milestones
                1. A loose daily rhythm of three meals and two or three snacks written down.
                2. Gaps of no more than about four hours kept on most days for three weeks.
                3. A first meal eaten within a couple of hours of waking on most days.
                4. A note made at the end of each week on whether evening hunger has eased.

                ## Notes
                This is mechanical eating on purpose. It can feel like the opposite of intuitive eating, but it is scaffolding until your signals return.

# Intuitive Eating Practice

This area is for anyone who has spent years on and off diets and wants to eat by hunger, fullness and satisfaction instead of rules. It starts with foundations (a diet history, a rules inventory, a safety screen, a regular eating rhythm and your own hunger scale), then the daily and weekly routines, the skills behind the ten principles, decisions about scales, language and fear foods, occasions to prepare for, versions for parents, carers, athletes, shift workers and people with health conditions, and finally relapse planning, body image work and peer support.

What repeats is a daily hunger check-in and halfway pause, a Saturday meal sketch and Sunday reflection, a weekly fear food step, monthly rules reviews and snack restocks, and quarterly check-ins with your practitioner and your relapse plan. The Habit tracker, Weekly meal plan, Meeting notes, Reading queue and Trip templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
