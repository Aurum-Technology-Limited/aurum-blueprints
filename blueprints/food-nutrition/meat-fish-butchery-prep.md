# Meat & Fish Butchery Prep

This area is for anyone who wants to buy meat and fish whole or on the bone, prepare it at home, and get far more out of a good butcher and fishmonger. It starts with the foundations (a butchery kit, a raw prep station, finding your shops, learning the primal cuts and judging fresh fish), then the weekly and monthly routines, the core skills from jointing a chicken to filleting flat fish and tying a roast, the decisions about cuts, welfare and sustainability, the events worth planning, the situations that change the approach, and finally specialist work such as game, shellfish, sausages and seam butchery.

What repeats is a weekly whole chicken and a weekly fishmonger visit on delivery day, a monthly butcher's order, stock day, knife sharpening and log review, a quarterly bulk portioning session and seasonal fish check, and yearly checks on kit, seafood ratings and game seasons.
