              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Most parents guess they eat together more often than they do, because one good Sunday hides four weeknights of staggered plates. Writing down, for fourteen days, which meals had two or more of you sitting at the same table gives an honest starting number and shows exactly which nights fall apart and why.

                ## Milestones
                1. A simple grid of fourteen days by breakfast, lunch and dinner, kept on the fridge or in a phone note.
                2. Every meal marked with who was at the table, including meals eaten in the car or in front of a screen.
                3. The total number of genuinely shared meals for the fortnight worked out.
                4. The two or three recurring reasons meals were missed written down, such as late clubs or a long commute.

                ## Notes
                Count a meal as shared only when people sit down together without screens for at least ten minutes. Breakfasts and weekend lunches count just as much as dinner.
