# Sugar & Ultra-Processed Food Reduction

This area is for any household that wants to eat less added sugar and fewer ultra-processed foods without turning every meal into a rule, and it is written with parents in mind as much as adults changing their own habits. It starts with the foundations (a seven-day diary, a household agreement, a kitchen definition of ultra-processed, cupboard and drinks sweeps and a slow sweetness step-down), then the weekly machinery of snack, breakfast and sauce batches, the label-reading and cooking skills, the swaps for bread, ready meals, takeaways and processed meat, dated events like a no-added-sugar month and a lower-sugar birthday party, versions for toddlers, teenagers, grandparents, shift workers and reluctant partners, and finally the specialist work of reformulating bakes, tracking spend and reading the research critically.

What repeats is a Sunday snack batch, a Saturday breakfast batch, a Friday basket check, planned treat days, a daily water routine, a monthly sauce batch, label spot-check and progress review, a quarterly reset and yearly festival and dental plans. The Metrics log, Habit tracker and Purchase decision templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
