# Work Lunch Meal Prep

This area is for anyone whose midday meal is currently a meal deal, a third coffee or whatever the canteen has left at two o'clock, and especially for desk workers whose days are eaten by meetings. It starts with the foundations (what you spend now, where and when you actually eat, containers, a cold bag, six lunches you like and a first prep session), then the weekly machinery of planning, prepping, topping up and packing, the cooking skills that keep salads crisp and chicken moist by noon, the decisions about how many days to bring and what to buy on the others, dated plans for deadline weeks, conferences and a new job, versions for hybrid, commuting, shift, field and parent workers, and finally the lunch clubs, recipe books and seasonal menus of someone who has made this routine for years.

What repeats is a Thursday plan and shopping list, a Sunday prep hour, a Wednesday top-up cook, an evening bag pack, a Friday container wash, a monthly spend check and desk stash restock, and a quarterly refresh of the lunches you rotate. The Purchase decision, Weekly meal plan, Metrics log and Habit tracker templates pair with the projects that name them.
