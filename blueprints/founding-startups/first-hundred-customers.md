              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                A hundred newsletter sign-ups and a hundred teams paying every month are different companies, and founders who never define the word quietly count whichever number looks better that week. Deciding in writing what makes someone a customer, such as paid at least once, or used the core feature three times in a fortnight, gives every later target and review a fixed meaning.

                ## Milestones

                4. The definitions agreed with any co-founder and pinned at the top of the tracker.

                ## Notes

# First Hundred Customers

This area is for founders who have something people can use or buy and now need the first hundred of them, one by one, mostly through their own effort. It starts with foundations (what counts as a customer, a tracker, a narrow first segment, a named prospect list, the communities where buyers gather and a dated target), then the weekly machinery of outreach, follow-ups, welcome calls and channel scoring, the skills of cold messages, community posting, concierge service and referrals, decisions about which channels and customers to keep, dated checkpoints at the first sale and customers ten, fifty and one hundred, variants for business, consumer, developer, island-market, part-time, local and regulated founders, and finally the specialist work of hand attribution, a customer council and a playbook a first hire can run.

What repeats is an outreach block three days a week, a weekly channel scorecard and conversation digest, a Friday follow-up sweep, community time twice a week, monthly cohort and silent-customer reviews and a quarterly look at referral partners. The **Metrics log**, **Meeting notes**, **Feedback themes** and **Purchase decision** templates pair with the customer tracker, welcome calls, the weekly digest and the outreach tool choice. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
