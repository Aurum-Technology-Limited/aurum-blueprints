              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Most founder partners agree to the company in general and then discover the specifics: the evenings, the salary cut, the weekend investor calls. A single planned conversation, held before the next big commitment such as a raise or a hire, puts the real asks on the table so your partner is choosing them with you rather than absorbing them.

                ## Milestones
                1. A written list of what the next twelve months will ask of the household in hours, money, travel and risk.
                2. A two-hour conversation held with your partner away from the laptop and the children.
                3. Your partner's own conditions and worries written down in their words.
                4. A short shared note of what you both agreed, with a date to revisit it.

                ## Notes
                Go in with the bad case, not the pitch. Partners forgive a hard year they saw coming far more readily than one they were sold as easy.

# Founder and Family Life

This area is for founders with a partner, children or both, who want the company and the family to grow up together rather than compete. It starts with foundations (the first honest conversation at home, red lines, a savings floor, a shared calendar, childcare cover, a will and a file your partner can use), then the weekly and monthly rituals that keep everyone connected, the skills of talking about the company at home, household decisions, dated events like parental leave and a launch crunch, plans for each life stage from a newborn to an ageing parent, and finally the harder specialist work around exits, counselling and the story you leave your children.

What repeats is a Sunday planning session, a partner evening and one-to-one time with each child every week, a daily device-free dinner, a monthly check-in and money talk with your partner, a termly calendar sync and a quarterly family review, plus yearly checks of the will and the important dates. The **Purchase decision**, **Weekly meal plan**, **Trip** and **Reading queue** templates pair with the household help choice, the family meal plan, the annual holiday and the reading list. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
