              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Exhaustion and cynicism feel like normal startup life, so founders are usually the last to notice they are burning out. Taking a recognised burnout questionnaire once now, while things are reasonably calm, gives you a number to compare against when the next crunch arrives instead of relying on how you feel that week.

                ## Milestones
                1. A recognised burnout or wellbeing questionnaire chosen from a reputable health or occupational source.
                2. Your first score recorded with the date and a line on what the company was going through.
                3. The three questions you scored worst on noted as the areas to watch.
                4. A date set to retake it in one month.

                ## Notes
                A self-check is a prompt, not a diagnosis. If the score or how you feel worries you, take it to your doctor rather than waiting for the next retake.

# Founder Burnout Prevention

This area is for founders who want to still be well, and still like the work, three years into the company. It starts with foundations (a burnout self-check, your own warning signs, a doctor's check, a sleep and hours baseline, written limits, a support map and a stoplight plan), then the routines that hold them in place, the skills of saying no and switching off, the decisions that remove load at the source, dated pressure points like a fundraise or a launch, variants for solo, remote, neurodivergent and recovering founders, and finally specialist work on team pace and long-term sustainability.

What repeats is a daily shutdown, a weekly energy check-in and a protected day off, a monthly burnout score and talk with your closest person, quarterly recovery breaks and a yearly health and pay review. The **Sleep review**, **Metrics log**, **Habit tracker**, **Weekly meal plan** and **Trip** templates pair with the sleep baseline, the monthly score, the movement floor, the food routine and the real holiday.
