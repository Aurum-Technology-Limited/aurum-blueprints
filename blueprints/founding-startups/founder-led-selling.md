# Founder-Led Selling

Founder-Led Selling is for the stretch when the founder is the whole sales team: finding the right buyers, running the calls, closing the first contracts and learning what actually makes people sign. It starts with foundations (a buyer profile, a value narrative, a pipeline with honest stage rules and baseline numbers), moves through the weekly machinery of reviews, prospecting, debriefs and forecasts, then sales skills, decisions about tools, paperwork and discounts, dated moments such as the first paid contract and a big final presentation, variants for technical, introverted, solo and cross-border founders, and finally the playbook and handover to a first salesperson.

The rhythm is a daily prospecting block, a Monday pipeline review, midweek follow-up checks and Friday debrief reviews, with monthly win-loss, forecast, objection and product feedback sessions and a quarterly retrospective on the whole motion. The Metrics log, Meeting notes, Feedback themes, Purchase decision and Onboarding 30/60/90 templates pair with it.
