# Founder Visa and Relocation

This area is for founders moving to another country to start or grow a company there, whether alone, with co-founders or with a family in tow. It runs from the foundations (why move, which route, eligibility, a lawyer, documents, money and a timeline), through learning the rules and the new market, building the application with an endorser, a visa-grade plan and an evidence pack, then the move itself from scouting trip to arrival registrations and bank accounts, the routines that keep the visa safe, variants for families, co-founder teams, graduates, smaller-market founders and people already on a work visa, and finally renewals, settlement, citizenship and what to do when the visa ends.

What repeats is a monthly compliance and evidence check, a travel log and home-ties review, the founder payroll check, quarterly endorser, risk and change-of-circumstances reviews, a weekly language and networking habit, and the yearly tax and insurance cycle. The **Purchase decision**, **Monthly budget and bills**, **Trip**, **Vendor**, **Compliance control** and **Risk register** templates pair with choosing a lawyer, the relocation budget, the scouting trip, the mover, the conditions calendar and the risk review.
