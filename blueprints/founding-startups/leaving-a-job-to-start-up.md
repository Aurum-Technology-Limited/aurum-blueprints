# Leaving a Job to Start Up

This area is for anyone with a salary who is planning to give it up to found a company, whether you are a knowledge worker with an idea taking shape or a founder already building in the evenings. It starts with the foundations (your real cost of living, the savings number, the contract, IP and vesting checks, and written criteria for the leap), then the routines that carry you through, the skills and decisions that shape when and how you go, the dated moments from resignation day to the twelve-month review, variants for different lives and contracts, and a few specialist moves such as turning your employer into a customer.

What repeats is a monthly savings tally before the leap and a personal runway review after it, protected build hours each week, a bridge income pipeline, tax set-asides, a weekly founder log and a yearly check of cover and pension. The **Monthly budget and bills**, **Savings goal**, **Metrics log**, **Purchase decision** and **Operational checklist** templates pair with the cost baseline, the leap fund, the runway review, health cover and your last weeks of paperwork. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
