# Pivot or Persevere Decisions

This area is for founders who suspect the current plan is not working, or who fear they will keep going too long out of hope and sunk cost. It starts with foundations (the core bet written down, an evidence baseline, persevere criteria and a decision date set before anyone is attached to the answer), then the review machinery that keeps the question honest, the skills of reading weak signals and resisting your own biases, the options, tests and memo that produce the call, the dated events where it is made and announced, variants for solo, bootstrapped, investor-backed and accelerator founders, and finally the advanced work of founders who have pivoted before.

What repeats is a Friday evidence review, a weekly customer pull log, a monthly pivot or persevere checkpoint, a monthly decision journal entry and team confidence pulse, and a quarterly direction retreat. The Metrics log, Risk register, Feedback themes, Meeting notes and Board meeting templates pair with the projects that name them.
