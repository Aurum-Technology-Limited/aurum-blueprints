              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Naming goes in circles when nobody has written down what the name has to do. A one-page brief covering the customer, the category, the feeling the brand should give, the markets you will sell in and the hard constraints turns a matter of taste into a set of tests every candidate can be held against.

                ## Milestones
                1. A one-page brief naming the target customer, the category and the three qualities the name should signal.
                2. Hard constraints listed, such as maximum length, languages it must work in and words to avoid.
                3. Two or three names you admire in other categories noted, with what each does well.
                4. The brief read and agreed by every co-founder or the one adviser you trust most.

                ## Notes
                Keep the brief about the company you expect to be in five years, not only the first product. A name tied to one feature, city or technology can feel cramped after the first pivot.

                Without a scorecard the last name anyone mentioned tends to win.
