              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Founders usually look for a coach after a bad month, then cannot say what they want from it, and the first three sessions drift. Writing one page on the situations where you lead worst, what you have already tried and what would be different in six months gives every coach you speak to something real to respond to, and gives you a way to judge them.

                ## Milestones
                1. Three recent situations where your leadership fell short written down in plain words.
                2. A short list of what you have already tried, such as books, a mentor or a peer group, and why it was not enough.
                3. A sentence describing what people around you would notice if coaching worked.
                4. The page saved where you will reread it before every chemistry call.

                ## Notes
                Be specific about the moments, not the traits. "I go quiet and decide alone when the numbers are bad" is something a coach can work with; "I want to be a better leader" is not.

# Working with a Founder Coach

This area is for founders who want a coach to make them a better leader under pressure, not just someone pleasant to talk to every other week. It starts with foundations (why you want a coach, which kind of help fits, budget, a longlist, chemistry calls, an agreement, goals and a feedback baseline), then the routines that make sessions count, the skills of being coached well, the decisions about whether the coach is working, the dated moments like a raise or a hard board meeting, variants for first-time, technical, solo and newly funded founders, and finally specialist work on measuring change and spreading coaching habits to your team.

What repeats is session preparation and a commitments log each month, a weekly practice experiment and Friday self-coaching, a daily two-line note on the hardest moment, monthly goal and co-founder check-ins, quarterly sponsor reviews and theme reviews, and a yearly renewal decision. The **Purchase decision**, **Feedback themes**, **Development plan** and **1:1** templates pair with choosing a coach, the 360 baseline and follow-up, the coaching plan and your one-to-ones with your team.
