            - name: Accelerator, incubator or neither decision
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Accelerators trade a fixed slice of equity for a short, intense programme, some cash and a network; incubators usually take little or no equity and offer space and slower support over months; some founders need neither. Deciding which fits your stage, sector and runway before you write a single application saves weeks spent on programmes that were never right for you.

                ## Milestones
                1. A one-page note comparing accelerators, incubators, venture studios and pre-accelerators on equity, length, location and what each provides.
                2. Your stage, runway and biggest bottleneck written in three lines.
                3. A decision recorded: accelerator, incubator, both, or not now, with the reason.
                4. A date set to revisit the decision if the answer was not now.

                ## Notes
                The strongest reason to join is a specific gap the programme fills, such as investor access or a first customer network. 'Everyone else is doing one' is not a reason.

                Before you start: this plan is general information to help you organise your own money decisions. It is not financial, investment, tax or legal advice. Check decisions that matter with a qualified, licensed adviser in your country, and remember that investing and trading can lose money.
              priority: high

                mode: research

# Accelerator & Incubator Applications

This area is for founders and student founders deciding whether an accelerator or incubator is worth it, getting into the right one and making the weeks count. It runs from the foundations (the first decision, a ranked list of needs, a longlist, deal terms and an application calendar) through the routines of an application season, the skills of writing answers and handling interviews, decisions about offers and relocation, the dated events from submission to demo day, situations such as studying, employment or applying from abroad, and the specialist work of experienced alumni.

What repeats is a weekly application sprint and tracker update in open season, a monthly traction refresh, alumni calls and open-call scan, quarterly rejection reviews, and during a programme the weekly office hours preparation and goal check-in. The Metrics log, Meeting notes, Person and Onboarding plan templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
