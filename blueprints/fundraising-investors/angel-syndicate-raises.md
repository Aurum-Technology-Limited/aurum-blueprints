            - name: Choosing between syndicates, angel networks and solo angels
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Syndicates, organised angel networks and individual angels raise the same money in very different ways: a syndicate lead can fill an allocation in two weeks through one SPV, while a network may take three months of screening, pitching and group diligence before cheques arrive. Deciding which route suits your round size, timeline and appetite for process stops you applying everywhere at once and running out of runway halfway through.

                ## Milestones
                1. The amount you need from angels and the latest date the money must land written down.
                2. The three routes compared on time to close, typical cheque, paperwork and help after close.
                3. A primary route chosen, with one fallback route named.
                4. The decision and its reasons shared with your co-founders or advisers.

                ## Notes
                Many rounds mix routes: a network's members invest directly while a syndicate lead fills the rest through an SPV. Decide which one sets the timeline.
              priority: high
              deadlineOffsetDays: 14

                mode: research
                output_kind: decision

# Angel Syndicate Raises

This area is for founders raising from angel syndicates and organised angel networks: the SPV deals a syndicate lead carries to their backers, the screening, pitch nights and group diligence of angel networks, and the lead investors who make either route work. It starts with choosing the route, mapping leads and networks and getting terms and materials ready, then moves through the trackers and check-ins that run a live raise, the skills of pitching to a room of angels, decisions on allocation and structure, the dated events from screening to close, situations such as solo founders and regional networks, and specialist work like multi-network rounds and roll-up vehicles.

The rhythms are a weekly pipeline review and lead check-in while a deal is live, monthly calendar checks, lead notes and feedback reviews, quarterly updates for SPV backers and yearly reporting to the networks that backed you. The Person template suits lead investor records, Meeting notes the first screening meeting and Operational checklist the pitch night.
