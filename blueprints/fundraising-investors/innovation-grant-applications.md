              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Before reading a single competition brief, find out which doors are open to you. Innovation agencies test the same handful of things: where the company is registered, whether it meets the small and medium enterprise definition once linked and partner companies are counted, how long it has traded, and whether the work is genuine research and development rather than routine product building. Answering those once, in writing, saves hours on applications you could never have won.

                ## Milestones
                1. Company registration details, headcount, turnover and balance sheet total written on one page.
                2. Linked and partner enterprises identified and their figures added where the size rules require it.
                3. The work you want funded described in two sentences and checked against a published definition of research and development.
                4. A list of eligibility tests you pass, fail or are unsure about, with the unsure ones put to the agency.

                ## Notes
                Ownership by a larger company or a venture fund can change your size category. Check the agency's own definition rather than assuming you are small because the team is.
              priority: high

              frontmatter:
                mode: building
                output_kind: knowledge

                - "Email the agency helpdesk any eligibility question the guidance does not answer"

                Innovation grants rarely pay the whole cost or pay it up front.                 ## Notes

# Innovation Grant Applications

This area is for founders, small business owners and researchers who want public money for genuine innovation and research and development, from a first feasibility grant to a multi-country consortium. It starts with the foundations (eligibility, a funder map, a pipeline, portal registrations, match funding and a record of public support), then the routines that keep awards paid and compliant, the writing and costing skills assessors reward, the decisions about funding routes, partners and resubmissions, the events from submission to audit, the situations that change the approach, and finally the specialist work of consortia, multi-award portfolios and innovation procurement.

What repeats is a Monday scan for new calls, Friday timesheets, monthly cost coding, evidence filing and cash and double-funding checks, quarterly claims, progress reports and compliance reviews, and a yearly strategy review alongside impact reporting and portal renewals.
