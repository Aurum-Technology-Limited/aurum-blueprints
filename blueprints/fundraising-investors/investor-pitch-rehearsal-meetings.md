            - name: Five-minute spoken pitch script
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Most first investor meetings give you five uninterrupted minutes at best before questions start, and founders who have never said the pitch aloud fill them with product detail. Writing the spoken version word for word, separate from the deck, forces choices about the problem, the proof and the ask, and gives every later rehearsal something fixed to improve.

                ## Milestones
                1. A spoken script of about 700 words covering problem, solution, traction, market, team and the ask.
                2. The script read aloud and timed at between four and a half and five and a half minutes.
                3. Every number in the script checked against the current metrics.
                4. A co-founder or adviser has heard it once and given three written notes.

                ## Notes
                Write for the ear, not the page: short sentences, one number per sentence, and the company name said early. The slides belong in their own project; this is what you say.

                Before you start: this plan is general information to help you organise your own money decisions. It is not financial, investment, tax or legal advice. Check decisions that matter with a qualified, licensed adviser in your country, and remember that investing and trading can lose money.
              priority: high
              deadlineOffsetDays: 10
              frontmatter:
                mode: building
                output_kind: artifact
                success_criteria: "A timed spoken script of four and a half to five and a half minutes exists, with every number checked and three notes from a listener recorded."
                cadence: one-shot
                effort_hours_estimate: "5"
              tasks:
                - "Write the first sentence you will say after the introductions"
                - "Draft the full spoken script in short sentences, one idea each"
                - "Read the script aloud with a timer and cut until it fits five minutes"
                - "Check every number in the script against this month's metrics"
                - "Read it to a co-founder or adviser and write down their three notes"

# Investor Pitch Rehearsal & Meetings

This area is for founders getting ready to sit across the table from investors, whether that is a first angel coffee or a full partner meeting. It starts with the foundations (a spoken script, a thirty-second answer, a baseline recording, an answer bank, meeting briefs and a meeting log), then the routines of weekly rehearsal, debriefs, follow-ups and next steps, the speaking and questioning skills, the decisions about format, order and what to share, the dated meetings that need their own preparation, versions for particular founders, and finally the work of a founder who has done this several times.

What repeats is a weekly rehearsal slot, a weekly debrief review, follow-up and open question checks and a next-step board during a raise, a monthly refresh of the numbers you quote and a recording swap with a peer, quarterly relationship meetings between raises and a yearly playbook refresh. The Meeting notes, Operational checklist, Feedback themes and Metrics log templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
