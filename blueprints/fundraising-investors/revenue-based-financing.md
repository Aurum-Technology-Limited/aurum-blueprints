              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Revenue-based financing suits businesses with steady revenue, healthy gross margins and a use for money that pays back within months, and it can strangle one with thin margins or volatile sales. Checking your gross margin, months of trading history and revenue swings against what providers typically ask for tells you in an afternoon whether to pursue it or look elsewhere.

                ## Milestones
                1. Gross margin, monthly revenue for the last twelve months and the largest month-on-month drop written on one page.
                2. Typical provider minimums for trading history and monthly revenue noted from three provider websites.
                3. The purpose of the money described in one sentence, with how quickly it should earn back.
                4. A pursue, wait or rule-out decision recorded with the reason.

                ## Notes
                A business that needs money to cover losses rather than fund growth is usually a poor fit, because repayments start with the first sales and leave less cash for the problem the money was meant to fix.
