              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Lenders open every file with the same three questions: what is the money for, how much is needed, and how will it be paid back. Writing a one-page answer before you speak to anyone stops you asking for a round number that does not match the quotes, and lets you match the term to the life of what you are buying, so a five-year van is not on a ten-year loan.

                ## Milestones
                1. The purpose of the borrowing written in two sentences a stranger could repeat.
                2. An amount built up from quotes, fees and a contingency rather than a round guess.
                3. A proposed term matched to how long the asset or need will last.
                4. The repayment source named, with the monthly repayment you expect to afford.

                ## Notes
                If you cannot say in one sentence how the loan is repaid, the business may need a different kind of money, such as equity or a grant, rather than a loan.

                Before you start: this plan is general information to help you organise your own money decisions. It is not financial, investment, tax or legal advice. Check decisions that matter with a qualified, licensed adviser in your country, and remember that investing and trading can lose money.
              priority: high

            - name: Repayment capacity check before applying
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Before a lender reads your plan, it checks whether last year's profit could have paid the new repayments with room to spare, often expressed as a debt service cover ratio. Running that sum yourself tells you whether the amount in your loan statement is realistic, or whether a smaller loan, a longer term or more security is needed to reach a yes.

                ## Milestones

                3. A cover ratio calculated and compared with the range your accountant says lenders expect.
                4. A decision to apply as planned, borrow less or lengthen the term, written down.

                ## Notes
                Ask your accountant which profit measure lenders in your market use for this test. Definitions vary, and the difference can be large.

# Small Business Loan Applications

This area is for small business owners and founders applying for a bank loan, government-backed lending, asset finance or a commercial mortgage, and for anyone who has been declined and wants a stronger second attempt. It starts with the foundations (purpose and amount, credit files, affordability, security, filings and the document pack), then the lender-ready plan and forecasts and the routines that keep a borrower credible, the skills of reading offers and guarantees, the choices between products and lenders, the dated moments from submission to drawdown, variants for start-ups, sole traders, franchisees and disaster recovery, and finally covenant, guarantee and long-range borrowing work.

What repeats is a Friday update of the application tracker, monthly management accounts, forecast roll-forward, debt register, overdraft check and covenant reporting on different days of the month, quarterly credit file checks, pack refreshes and trading updates to your lending contact, and yearly reviews of insurance, guarantees, filings and the borrowing plan.
