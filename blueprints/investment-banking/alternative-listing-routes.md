# Alternative Listing Routes

This area is for the banker asked whether a client should go public some way other than an underwritten IPO: a direct listing, a merger with a SPAC, a listing by introduction, a cross listing with depositary receipts, a growth market admission or a reverse takeover into a listed shell. It starts with foundations (a map of the routes, eligibility rules, the client intake, costs, the mandate, disclosure documents and price discovery without a bookbuild), moves into the trackers that keep your judgement current, then the skills behind each structure, the decisions a board actually takes, the dated moments from the pre-filing meeting to the first trade, projects for each seat on the team and for different clients, and finally specialist work on dual listings, homecoming listings, failed de-SPACs and liability where no underwriter stands behind the document.

What repeats is a Monday read of the SPAC market and a monthly look at trust balances and deadlines, a monthly precedent and rule change check, weekly workstream calls and redemption tracking on a live deal, quarterly reviews of post-listing trading, and a monthly reading slot. The Metrics log, Reading queue, Meeting notes, Operational checklist, Board meeting and Risk register templates pair with the projects that name them. Keep client names, draft numbers and investor lists inside your firm's systems, and leave securities law and listing rule conclusions to counsel; these projects hold your own working method, not a client's confidential data.
