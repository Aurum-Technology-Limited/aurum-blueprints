# Analyst to Associate Promotion

This area is for the analyst who wants the associate title, or wants to decide on purpose not to take it. It starts with foundations (the bank's written criteria, the review calendar, an evidence file, an honest self-assessment and a sponsor), moves to the routines that make reviews predictable, then the associate skills you can practise before the title, the third-year decision itself, the dated moments around reviews and the promotion, variants for boutiques, small markets, international analysts and people returning from leave, and specialist work such as negotiating the terms of promotion.

What repeats is a Friday entry in the evidence file, a Monday stretch-task ask, a monthly feedback check-in, a monthly page in the decision journal, a quarterly sponsor conversation and the mid-year and year-end review cycles, spread across different days of the month. The **Development plan**, **1:1**, **Remediation plan** and **Onboarding 30/60/90** templates pair with the skills plan, the feedback conversations, a weak review theme and the first month with the new title. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
