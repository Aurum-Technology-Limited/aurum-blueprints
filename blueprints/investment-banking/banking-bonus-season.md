# Banking Bonus Season

This area is for the banker who wants bonus season to be the end of a year of evidence rather than a single nervous meeting in January. It starts with foundations (the bank's bonus calendar, the plan rules, a backfilled contribution log, your pay baseline and a written goal), then the routines that run all year, the knowledge that makes a compensation statement readable, the decisions before and after the number, the dated moments of the season itself, variants for first-years, people on leave, boutiques, regional banks and weak years, and specialist work for VPs who now feed into other people's bonuses.

What repeats is a Friday entry in the contribution log, a monthly deal outcome sweep and praise filing, quarterly pay data, results reads, vesting checks and staffer reconciliation, and a yearly cycle of calendar checks, visibility planning, a payslip check and a season retrospective, spread across different days of the month. The **Metrics log**, **1:1**, **Meeting notes**, **Savings goal**, **Monthly budget and bills** and **Development plan** templates pair with the contribution log, the sponsor conversations, the bonus meeting, the bonus money plan, the spending guardrail and recovering from a weak year.
