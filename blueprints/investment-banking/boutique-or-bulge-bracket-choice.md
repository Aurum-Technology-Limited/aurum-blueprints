              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Most candidates decide by prestige and only later discover they cared more about hours, a specific city or a buy-side exit. Writing five to eight criteria with weights before the first superday gives you a yardstick that does not shift every time a recruiter is charming.

                ## Milestones
                1. Five to eight criteria written, such as training, deal flow, hours, pay, location, culture and exit routes.
                2. A weight out of 100 given to each criterion, adding to 100.
                3. A one-line reason for each weight, in your own words.
                4. The weighted list shared with one trusted person who knows you well, and their challenge noted.

                ## Notes
                If every criterion gets the same weight, you have not decided anything yet. Force at least one criterion above 25.
