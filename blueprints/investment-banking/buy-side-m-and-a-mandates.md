# Buy-Side M&A Mandates

Buy-side work is advising someone who wants to buy: helping them decide what to look for, finding and approaching the right targets, setting a price they can defend, coordinating diligence and financing, and negotiating to a signed deal or a clean walk away. This area starts with foundations (the mandate letter, acquisition criteria, the kick-off and the decision gates), then the operating routines that keep a search and a live bid moving, the buyer-side skills of screening, profiling and reading a seller's documents, the decisions on approach, bid authority and price, the events from management meetings to signing, variants for analysts, associates, vice presidents, sponsor clients, first-time acquirers and boutique search mandates, and specialist work such as off-market approaches and take-privates.

What repeats is mostly weekly: a Monday pipeline review, a Tuesday market scan, the Wednesday diligence and financing trackers, the Thursday client update and a Friday check of the red flag register, with monthly mandate reviews with your MD, contact log upkeep and valuation refreshes, and quarterly refreshes of criteria, profiles and the won and lost log. The **Meeting notes**, **Risk register**, **Board meeting**, **Operational checklist** and **Feedback themes** templates pair with the kick-off, diligence findings, the client's board approval, bid day and the post-deal review. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
