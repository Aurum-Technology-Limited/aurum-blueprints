# Deal Staffing and Hours

This area is about the load itself: which deals you end up on, how many at once, how many hours they really take and how you talk about all of it with the staffer and the seniors who ask for you. It starts with foundations (how staffing works in your group, an hours log, an inventory of your current deals, the bank's hours rules and your fixed dates), moves to the weekly and monthly routines that keep the staffer informed, then the skills of accepting, deferring and coming off deals, the decisions that rebalance a heavy stretch, dated moments such as a launch week or a two-week holiday, variants for summer analysts, carers, exam candidates and boutique teams, and specialist work for associates, VPs and whoever runs the staffing sheet.

What repeats is a nightly hours entry, a Friday capacity note to the staffer, a Sunday check that protected blocks held, a mid-week scan for deals going quiet, monthly reviews of hours, fixed dates and pitch count on different days, and a quarterly look at your deal mix against your goals. The **Metrics log** template suits the hours log, the **1:1** template suits staffer and mentor conversations, and the **Development plan** template suits your staffing goals for the year.
