# Health Under Banking Hours

This area is for analysts, associates and VPs who want to come out of the banking years in good health, without pretending the hours are normal. It starts with foundations such as a doctor, a baseline and an honest sleep log, moves through the weekly routines for sleep, food and exercise, then covers skills, decisions, deal events, situations from internship to parenthood, and finally the yearly question of whether the hours still work for you.

The rhythms are a weekly review of wake times, short workouts and a food restock, a monthly scorecard and metrics log, a quarterly appointments check and a yearly health check and review. The Sleep review, Habit tracker, Metrics log, Purchase decision, Trip, Training program and Reading queue templates pair with it.
