# IPO Execution (ECM)

This area is for the banker working an initial public offering from the equity capital markets side, from the analyst on a first listing to the vice president the issuer's finance director calls every evening. It starts with the foundations (the process map, syndicate roles and fees, readiness, the bake-off, underwriting terms, the timetable and publicity rules), moves into the operating rhythm of drafting sessions, comment logs, regulator letters, market window and diligence trackers, then the skills of prospectus anatomy, IPO valuation, offer structure, stabilisation and allocation, the decisions on price range, deal size, cornerstones and retail, the dated moments from kick-off to first trade, situations for different seats and issuers, and finally specialist work on research initiation, the greenshoe, cross-border tranches and governance debates.

What repeats is a weekly all-party drafting session and a comment log review, a Monday read of the market window, weekly diligence and regulator comment tracking, a daily book update while the order book is open, and monthly checks on recent IPO aftermarkets and your own precedent library. The Metrics log, Meeting notes, Reading queue, Risk register and Operational checklist templates pair with the projects that name them. Keep issuer names, draft numbers and investor orders inside your firm's systems; these projects hold your own working method, not a client's confidential data. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
