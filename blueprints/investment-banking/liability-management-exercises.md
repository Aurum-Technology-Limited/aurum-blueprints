# Liability Management Exercises

Liability management is the work of changing a company's existing debt without a court: buying bonds back through a tender offer, swapping them for new paper in an exchange offer, changing the terms through a consent solicitation, or pushing out a loan maturity with an amend and extend. This area starts with foundations (the vocabulary, the four tools side by side, the debt stack, the document terms that decide what is possible, the holder base and the rules), then the coverage machinery that finds opportunities, the pricing and participation skills, the structuring choices an issuer has to make, live offers from launch to settlement, variants for analysts, associates, vice presidents, creditor-side and regional bankers, and specialist work such as uptiers, drop-downs and distressed exchanges.

What repeats is a Monday maturity wall screen and a Wednesday secondary price check, a daily tender count while an offer is live, and monthly updates to the opportunity pipeline, the precedent tracker, holder group activity and the post-deal results log, with quarterly upkeep of call schedules and the document precedent bank. The **Operational checklist**, **Meeting notes**, **Vendor**, **Development plan**, **Feedback themes** and **Risk register** templates pair with launch day, the bondholder meeting, choosing the tender agent, your first offer, the debrief and distressed exchanges.
