              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Every bank has a house shape for its pitch books, and juniors who learn it from real past decks get far fewer structural comments on their first drafts. Reading five recent books from your group, ideally a sell-side pitch, a buy-side idea, a financing pitch, a listing bake-off and a general relationship book, shows which sections are always there and which are written fresh each time.

                ## Milestones
                1. Five recent pitch books from your group pulled from the shared drive or the presentations archive.
                2. A one-page map of the sections each book uses, in the order they appear.
                3. The sections common to all five marked as house standard, the rest marked as bespoke.
                4. Three questions about structure taken to an associate and answered.

                ## Notes
                Read the final printed version, not a working draft, so you learn what survived the MD's markup rather than what was cut.
              priority: high
              deadlineOffsetDays: 14

            - name: Source file and footnote conventions
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                A client who spots one wrong share count stops trusting every other number in the book.                 ## Notes

# Pitch Book Preparation

Pitch books are where most banking hours go: the situation overview, the credentials, the valuation and buyer pages, the process options and the overnight turn before the meeting. This area starts with foundations (the house shape, a master page set, a kick-off brief, a ghost deck, sourcing and compliance sign-off), then the operating routines that keep live pitches moving, the page-by-page skills, the improvements that cut errors and hours, the pitch events themselves, variants for interns, boutique bankers, associates and vice presidents, and specialist work such as joint and cross-product pitches.

What repeats is weekly: the Monday and Thursday pitch tracker review, a Wednesday market data refresh and a Friday check of the turn log, with monthly upkeep of standing client pages, the asset library, the page archive and your proofing checklist. The **Meeting notes**, **Operational checklist** and **Feedback themes** templates pair with the kick-off, the proof and the error post-mortem. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
