# Vice President Deal Leadership

The vice president is where banking stops being about producing the work and starts being about owning it: the client calls you first, the associate and analysts look to you for direction, and the MD expects the deal to run without being chased. This area starts with foundations (your first 90 days, a map of every live mandate, working agreements with your MDs, team charters, kick-off packs and a review standard), then the weekly and monthly machinery of status calls, client updates, pre-send gates and risk reviews, the skills of steering calls, strategy memos and fast model reviews, the judgement calls when a deal drifts or a buyer walks, the big days from kick-off to signing night, variants for inherited deals, boutiques, offshore teams and parental leave, and finally the specialist work of originating ideas and building the director case.

What repeats is a daily ten-minute stand-up on live executions, a Monday status call with each team, a Tuesday load check, a Wednesday portfolio refresh, Thursday client updates and a Friday process tracker review, with monthly 1:1s, pipeline notes, risk register and decision log reviews, and quarterly lessons, fee and relationship reviews. The **Onboarding 30/60/90**, **Person**, **Operational checklist**, **1:1**, **Risk register**, **Board meeting** and **Development plan** templates pair with the first 90 days, MD agreements, the pre-send gate, team check-ins, deal risks, client board support and the director case. Keep every record on firm systems under deal code names. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
