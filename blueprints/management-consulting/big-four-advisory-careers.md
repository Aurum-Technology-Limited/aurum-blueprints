# Big Four Advisory Careers

Big Four advisory is a different career from a strategy house: bigger teams, longer implementation work, chargeable hours, audit independence rules and a professional qualification sitting alongside the day job. This area starts with foundations (the service lines mapped, the comparison with strategy firms, a practice chosen, the grade ladder, independence and your first qualification decision), then the routines that keep a practitioner in good standing, the technical skills of deal, risk, technology and forensic work, decisions about transfers, secondments and lateral moves, the dated events from assessment centre to first budget in charge, variants for students, returners, part-time and experienced hires, and finally the specialist work of selling, building an offering and the run to director.

The rhythm is a Friday timesheet, monthly checks on utilisation, feedback requests, sector reading, cross-practice contacts, file archiving, WIP and pipeline, a quarterly CPD log and career conversation, and yearly independence and training confirmations. The Metrics log, Course, Development plan, Job application, Onboarding 30/60/90 and Skills matrix templates pair with the utilisation baseline, qualification study, the career plan, assessment centres, the first 90 days and the skills map.
