# Consulting Case Interview Prep

Case interviews reward a small set of habits done under pressure: asking the right opening questions, building a structure that fits this client rather than a memorised framework, reading a chart for its point, and closing with a clear answer. This area is for students and working professionals heading into consulting interviews, and it runs from foundations (firm formats, a baseline case, a practice log and a calendar) through the weekly practice machinery, the core skills and case types, fixes for common plateaus, the interview days themselves, plans for particular situations such as an exam term or a full-time job, and the harder partner-style cases at the end.

The rhythm is live cases with partners twice a week, one case given as interviewer, a short daily structuring and chart drill, a Sunday review of the log and a monthly recorded replay and mock with a working consultant. The Metrics log, Reading queue, Feedback themes, Remediation plan and Purchase decision templates pair with the log, the source list, feedback, plateau fixes and the coaching decision.
