# Consulting Fit Interviews

This area is for anyone facing the fit or personal experience half of a consulting interview, from an undergraduate with campus stories to an experienced hire with ten years of work behind them. It starts with foundations (how your target firms run fit, a timeline of raw material, a coverage matrix, the walkthrough and why consulting answers, a prep plan), moves to the routines that keep stories sharp, then the skills interviewers test, the decisions about which stories to lead with, the dated rounds themselves, variants for students, MBA switchers, PhDs, returners and reapplicants, and specialist work such as handling partner pushback and interviewing candidates yourself.

What repeats is a twice-weekly rehearsal, a weekly mock with a partner, weekly recording reviews and feedback entries, a monthly story bank update, firm news refresh and work wins entry, and a quarterly refresh of the walkthrough, spread across different days. The **Feedback themes**, **Meeting notes** and **Development plan** templates pair with the mock feedback log, the post-interview debrief and the plan to create new story material. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
