            - name: Map of your firm's knowledge sources
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Most firms keep past work in four or five places at once: a knowledge portal, practice pages, a research or information centre, an expert directory and the old shared drives that never quite got migrated. Knowing which source holds what, and how current each one is, turns a vague two-hour search into a fifteen-minute pull you can repeat on every engagement.

                ## Milestones
                1. A one-page map listing each knowledge source, what it holds, who owns it and how to request access.
                2. Access confirmed to the portal, your practice pages and the research request channel.
                3. The two sources your practice actually relies on most marked, after asking two experienced colleagues.
                4. The map saved where you will find it at the start of every engagement.

                ## Notes
                Ask a manager which source they open first. The official answer and the real one often differ, and the real one is usually a practice page kept by one diligent person.
              priority: high

            - name: Rules for using past client material
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Reusing a slide that still carries another client's numbers, logo or unusual phrasing is one of the fastest ways to end up in a conversation with the risk team. Reading your firm's confidentiality and knowledge-use policy before you reuse anything tells you what can be shared internally, what must be disguised, what needs the original partner's permission and what may never leave the original case team.

                ## Milestones
                1. The firm's knowledge-use and client confidentiality policy read in full.
                2. A one-paragraph personal summary of what may be reused freely, what must be disguised and what needs permission.
                3. The person in risk or knowledge management who answers reuse questions identified by name.
                4. Any unclear point in the policy asked and answered in writing.

                ## Notes
                Client engagement letters sometimes restrict reuse more tightly than the firm policy. When the original case is with a sensitive client, ask the original partner before you lift anything.
              priority: high
              deadlineOffsetDays: 7

# Consulting Knowledge Reuse

Every consulting firm sits on years of decks, models, interview guides and benchmarks, and the consultants who do well are the ones who find the right ten pages on day one instead of rebuilding them by Thursday night. This area starts with foundations (a map of the knowledge sources, search technique, the confidentiality rules, a personal library and the people behind the portal), then the weekly and monthly routines that keep reuse and contribution going, the skills of judging, adapting and sanitising material, decisions about building versus reusing, the dated debriefs and practice sessions, variants for new analysts, lateral hires, managers and people changing practice, and finally specialist work such as curating starter packs and measuring reuse across a practice.

The rhythm is a Monday knowledge pull for the live engagement, a Tuesday check on research requests, a Wednesday share in the team meeting, a Thursday reuse log and a Friday skim of new publications, with a monthly contribution to the knowledge base and quarterly prunes of your own library. The Meeting notes, Reading queue, Metrics log and Operational checklist templates pair with expert conversations, the publications skim, the reuse log and the end-of-engagement submission. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
