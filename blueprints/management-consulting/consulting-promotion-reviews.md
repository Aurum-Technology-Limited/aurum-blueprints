                Firms publish what an analyst, associate or manager must show to move up, but few people read the next grade's rubric until the review itself, when it is too late to collect evidence. # Consulting Promotion Reviews

Promotion in consulting is decided in a room you are not in, by people reading reviews written months earlier, so the work is less about one heroic project and more about evidence, timing and having someone argue your case. This area starts with foundations (the criteria for the next grade, the review calendar, an honest self-assessment, your counsellor and your up-or-out clock), then the routines that build a file all year, the skills of self-reviews and feedback in both directions, decisions about stretch roles and early or late promotion, the dated review events, variants for leave, part-time, secondments and experienced hires, and finally the specialist work of reviewing others and sitting on a panel.

The rhythm is a Friday evidence log, a Wednesday feedback ask, monthly check-ins with your counsellor and sponsors, monthly checks on pending engagement reviews and utilisation, and a quarterly self-rating against the rubric. The Skills matrix, 1:1, Development plan, Feedback themes, Remediation plan and Meeting notes templates pair with the self-assessment, the counsellor meetings, this cycle's plan, the themes tracker, a weak review recovery and the mid-year meeting. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
