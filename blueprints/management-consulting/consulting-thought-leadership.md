              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Buyers remember a consultant for one thing, and a profile that covers strategy, operations, digital and culture is remembered for none of them. Picking a single topic at the intersection of what you have delivered, what clients pay for and where few credible voices exist gives every later article, talk and webinar a reason to exist.                 ## Milestones

                2. Three candidate topics scored against your evidence, buyer budget and the number of competing voices.
                3. One topic chosen and written as a single sentence a client would repeat to a colleague.
                4. Two adjacent topics you will deliberately not write about, written down.

                ## Notes
                Narrow feels risky and is almost always right. Wording like pricing for mid-sized distributors beats commercial excellence, because a buyer can recognise themselves in it.

# Consulting Thought Leadership

Thought leadership for a consultant is not content for its own sake: it is a narrow, well argued point of view that makes the right buyer think of you when the problem lands on their desk. This area starts with foundations (one topic, a clear buyer, the insight you already hold, a written point of view, confidentiality rules and lead tracking), then the weekly and monthly publishing machinery, the craft skills of writing, research, exhibits and speaking, decisions about formats, gating and promotion, dated events such as a flagship report, a conference talk and a buyer roundtable, variants for a solo freelancer, a small partner group and a newly independent consultant, and finally specialist work such as an annual index, a press presence and a three-year canon.

The rhythm is a Monday writing block, short posts on Tuesdays and Thursdays, a Friday enquiry log, a long article and a newsletter each month, a monthly lead attribution review and speaking pipeline check, and quarterly reviews of topic, list and back catalogue. The Content pipeline, Metrics log, Purchase decision and Marketing campaign templates pair with the editorial calendar, the lead review, choosing editorial help and the flagship report launch.
