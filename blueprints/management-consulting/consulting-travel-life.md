# Consulting Travel Life

This area is for the consultant who flies out on Monday morning and lands home on Thursday night, week after week, and wants that rhythm to stay sustainable for years rather than months. It starts with foundations (the firm's travel policy, a booking profile, a packed bag, an expense workflow, documents and written travel limits), then the weekly machinery of packing, expenses, returns and loyalty status, the skills of working, sleeping and staying safe on the road, decisions about airports, long stays and relocation, dated moments such as a new out-of-town engagement or a roll-off, variants for parents, carers, dual-travel couples and people returning from leave, and finally the advanced work of spotting burnout, setting team travel norms and planning a lower-travel future.

The rhythm is weekly: a Sunday packing routine, Friday expenses and reset, a Saturday calendar sync at home and two hotel workouts, with a monthly look at travel load and loyalty balances, a quarterly kit refresh and yearly checks of passports, vaccinations and the three-year travel plan. The Operational checklist, Metrics log, Habit tracker, Sleep review, Purchase decision and Trip templates pair with the packing routine, the travel load log, the hotel workout, sleep on the road, the airport choice and international engagements.
