# Ethics and Conflicts in Consulting

Consulting sells judgement and trust, and both are lost faster than they are earned. This area starts with the foundations (your firm's code, your own red lines, who to call, what you must disclose), then builds the routines that catch conflicts and leaks before they happen, the knowledge behind them, the hard calls about clients, fees and findings, the moments when something goes wrong, the situations specific to firm consultants and solo practitioners, and finally the work of setting ethical standards for others.

The rhythms are light but steady: a monthly pass over the conflicts register, gifts log and confidential files, a weekly honest timesheet, quarterly checks of personal holdings and a trusted-senior conversation, and a yearly certification and reread of the rules. The Risk register, Compliance control, Meeting notes and Reading queue templates pair with the register, the gifts control, the concern record and the case reading.
