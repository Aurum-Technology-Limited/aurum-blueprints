              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Most employment contracts, codes of conduct and outside-business-interest policies say something about paid consulting, and some ban it outright or require written approval first. Reading yours before you accept a single call protects your job, and a written yes from your employer is the strongest answer you can give a network's compliance team when they ask.

                ## Milestones

                4. Any restrictions from a past employer, such as a non-compete or post-employment confidentiality term, noted with end dates.

            - name: Personal confidentiality boundary map
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Every expert network will ask you to confirm that you will not share material non-public information or anything you owe a duty of confidence on, but the hard part is knowing in advance where that line sits for you. A one-page map of the topics you can discuss freely, the ones you can discuss only in general terms and the ones you will always decline makes the call itself much easier.

                ## Milestones

                4. The map reviewed against your employer's confidentiality policy.

                ## Notes

# Expert Network Calls

This area is for industry professionals who get paid by expert networks to talk to investors and consultants about what they know: people still in a job, people between roles, retired executives and independent consultants. It starts with the foundations (employer permission, a map of what you must never discuss, a conflicts register, the right networks, an honest profile, a rate and the money records), then the weekly and monthly machinery, call skills, decisions about rates, formats and structure, dated events such as your first call and a cooling-off period, variants for different situations, and specialist work like handling a request for inside information or a retained advisory role.

What repeats is a Monday triage of new requests, a Friday capacity check, a Saturday read of public industry news, a pre-call compliance check, a monthly earnings log and payment reconciliation on different days, a quarterly profile and conflicts refresh, and a yearly tax pack and review of the whole sideline. The **Risk register** template pairs with the conflicts register, **Compliance control** with the pre-call check and **Metrics log** with the call and earnings ledger.
