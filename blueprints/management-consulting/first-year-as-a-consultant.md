# First Year as a Consultant

The first year in consulting is mostly about becoming someone a manager can hand a page, a model or a client conversation to and stop worrying. This area is for new analysts and associates, including students counting down to a start date, and it runs from foundations (pre-start logistics, laptop and shortcuts, a first 90 days plan, a note-taking system, the questions to ask on every new project) through the weekly operating routines, the core skills of Excel, slides, emails and sketches, client etiquette, the harder moments such as a mistake that reaches the client, the dated milestones of the year, variants for experienced hires, non-business backgrounds and remote teams, and finally the habits of a strong second-year.

What repeats is a short end-of-day status message and a clear inbox each day, a Monday plan and a Friday review with timesheets and expenses, a Tuesday read of the client's industry, a Wednesday Excel drill, and monthly feedback, mentor, shortcut library and error log reviews. The Onboarding 30/60/90, Meeting notes, Skills matrix, Feedback themes, 1:1 and Development plan templates pair with the first 90 days, client meeting notes, the skills baseline, the feedback log, the mentor conversations and the one-year plan. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
