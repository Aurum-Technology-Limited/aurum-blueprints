              description: |-

                4. A floor day rate and a target day rate written down, with the maths shown.

                ## Notes
                The floor is a private number, not a quote. How you package fees for a client is a separate pricing question.

# Going Independent After a Firm

Leaving a consulting firm to work for yourself is part legal exit, part small business launch and part reinvention of how you do the work without analysts, a knowledge library or a partner to sell for you. This area runs from foundations (your exit clauses, a lawyer's view of them, runway, the go decision, a one-line offer, a rate floor, the company, banking, insurance and your standard terms), through the operating routines of a one-person practice, the skills of selling and delivering alone, practical decisions about tools, space, benefits and pensions, the dated moments from resignation to first paid invoice, variants for senior leavers, early-career leavers, returners home and people on garden leave, and finally specialist work such as teaming with other independents and taking a former employer as a client.

The rhythm is a Monday pipeline review and a Friday admin hour, a monthly close of the books, a monthly peer review of your work and steady contact with your network, with quarterly tax set-asides and practice reviews and a yearly check of insurance, terms and subscriptions. The Month-end close, Metrics log, Development plan and Purchase decision templates pair with the monthly finances close, the utilisation log, the learning plan that replaces firm training and the choice of a back-office tool stack. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
