              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Most boutiques hire either too early, on the strength of one promising proposal, or too late, after the founders have worked six months of weekends. Setting out current utilisation, weighted pipeline and the work you are turning down gives a clear answer to whether a permanent hire, a freelancer or nobody is the right move this quarter.

                ## Milestones
                1. Utilisation for each current consultant over the last six months calculated from timesheets or invoices.
                2. A weighted pipeline for the next two quarters, with each opportunity's probability written beside it.
                3. Work declined or delayed for lack of capacity listed with its approximate fee value.
                4. A one-page decision recorded: hire now, use a freelancer, or wait, with the trigger that would change it.

                ## Notes
                A common rule of thumb is to hire when sold work would keep the new person above about 60 percent utilised for six months, but set your own threshold from your margins.

            - name: Consultant hiring scorecard
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Interviewers who score against their own private picture of a good consultant reach different verdicts on the same candidate and cannot explain why. A scorecard with five or six competencies, each with anchored descriptions of weak, solid and strong, turns debriefs into evidence instead of impressions and is the backbone of every interview you run.

                ## Milestones
                1. Five or six competencies chosen, such as structuring, quantitative judgement, client presence, written communication and ownership.
                2. Each competency described at three anchor points with an observable example.
                3. A rule agreed for how scores combine into hire, no hire or second look.
                4. The scorecard used on at least two candidates and adjusted where interviewers disagreed.

                ## Notes
                Start from the **Skills matrix** template. Keep culture fit out of the scorecard as a vague category; name the behaviour you actually need instead.
              priority: high
              deadlineOffsetDays: 45

                ## Purpose
                Pay decided candidate by candidate creates gaps that surface the day two consultants compare notes. Setting a band for each level, using salary surveys, recruiter data and what you can afford at your day rates, lets you make offers quickly and defend them, and shows how base, bonus and benefits fit together.

# Hiring Consultants for a Boutique

Hiring is where a boutique either keeps its quality or quietly loses it, because every new consultant is a large share of what clients see. This area is for founders and small firm owners who recruit without an HR department: it starts with foundations (the case for a hire, role profiles, a scorecard, pay bands, the contractor or employee question and contracts), then the operating routines that keep a pipeline moving, the interviewing skills you need, onboarding new people to your methods, decisions on tools and sourcing, dated hiring events, situations such as hiring alone or scaling past five, and specialist work such as checking whether your interviews predict performance.

The rhythms are a Monday pipeline review, a Friday sweep for candidates waiting on you, monthly checks on the freelance bench and on probation reviews, a quarterly headcount plan, interviewer calibration and methods handbook update, and a yearly pay review, budget refresh and fairness audit. The Skills matrix, Onboarding 30/60/90, Purchase decision, 1:1 and Risk register templates pair with it. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
