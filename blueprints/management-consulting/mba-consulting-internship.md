            - name: Reading the internship offer and its terms
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Offer letters for MBA consulting internships carry more than a salary: start and end dates, the office, relocation support, whether a return offer can be extended at the end of the summer and any conditions on accepting it. Reading the letter line by line in the first fortnight, with questions sent to the recruiter in one email, avoids surprises in August when the decisions get bigger.

                ## Milestones
                1. Start date, end date, office and pay written into a one-page summary.
                2. Relocation, housing and travel support listed with how to claim each.
                3. Any conditions on the offer, such as background checks or degree progress, noted with their dates.
                4. One email of clarifying questions sent to the recruiter and the answers filed with the letter.

                ## Notes
                Ask now, not in week nine, whether a return offer is normally made at the end of the summer and how long you would have to answer it.
              priority: high
              deadlineOffsetDays: 14

                mode: research

# MBA Consulting Internship

This area is for MBA students heading into a consulting summer internship, from the day the offer letter lands to the day the return offer is signed or declined. It starts with foundations (the offer terms, how interns are evaluated, a ten-week plan, housing, money and the training week), then the daily and weekly routines that make an intern look like a hire, the skills a short project tests, the decisions about staffing, feedback and offers, the dated reviews and presentations, variants for international students, parents, career switchers, split summers and remote placements, and finally the work of turning one summer into sponsorship and a strong start.

What repeats during the summer is a daily check-out with your manager, a weekly feedback ask, a Friday entry in your evidence log, coffee chats, timesheets and a Sunday check on hours and sleep, with a few monthly touches before the summer and after it. The Onboarding plan, Monthly budget and bills, 1:1, Feedback themes and Resume templates pair with the projects that name them. Client names and client data stay on the firm's systems; these projects hold your own plans, notes and reflections. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
