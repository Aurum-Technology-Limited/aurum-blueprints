# Multilateral and Donor-Funded Consultancies

This area is for freelancers, small firms and experienced professionals who want work funded by development banks, donors and UN agencies, such as the IDB, CDB, World Bank, EU and UNDP. It starts with foundations (a funder map, portal registration, a donor-format CV and an evidence library), then the weekly bid machinery and the skills of selection methods, terms of reference, EOIs and proposals, before moving through money and partnering decisions, dated deliverables from proposal to final report, situations such as a first individual contract or leaving a bank job, and specialist work like framework contracts and regional assignments.

The rhythms are a weekly notice scan and pipeline review, weekly deliverable chasing and timesheets, monthly checks on associates, payments and receipts, and quarterly win rate and audit file reviews. The Resume, Skills matrix, Metrics log, Meeting notes, Risk register and Trip templates pair with the CV, bench, win rate, negotiation, inception and field mission projects.
