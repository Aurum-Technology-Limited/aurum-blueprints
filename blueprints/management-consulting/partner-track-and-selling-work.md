            - name: Securing a senior partner sponsor
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Partner elections are decided in rooms you will not be in, and a case without a senior partner willing to argue for it rarely survives. Choosing a sponsor deliberately, someone with standing in the election process who has seen your work, and asking them openly to back you, is one of the highest return conversations on the track.

                ## Milestones

                4. An agreed rhythm of check-ins and the sponsor's first piece of advice recorded.

# Partner Track and Selling Work

Partner track is the stretch of a consulting career where being excellent at delivery stops being enough and the firm starts asking who your clients are, what you have sold and what you stand for. This area is for senior managers, principals, associate partners and directors working toward election, and it runs from foundations (the written partner bar, a gap assessment, a dated plan, a sponsor, a relationship inventory and an origination baseline), through the selling machinery that has to run every week, the skills of needs conversations, qualification, proposals, pricing and orals, decisions about where to focus and whether to stay, the nomination and election events, variants for parental leave, experienced hires, regional offices, deferral and part-time working, and finally the specialist work of building a new offering, running a large account and reading the partner agreement.

The rhythm is a weekly block of selling time and a weekly round of client touches, a monthly pipeline review, origination log and sponsor check-in, quarterly account plans and network refreshes, and a yearly refresh of the evidence portfolio the election panel will read. The Development plan, Person, Metrics log, Meeting notes and Feedback themes templates pair with the track plan, the key client records, the origination log, sponsor check-ins and partner feedback. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
