                An action title is a full sentence that states the conclusion of the page, so a busy reader gets the argument without looking at the chart. # Slide Storylining and Action Titles

Consulting decks are read title by title, so the argument has to exist before the charts do. This area starts with foundations (a baseline of your own pages, a title standard, a slide anatomy checklist and a ghost deck template), then the operating rhythm that keeps the storyline ahead of the analysis, the specific skills of pyramid key lines, so-what titles and exhibits that prove them, decisions about format, length and audience, the dated moments where a storyline gets tested, situations such as owning your first section or writing in a second language, and finally specialist work such as integrating several workstreams into one master storyline and teaching others.

The rhythm is a weekly paper storyline before PowerPoint, a weekly title-only read of the working deck, a ghost deck refresh as findings land, a short daily title rewrite, and monthly reviews of your swipe file, exhibit library and manager comments, with a quarterly self-assessment. The Skills matrix, Metrics log, Feedback themes and Course templates pair with the self-assessment, the title drill, the comment log and the team training module.
