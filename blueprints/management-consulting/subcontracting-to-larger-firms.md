# Subcontracting to Larger Firms

Large consulting firms bring in independents and small boutiques all the time, to fill a skills gap, add a sector expert or staff a peak, and the people who do well at it treat the prime firm as a demanding client with its own procurement, brand and legal rules. This area starts with foundations (your offer, a target list of primes, the supplier pack, insurance, your contract positions, a rate floor and your status as a contractor), then the routines that keep subcontract work flowing and paid, the clauses and negotiations worth learning, decisions about pricing models, exclusivity and dependence, the dated moments of an assignment, variants for solo freelancers and small firms, and finally specialist work such as teaming agreements, alliances and disputes.

The rhythm is a Monday pipeline look across your primes, Thursday quality notes and Friday timesheets, monthly invoicing, chasing, utilisation and obligation checks on staggered days, quarterly supplier status and credential refreshes, and a yearly rate, insurance and portfolio review. The Person, Risk register, Metrics log and Meeting notes templates pair with prime contacts, contract obligations, utilisation and assignment debriefs.
