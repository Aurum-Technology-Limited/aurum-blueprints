# Written and Group Case Exercises

Written and group cases test different muscles from a live case interview: you read a thick data pack alone against the clock, build a handful of slides or a memo, present to interviewers, and in group rounds show that you make other candidates better rather than louder. This area starts with foundations (the formats you will face, a timed baseline and a time budget), then the weekly practice machinery, the specific skills of triage, exhibits, presenting and group behaviour, the decisions that fix weak spots, the assessment days themselves, variants for undergraduates, experienced hires and others, and finally specialist work such as writing packs and running mocks for a club.

The rhythm is a weekly timed data pack drill, a weekly group practice circle and a recorded presentation review, with a monthly mock assessment day, a mistake log review and a feedback exchange with a practice partner. The Skills matrix, Metrics log and Feedback themes templates pair with the self-assessment, the drill log and the feedback exchange. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
