                ## Purpose
                Assessors want to know how attention, restlessness and impulsivity affect your life now, in at least two settings such as work and home, and most people go blank when asked on the day.                 4. A one-page summary ready to send or bring to the assessment.

            - name: Choosing an adult ADHD assessment route
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Routes to an adult ADHD assessment vary widely by country and region: a referral from your doctor onto a public waiting list, a right to choose another provider in some health systems, workplace or university schemes, or paying privately. Each differs in wait, cost and whether your usual doctor will accept the diagnosis and continue prescribing, so comparing them on paper first prevents paying for an assessment that leads nowhere.

                ## Milestones
                1. Your doctor asked how adult ADHD referrals work locally and how long the wait is.
                2. Two or three routes compared on wait time, cost, clinician qualifications and follow-up care.
                3. Each private or alternative provider checked for whether your doctor would take over prescribing under shared care.
                4. A chosen route with the referral or booking confirmed.

                ## Notes
                Shared care, where a specialist starts treatment and your usual doctor takes over prescribing, is not automatic. Ask your doctor in writing before paying for a private assessment.
              priority: high
              deadlineOffsetDays: 30

# Adult ADHD Self-Management

This area is for adults who suspect or know they have ADHD, including knowledge workers whose jobs run on attention and memory. It starts with the foundations (gathering evidence, choosing an assessment route, using the wait, and setting up a capture inbox, one calendar and a launch pad by the door), then the routines that keep those systems alive, the skills of starting, estimating time and handling hyperfocus and big emotions, the decisions about medication, food, phone, workspace, driving and disclosure, the events of assessment day, travel, exams and a new job, the versions for students, parents, couples, women, late diagnosis and the self-employed, and finally co-occurring conditions, medication breaks and mentoring others.

What repeats is a daily three-item plan and a bedtime calendar glance, twice-weekly inbox sorting and medicine timing notes, a Sunday reset, weekly body doubling, clutter, movement and deep work planning, a monthly prescription request, statement scan, systems review and admin day, quarterly waiting list and review bookings, and a yearly review on your diagnosis anniversary. The Habit tracker, Metrics log, Reading queue, Operational checklist, Purchase decision, Trip, Onboarding 30/60/90 and Household chores templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
