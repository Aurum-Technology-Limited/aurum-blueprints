# Climate & Eco-Anxiety

This area is for anyone who lies awake doing climate arithmetic, freezes when a heatwave headline lands, or feels that caring this much and doing so little cannot both be true. It starts with the foundations (a distress check-in, your triggers, knowing when to get help, a calmer set of news sources and the science without the doom), then the routines that turn worry into steady action and connection, the emotional skills of naming, grounding and living with uncertainty, the decisions about your role, your money, your work and your household, the report weeks, summers and gatherings worth planning for, versions for parents, teenagers, students, couples, grandparents, disaster survivors and climate professionals, and finally the specialist work of activism that lasts, climate-aware therapy and holding space for others.

What repeats is a Sunday climate news window, a midweek feelings journal, a Saturday hour outdoors, a monthly action hour, climate cafe and good-news ledger, a quarterly distress re-check and a yearly look at what your action and your wellbeing both did. The Risk register, Metrics log, Habit tracker, Reading queue, Purchase decision and Meeting notes templates pair with the projects that name them.
