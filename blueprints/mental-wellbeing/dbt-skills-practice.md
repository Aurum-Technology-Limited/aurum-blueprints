              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Dialectical behaviour therapy was built for people whose emotions rise fast, hit hard and take a long time to come back down, and it is now used well beyond its original setting. Spending a couple of hours on what the four skills modules teach, and how a full programme differs from skills-only learning, lets you walk into a referral conversation knowing what to ask for.

                ## Milestones
                1. A one-page summary of the four modules: mindfulness, distress tolerance, emotion regulation and interpersonal effectiveness.
                2. The difference between comprehensive DBT, a skills-only group and self-guided workbook practice written in your own words.
                3. Three reasons DBT might fit your situation, and any doubts, noted.
                4. Your doctor or therapist's view on whether DBT is a sensible next step recorded.

                ## Notes
                DBT was first developed for people with chronic suicidal and self-harming behaviour. If that applies to you, self-guided practice is a supplement to professional care, never a replacement for it.

                Before you start: this plan is general information to help you look after your own wellbeing. It is not medical or psychological advice and does not replace a doctor, therapist or counsellor. If you are in crisis, in danger or thinking about harming yourself, contact your local emergency number or a crisis line now.

                ## Purpose
                Intake for a DBT programme usually covers your history, the behaviours you want to change and how ready you are to commit, often across one to three appointments.             - name: Target behaviours ranked in DBT order
              description: |-

                Standard DBT works on behaviours in a set order: anything life-threatening first, then whatever gets in the way of therapy, then problems that damage quality of life, with the skills you want more of running alongside.                 3. Two or three skills you want to use more of named for each level.
                4. The ranked list agreed with your therapist, if you have one, and copied onto the diary card.

                ## Notes

            - name: Chain analysis after problem behaviour
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Chain analysis traces a problem behaviour link by link: the vulnerability that left you raw, the prompting event, the thoughts, feelings and urges, the behaviour itself and what followed. Writing one within a day or two of each target behaviour, and reading them together monthly, shows exactly where a skill could have broken the chain.

                ## Milestones
                1. A blank chain analysis worksheet ready in the binder and on your phone.
                2. Every target behaviour followed by a written chain within 48 hours.
                3. Each chain marked with at least two points where a skill could have been used.
                4. A monthly read across chains for repeated links.
                5. One repeated link turned into a specific prevention plan.

                ## Notes
                Write chains in plain, non-judgemental language. The point is to understand what happened, not to build a case against yourself.
              priority: high
              frontmatter:
                mode: operating
                output_kind: habit
                success_criteria: "Every target behaviour over three months has a written chain within two days, and one repeated link has a prevention plan."
                cadence: rolling
              tasks:
                - "Save a blank chain analysis worksheet to your phone"
                - "Write a chain within 48 hours of any target behaviour"
                - "Mark two links on each chain where a skill could have helped"
                - "Read the month's chains together and note links that repeat @recurring(monthly:12)"

# DBT Skills Practice

This area is for anyone learning dialectical behaviour therapy skills, whether in a full programme, a skills-only group or from a workbook while waiting for a place. It starts with understanding DBT, finding a programme, ranking your targets and setting up diary cards and a survival kit, then builds the weekly machinery, works through the mindfulness, distress tolerance, emotion regulation and interpersonal skills one at a time, and ends with plans for parents, families, work, study and life after the programme.

What repeats is a nightly diary card and a few minutes of mindfulness, a weekly skill, homework slot and PLEASE check, monthly chain analysis reviews and skill tallies, and a quarterly look at progress against your targets. The Habit tracker, Course, Metrics log and Purchase decision templates pair with it. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
