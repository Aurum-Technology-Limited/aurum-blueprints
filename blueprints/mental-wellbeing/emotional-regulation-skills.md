# Emotional Regulation Skills

This area is for anyone whose feelings sometimes run the show: the person who snaps and regrets it, goes silent for days, cries in meetings or sends the message they wish they had not. It starts with foundations (a baseline questionnaire, an emotion map, a reaction diary and a pocket card of skills), moves through the weekly machinery of check-ins and reviews, then the core skills such as labelling, reappraisal, urge surfing and acceptance, followed by practical changes, hard events to prepare for, plans for parents, couples, carers, students and managers, and finally the specialist work of keeping old patterns from returning.

What repeats is a brief check-in each day, a Friday review and a Sunday look at the week ahead, twice-weekly practice while calm, a monthly skills audit and a quarterly retake of the baseline questionnaire. The Metrics log, Habit tracker, Skills matrix, Reading queue and Purchase decision templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
