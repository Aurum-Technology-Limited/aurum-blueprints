# Gratitude & Positive Psychology

This area is for anyone who wants a steadier, more hopeful outlook built from exercises that have actually been tested, rather than slogans on a mug. It starts with foundations (baseline scores, realistic expectations, a journal, a first week of three good things and your signature strengths), then the routines that keep gratitude, savouring and kindness going, the skills behind them, decisions about what to change, events such as a gratitude visit, versions for families, students, couples, teams and later life, and finally the experiments and teaching that experienced practitioners take on.

What repeats is a Sunday gratitude list, a short daily savouring pause, a monthly thank-you message and practice review, a weekly kindness day and a weekly new use of a strength, and a quarterly retake of your scores with a fresh exercise rotated in. The Habit tracker, Reading queue, Metrics log and Course templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
