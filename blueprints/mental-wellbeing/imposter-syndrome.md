            - name: Spotting the overpreparation cycle

                Clance's imposter cycle runs like this: a task arrives, anxiety rises, you overprepare, it goes well, and the success gets credited to the extra effort rather than your ability. # Imposter Syndrome

This area is for knowledge workers, students and founders who quietly suspect they are less capable than others think and are waiting to be found out. It starts with foundations (a trigger map, an evidence file, the feedback you already hold and an honest check on real skills gaps), then the weekly and monthly habits that keep that record current, the skills of reframing and describing your work plainly, decisions about stretch roles and support, high-stakes events such as reviews, vivas and investor pitches, situations like a first PhD year or a first CEO role, and finally mentoring others and taking on expert roles yourself.

What repeats is a Friday wins log, a monthly evidence file update and peer check-in, quarterly feedback requests and self-reviews, and a yearly retrospective that sets the year's fears beside what happened. The Feedback themes, Development plan, Job application, 1:1, Onboarding 30/60/90 and Portfolio piece templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
