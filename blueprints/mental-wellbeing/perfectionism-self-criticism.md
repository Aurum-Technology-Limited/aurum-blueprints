            - name: Seven-day shortfall and reaction log
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                The damage done by perfectionism lives less in the standard itself and more in what happens when you miss it: the late-night redo, the third proofread, the silent verdict that you are useless. Logging every noticeable shortfall for one week, with what you did and said to yourself next, turns a vague feeling into a record of your actual reactions, which is what the later experiments are designed to change.

                ## Milestones

                2. At least ten entries recorded across seven days, including small ones.
                3. Reactions grouped into checking, redoing, avoiding, overworking and self-criticism.
                4. The most common reaction and the most common thought written at the bottom of the log.

                ## Notes
                Write entries as soon as you can after the event. Reactions remembered at the end of the week tend to be tidied up.

# Perfectionism & Self-Criticism

Perfectionism is not the same as high standards: it is the habit of judging your whole worth on whether you hit standards that keep moving, then paying for every shortfall with overwork, checking, avoidance or a harsh inner verdict. This area is for anyone who recognises that pattern, with particular attention to students and knowledge workers, and it runs from foundations (a questionnaire baseline, a map of where perfectionism shows up, a costs and benefits ledger and good-enough definitions for your core tasks) through the weekly routines that loosen it, the skills of behavioural experiments and continuum thinking, specific changes and decisions, dated events such as results days and performance reviews, versions for students, managers, new starters and parents, and finally structured treatment and the standards you place on other people.

What repeats is a weekly good-enough experiment, an evening done list, a Friday fair self-evaluation, time-box and checking tallies, a monthly standards audit and mistakes ledger review, and a quarterly re-score of the questionnaire you started with.
