                Before changing how you talk to yourself, it helps to know where you are starting.                 4. A reminder set to retake the same form in three months.

            - name: One-week inner critic audit
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Most self-criticism runs so automatically that people underestimate how often it happens and how harsh it sounds. Writing down the exact words your inner critic uses for seven days, along with what triggered them, turns a vague sense of being hard on yourself into a record you can actually work with. Many people find the same five or six sentences account for most of it.

                ## Milestones
                1. Seven days of critic sentences captured in their exact wording, with time and trigger.
                2. The entries sorted into themes such as competence, appearance, worth or relationships.
                3. The five sentences that came up most often listed in order.
                4. One line written on what the critic seems to be trying to achieve or prevent.

                ## Notes
                Capture the words as they arrive, even if they seem petty or cruel. Softening them on paper hides the very thing you are trying to change.
              priority: high
              deadlineOffsetDays: 21
              frontmatter:
                mode: building
                output_kind: artifact
                success_criteria: "A seven-day log of verbatim critic sentences exists, sorted into themes, with the five most frequent sentences ranked."
                cadence: one-shot
                effort_hours_estimate: "3"
              tasks:
                - "Set up a phone note called critic log with columns for time, trigger and words"
                - "Write down each self-critical sentence in its exact wording for seven days"
                - "Sort the week's entries into three to five themes"
                - "Rank the five sentences that appeared most often"
            - name: Friend versus self response comparison
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Ask most people how they would speak to a close friend who had just failed an exam or lost a job, then how they speak to themselves in the same spot, and the gap is striking. Writing both responses side by side for three real situations from your own life shows the difference in tone, words and body language, and gives you a ready model of the kinder voice you already know how to use.

                ## Milestones
                1. Three recent situations chosen where you struggled, failed or felt inadequate.
                2. For each, a written account of what you would say and do for a close friend in the same position.
                3. For each, a written account of what you actually said to yourself.
                4. A short list of the specific words and tones that differ between the two.

                ## Notes
                This is the opening exercise of most self-compassion programmes for a reason. If you cannot imagine speaking kindly to yourself yet, imagining a friend is the bridge.

# Self-Compassion Practice

This area is for anyone whose inner voice is harsher than they would ever be with a friend, and who wants to change that with practice rather than willpower. It starts with foundations such as a baseline score, a critic audit, your own phrases and a daily slot, moves into the routines that keep practice going, then skills like compassionate letters, imagery and meeting shame, improvements such as self-forgiveness and dropping workplace put-downs, dated events like a course or a home retreat, versions for parents, carers, students, workers and later life, and finally specialist work on fears of compassion, therapy and leading a circle.

What repeats is a daily self-compassion break, twice-weekly critic tallies, a Sunday journal entry, a Friday mistake debrief and a Wednesday meditation, then a monthly practice review and partner call, a quarterly re-score and a yearly letter from your compassionate self. The Habit tracker, Metrics log, Reading queue, Purchase decision and Course templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
