            - name: Graded exposure ladder of 15 situations
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Fifteen specific situations, ranked from about 20 to 90 on a 0 to 100 anxiety scale, turn vague dread into a sequence of steps. Each rung should be hard enough to teach you something and easy enough to actually attempt this week. Specific rungs, like asking a stranger for the time at a bus stop, can be practised; vague ones like talking to people more cannot.

                ## Milestones
                1. Fifteen situations listed, each specific enough to do on a set day.
                2. Every rung rated 0 to 100 and ordered from lowest to highest.
                3. Variations noted for each rung, such as longer, busier or with someone you do not know.
                4. A first rung rated around 30 to 40 chosen for this week.

                ## Notes
                Starting too low wastes weeks; starting too high often ends in escape, which teaches the opposite lesson. If you are working with a therapist, build the ladder together.

# Social Anxiety Exposure Work

This area is for anyone whose fear of being judged, embarrassed or found boring keeps shrinking their life, and for students facing seminars, presentations and a whole new social world at once. It starts with the foundations (a map of what you avoid, a baseline score, your safety behaviours and a graded exposure ladder), then the weekly practice that does the real work, the skills of testing predictions and shifting attention outward, a run of situation-specific exposures, decisions about alcohol and invitations, dated events to prepare for, projects for students, parents and people rebuilding after long avoidance, and finally the advanced work of deliberate mishaps, leading a room and keeping the gains.

What repeats is three planned exposures a week, one small social act each day, a Sunday scan of the week's events, a monthly re-rating of the ladder, a monthly progress review and a quarterly retest of your questionnaire score. The Metrics log, Habit tracker and Reading queue templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
