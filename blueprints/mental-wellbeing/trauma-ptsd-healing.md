            - name: Safety check before starting trauma work
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Trauma-focused therapy works best once the danger is over, and most therapists will ask first whether you are still in contact with the person who harmed you or living somewhere unsafe. If the threat is ongoing, getting specialist support for practical safety comes before processing memories, and naming that honestly saves weeks of stalled sessions.

                ## Milestones
                1. An honest written answer to one question: is anyone or anything still putting me in danger?
                2. If the answer is yes, a specialist service such as a domestic abuse or victim support line contacted.
                3. Practical safety steps agreed with that service and written down.
                4. Your therapist or doctor told about any ongoing risk.

                ## Notes
                If you are in immediate danger, call your local emergency number. If you are thinking about harming yourself, use your crisis safety plan or a crisis line now; that comes before anything in this area.

# Trauma & PTSD Healing

This area is for anyone living with the after-effects of a traumatic event, whether it happened last month or thirty years ago, and for the people close to them. It starts with the foundations (a symptom picture, an assessment, a safety check, a grounding kit and the right trauma-focused therapy), then the weekly and monthly routines that track triggers, nightmares and progress, the skills that make flashbacks and dissociation manageable, the decisions about reporting, medication and disclosure, the dates worth preparing for, the situations of parents, partners, drivers, responders, veterans and patients, and finally the longer work of complex trauma, intensive programmes and staying well.

What repeats is a few minutes of grounding each day, a weekly read of the trigger log and window of tolerance, a monthly symptom score and avoidance audit, a quarterly progress review with your therapist and a yearly look at the hard dates ahead. The Reading queue, Metrics log, Habit tracker, Sleep review and Purchase decision templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
