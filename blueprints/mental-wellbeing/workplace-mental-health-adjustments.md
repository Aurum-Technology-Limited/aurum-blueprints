# Workplace Mental Health Adjustments

This area is for anyone holding down a job while managing a mental health condition, from office and remote knowledge workers to shift workers, managers and freelancers. It starts with the foundations (mapping how the condition affects your work, the disclosure decision, a wellness action plan and a written adjustment request), then the routines that keep agreements alive, the skills and options worth knowing, the meetings and returns you prepare for, situations such as a new job or a hospital stay, and finally grievances, records requests and exit decisions.

The rhythms are a Monday capacity plan, a Friday trigger log, a monthly check-in with your manager, monthly absence and appointment records, and a quarterly review of every adjustment, with the wellness action plan and relapse plan refreshed once a year. The 1:1, Metrics log, Meeting notes and Onboarding 30/60/90 templates pair with the check-ins, the trigger log, the key meetings and a new start. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
