# Worry Time & Rumination Control

This area is for anyone whose mind keeps circling: the what-ifs about tomorrow, the replays of yesterday, the decision that never quite gets made. It starts with the foundations (a two-week log, your worry themes, a fixed worry slot and a capture note), then the daily and weekly routines that contain worry, the skills borrowed from CBT and metacognitive therapy, the decisions about habits that feed overthinking, the events that make it spike, versions for parents, carers, students and people with quiet days, and finally the specialist therapy routes.

What repeats is a short daily worry session and two rumination check-ins, a weekly sort into practical and hypothetical worries followed by a problem-solving hour, a monthly check of predictions and worry minutes, and a quarterly refresh of your worry themes. The Metrics log, Habit tracker, Reading queue and Purchase decision templates pair with the projects that name them.
