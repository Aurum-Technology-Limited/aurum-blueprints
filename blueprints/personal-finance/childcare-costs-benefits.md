# Childcare Costs & Benefits

This area is for parents paying for nursery, a childminder, a nanny, breakfast and after-school clubs or holiday care, and for anyone about to start. It opens with the foundations (a map of the care each child needs, local prices, what support you qualify for, when funded hours begin and whether extra working hours pay once care is costed), then the monthly and termly routines that keep claims live and invoices right, the schemes worth understanding properly, the decisions between care types and support schemes, the dated moments such as returning from leave and starting school, versions for single, separated, self-employed, student and shift-working parents and for disabled children, and finally the specialist work of employing a nanny and challenging a wrong decision.

What repeats is a monthly top-up of any government childcare account, a monthly invoice check and benefit report, a holiday pot transfer, a three-monthly eligibility reconfirmation, a termly booking round and a yearly fee and tax-claim review. The Monthly budget and bills, Metrics log, Savings goal and Purchase decision templates pair with the projects that name them.
