# Christmas & Gift Spending Plan

This area is for anyone whose December, or whose run of family birthdays, keeps costing more than planned and quietly lands on a credit card in January. It starts with the foundations (last year's real cost, a tiered recipient list, a total and a per-person amount, a household agreement and a pot that fills itself), then the routines that keep saving and spending on plan all year, the skills of judging discounts, returns and cheaper meaningful gifts, the decisions about Secret Santa, credit and where to save, the dated events from Black Friday to the January returns, versions for parents, separated families, couples and lean years, and finally the specialist work of multi-year forecasts and family gift rules.

What repeats is a monthly pot check, a gift idea review and a birthday look-ahead each month, a running spend total in the season, a quarterly look at the receipts folder and the party present stash, and a yearly January review that sets next year's numbers. The Savings goal, Purchase decision, Trip and Weekly meal plan templates pair with the projects that name them.
