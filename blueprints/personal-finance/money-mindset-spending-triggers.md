            - name: Money beliefs inventory
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Beliefs such as "money is meant to be enjoyed", "rich people are greedy" or "I am bad with money" quietly steer hundreds of small decisions. Researchers group these into patterns like money avoidance, money status and money vigilance, and scoring yourself against them gives you language for what drives you and a baseline to revisit.

                ## Milestones
                1. A list of at least fifteen statements about money you hold, written in your own words.
                2. Each statement sorted into a pattern such as avoidance, worship, status or vigilance.
                3. The two strongest patterns identified with a real example of each from the last month.
                4. The inventory dated and saved so it can be repeated in a year.

                ## Notes
                Published money script questionnaires exist and are a good starting point. Treat the result as a mirror, not a diagnosis.

# Money Mindset & Spending Triggers

This area is for anyone whose spending follows their mood more than their plan: the late-night basket after a hard day, the payday splurge, the round of drinks you could not afford to refuse. It starts with foundations that show you what is really going on (a thirty-day trigger diary, your money story and beliefs, a regret audit, a values statement and the friction and pause rules that slow things down), then the routines that keep you steady, the skills of spotting sales tactics and riding out an urge, the changes and decisions that follow, the dated moments that test you, versions for parents, couples, students, retirees and people with ADHD, and finally the deeper work of compulsive buying, hidden spending and helping someone else.

What repeats is a daily balance glance, a shopping curfew, a Sunday check-in, a monthly look at the trigger log and the wishlist, a quarterly feed audit and planned treat, and a yearly re-run of the beliefs inventory. The Metrics log, Habit tracker, Reading queue, Purchase decision and Meeting notes templates pair with the projects that name them.
