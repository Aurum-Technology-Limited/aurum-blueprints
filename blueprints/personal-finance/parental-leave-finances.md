                ## Purpose
                Statutory pay is usually calculated from earnings in a short, fixed window before the birth, so a pay rise, bonus or unpaid week in that window can change the amount for months. # Parental Leave Finances

This area is for parents expecting a baby or an adoption who need the household to keep running while one or both of you earn less for months. It starts with the foundations (what you will be paid and when, the notice deadlines that protect that pay, the new costs of a baby and the size of the gap), then the routines that run through the leave year, the rules worth understanding properly, the decisions about when to start, how long to take and how to split leave, the dated moments from the last full payday to the first one back, versions for self-employed, single, adoptive and new-job parents and for twins or neonatal stays, and finally the specialist work of negotiating with an employer and handling clawback, salary sacrifice and the leave-year tax return.

What repeats is a monthly budget check-in, a payslip check on every leave payday, a monthly transfer from the leave fund, a weekly tally of baby spending, a monthly couple money meeting and a quarterly look at pension and paused savings.
