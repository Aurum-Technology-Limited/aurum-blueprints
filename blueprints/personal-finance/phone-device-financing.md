                4. The month each device share ends noted beside the line.

# Phone & Device Financing

This area is for anyone paying for a phone, tablet, smartwatch or laptop in monthly instalments, or about to choose how to pay for the next one. It starts with the foundations (every device agreement in one list, the handset and airtime split on each bill, the real cost of your current phone, a needs list and a yearly ceiling), then the routines that keep instalments, savings and battery health in order, the knowledge that makes contracts and refurbished grades readable, the decisions between contract, SIM-only and outright purchase, the events of upgrade day, launches and lost phones, the situations of parents, students, families, older relatives and the self-employed, and finally the specialist work of cost models, replacement schedules, early exits and disputes.

What repeats is a monthly instalment check, a monthly transfer into the replacement pot, a price watch and spending log, a quarterly battery check, and a yearly review of every device in the house. The Monthly budget and bills, Savings goal, Metrics log, Purchase decision and Operational checklist templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
