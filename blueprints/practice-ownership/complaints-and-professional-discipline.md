            - name: Written complaints procedure for the practice
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Most professional regulators expect a practice to have a written complaints procedure, and many inspections ask to see it alongside two or three recent cases. A procedure of two or three pages that says who handles complaints, how fast you reply, how you investigate and where the complainant can go next turns an upsetting surprise into a known process for staff and clients alike.

                ## Milestones
                1. The regulator's published expectations for complaints handling read and noted for your profession.
                2. A two to three page procedure covering receipt, acknowledgement, investigation, reply and escalation.
                3. The procedure approved by the owners and dated, with a named version number.
                4. Every member of staff told where the procedure lives and who to pass complaints to.

                ## Notes
                Keep the procedure short enough that a receptionist can follow it on a busy day. The detail belongs in the log and the case file, not the policy.
              priority: high
              deadlineOffsetDays: 30
              frontmatter:
                mode: building
                output_kind: artifact
                success_criteria: "A dated, owner-approved complaints procedure exists that names the lead, the reply deadlines and the escalation route, and staff know where to find it."
                cadence: one-shot
                effort_hours_estimate: "6"
              tasks:
                - "Find your regulator's guidance or standard on complaints handling"
                - "Draft the procedure under five headings: receive, acknowledge, investigate, reply, escalate"
                - "Ask the owners to approve and date the procedure at the next meeting"
                - "Send the procedure to all staff with a one-line summary of who to tell"

# Complaints and Professional Discipline

This area is for the owner who has to answer when a patient or client is unhappy, whether that is a grumble at reception, a formal letter, a complaint to the regulator or a summons to a discipline hearing. It starts with the foundations (a written procedure, a named lead, a log, response deadlines and a map of where unresolved complaints go), then the weekly, monthly and yearly machinery that keeps cases moving and lessons visible, the skills of investigating fairly and writing replies that settle things, decisions about refunds, referrals and unreasonable behaviour, the regulator notice, investigation and hearing themselves, situations such as a complaint about the owner or an inherited caseload, and finally specialist work on parallel processes, sanctions and long-run patterns.

What repeats is a weekly review of open complaints, a monthly reconciliation of the log with themes and learning actions, a quarterly report to the owners and a yearly review of the procedure, the published summary and staff training. The Meeting notes, Remediation plan, Metrics log and Purchase decision templates pair with the projects that keep those records.
