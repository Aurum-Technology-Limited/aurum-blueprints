            - name: Second-site readiness test
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Opening a second location doubles the overheads long before it doubles the income, and many owners expand because the first site is busy rather than because it runs without them. A short readiness test against waiting lists, margins, management depth and your own capacity tells you whether to expand now, in a year, or not at all.

                ## Milestones
                1. Twelve months of demand evidence gathered: waiting times, turned-away enquiries and referral volumes.
                2. The founding site's profit margin and cash position compared with what a second site would need.
                3. Each of the five readiness questions answered yes, no or not yet, with the evidence noted.
                4. A written go, wait or stop decision with the date you will test again.

                ## Notes
                A full diary is not the same as surplus demand. Check how many enquiries you actually turn away, and from which postcodes or districts.
              priority: high
              deadlineOffsetDays: 30
              frontmatter:
                mode: research

                - "Pull twelve months of profit and cash figures for the founding site"

            - name: Founding site playbook for replication
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Everything that makes the first site work tends to live in the owner's head and the habits of two long-serving staff. Writing it down as a playbook (opening routine, booking rules, intake, billing, complaints, cleaning, closing) is what lets a second team run the same practice without you standing over them.

                ## Milestones
                1. A contents list of the twenty routines a site must run from day one.
                2. Each routine written as a one-page procedure with an owner role, not a named person.
                3. The procedures tested by a member of staff who did not write them.
                4. Gaps found in testing fixed and the playbook version dated.

                ## Notes
                Ask staff to time and narrate a real shift. The procedure is what they actually do, not what the policy file says they do.
              priority: high
              frontmatter:
                mode: building
                output_kind: artifact
                success_criteria: "A dated playbook of at least twenty one-page procedures has been followed successfully by a staff member who did not write it."

              tasks:

                - "Ask the agent to turn your voice notes on one routine into a draft procedure"

# Group Practice and Multi-Site Growth

This area is for the owner of a clinic, firm or studio who is ready to run more than one location, or already does and feels the strain. It starts with foundations (a readiness test, a written playbook of the founding site, the growth model, capital, structure, registration and site selection), then the operating rhythm of a group, the management skills that change when you stop being in every room, the decisions about a central team, shared systems and site leads, the dated openings and integrations, the situations of a solo founder, co-founders, distant sites and closures, and finally specialist work on governance, benchmarking, lenders and joint ventures.

What repeats is a weekly group huddle and cover rota, monthly site reviews, cost centre and intercompany checks, feedback comparisons and site lead one-to-ones, quarterly standards audits, purchasing reviews and site lead meetings, and a yearly strategy day with lease and handbook reviews. The Risk register, Metrics log, Vendor, Board meeting, Onboarding 30/60/90 and Operational checklist templates pair with the projects that keep those records.
