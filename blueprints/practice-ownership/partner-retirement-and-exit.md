# Partner Retirement and Exit

This area is for anyone involved when a partner leaves a practice: the retiring partner planning the last few years, the continuing partners who must fund and absorb the departure, and the practice manager who keeps the paperwork straight. It starts with foundations (the exit clauses in your agreement, the capital account, the notice date, advisers, the client list, indemnity run-off and regulator changes), then the recurring machinery of trackers, payment ledgers and covenant checks, the knowledge that makes negotiations shorter, the decisions on funding, goodwill, payment terms and consultancy roles, the dated events from formal notice to completion day, the harder situations of ill health, death, expulsion and two-partner firms, and finally disputed exits and legacy annuity liabilities.

What repeats is a monthly exit tracker meeting, a weekly client handover log during the transition, monthly buyout payments on both the paying and receiving side, monthly checks on the departing partner's unbilled work and debtors, quarterly covenant and exit horizon reviews and a yearly look at the exit provisions themselves. The Meeting notes, Metrics log, Operational checklist and Risk register templates pair with the projects that keep those records.
