              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Every regulator draws the advertising line in a different place: some ban testimonials outright, some restrict the word specialist, some forbid unsolicited approaches or fee comparisons, and many lawyers in smaller jurisdictions still work under rules far tighter than their clients expect. Reading the current code and guidance once and reducing it to a single page of dos and don'ts gives every later marketing decision a reference point you can show staff, agencies and, if it ever comes to it, the regulator.

                ## Milestones

                2. One page lists what is required, what is banned and what needs care, each line with its clause reference.

                4. Open questions sent to the regulator's helpline or your professional body, with the answers recorded.

                ## Notes
