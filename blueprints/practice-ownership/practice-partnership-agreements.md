# Practice Partnership Agreements

This area is for the owners of a professional practice who share it with at least one other person, or are about to, and need the arrangement written down properly: doctors, dentists, lawyers, accountants, vets, engineers and architects alike. It starts with the foundations (finding out what already governs you, choosing the vehicle, instructing a lawyer, checking the regulator's ownership rules and agreeing heads of terms), moves through the operating routines that keep capital, drawings and resolutions in order, the knowledge every partner should have before signing, the clauses that need real decisions, the signing and notification events, the situations that change what the agreement must cover, and finally specialist work such as mergers and partners licensed in more than one country.

What repeats is a monthly partners' meeting with a resolution log, monthly drawings and tax reserve checks, a quarterly capital account reconciliation and outside interests declaration, and a yearly agreement health check, insurance review, year-end profit allocation and exit formula refresh.
