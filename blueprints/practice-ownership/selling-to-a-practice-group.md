# Selling to a Practice Group

This area is for the practice owner who has been approached by, or is thinking of approaching, a dental or veterinary group, a private equity backed platform, a hospital system or an accounting roll-up, and wants to decide with open eyes. It starts with foundations (why you would sell, the ownership rules of your profession, partner alignment, confidentiality, advisers and your walk-away terms), then the knowledge that makes group offers readable, the decisions on structure, autonomy and your employment contract, the dated events from first meeting to completion day, the routines that run during and after the deal, the situations of near-retirement, mid-career, divided-partner and island-market owners, and finally specialist work on minority stakes, the platform's own resale and life after the group.

What repeats is a weekly deal tracker while diligence runs, a monthly performance pack that protects the price, monthly earn-out, autonomy and staff check-ins after completion, quarterly rollover statements and market watch, and a yearly review of your employment contract and covenants.
