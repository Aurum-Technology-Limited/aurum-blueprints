            - name: Hundred-day plan charter and scope
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Before the first workstream starts, the deal team and management need to agree what the hundred days are for: stabilising the business, landing the governance the new owner needs and starting two or three value levers, not delivering the whole investment case. A one-page charter with objectives, owners and what is deliberately out of scope stops the plan swelling into 200 actions that nobody finishes.

                ## Milestones
                1. A one-page charter naming three to five objectives to be met by day 100.
                2. An out-of-scope list agreed with the CEO and the deal partner.
                3. A deal team owner and a management owner named against each objective.
                4. The charter signed off before completion or in the first week after it.

                ## Notes
                Keep the charter separate from the value creation plan. The hundred days set up the machinery and the early fixes; the value creation plan runs for the whole hold.
              priority: high
              deadlineOffsetDays: 14
              frontmatter:
                mode: building
                output_kind: artifact
                success_criteria: "A signed one-page charter with objectives, owners and an out-of-scope list exists within two weeks of completion."
                cadence: one-shot
                effort_hours_estimate: "6"
              tasks:
                - "Write the three outcomes the deal partner expects by day 100 on one page"
                - "List the items that will deliberately wait until after day 100"
                - "Name a deal team owner and a management owner for each objective"
                - "Get the CEO and the deal partner to sign off the charter"
            - name: Diligence findings turned into day-one actions
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Diligence reports flag dozens of issues, from a slow month-end close to an expiring customer contract, and most are forgotten once the deal signs. Converting every finding in the adviser reports and the IC memo into an owned action, a parked item or a consciously accepted risk means the plan starts from what the firm already paid to learn.

                ## Milestones
                1. The final financial, commercial, legal, tax and any technical diligence reports gathered in one place with the approved IC memo.
                2. Every finding listed once in a single tracker, with duplicates across reports merged.
                3. Each finding marked act now, park until after day 100, or accept, with a reason.
                4. Every act-now item carrying an owner and a target week.

                ## Notes
                Ask each adviser which three findings they would fix first if it were their money. Their answer is often sharper than the report.
              priority: high

            - name: Sign-to-close preparation within the rules
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                The weeks between signing and completion are the best time to prepare, but the buyer must not start running the business before it owns it, and merger control rules can make that a serious problem. Agreeing with deal counsel what preparation is allowed lets you draft the plan, the communications and the first board papers without crossing that line.

                ## Notes

# 100-Day Plans

This area is for the deal professionals and portfolio company managers who carry a business through its first hundred days under new ownership, from the associate holding the tracker to the CEO and CFO doing the work. It starts with the foundations (the charter, diligence findings turned into actions, sign-to-close preparation, day-one communications and changeovers, authority and governance, and an early risk register), then the weekly and monthly machinery, the skills the period demands, the quick wins and finance upgrades, the dated events from the town hall to the day-100 review, the variants for founders, carve-outs, turnarounds and new leaders, and finally the specialist work of minority, cross-border and firm-wide playbooks.

What repeats is a weekly programme meeting, deal partner check-in, thirteen-week cash update and customer watch list, a monthly reporting pack, close review, pulse survey and lender calendar check, quarterly access reviews and compliance certificates, and a yearly refresh of the firm's playbook. The Operational checklist, Risk register, Meeting notes, Month-end close, Remediation plan and Onboarding 30/60/90 templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
