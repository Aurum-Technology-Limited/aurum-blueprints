                A roll-up without a written strategy tends to become a collection of whatever owners agreed to sell. # Add-On Acquisitions

This area is for the deal professional running add-ons for a platform company, whether you are the associate doing the work or the partner who answers for the programme. It starts with the foundations (a buy-and-build strategy, criteria, a target map, a readiness check on the platform and the add-on room in the credit agreement), moves into the operating rhythm of pipeline calls, owner outreach and integration tracking, then the skills of owner conversations, earn-outs and synergies estimates, the decisions about brands, systems and funding, the dated moments from first add-on to day one and earn-out settlement, situations for different roles, and finally the specialist work of cross-border add-ons, carve-outs, mergers of equals and roll-ups in licensed sectors.

What repeats is a weekly pipeline call with the platform CEO, monthly owner outreach and a blended multiple ledger updated after every close, monthly integration and covenant headroom reviews, a quarterly target refresh and synergies check, and a yearly buy-and-build review with the board. The Meeting notes, Risk register, Board meeting, Reading queue and Operational checklist templates pair with the projects that name them. Keep target names and live deal terms inside your firm's systems; these projects hold your own working method, not a firm's confidential data.
