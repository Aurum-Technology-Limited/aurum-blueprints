# Breaking Into Venture Capital

This area is for anyone trying to land a first venture capital job, whether you are a student, a banker or consultant, an operator at a startup or a founder deciding what comes next. It starts with foundations (how venture roles really work, an honest read of your route, a target fund list, your story and CV), moves into the weekly machinery of reading, writing, founder conversations and partner networking, then the skills interviewers test, the decisions about MBAs, operating roles and small cheques, the applications and interviews themselves, variants for students, career changers and smaller markets, and finally the specialist work that makes a candidate hard to ignore.

What repeats is a weekly reading and teardown habit, Tuesday networking and Thursday founder calls, a Monday application review, monthly writing and fund raise checks, and a quarterly look at whether the search is working. The Resume, Job application, Reading queue, Cover letter and Onboarding 30/60/90 templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
