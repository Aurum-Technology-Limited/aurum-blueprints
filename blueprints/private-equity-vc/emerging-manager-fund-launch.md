            - name: Fund launch go or no-go assessment
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Most first funds take 18 to 24 months to reach a first close, and many never do, so the decision to launch deserves the same rigour you would give a deal. Scoring yourself honestly on track record, a differentiated strategy, a team, anchor interest and personal runway before resigning or committing legal fees turns a hope into a dated decision you can defend to a spouse, a co-founder and an LP.

                ## Milestones
                1. A scorecard covering track record, strategy, team, anchor interest, runway and timing, each rated with evidence.
                2. Five experienced GPs or LPs asked for a candid view of the scorecard.
                3. A written go, no-go or not-yet decision with the conditions that would change it.
                4. A date fixed for revisiting the decision if the answer is not yet.

                ## Notes
                Weak anchor interest is the most common reason first funds stall. If no one has said they would seriously consider a cheque, treat that as a not-yet, not a go.
              priority: high
              deadlineOffsetDays: 30
              frontmatter:
                mode: research

              tasks:

            - name: One-page first fund strategy
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                LPs see hundreds of first-time managers a year and decide within minutes whether a strategy is distinct, repeatable and sized to the opportunity. A single page that states the target companies, cheque size, ownership or control stance, sourcing edge, value creation approach and expected number of deals is the document every later piece, from the PPM to the pitch, is built from.

                ## Milestones
                1. A one-page strategy naming sector, geography, company size, cheque size, deal count and holding period.
                2. A sourcing edge and a value creation approach each stated in two sentences, with evidence from past deals.
                3. The page tested on three LPs or advisers and revised after their questions.
                4. A final version dated and stored as the master that the PPM and pitch must match.

                ## Notes
                If the strategy needs more than a page, it is usually two strategies. Pick the one your track record proves.

              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                A first fund is raised on a record built somewhere else, and LPs will ask what you personally did on each deal, who can confirm it and whether you are allowed to show it. # Emerging Manager Fund Launch

This area is for the founder raising a first fund: the deal partner leaving an established firm, the independent sponsor with a string of deals, the operator turned investor, the solo GP.
