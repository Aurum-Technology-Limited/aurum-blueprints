# Fund Terms and LPA Negotiation

This area is for the general partner negotiating the terms of their own fund with limited partners, whether it is a first fund with one anchor or a Fund II with a crowded data room. It starts with foundations (the anatomy of an LPA, a market benchmark, your term sheet, walk-away lines, a counsel brief and an ILPA gap review), moves into the operating machinery of comment trackers, side letter registers, concession logs and post-close obligations, then the skills of reading markups and holding a position across the table, the decisions on fees, carry headlines, term, recycling and expenses, dated moments such as the first close sign-off and the MFN election round, situations for different kinds of manager and LP, and finally specialist work on GP stakes, co-investment rights and excuse provisions.

What repeats is a weekly comment tracker review and a weekly call with fund counsel during the raise, a monthly concession tally and side letter obligations check, quarterly key person, fee offset and market terms reviews, and a yearly look at the obligations you signed up to. The Reading queue, Risk register, Meeting notes and Compliance control templates pair with the projects that name them. Nothing here is legal or tax advice: fund counsel and tax advisers make those calls, and these projects keep you prepared, consistent and on record.
