# Growth Equity Investing

This area is for the growth equity investor, from the associate building the cohort analysis to the partner negotiating the preference stack, who backs profitable or nearly profitable companies without taking control. It starts with foundations (criteria, a standard minority term sheet, a protective provisions checklist, a diligence workplan, a valuation framework and a minority returns model), moves into the operating rhythm of founder outreach, information rights, consent requests and comparables, then the skills of retention, unit economics, preferences and anti-dilution, the decisions about primary and secondary mix, board seats and structure, the dated moments from term sheet to completion and a later IPO, situations for people arriving from buyouts or venture, and finally the specialist work of underperformance, deadlock and crossover rounds.

What repeats is a weekly founder outreach slot, monthly checks that information rights are being honoured, monthly relationship and market notes, a monthly read of founder updates, quarterly consent logs, comparables and returns reruns, and a yearly review of your criteria against what actually happened. The Meeting notes, Metrics log, Reading queue and Risk register templates pair with the projects that name them. Keep company names and live terms inside your firm's systems; these projects hold your own working method, not a firm's confidential data.
