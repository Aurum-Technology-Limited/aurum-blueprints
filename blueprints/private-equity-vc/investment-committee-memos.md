# Investment Committee Memos

This area is for deal professionals who write, lead or vote on investment committee memos, from the associate drafting a first one to the partner who has sat through hundreds. It starts with the foundations (your firm's memo standard, a library of past memos, the IC process, the thesis paragraph, the ask and a fact-check trail), then the routines that keep live memos moving, the writing skills each section needs, the choices about format and structure, the IC meetings themselves, the variants for different roles and deal types, and finally the specialist work of calibrating memos against what really happened.

What repeats is a weekly drafting block and partner redline during live deals, monthly upkeep of the memo library, exhibit bank and question log, a quarterly look-back comparing past memos with results, and a yearly refresh of the house standard. The Meeting notes, Risk register and Development plan templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
