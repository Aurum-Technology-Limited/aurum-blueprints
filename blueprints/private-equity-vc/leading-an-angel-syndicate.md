            - name: Syndicate thesis and deal criteria one-pager
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Backers join a syndicate for the lead's judgement in a particular corner of the market, and they leave when every deal looks like a different person chose it. A one-page thesis that names your stages, sectors, cheque range and what you will never bring them gives backers a reason to stay and gives you a quick way to say no to a deal that does not fit.

                ## Milestones
                1. Stages, sectors and geographies you lead in written in plain words.
                2. The edge you bring named in one sentence, such as operating years in a sector or a founder network.
                3. Three to five hard deal criteria and an explicit list of exclusions.
                4. The one-pager published on your syndicate page and pinned for new backers.

                ## Notes
                Keep it narrow at the start. A thesis that covers everything protects nobody, least of all you when a deal goes wrong.
              priority: high

# Leading an Angel Syndicate

This area is for the angel who leads deals and brings other people's money with them through a syndicate or a single-deal SPV, whether you are an operator with a day job, a founder who invests on the side or someone building a serious backer base. It starts with foundations (a thesis, a platform, carry terms, advisers, conflict rules and a founding backer list), moves into the machinery of pipeline reviews, deal launches, closings, backer updates and annual reporting, then the skills of term sheets, SPV economics and pitching to backers, decisions on allocations and co-leads, the events every lead eventually meets from a first launch to an exit or a wind-down, situations that shape how you run it, and specialist work on marks, concentration and post-mortems.

What repeats is a Monday pipeline review, a monthly backer update and metrics log, quarterly portfolio updates, marks and compliance checks, and a yearly cycle of annual reports, tax documents, a backer evening and a thesis refresh. The Purchase decision, Risk register, Operational checklist, Metrics log and Meeting notes templates pair with the projects that name them.
