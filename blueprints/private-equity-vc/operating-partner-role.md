            - name: Operating partner mandate and role charter
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Operating partner titles cover everything from a retired CEO who joins two boards to a full-time team running pricing projects, and confusion about which one you are is the most common reason the role disappoints both sides. A one-page charter agreed with the managing partners sets out your scope, the companies you cover, what you own versus advise on, and how your work will be judged.

                ## Milestones
                1. A one-page charter naming your scope across pre-deal work, portfolio support and interim roles.
                2. The companies you cover listed, with the depth of involvement agreed for each.
                3. Decision rights stated: what you can commission, approve or only recommend.
                4. Three to five measures of success for the first year agreed with the managing partners.
                5. The charter signed off and shared with the deal partner on each covered company.

                ## Notes
                Write down what is out of scope as well. Most friction comes from a deal partner assuming you will run something the managing partners never asked you to own.
              priority: high
              deadlineOffsetDays: 30
              frontmatter:
                mode: building
                output_kind: artifact
                success_criteria: "A signed one-page operating partner charter with scope, covered companies, decision rights and first-year measures, shared with every relevant deal partner."
                cadence: one-shot
                effort_hours_estimate: "6"
              tasks:
                - "Write down in five lines what you were told the role is when you were hired"
                - "List every portfolio company and mark your intended depth of involvement"
                - "Draft decision rights for commissioning advisers, hires and projects"
                - "Review the draft charter with the managing partners and record their changes"
                - "Send the agreed charter to each deal partner on your covered companies"

            - name: Compensation, carry and fee terms as an operating partner
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Pay for operating partners mixes salary or retainer, carried interest, co-investment rights and sometimes board or consulting fees from portfolio companies, and each piece sits in a different document. Knowing exactly what you are entitled to, under which vehicle and on what vesting and leaver terms, avoids surprises at the first distribution or on the day you leave.

                ## Milestones
                1. Salary or retainer, bonus, carry points, co-invest rights and any portfolio fees summarised in one table.
                2. Vesting, leaver and clawback terms for each element noted with the clause reference.
                3. Questions about tax treatment and personal liability listed for your own adviser.
                4. Any portfolio company fee arrangements confirmed as disclosed under the firm's policy.

                ## Notes
                This is a record, not advice. Take tax and legal questions on carry and co-investment to your own adviser rather than the firm's counsel, whose client is the firm.

# Operating Partner Role

This area is for the operating partner, head of portfolio operations or independent operating adviser, and for the portfolio executives who work alongside them. It starts with the foundations of the role (the charter, the engagement model with deal teams, pay terms, the maturity map, the first ninety days, fee allocation and conflicts), moves through the operating machinery of triage, CEO check-ins, forums, networks, the bench and group purchasing, then the skills of playbook writing, diagnostics, coaching and executive search, the decisions on team design and interim seats, the dated events, variants for different situations and finally the specialist work of attribution, benchmarking, automation and turnarounds.

What repeats is a Monday triage, a Thursday check on live deals, monthly CEO check-ins and CFO network calls, monthly time and engagement reconciliations, quarterly operating reviews, CEO forums and attribution updates, and a yearly contribution review and summit. The Onboarding 30/60/90, Skills matrix, 1:1, Meeting notes, Vendor and Metrics log templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
