# PE Associate Years

This area is for the private equity associate, usually two to three years out of banking, consulting or an operating role, who now carries the model, the diligence tracker and the late-night turn of the committee paper. It starts with the foundations (agreeing the role, a first ninety days, fund fluency, compliance rules and a working rhythm with your VP), moves through the weekly routines that keep live deals and portfolio duties in order, the execution skills associates are judged on, the decisions about staffing, pay, carry and sector teams, the dated moments such as a first committee, signing week and the annual review, variants for associates arriving from different backgrounds or living different lives, and ends with the senior associate work of leading a workstream, training analysts and executing take-privates and carve-outs.

What repeats is a Monday deal status note, a midweek diligence tracker review, a Thursday comments log, a Friday personal deal log, a Sunday capacity check, monthly portfolio reporting duty, network and self-review sessions, quarterly feedback requests and a yearly pay, carry and review cycle. The Onboarding 30/60/90, Habit tracker, Meeting notes, Skills matrix, Operational checklist and Development plan templates pair with the projects that name them. Keep the fund's and its targets' confidential data inside the firm's systems; what lives here is your own plan, your own notes and your own record of the work.
