            - name: Written promotion criteria from VP to partner
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Most firms say promotion rests on judgement and origination, but the tests that actually decide cases are rarely written: how many deals you have led, how much you sourced, whose votes count and whether LPs know your name. Writing down the criteria for VP, principal and partner at your firm, checked with two people promoted in the last three years, turns a vague hope into a target you can plan against.

                ## Milestones
                1. The firm's stated criteria for each senior title collected from the handbook, review forms or HR.
                2. Two recently promoted colleagues asked what actually decided their case.
                3. A one-page note splitting written criteria from unwritten ones for principal and partner.
                4. Each criterion marked green, amber or red against your own record.

                ## Notes
                The unwritten criteria often matter more than the form: whether you have been seen in front of LPs, whether you have argued against a deal and been right. Write them down without judging them.
              priority: high

# PE Career Progression to Partner

This area is for private equity investors working their way from VP towards principal and partner, and for the partners deciding whether their next step is a firm of their own. It starts with the foundations (the real promotion criteria, an honest audit of your record, a sponsor map, your carry letter and your leaving terms), moves through the operating rhythms of origination tracking, sponsor conversations, relationship upkeep and team coaching, then the senior skills of leading deals, presenting at committee and owning a CEO relationship, the decisions on carry, packages, lateral offers and spinning out, the promotion and admission events, variants for parents, lateral hires and people at founder-led firms, and finally the specialist work of a clean spin-out and running a firm.

What repeats is a monthly origination and relationship review, a quarterly conversation with your sponsor partner, a weekly coaching slot with your deal team, a Friday time check and a Sunday workload check, with a yearly review pack and personal capital plan. The Skills matrix, Person, 1:1, Development plan, Onboarding 30/60/90 and Habit tracker templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
