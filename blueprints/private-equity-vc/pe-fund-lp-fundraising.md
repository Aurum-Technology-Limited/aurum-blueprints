              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Most first-time raisers underestimate how long a fund takes: twelve to twenty-four months from first meeting to final close is common, and institutional LPs rarely commit before they have met you several times. Mapping the stages, from pre-marketing through first close, subsequent closes and the final close, gives you a timeline you can plan cash, people and deals around.

                ## Milestones
                1. A one-page map of the stages of a fundraise with typical durations and what each stage needs from you.
                2. The difference between pre-marketing, a soft circle, a commitment and a closing written down with an example of each.
                3. Three experienced GPs or IR professionals asked what surprised them most in their last raise.
                4. Your own planned timeline drafted against the map, with the riskiest stage marked.

                ## Notes
                Industry bodies such as ILPA and the regional private equity associations publish guides to fund formation and fundraising. Read them for structure; your fund counsel decides what you may say to whom and when.
