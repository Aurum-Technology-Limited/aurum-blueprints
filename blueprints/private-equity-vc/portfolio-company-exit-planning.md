            - name: Exit readiness baseline early in the hold
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Most of what decides an exit price is fixed or broken years before the sale: the quality of the numbers, the depth of the team, customer concentration and how clean the legal record is. Scoring the company against what a buyer will test, within the first year of ownership, turns exit preparation into a list of things to fix rather than a scramble in the final six months.

                ## Milestones
                1. A readiness checklist covering financial reporting, KPIs, team, customers, legal, tax, technology and ESG agreed with the deal lead.
                2. Each line scored red, amber or green with a one-line reason.
                3. The five gaps most likely to cost value at exit ranked with a rough owner and timeline.
                4. The baseline filed with the deal records so later scorecards compare against it.

                ## Notes
                Score as a sceptical buyer would, not as the deal team that wrote the thesis. Ask a colleague who did not work on the deal to challenge the greens.
              priority: high
              deadlineOffsetDays: 45
              frontmatter:
                mode: research
                output_kind: artifact
                success_criteria: "A dated readiness baseline scoring at least eight exit dimensions, with the top five gaps ranked and owned, filed with the deal records."
                cadence: one-shot
                effort_hours_estimate: "8"
              tasks:
                - "List the eight to ten areas a buyer's diligence team will test first"
                - "Score each area red, amber or green with a one-line reason"
                - "Ask a colleague outside the deal team to challenge every green score"
                - "Rank the five gaps most likely to cost value and name an owner for each"

              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Sales reach their best price when enough credible buyers compete, and the time to find out whether they exist is long before launch.                 ## Milestones

# Portfolio Company Exit Planning

This area is for the deal partner, principal or associate who will one day have to sell a portfolio company, and for the operating partner and portfolio company CFO who carry much of the work. It runs from the foundations (an exit readiness baseline, the exit hypothesis from the IC memo, the buyer universe, timing triggers, route choice and the legal housekeeping buyers will test), through the machinery that keeps a company sellable, the skills behind sale processes, locked boxes and warranty insurance, the decisions about advisers, remediation and whether to sell at all, the dated steps of a live process, the variants for a CFO, associate, operating partner, minority holder or a fund near the end of its term, and finally dual tracks, paused processes and the firm's own exit playbook.

What repeats is a monthly KPI history kept in the form buyers will ask for, a monthly look at multiples and financing conditions, buyer relationship touchpoints, a quarterly readiness scorecard, a weekly steering rhythm and diligence log once a sale is close, and a yearly exit plan refresh. The Metrics log, Risk register, Meeting notes, Vendor, Remediation plan and Board meeting templates pair with the projects that name them.
