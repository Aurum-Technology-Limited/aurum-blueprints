            - name: One-page acquisition criteria for the search
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Every owner, broker and investor asks the same question in the first minute: what are you looking for? A single page with revenue and EBITDA range, geography, industry traits, owner situation and deal-breakers lets you answer in one breath, screen a teaser in five minutes and show investors you are hunting for the same company they funded.

                ## Milestones
                1. A one-page criteria sheet stating EBITDA range, revenue range, geography and the five business traits you want, such as recurring revenue and fragmented customers.
                2. A short list of hard exclusions written down, for example customer concentration above a set share or heavy capital spending.
                3. The sheet reviewed by at least two lead search investors and their comments folded in.
                4. A broker-facing version saved as a PDF and ready to attach to emails.

                ## Notes
                Keep the range honest. A searcher who says yes to everything gets sent everything, and brokers stop reading your emails within a month.

# Search Fund Acquisition Phase

This area is for the searcher who has raised search capital and now has to find, win and close one good small company, usually within two years and usually alone or with one partner. It starts with foundations (criteria, stage gates with investors, an industry shortlist, a pipeline and an adviser bench), then the weekly machinery of owner outreach, broker coverage and pipeline reviews, the skills of reading small-company books and talking with owners, the decisions on structure, debt and seller terms, the dated events from first LOI to closing day, variants for solo, partnered, family and self-funded searchers, and finally the step-up, the owner transition agreement and the call on whether to extend the search.

What repeats is twice-weekly owner calls and a Tuesday letter batch, a Friday pipeline review, a Wednesday reading session on target financials, a monthly investor update on the 5th, broker rotations, runway checks and a quarterly strategy reset. The Metrics log, Vendor, Purchase decision, Meeting notes, Risk register and Operational checklist templates pair with the projects that name them. Everything here is organisational: valuation, tax structure, purchase agreement terms and lending are calls you make with your lawyer, accountant, lender and lead investors, and owners' confidential information stays inside what each NDA allows.
