# Secondaries and Continuation Funds

This area is for the person working on secondary transactions, whether you sit at a secondaries buyer, at a GP considering a continuation vehicle, inside an LP selling fund interests, or on an adviser's team running the process. It starts with foundations (the market map, LP-led and GP-led mechanics, pricing conventions, transfer steps and information barriers), moves into the operating rhythm of pricing logs, live process tracking, NAV roll-forwards and conflicts records, then the skills of discount analysis, fund cash flow modelling and underwriting underlying companies, the decisions about advisers, structures and status quo options, dated moments such as an LP sale process, an LPAC meeting and an election period, situations for different seats, and finally specialist work on strip sales, tail-end portfolios, carry resets and cross-border transfers.

What repeats is a weekly process tracker during live deals, monthly pricing and pipeline reviews, a monthly check of deferred payments and GP consent history, quarterly NAV roll-forwards and continuation vehicle monitoring, and a yearly look at December reference date bidding and the conference season.
