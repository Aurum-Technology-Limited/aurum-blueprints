# Track Record and Attribution

This area is for the deal professional who wants a record that travels, and the founder who will one day put that record in front of an LP's diligence team. It starts with the foundations (an inventory of every deal you touched, the confidentiality check, scope rules, roles, dated cash flows, metric definitions and a single workbook), then the operating machinery that keeps the record current, the knowledge behind IRR, net returns, PME and the performance rules, the analyses and decisions that make it honest, the dated moments when it is shown or tested, variants for associates, operators, spinout teams, venture investors, sponsors and smaller markets, and finally the specialist work of portability and skill versus luck.

What repeats is a quarterly refresh after each valuation round, a monthly evidence capture for live deals, a monthly change log, quarterly referee and footnote reviews, and a yearly reconciliation, benchmark refresh and sign-off. The Meeting notes, Vendor and Operational checklist templates pair with the projects that name them. Everything here is organisational: whether you may use or disclose a record, and how performance may be advertised where you live, are questions for counsel and compliance, and nothing in these projects is legal, tax or investment advice.
