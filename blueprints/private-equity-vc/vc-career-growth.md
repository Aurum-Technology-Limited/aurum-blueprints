              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Small venture firms rarely have a written ladder, and the unwritten one often rewards things nobody told you about, such as sourcing a deal that closed or being asked for by a founder. # VC Career Growth

This area is for the venture investor who already has the job and wants the next title, the bigger carry allocation and, eventually, a seat at the partnership table. It starts with foundations (a career baseline, your fund's real promotion criteria, a one-page thesis, your compensation and carry terms in plain words, a development plan), moves through the weekly and monthly machinery of sourcing, prediction logging and self-review, then the skills partners judge you on, the decisions that shape a career (a sector to own, an MBA, an outside offer), dated moments such as your first championed deal and your promotion case, variants for analysts, former operators, parents and investors in smaller ecosystems, and finally the partner-level work of building a franchise and proving judgement over a whole fund cycle.

What repeats is a weekly deal log, sourcing scorecard, prediction log and partner meeting preparation, monthly founder network, market map and feedback rounds, quarterly self-reviews against the ladder and a yearly compensation conversation. The Development plan, Onboarding 30/60/90, 1:1, Metrics log and Skills matrix templates pair with the projects that name them. Compensation, carry and contract questions are organisational here: take tax and legal points to your own adviser, not the fund's.
