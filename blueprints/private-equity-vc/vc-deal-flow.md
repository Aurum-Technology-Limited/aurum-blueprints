# VC Deal Flow

This area is for the venture investor whose week depends on meeting the right founders early, from a new associate building a first pipeline to a partner who wants proof of which sources pay off. It starts with foundations (a focus statement founders can check themselves against, an audit of where past meetings came from, source attribution rules and an intake route), moves into the weekly and monthly machinery of inbound triage, new-company scans and referrer follow-up, then the skills of first calls and warm introductions, decisions about data tools and which communities to commit to, the demo days and dinners worth preparing for, situations such as a new fund or a part-time role, and finally specialist channels like university spinouts and alumni of fast-growing companies.

What repeats is a Monday inbound triage, a Thursday scan for new companies, monthly referrer thank-yous, watch list reviews and a source scorecard, quarterly accelerator calendars and co-investor swaps, and a yearly sourcing plan. The Meeting notes, Metrics log, Purchase decision and Risk register templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
