            - name: Written support promise founders can hold you to
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Founders hear the same pitch about value added from every fund, and most can tell within a year which promises were real. Writing down the five or six kinds of help you will actually give, how fast you respond and what you will not do sets expectations you can meet and gives you something to measure against.

                ## Milestones
                1. A list of no more than six support offers, each with who delivers it and a realistic response time.
                2. A short list of things the fund does not do, such as writing code or running payroll.
                3. The promise checked against last year's actual help so nothing on it is aspirational.
                4. The final version shared with every portfolio founder and attached to new investment welcome notes.

                ## Notes
                Promise less than you can deliver. A founder remembers one missed promise longer than three useful introductions.

# VC Portfolio Support

This area is for the venture investor who wants founders to say, unprompted, that your help was worth having, whether you sit on a platform team, lead deals or are a new associate with a thin address book. It starts with foundations (a support promise, a needs conversation after each cheque, a request log, a map of who you can really introduce and rules for confidentiality and introductions), moves into the weekly and monthly machinery of triage, check-ins, customer and talent introductions and a founder digest, then the skills of mock pitches, startup hiring and coaching, decisions about tools and offers, the fundraise sprints and summits worth preparing for, hard situations such as a runway crisis or a co-founder split, and finally specialist work on measuring whether support changes outcomes.

What repeats is a Monday triage of open requests, a Wednesday pass on customer introductions, a Thursday sync with colleagues, monthly founder check-ins, runway reviews, talent shares and an asks digest, quarterly support reviews and community events, and a yearly founder survey and refresh of the support promise. The Purchase decision, Meeting notes, Feedback themes and Metrics log templates pair with the projects that name them.
