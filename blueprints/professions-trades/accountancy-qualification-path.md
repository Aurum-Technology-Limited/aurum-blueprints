            - name: Choosing between ACCA, CPA and other accountancy bodies
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Recognised accountants come out of ACCA, the US CPA, CPA Canada, ICAEW's ACA and CIMA alike, but the routes differ in where they are respected, how the exams run, what experience they require and how far a member can move between countries. Across most of the Caribbean ACCA is the common route, the US CPA suits people heading to US firms or employers, and CIMA suits those set on management accounting in business. Choosing on purpose, with your target country and role in mind, avoids switching bodies two years in.

                ## Milestones
                1. The country you expect to work in for the next five years and the role you want written down.
                2. Three bodies compared on recognition, exam structure, experience rules, cost and time to qualify.
                3. What local employers ask for checked in at least ten current job adverts.
                4. One body chosen, with the reason recorded in a sentence.

                ## Notes
                Your local professional institute, such as ICATT, ICAJ or ICAB in the Caribbean, can tell you which qualifications lead to membership and a practising licence in your country.

This area takes you from wondering whether accountancy suits you to holding a full membership card, whether you start from CSEC Principles of Accounts, a degree in another subject or ten years in a different career.
