            - name: Refrigerant handling rules in your country
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Most countries restrict who may open a refrigerant circuit or buy refrigerant, because the gases damage the ozone layer or warm the climate. In the US that means EPA Section 608 certification, in the UK an F-gas certificate, in Canada an ozone depletion prevention card, and across the Caribbean the national ozone unit usually runs technician registration and good-practice training under the Montreal Protocol. Finding out exactly which rule applies to you tells you what to sit and when.

                ## Milestones

                2. The certificate or registration a technician needs written down, with its category or level.
                3. The rules on buying refrigerant, recovering it and keeping records noted.

                ## Notes
                Rules change as the Kigali Amendment phases down HFC refrigerants, so check the regulator's current page rather than an old course handout.
