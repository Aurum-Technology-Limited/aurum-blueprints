              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Carpentry means lifting sheets of plywood in the midday sun, working on roofs and scaffolds, and measuring to the millimetre while the rest of the site is shouting. Spending a few days with working carpenters, before you pay for a course, tells you whether you like the work itself and not just the idea of it.                 4. A dated decision to pursue carpentry, wait a year or look at another trade.

                ## Notes
                Ask about injuries honestly. Most experienced carpenters will tell you which machine or which roof taught them respect, and that conversation is worth more than any brochure.

            - name: Timber storage and moisture checks
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Timber stored flat on the ground in a humid yard soaks up water, twists and grows mould, and wood fixed while wet shrinks and opens its joints a month later. In the tropics, where the air stays damp all year, this matters more than any textbook written for a cold climate suggests.

                ## Milestones

                2. Boards stacked with spacer sticks at regular intervals and the ends of hardwoods sealed.
                3. A pin moisture meter bought and checked against a known dry sample.

              priority: medium

# Carpentry and Joinery Trade

This area is for anyone heading into carpentry or joinery, from a fourth-form student in Industrial Technology or a school leaver on a TVET course to a career changer, an apprentice counting hours or a qualified carpenter looking at roofs, formwork or fine joinery.
