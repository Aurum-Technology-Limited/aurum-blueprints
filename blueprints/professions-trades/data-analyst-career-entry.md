# Data Analyst Career Entry

This area is for anyone aiming at a first data analyst job: a CSEC or CAPE student who is good at maths, a graduate in economics or business, or an administrator, teacher or accountant who already builds the spreadsheets everyone else relies on. It runs from the foundations (a target role, a learning route, a toolkit and a plan) through the routines that build skill and evidence, the core skills from spreadsheets and SQL to statistics and presenting, the certificate, portfolio and CV decisions, the tests, interviews and first 90 days, situations from school to career change and working abroad, and finally the specialist work that follows the first year.

What repeats is twice-weekly SQL practice, daily flashcards, weekly application, reading and study check slots, a monthly mini-analysis, write-up, meetup and plan review, and a quarterly skills rescore. The Skills matrix, Development plan, Job application, Portfolio piece, Resume and Onboarding 30/60/90 templates pair with the projects that name them.
