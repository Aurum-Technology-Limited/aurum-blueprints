# Early Childhood Educator Career

Early Childhood Educator Career follows the route from a first morning volunteering in a nursery to running a centre of your own, for school leavers, parents, career changers and practitioners already working in the Caribbean, the UK, the US and Canada. It starts with roles, entry qualifications, training routes, funding and the checks and certificates every setting needs, moves through child development, play, observation, language, behaviour and baby room skills, then the practicum, registration and first post and the daily and termly routines of a key person, and closes with qualifying in post, childminding, nannying, working abroad, room leadership, mentoring and opening a centre.

The rhythm is daily safety checks and handovers, weekly planning, observations and key child notes, monthly supervision, records and development logs, termly family meetings and drills, and yearly certificate, pay and registration checks. The Savings goal, Portfolio piece, Job application, Operational checklist, 1:1 and Development plan templates pair with the projects that point to them.
