# Firefighter Career

This area is for anyone aiming at the fire service, whether a fifth-form student choosing CSEC subjects, a sixth-form or college leaver, a former athlete looking for a team job with real stakes, a career changer in their thirties or a serving firefighter eyeing the next rank. It starts with the foundations (a station visit, the entry rules where you live, the medical and fitness baselines, your route and your documents), then the routines that keep you fit, rested, clean and recorded, the knowledge every recruit is tested on, the decisions about which services and courses to pursue, the dated events from application to the end of probation, the situations that change the route, including applying abroad, and finally the specialist work of driving, rescue, airport firefighting, investigation and promotion.

What repeats is a weekly training plan with a monthly retest, weekly study of equipment, knots and test questions, a monthly check of recruitment notices, training records, protective kit and your own wellbeing, and the yearly medical, certificate renewal and pay statement checks. The Resume, Training program, Metrics log, Job application and Development plan templates pair with the projects that name them.
