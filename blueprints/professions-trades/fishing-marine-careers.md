# Fishing and Marine Careers

This area is for anyone drawn to earning a living from the sea, from a fifth-form student wondering whether marine biology or a fishing boat is the better start, to a school leaver looking for a crew place, an adult changing career, and a working fisher ready to skipper, farm or protect the reef. It starts with the foundations (testing the fit, subjects, routes, swimming, safety certificates, registration and the boat licence), then the routines that keep a fisher safe and paid, the seamanship and species skills, the big decisions about specialism, boat and market, dated events from the first week at sea to hurricane season, situations such as supporting a teenager or working abroad, and finally aquaculture, reef restoration, charters and a seat at the fisheries management table.

What repeats is a daily forecast check, weekly logbook totals, engine flushes and fish hold cleaning, monthly safety gear and money checks, quarterly drills and rule reviews, and the yearly round of inspections, renewals and hurricane preparation. The Operational checklist, Metrics log, Monthly budget and bills, Purchase decision, Risk register and Job application templates pair with the projects that name them.
