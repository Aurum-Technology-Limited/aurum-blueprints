# Flight Attendant Career

This area is for anyone who wants to work as cabin crew, whether a CSEC or CAPE student checking the entry rules, a school leaver in the UK, US or Canada, a career changer from nursing or hospitality, or a new joiner finding their feet on the line. It starts with the foundations (requirements, swimming, documents, vetting, scams and an airline shortlist), moves through applications, video interviews and assessment days, then safety, first aid, security and service training, the routines that keep rosters, rest, pay and expiry dates in order, and the decisions about haul, base and contract, ending with probation, the situations that change the route, and senior crew and ground roles.

What repeats is a weekly careers page check while you apply, twice-weekly language and fitness sessions, a weekly duty log, sleep plan and bag restock, monthly roster bids, pay checks, expiry checks and safety report reviews on varied days, and the yearly recurrent training. The Resume, Job application, Metrics log, Operational checklist, Savings goal and Development plan templates pair with the projects that name them.
