                ## Purpose
                Graphic design is mostly solving other people's communication problems to a brief and a deadline, with plenty of revisions, which is a different life from making art for yourself.             - name: Comparing degrees, diplomas, short courses and self-taught routes
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Graphic designers are hired on their portfolio more than their certificate, which is why people arrive through bachelor's degrees, two-year associate degrees or college diplomas, short courses and teaching themselves. Comparing three real options on cost, length, the quality of graduates' portfolios and links to internships stops you paying for a route that does not suit your money or your timetable.

                ## Milestones
                1. Three routes shortlisted, such as a local college diploma, an overseas degree and an online course with mentoring.

                4. A chosen route recorded with the reason and a start date.

# Graphic Design Career

This area is for anyone who wants to make a living from design, from a fourth-form student weighing CSEC Visual Arts to a career changer building a first portfolio in the evenings and a freelancer ready to specialise or open a studio. It starts with the foundations (testing the fit, subjects, routes, the admissions portfolio, funding, kit and file habits), then the routines that keep work and money flowing, the type, colour, layout, software, print, screen and motion skills employers check, the decisions about settings, pricing, contracts, rights and specialisms, the milestones from degree show to first client, the situations that change the route, and finally brand systems, packaging, art direction, running a studio and mentoring.

What repeats is a daily sketch, a weekly self-set brief, pipeline review, backup check and payment chase, monthly invoicing, bookkeeping, critique and learning logs, quarterly portfolio refreshes and tax checks, and the yearly round of licence, rights, salary and career reviews. The Purchase decision, Portfolio piece, Development plan, Job application and Onboarding 30/60/90 templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
