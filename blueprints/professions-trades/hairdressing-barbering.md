# Hairdressing and Barbering

This area is for anyone drawn to hair, from a student cutting friends' hair at home or choosing CSEC subjects to an adult changing career, a newly licensed barber with an empty chair or a stylist ready to open a shop. It starts with foundations (testing the fit, choosing barbering or hairdressing, training routes, kit, hygiene, chemical safety and licensing), then the routines of a working chair, the core skills from fades and line-ups to colour, relaxers, braids, locs and installs, the business decisions, dated milestones such as the first job, the licence exam and a wedding day, situations for parents, career changers and those working abroad, and finally hair loss referrals, running staff and teaching.

What repeats is daily disinfectant, appointment reminders and closing checks, weekly clipper care, client notes, posts, practice and follow-ups, monthly money, stock, rebooking and staff reviews, quarterly classes and shear sharpening, and the yearly round of renewals, price reviews and peak season planning.
