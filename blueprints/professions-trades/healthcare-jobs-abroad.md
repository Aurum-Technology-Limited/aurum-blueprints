# Nursing and Healthcare Jobs Abroad

This area is for nurses, midwives, carers and other health workers who want to practise in another country, whether that is a Caribbean nurse eyeing the UK, US or Canada, a student planning ahead, or someone already abroad deciding what comes next. It starts with the decision, the destination and the paperwork, moves through English tests, credential checks, licensing exams and overseas registration, then recruiters, offers, contracts and visas, the move itself and the first year on the ward, and ends with settlement, specialising, moving on, coming home and helping the next nurse follow.

The recurring rhythms are weekly study and application reviews while you prepare, daily question practice in the run-up to a licensing exam, monthly checks on money sent home, payslips, CPD hours and wellbeing, and quarterly or yearly reviews of every visa, registration and certificate date.
