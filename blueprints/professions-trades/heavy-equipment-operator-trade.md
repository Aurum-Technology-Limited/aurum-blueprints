                ## Purpose
                Operating plant looks like sitting down all day, but the job means early starts, long hours in heat and dust, constant attention to people on foot around the machine and weeks without work when a contract ends.             - name: Excavator controls and trench digging
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                The tracked excavator is the machine most operators start on and the one most jobs ask for. Smooth control means moving two or three functions at once without jerking the bucket, then digging a trench to a given depth and width and placing spoil where it will not fall back in. Practising these in order, with an instructor watching, builds the habits that tests and employers check for.

                ## Milestones
                1. Both common control patterns understood and the machine's pattern confirmed before every start.
                2. Smooth multi-function movements practised until the bucket follows a steady line.
                3. A straight trench dug to a set depth and width, checked with a level.
                4. Spoil placed at a safe distance from the trench edge every time.
                5. An instructor's sign-off on trenching and on loading a truck.

                ## Notes
                Never enter, or let anyone enter, a trench deeper than the site's limit without proper support. Spoil and machines too close to the edge are a common cause of collapse.
              priority: medium
              frontmatter:
                mode: learning
                output_kind: knowledge
