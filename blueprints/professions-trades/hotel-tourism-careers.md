# Hotel and Tourism Careers

Hotel and Tourism Careers follows the route from a first shadow shift to running a hotel, for school leavers, hospitality students and career changers in the Caribbean and for anyone building a hotel career in the UK, the US or Canada. It starts with entry qualifications, training routes and the permits employers ask for, builds front desk, reservations, night audit, restaurant, bar, housekeeping, complaint handling and guiding skills, then covers the internship, the first job and the first ninety days, the routines of shift life, specialist choices and life situations, and closes with supervision, revenue, sales and the long road to general manager.

Shift life brings a weekly rhythm of rota planning, tip records, language practice and childcare checks, with monthly supervisor one-to-ones, compliment filing and network contacts, quarterly safety and appraisal notes, and yearly certificate renewals, hurricane season readiness and pay reviews.
