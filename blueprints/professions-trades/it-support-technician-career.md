# IT Support Technician Career

This area is for anyone heading into IT support, from a fifth-form student who already fixes the family laptop to a call centre agent changing careers at thirty or a first line technician eyeing the next rung. It starts with the foundations (whether the work suits you, entry qualifications from CSEC and CAPE to T Levels and college diplomas, a training route, a first certification, a home lab, tools and a CV), then the routines that keep study, job hunting and skills moving, the core skills from hardware and Windows to networking, directories, cloud suites and calm calls, the decisions about employers, pay and direction, the interview, exam and first-job milestones, situations for students, career changers, parents, side businesses and moves abroad or back home, and finally the specialist work that leads into systems administration and team leadership.
