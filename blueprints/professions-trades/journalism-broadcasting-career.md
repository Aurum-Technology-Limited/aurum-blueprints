                Watching a working newsroom for a full shift shows you what the job is really made of: the morning conference, the phone calls that go nowhere, the rewrite at five minutes to deadline and the evening bulletin. # Journalism and Broadcasting Career

Journalism and Broadcasting Career follows the route from a curious reader or a campus radio volunteer to a reporter, presenter or editor with a beat of their own, for students, career changers and creative people in the Caribbean, the UK, the US and Canada. It starts with a newsroom visit, entry qualifications, training routes, a budget kit and a first clips portfolio, moves through interviewing, shorthand, media law, ethics, verification, records requests, numbers, feature writing and the radio and TV skills of writing for the ear, voice, packages and mobile video, then sets up the routines of a working journalist before covering internships, showreels, the newsroom writing test, accreditation, elections and hurricanes, and closes with freelancing, stringing for international outlets, working abroad, choosing a beat, sport, investigations, the editing desk and long-form documentary.

The rhythm is daily reading and listening, a weekly news diary, weekly pitches and voice practice, monthly clips, contacts, security and invoicing sessions, and yearly accreditation, hurricane kit and pay checks. The Savings goal, Purchase decision, Portfolio piece, Job application, Risk register and Development plan templates pair with the projects that point to them.
