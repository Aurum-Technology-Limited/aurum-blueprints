# Logistics and Port Careers

This area is for anyone drawn to the work that moves goods: a school leaver in Kingston, Port of Spain or Georgetown eyeing a job at the port, a student weighing CAPE Logistics against a diploma, or a warehouse worker in Birmingham or Toronto who wants a desk and a career. It runs from the foundations (job families, subjects, shipping documents, applications, port passes and site safety), through the routines that keep you safe, paid and progressing, the core skills from forklifts and scanners to Incoterms, dangerous goods, tariff classification and freight quotes, then the decisions, assessment days and busy seasons, situations such as placements, apprenticeships, career changes, parenting on shifts and moving abroad, and finally the specialist routes into customs brokerage, planning, cold chain and management.

What repeats is a weekly roster plan, performance log, safety observation, application follow-up, study block, classification practice and apprenticeship portfolio entry, monthly news, networking, CPD, pay slip, KPI and family shift checks, quarterly certificate and career reviews, and a yearly round of pay benchmarking, peak season and hurricane season preparation and licence renewals. The Resume, Job application, Development plan, Course, Onboarding 30/60/90 and Operational checklist templates pair with the projects that name them.
