            - name: Joining a performing rights organisation
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Every time your song is played on radio, in a club, on a stream or at a fete, a performance royalty is owed to the writer, but it only reaches you if you belong to a collecting society and have registered the work. Joining the right society for your territory, for example PRS for Music in the UK, ASCAP or BMI in the US, SOCAN in Canada, or JACAP, COTT, COSCAP or ECCO in the Caribbean, is the step most new writers skip.

                ## Milestones
                1. The collecting society that covers your home territory identified, with its membership rules noted.
                2. Writer membership approved and your member number recorded.
                3. Every released or performed song registered with correct writer splits.
                4. A note made of whether you also need a publisher account with the same society.

                ## Notes
                Join only one performing rights society as a writer for the same territory. Societies collect for each other abroad through reciprocal agreements, so one membership usually covers your foreign plays.
              priority: high
