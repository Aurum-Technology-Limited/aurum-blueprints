# Pharmacy Career

This area is for anyone heading towards a pharmacy counter or hospital dispensary, from a fourth-form student weighing chemistry at CSEC to a technician thinking about the degree and a registered pharmacist with an eye on ward work or a shop of their own. It runs from the foundations (testing your reasons, choosing between the pharmacist and technician routes, subjects, recognised programmes, funding and applications) through the routines of study and practice, the core skills from calculations to aseptic work, the big decisions, the dated milestones of internship and registration exams, situations such as studying overseas or returning after a break, and finally the work of a senior pharmacist.

What repeats is a weekly study plan with a daily drug flashcard review, weekly placement sign-offs, monthly CPD entries, safety alert reading, near-miss and expiry reviews, quarterly mentor check-ins and the yearly round of registration, indemnity and life support renewals.
