# Plumbing Trade

This area is for anyone drawn to plumbing, from a fourth-form student choosing CSEC subjects or a school leaver hunting for an apprenticeship to an adult changing career, and for working plumbers ready to run their own jobs. It begins with the foundations (testing the fit, subjects, training routes, the apprenticeship, funding, tools, safety and the code), then the routines that keep a working plumber organised, the core pipework, drainage, hot water and pump skills, the business decisions, dated milestones from the first month on site to the licence exam, situations such as working abroad or coming home, and finally specialist systems and training an apprentice of your own.

What repeats is a weekly logbook entry, van stock check and job filing, monthly callback reviews, money checks and supervisor sign-offs, quarterly price and training reviews, and the yearly round of renewals and hurricane season stock. The Job application, Monthly budget and bills, Purchase decision, Skills matrix, Operational checklist and Development plan templates pair with the projects that name them.
