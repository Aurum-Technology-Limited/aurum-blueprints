# Quantity Surveying Career

This area is for anyone heading towards the money side of construction, from a fourth-form student who likes Mathematics and building sites to a trainee logging experience for chartership and a senior surveyor running cost control on a major project. It starts with the foundations (testing the fit, subjects, routes, funding and applications), then the routines that build a professional record, the measurement, estimating, contract and valuation skills the job runs on, the decisions about sides, sectors and employers, the milestones from dissertation to final assessment, the situations that change the route, and finally claims, reconstruction costing and running a consultancy.

What repeats is a weekly measurement exercise and experience diary entry, monthly rate library and materials price updates, CPD and mentoring, quarterly standards checks and mentor reviews, and a yearly round of renewals, declarations and career review. The Purchase decision, Metrics log, Development plan, Job application, Onboarding 30/60/90 and Risk register templates pair with the projects that name them.
