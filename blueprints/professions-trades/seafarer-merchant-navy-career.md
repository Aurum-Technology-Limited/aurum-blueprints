                ## Purpose
                Deep-sea contracts commonly run four to nine months, with watches every day, no weekends and long passages where shore leave may be an hour or nothing at all.                 4. A dated decision to pursue a seagoing career, gather more information, or aim for a shore maritime role.

# Seafarer and Merchant Navy Career

This area is for anyone who wants to go to sea as a professional seafarer, from a CSEC, CAPE or GCSE student in Kingston, Port of Spain or Liverpool weighing up maritime school to an able seafarer in Manila or Halifax aiming for an officer's certificate. It starts with the foundations (whether the life suits you, deck, engine or electro-technical, entry subjects, the medical, academies, funding, sponsorship, documents and STCW basic safety), then the routines of a working seafarer, the skills from collision rules to engine room systems, the decisions about contracts, agencies, ship types, tax and endorsements, the dated milestones of joining, sign-off, exams and revalidation, life stages such as upgrading from rating or coming ashore, and finally tanker, dynamic positioning and management-level certificates.

What repeats is a daily rest hours entry, weekly training record sign-offs, sleep and wellbeing notes, family calls and study drills, monthly sea service, wage, allotment, savings, certificate and safety gear checks, and a yearly round of tax, insurance and revalidation checks.
