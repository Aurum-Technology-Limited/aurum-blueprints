            - name: Shadowing a social worker before committing
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Most people picture social work as either rescuing children or filling in forms, and the real week is neither: it is home visits, phone calls chasing other agencies, recording, supervision and the occasional crisis that wipes out the plan. Spending two or three days alongside a qualified social worker, or a long conversation with two of them, lets you test the work against your temperament before you spend three or four years and real money on a degree.

                ## Milestones
                1. At least two qualified social workers, ideally in different settings, have talked you through a typical week.
                2. One or more days spent shadowing in an office, hospital, NGO or community setting, within the confidentiality limits they set.
                3. A list of the parts of the work that drew you in and the parts that worried you, written the same evening.
                4. A yes, not yet or no decision on applying, with three reasons written beside it.

                ## Notes
                Statutory child protection teams often cannot take observers because of confidentiality, so ask hospitals, NGOs, older people's services, schools and community centres too. A national social work association can sometimes connect you with a member willing to talk.
              priority: high
              deadlineOffsetDays: 45
              frontmatter:
                mode: research
                output_kind: decision
                success_criteria: "A written decision on whether to apply for social work training, based on conversations with at least two practitioners and one shadowing day."
                cadence: one-shot
                effort_hours_estimate: "16"
              tasks:
                - "Write down five questions you would ask a social worker about their week"
                - "Contact your national social work association to ask for a practitioner to talk to"
                - "Ask a hospital, NGO or school social worker whether you can shadow for a day"
                - "Write your decision and three reasons for it in a short note"

              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Admissions tutors look for evidence that you have spent time with people in difficulty and stayed steady, and you learn quickly whether you can listen without rushing to fix. Six months of regular volunteering with a youth club, a shelter, a children's home, an older people's day centre or a helpline gives you that evidence and the stories to tell at interview.

                1. One organisation chosen that works directly with vulnerable children, families or adults.

            - name: Case recording and report writing that stands up to scrutiny
              description: |-
                ## Purpose

# Social Work Career

Social Work Career follows the route from wondering whether you could do this work to supervising students and leading a team, for school leavers, career changers and serving practitioners in the Caribbean, the UK, the US and Canada. It starts with shadowing, volunteering, entry subjects, accredited programmes, funding and applications, builds the law, ethics, assessment, interviewing, recording and culturally grounded practice skills, takes you through placements, the licensing exam where one exists, registration and the first post, then covers the casework routines, court work, specialisms from child protection to hospitals and disaster recovery, and the later moves abroad, back from a break and into practice education or management.

Once you are in post the rhythm is weekly: caseload planning on Mondays, case notes cleared on Thursdays and a burnout check midweek, with monthly supervision preparation, CPD logging and caseload reviews, quarterly updates to your referral directory and safety check-ins, and yearly law, appraisal and registration renewals. The Savings goal, Job application, Onboarding 30/60/90, 1:1, Meeting notes and Development plan templates pair with the projects that point to them.
