# Solar and Renewable Energy Technician

This area is for anyone heading into solar and renewable energy work, whether a fifth-form student who likes Physics and being outdoors, a school leaver on a TVET course, a roofer or electrician adding PV to their trade, or an installer ready to run commercial arrays and battery systems. It starts with the foundations (testing the fit, the certification map, electrical basics, a course, working at height, the first tools and the first crew job), then the routines that keep hours, instruments, records and monitored systems in order, the core knowledge from irradiance and string sizing to batteries and commissioning, the decisions about market, employment, insurance, pricing and grid rules, the exam and assessment days, the situations that change the route, including work abroad and coming home to the Caribbean, and finally the specialist work of battery retrofits, maintenance contracts, wind, microgrids and a business of your own.

What repeats is a weekly hours log and a monthly supervisor sign-off, a pre-start check before every roof, monthly instrument and PPE inspections, filing of commissioning records, weekly monitoring checks on systems you installed, monthly code reading and near-miss reviews, a weekly stock count, quarterly certificate and contract reviews, and the yearly round of insurance, supplier and hurricane season preparation. The Purchase decision, Job application, Operational checklist, Metrics log, Vendor and Development plan templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
