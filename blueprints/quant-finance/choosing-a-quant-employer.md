# Choosing a Quant Employer

The word quant covers very different jobs: a researcher in a multi-manager pod, a trader at a market maker, a strats developer at a bank and a portfolio engineer at a long-only asset manager share a title and little else. This area is for students choosing a first employer and working professionals weighing a move. It runs from foundations (the employer map, role families, how pay is built, your own constraints and a longlist), through the routines that keep your view of firms current, the knowledge that separates one business model from another, the decisions between employer types, the visits and decision days themselves, variants for different starting points, and the senior work of lateral moves, guarantees and founding hires.

What repeats is a monthly watch on firm news and people moves, a log of conversations with people inside firms, a recruiter register, a quarterly re-rank of the shortlist and pay data, an events calendar and a yearly check on whether your current employer still fits. Nothing here is legal, tax or investment advice, and no firm's pay or culture is guaranteed: confirm terms with the firm and take contract questions to an employment lawyer. The Skills matrix, Meeting notes, Job application and Development plan templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
