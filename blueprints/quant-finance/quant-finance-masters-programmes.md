# Quant Finance Masters Programmes

A financial engineering or quant finance master's is one of the most expensive single decisions a student makes, and the brochures all look alike. This area starts with the groundwork (why you want the degree, a prerequisite audit, the programme types, a calendar and a full cost budget), adds the weekly and monthly routines that carry an application season, then builds the skills admissions committees test, the decisions about which programmes and offers to take, the dated events from submission to orientation, variants for final-year students, career changers, international applicants and part-time students, and finally the first-term work of internships, practicums and research.

The rhythms are a weekly application block, twice-weekly portal checks, a Friday status review, monthly webinars, student conversations, fee tracking and recommender updates, and a yearly funding check. Pair the budget with the **Monthly budget and bills** template, information sessions with **Meeting notes**, prerequisite study with **Course**, the admissions CV with **Resume** and a campus visit with **Trip**. Nothing here is financial advice, and placement figures are claims made by the programmes, so read them as such.
