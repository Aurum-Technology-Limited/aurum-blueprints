# Appointing a Design Team

This area covers the part of a development most first-time developers underestimate: choosing the architects, engineers and other consultants, agreeing what each one does and for how much, and signing appointments that will still protect you when a lender, a buyer or a contractor asks for them. It is written for founders and small development businesses running their own schemes. It starts with the foundations (roles, brief, fee budget and the appointing company), moves through the knowledge you need to read scopes, forms and warranties, then the procurement steps from fee bid to signed appointment, the operating machinery that keeps the team coordinated, the dated moments such as kick-off, design freeze and novation, situations for different kinds of developer, and specialist work on larger or taller schemes.

The rhythms are a weekly programme update and design team meeting during the design stages, a weekly sweep of open queries, monthly invoice approvals against the fee budget, a monthly look at the change and drawing registers, quarterly checks on insurance certificates and the consultant bench, and a yearly round of renewed indemnity certificates. The Purchase decision template suits comparing fee bids, Metrics log suits the fee tracker, Meeting notes suits design team meetings and Vendor suits keeping a record on each consultant. None of this is legal or insurance advice: have appointment documents, warranties and insurance levels reviewed by your solicitor and broker.
