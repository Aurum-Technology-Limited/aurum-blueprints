# Industrial and Logistics Property

Industrial and logistics property is the business of owning the sheds, trade counters and small business units that goods pass through, judged on access, eaves height, yard depth, power and the strength of the occupier. This area is for founders and small businesses buying their first warehouse or multi-let estate, and for owner-occupiers who want their premises to work as an investment too. It runs from an investment thesis, a market map and a specification checklist, through the operating rhythms of deal flow, lease events and estate inspections, then the knowledge of industrial leases, power and contamination, the upgrade and letting decisions, the dated bids and negotiations, situations for different kinds of buyer and the specialist sectors of last-mile, cold storage and open storage.

The rhythms are a weekly deal review, a monthly market watch, lease event check and estate walk, quarterly covenant, yard and occupier reviews, and yearly roof surveys and insurance renewals. Metrics log suits the market data baseline, Operational checklist suits the inspection routines, Purchase decision suits upgrade choices, Risk register suits environmental risk and Vendor suits the estate managing agent. None of this is investment, legal, tax or valuation advice: agree prices, leases, structures and environmental conclusions with your surveyor, solicitor, accountant and specialist consultants.
