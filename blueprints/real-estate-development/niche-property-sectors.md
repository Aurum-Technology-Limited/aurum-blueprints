# Niche Property Sectors

Niche property sectors are the asset types that sit outside offices, shops, sheds and apartments: self-storage, purpose-built student accommodation, care homes, medical office and senior living, data centres and a handful of emerging uses. Most of them are operating businesses wearing a building, so this area is for founders deciding whether to enter one and for analysts building a sector specialism. It runs from a screening scorecard and a first sector choice, through the market watches and operator reviews that keep a thesis honest, then the economics of each sector, the site and structure decisions, the dated memos and launches, situations for different starting points, and the specialist work of opco and propco splits, data centre leases and portfolio aggregation.

The rhythms are a weekly deal review, monthly storage rate surveys, power watches and operator meetings, a student letting cycle each year, quarterly covenant, metrics and risk reviews, and a yearly thesis refresh. Reading queue, Purchase decision, Vendor, Metrics log, Risk register and Onboarding 30/60/90 pair with the projects that name them. Nothing here is investment, legal, tax or regulatory advice: agree prices, structures and compliance questions with your valuer, lawyer, accountant and the sector regulator.
