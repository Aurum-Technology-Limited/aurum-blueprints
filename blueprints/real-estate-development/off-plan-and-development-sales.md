                ## Purpose
                Every later sales choice, from the agent to the launch date, depends on a few early answers: who the buyers are, whether to sell off-plan or at completion, how many sales the lender needs and what share of GDV you can spend selling. Writing a two-page sales strategy before the first enquiry means the agent, the lender and your partners all work to the same plan. Revisit it at each funding or market change.

                ## Milestones
                1. Target buyer groups named, with the share of units each is expected to take.
                2. A decision recorded on selling off-plan, at a set build stage or at completion, with the reasons.
                3. Sales targets set by quarter, matched to the build programme and any lender pre-sale condition.
                4. The strategy signed off by partners and shared with the agent once appointed.

                ## Notes
                Write the reasons down. When the market turns, the first question from partners is why you chose that route, and a dated note answers it.

# Off-Plan and Development Sales

Off-plan and development sales is the work of turning a unit schedule into exchanged contracts and completed buyers: setting prices, choosing who sells, taking reservations and deposits, keeping every sale moving through long build periods and handing keys to the people who paid for them. It is written for founders and small developers selling their own schemes, from a terrace of four houses to a block of eighty flats. It starts with the foundations of strategy, pricing, legal sales packs and deposit handling, then the weekly and monthly sales machinery, the skills of contracts, valuations and honest marketing, the decisions on release phasing, show homes and incentives, the dated launch and completion events, situations such as small schemes, overseas buyers and slow markets, and the specialist work of absorption forecasting and learning from each sell-out.

The rhythms are a weekly sales meeting and progression review, a weekly show unit check, monthly reviews of the price list, deposits, mortgage offer expiries, marketing channels, buyer updates and the sales report to lenders and partners, and quarterly refreshes of sales evidence and particulars. Vendor suits choosing the agent, Meeting notes suits the weekly sales meeting, Metrics log suits progression and channel tracking, Purchase decision suits the sales software choice and Marketing campaign suits the launch. None of this is legal, tax or financial advice: agree contracts, deposit rules and consumer protection duties with your solicitor and the right regulated professional in your country.
