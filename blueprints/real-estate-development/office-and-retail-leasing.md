# Office and Retail Leasing

Office and retail leasing is the landlord's side of the deal: getting empty units in front of the right occupiers, agreeing terms that protect income and value, and keeping good tenants when their leases come round. This area is for small landlords with a parade, a few shops or an office building, and for leasing and asset managers who run lettings across a portfolio. It starts with the foundations of a vacancy schedule, market evidence, a letting pack, an agent and standard heads of terms, then sets up the leasing machinery, builds the knowledge behind incentives, lease structures and rent reviews, works through the big decisions on fit-out, flexibility and tenant mix, and finishes with dated launches, renewals, different landlord situations and specialist work such as regears, pre-lets and green leases.

The rhythms are a weekly pipeline review, viewing feedback and enquiry check, monthly lease event diary, agent activity, void inspection and deal register updates, quarterly tenant calls, market evidence refreshes and parade walks, and a yearly leasing plan and tenant mix review. Vendor suits the letting agent appointment, Metrics log suits the pipeline, Feedback themes suits viewing feedback, Meeting notes suits tenant review meetings, Marketing campaign suits a unit launch and Onboarding 30/60/90 suits a new leasing manager. None of this is legal, tax or valuation advice: agree leases, rent reviews and statutory notices with your solicitor and a qualified surveyor.
