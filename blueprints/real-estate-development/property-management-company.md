              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                A client account that is not reconciled regularly can hide a missing payment, a double payout or a fee taken too early for months.                 ## Notes
                Never cover a shortfall in one landlord's ledger with another landlord's money. If the account is short, top it up from business funds and find out why.
