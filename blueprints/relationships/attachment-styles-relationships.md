# Attachment Styles in Relationships

Attachment patterns shape how you handle closeness, distance and uncertainty with the people you love, and they can shift with understanding and steady practice. This area starts with the foundations (a primer, a research questionnaire, your relationship history, your triggers, your protest and distancing moves, and a calm-down plan), then the operating rhythm of reflection, activation logs and agreements with a partner, the skills of asking directly, tolerating uncertainty and staying present, decisions about therapy and fit, events such as a partner's long trip or a family visit, variants for dating, new parents and steady partners, and finally the specialist work of earned security.

What repeats is a weekly reflection page and weekly practice notes, a daily grounding minute and reassurance ritual, monthly reviews of your activation log, held messages, trigger list and felt safety as a couple, quarterly pattern and agreement reviews, and a yearly questionnaire retake. The Reading queue, Metrics log, Habit tracker and Meeting notes templates pair with the projects that name them.
