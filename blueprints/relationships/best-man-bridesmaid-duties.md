                ## Purpose
                Being asked to be best man, maid of honour or a bridesmaid is an honour that can also cost hundreds in money and a dozen weekends in time. Taking a few days to weigh the role against your budget, diary and the friendship lets you say yes wholeheartedly, or decline kindly before the couple has built plans around you.

                ## Milestones

                2. Your budget, work and family commitments set against those dates.

# Best Man & Bridesmaid Duties

This area is for anyone asked to stand beside a friend or sibling at their wedding, whether as best man, maid of honour, bridesmaid, groomsman, usher, best woman or man of honour. It starts with the foundations (deciding to accept, agreeing expectations, pricing the role and booking outfit and travel), then the routines that carry you through the engagement, the speech and day-of skills, the decisions about the stag or hen and gifts, the events themselves, the situations that change the role, and finally the specialist jobs of ushers, ring holders and day-of contacts.

What repeats is a monthly call with the couple, a monthly transfer into your attendant savings pot, weekly speech writing and rehearsal, a midweek wedding party sync, a Friday kitty update, and a yearly refresh of your toolkit if you are asked again. The Purchase decision, Savings goal, Trip and Operational checklist templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
