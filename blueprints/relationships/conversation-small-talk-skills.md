                ## Purpose
                Small talk rarely feels hard everywhere at once: plenty of people chat easily with a shop assistant but freeze with strangers at a party, or manage one to one but go silent in a group of five. # Conversation & Small Talk Skills

This area is for anyone who wants small talk to feel less like a test, from students in their first term to adults who have gone quiet in groups. It starts with foundations (a map of the hardest situations, a practice ladder, openers, exits and a method for names), moves through the weekly and monthly routines that keep practice going, then the core skills of open questions, listening, threading and storytelling, followed by habit fixes, real events, variants for students, parents and people coming back after time away, and finally the specialist work of introducing people, group conversation and a life-story interview.

What repeats is a weekly conversation quota and tally, a weekly read of your people notes and one follow-up question, a single daily greeting, monthly reflections, story additions and reading, and a quarterly re-rating of your baseline map. The Metrics log, Person, Habit tracker, Course and Reading queue templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
