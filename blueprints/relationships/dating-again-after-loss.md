            - name: Grief and readiness check before dating again
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                There is no correct waiting period after a death or the end of a long marriage, and the opinions of friends and family will range from too soon to long overdue. A written check on where your grief, energy and daily life stand right now gives you your own answer: start gently, wait, or look again on a set date.

                ## Milestones
                1. Honest answers written to five questions: why now, what you miss most, what you hope for, how your days feel, and who you would tell first.
                2. A note on whether grief still arrives daily, weekly or less often, without judging the answer.
                3. A decision recorded: start gently now, wait, or revisit on a date in the calendar.
                4. If the answers point to heavy, unchanging grief, a conversation booked with your doctor or a bereavement counsellor.

                ## Notes
                Wanting company does not betray the person you lost, and still grieving does not rule you out. Many people date while grieving; the check is about pace, not permission.
              priority: high

            - name: Romance scam safeguards for widowed daters
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Romance fraud deliberately targets widowed and recently divorced people through dating sites and social media, using patient fake profiles that end in an urgent request for money, gift cards or crypto. Setting firm rules before you start, and telling a family member what they are, protects both your savings and your confidence.

                ## Milestones
                1. A written rule that you never send money, gift cards, crypto or bank details to someone you have not met in person, whatever the story.
                2. A list of common warning signs: refusing video calls, moving off the app quickly, a job overseas, sudden emergencies.
                3. A habit of running a reverse image search on any new match's photos.
                4. A named person you will tell about any request for money before replying.
                5. Your country's fraud reporting service and your bank's fraud line saved in your phone.

                ## Notes
                Scammers are skilled and patient, and being taken in is not a sign of foolishness. If you have already sent money, contact your bank straight away.
              priority: high
              deadlineOffsetDays: 14
              frontmatter:
                mode: building
                output_kind: artifact
                success_criteria: "Written money rules and warning signs exist, a family member knows them, and the bank fraud line is saved in your phone."
                cadence: one-shot
                effort_hours_estimate: "1"
              tasks:
                - "Write your no-money rule on a card you keep by the computer"
                - "Save your bank's fraud line and your national fraud reporting number"
                - "Learn how to run a reverse image search on a profile photo"
                - "Tell one family member your rules and ask them to hold you to them"

# Dating Again After Loss

This area is for anyone thinking about a new relationship after a partner has died or a marriage of many years has ended, whether you are 45 or 85, widowed suddenly or after years of caring. It starts with the foundations (a readiness check, what you want now, telling your children, scam and safety rules, a budget and where to meet people), then the routines that keep grief and dating side by side, the skills of talking about a late partner and returning to dating after decades, the decisions about keepsakes, homes, money and commitment, the first occasions, versions for different circumstances, and finally the harder questions of care and legal agreements in a later-life partnership.

What repeats is a Sunday choice of the week's outing, a protected Wednesday evening, a read-back of date notes on the 2nd, a couple-friends meal on the 6th, a grief check-in on the 9th, a widowed group meeting on the 14th, a confidant catch-up on the 16th, peer support on the 19th, a scam review on the 23rd, a look at next month's hard dates on the 25th and a spending check on the 28th, plus a quarterly pacing review and contact with your late partner's family, and yearly checks of hard dates, beneficiaries and emergency contacts. The Monthly budget and bills, Habit tracker, Reading queue and Trip templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
