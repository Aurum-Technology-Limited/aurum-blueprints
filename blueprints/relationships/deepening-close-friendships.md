                ## Purpose
                Most people can sustain only about five truly close friendships at once, yet few ever decide who those five are, so time drifts to whoever messages most. Naming three to six people you want to be closer to turns a vague wish into a short list you can act on. Do it first, because every other project here assumes you know who it is for.

                ## Milestones
                1. A list of everyone you currently count as a good friend, written out in full.
                2. Three to six names circled as the friendships you want to deepen this year.
                3. One sentence beside each name on why that friendship matters to you.
                4. The list saved somewhere you will see it at each quarterly review.

                ## Notes
                Choose people who already show some interest in you. Deepening works far better with mutual warmth than with someone you are hoping to win over.

# Deepening Close Friendships

This area is for anyone who has a few good friends and wants them to become the people they could call at three in the morning. It starts with the foundations (choosing your inner circle, seeing how often you really meet and agreeing a rhythm), then the routines that keep contact alive, the skills of disclosure, listening and asking for help, the changes that move a friendship from group chat to real closeness, the trips and milestones worth sharing, versions for parents, partnered people, shift workers and later life, and finally the commitments close friends make to each other over decades.

What repeats is a Sunday reach-out, a monthly one-to-one on the 12th, midweek follow-ups, a long letter or voice note on the 20th, a check of friends' hard dates on the 25th and a quarterly review of the whole circle, plus yearly traditions and anniversaries.
