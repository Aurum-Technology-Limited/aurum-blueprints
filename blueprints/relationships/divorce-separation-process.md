                ## Purpose
                The days after a decision to separate are a blur of urgent and non-urgent worries, and people often sign, move out or tell everyone before they know what matters. A single page sorting what must happen this week from what can wait a month stops panic decisions that are hard to undo later.

                ## Milestones
                1. One page listing every worry, sorted into this week, this month and later.
                2. Three or fewer actions marked as genuinely urgent this week.
                3. A firm rule written down: nothing signed and nobody moves out before initial legal advice.
                4. One trusted person told what is happening and asked to check in.

                ## Notes
                Urgent usually means safety, money for the next few weeks and where the children sleep. Almost everything else, including who keeps the house, can wait for advice.

            - name: Safety and coercion check before negotiating
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Mediation and direct negotiation assume both people can say what they want without fear of what happens afterwards. If there has been intimidation, threats, control over money or any violence, the process needs to change shape, often with shuttle mediation, a solicitor speaking for you or protective orders. Checking this honestly before any joint meeting protects you and the outcome.

                ## Milestones
                1. A private, honest answer to whether you feel afraid to disagree with your ex.
                2. A domestic abuse helpline number saved somewhere your ex cannot see.
                3. A decision recorded on whether joint meetings, shuttle meetings or lawyer-only contact suit your case.
                4. Any safety concern raised with your solicitor or mediator at the very first contact.

                ## Notes
                If you are in danger now, call your local emergency number. Planning a safe exit from a controlling relationship is covered in its own area; this project is about shaping the legal process around the risk.
              priority: high
              deadlineOffsetDays: 7
              frontmatter:
                mode: building
                output_kind: decision
                success_criteria: "A written decision on the safe format for contact and negotiation exists, and any concern has been disclosed to your solicitor or mediator."
                cadence: one-shot

# Divorce & Separation Process

This area is for anyone going through a separation or divorce, whether it was your decision, your partner's or a slow mutual one, and it has a strong thread for parents who need to keep the children steady while the adults untangle. It starts with the foundations (the first week, a safety check, initial legal advice, documents, a full picture of assets and debts, and where everyone lives for now), then the routines that keep the process moving and civil, the skills of mediation, disclosure and calm replies, the big decisions about route, home, parenting plan and will, the dated moments such as telling the children and moving day, variants for young children, teenagers, later-life and unmarried separations, and finally the specialist work of varying orders and long-term co-parenting.

What repeats is a weekly deadline check and message slot, a weekly support call, a weekly check-in with each child, monthly reviews of legal fees, maintenance, the shared calendar and the case itself, and a yearly look at beneficiaries, holiday rotas and the parenting plan.
