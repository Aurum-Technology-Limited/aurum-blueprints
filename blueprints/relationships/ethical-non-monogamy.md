            - name: First written relationship agreement
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Unspoken assumptions cause most early hurt in open relationships: one partner thought kissing was fine, the other thought nothing would happen without a text first. A short written agreement covering what is allowed, what needs telling and when, and what is off limits turns those assumptions into something both of you chose.

                ## Milestones
                1. Sections drafted for sexual and romantic contact, disclosure, safer sex, time and shared spaces.
                2. Each item tagged as a rule, a request or a personal boundary.
                3. Both partners have read and edited the draft at least once.
                4. Version one dated and saved where both of you can find it.
                5. A review date written at the bottom.

                ## Notes
                Fewer items work better than many. Rules that try to stop feelings, such as never falling in love, rarely hold and break trust when they fail.

# Ethical Non-Monogamy

This area is for anyone considering opening a relationship, already living polyamorously or dating as a solo poly person, and it treats non-monogamy as something that runs on clear agreements and steady logistics rather than luck. It starts with foundations (your own reasons, the map of styles, the first conversation, a written agreement, hierarchy, safer sex, outness and a shared calendar), then the operating rhythm of syncs, check-ins and reviews, skills such as working with jealousy, NRE and being a hinge, decisions on apps, barriers, metamour contact and capacity, events like a first date or a first metamour meeting, situations for parents, shared homes, long distance, solo poly and mono-poly couples, and finally specialist work on family planning, legal protections, positive results and finding a poly-aware therapist.
