# Intimacy & Sex Life

This area is for couples who want a warmer, more honest physical relationship, whether things are fine but routine or have quietly stalled. It starts with foundations (private snapshots, ground rules for talking, shared words, consent signals and a health check), then the operating rhythm that keeps closeness going, skills such as understanding responsive desire and sensate focus, decisions on mismatched desire, dry spells and finding a sex therapist, events like a reconnection weekend, variants for new parents, menopause, illness, later life and shift workers, and finally specialist work on pain, erection difficulties, medicine side effects and trauma.

What repeats is daily affectionate touch, a weekly protected window and shared bedtimes, a weekly appreciation and private desire note, a monthly intimacy check-in, a quarterly look at your agreements and a yearly review, plus a yearly sexual health and contraception check.
