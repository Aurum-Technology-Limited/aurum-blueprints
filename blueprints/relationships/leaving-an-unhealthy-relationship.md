            - name: First confidential call to a domestic abuse helpline
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Helplines are free, confidential and staffed by people who hear situations like yours every day, and you do not need to have decided to leave before calling. One call can confirm what you are dealing with, explain local services and start a safety plan, which is far more than most people can piece together alone online.

                ## Milestones
                1. A national helpline number found and saved under a harmless contact name, or memorised.
                2. A safe time and place for the call identified, when your partner cannot overhear.
                3. The call made, or the helpline's web chat used if speaking is not safe.
                4. The advice and any referral written down in your private notes.

                ## Notes
                Many helplines offer web chat, text or email for when a call is not safe. Ask whether they can call you back, and what they will say if someone else answers.

# Leaving an Unhealthy Relationship

This area is for anyone who suspects their relationship has become controlling or abusive and wants to plan a way out safely, whenever they are ready. It starts with private foundations (device safety, a first helpline call, an emergency plan, trusted people, documents and money), then the routines that keep a plan current, the knowledge that makes sense of coercive control, the decisions about where to go, reporting and legal help, the leaving day and the weeks after, situations such as children, pregnancy, visas or a partner who is also a carer, and finally the specialist work of protecting a new address and undoing coerced debt.

What repeats is a weekly check-in with a trusted person, a monthly safety plan refresh and evidence backup, a quarterly go-bag and credit check, and, once you have left, weekly route planning and a monthly account sweep. The Operational checklist, Meeting notes and Monthly budget and bills templates pair with the projects that name them. If you are in immediate danger, call your local emergency number, and if your partner can open this app, think carefully about whether to install this area at all. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
