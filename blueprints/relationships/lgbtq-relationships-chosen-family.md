# LGBTQ+ Relationships & Chosen Family

This area is for queer and trans people, their partners and the people who love them, written by a friend who has sat through the awkward Christmas dinners and the brilliant chosen family ones. It starts with foundations (mapping your chosen family, your outness and your safety), then the routines that keep that family close, the skills for coming out and answering hard questions, the decisions about relatives, therapists, paperwork and work, the events worth preparing for, variants for situations such as coming out later in life, parenting or transition, and finally the community-building work that experienced people take on.

What repeats is a weekly check-in rotation, a monthly chosen family dinner, a monthly community event, a monthly couple conversation about outside pressure, quarterly reviews of outness, privacy and contact with relatives, and a yearly round of festive planning, Pride and paperwork. The Person, Risk register, Reading queue and Trip templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
