                ## Purpose
                Before learning your partner's preferences, it helps to know your own, and memory is a better guide than any quiz. Listing the ten moments in your relationship when you felt most cared for, and the times you felt overlooked, usually shows a clear pattern of what lands for you. # Love Languages & Appreciation

Feeling appreciated rarely comes from the big occasions; it comes from a partner who has learned what lands for you and does it on ordinary days. This area starts with the foundations (your own map of what makes you feel loved, the five love languages quiz, a love menu each and an honest count of thanks against criticism), then the weekly and monthly habits that keep appreciation flowing, skills such as specific thanks, receiving well and answering bids for connection, changes that close mismatches and cut criticism, a handful of occasions worth delivering, variants for new parents, shift workers, carers, cross-cultural and neurodivergent couples, long marriages and families with children, and finally the advanced work of re-mapping and keeping a playbook.

What repeats is a daily specific thank-you and a reunion hug, a Sunday swap of three appreciations, a weekly pick from your partner's love menu, two phone-free evenings, a monthly feeling-loved rating, a monthly job taken off your partner's plate, and quarterly menu refreshes and ratio reviews. The Habit tracker, Metrics log, Reading queue and Person templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
