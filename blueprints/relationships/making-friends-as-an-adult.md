# Making Friends as an Adult

Making friends after school and university means building on purpose what education used to supply for free: the same faces, week after week, with a reason to talk. This area starts with foundations (an honest look at your current circle, a brief for the friendships you want, protected hours, a shortlist of groups and one anchor group), then the operating habits that turn contact into friendship, the skills of asking, pacing and reading interest, decisions about where your evenings go, events such as the first one-to-one coffee, variants for new parents, retirees, remote workers and others, and finally the advanced work of organising, welcoming newcomers and working out where friendships stall.

What repeats is a weekly rhythm of protected friendship slots, staying on after your group, follow-ups within 48 hours and one small reach-out, then monthly invitations outside the group with pipeline, standing-plan, spending and buddy check-ins spread across different days, quarterly reviews and enrolment checks, and a yearly birthday invitation. The Person, Habit tracker, Metrics log, Monthly budget and bills and Trip templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
