                Before setting up a single profile, decide what the search is for: a long-term partner, something casual, companionship, or finding out what you want.             - name: Dealbreakers and nice-to-haves list
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Most people filter on height, age and photos, then discover on the third date that the person does not want children or plans to leave the country. Splitting what matters into a short list of true dealbreakers and a longer list of nice-to-haves keeps the search wide where it can be and firm where it must be.

                ## Milestones
                1. No more than five dealbreakers, each a value or life plan rather than a look.
                2. A separate list of nice-to-haves that will not stop a first date.
                3. Each dealbreaker matched to an app filter, a profile detail or a first-date question.
                4. Both lists reread after the first ten dates and adjusted.

                ## Notes
                Common true dealbreakers: wanting children or not, smoking, faith, where you plan to live, the kind of relationship you want. Common false ones: height, job title, one photo you did not like.
              priority: high
