            - name: Steadying the first fortnight after discovery
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                In the days after an affair comes to light, the betrayed partner often cannot sleep, eat or concentrate, and it is tempting to make permanent decisions at three in the morning. A short written plan for the first fourteen days covers the basics: who you can call, what you will eat, how you will sleep, what can wait at work, and an agreement not to decide the future of the relationship yet.

                ## Milestones
                1. One trusted person agreed as your first call, day or night.
                2. A simple plan for meals, sleep and one short walk each day written down.
                3. Work commitments for the next two weeks reduced or handed over where possible.
                4. An agreement, with yourself at least, that no permanent decisions are made in the first fortnight.
                5. A doctor's appointment booked if sleep or appetite has not returned after a week.

                ## Notes
                If you have thoughts of harming yourself, contact emergency services or a crisis line now. If there has been any threat or violence, your safety comes before every other step in this area.
              priority: high

              frontmatter:

            - name: Ending all contact with the affair partner
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Trust cannot start to rebuild while the affair partner can still reach either of you. For the partner who had the affair, a clear, final, written end to contact, sent with your partner's knowledge and followed by blocking on every channel, is usually the first thing an affair recovery therapist asks about.

                ## Milestones
                1. A short no-contact message drafted and read by your partner before it is sent.
                2. The message sent once, with no follow-up conversation or explanation.
                3. The affair partner blocked on phone, email, messaging apps and social media.
                4. A written rule that your partner is told within a day if the affair partner gets in touch.

                ## Notes
                Keep the message brief and unambiguous: the relationship is over, there will be no further contact, and you are committed to your partner. Long goodbyes and meetings to explain tend to restart things.
              priority: high
              deadlineOffsetDays: 10
              frontmatter:
                mode: building
                output_kind: deliverable
                success_criteria: "A no-contact message seen by both partners has been sent, every channel is blocked and the rule for reporting any approach is written down."
                cadence: one-shot
                effort_hours_estimate: "2"
              tasks:

# Rebuilding Trust After Infidelity

This area is for couples facing an affair or a major betrayal, and for each partner on their own: the one who was betrayed and the one who broke the trust. It moves from the first weeks after discovery (steadying yourself, ending contact, health screening and finding the right therapists) through the routines that rebuild trust, the skills and decisions recovery asks of you, the occasions worth preparing for, the situations that change the picture and finally the specialist work of long-term recovery.

What repeats is a short daily check-in, a weekly recovery meeting, a weekly look at triggers, set times for questions, a monthly trust inventory and transparency review, a quarterly review with the therapist and the yearly anniversary of discovery. The Meeting notes, Habit tracker, Metrics log and Reading queue templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
