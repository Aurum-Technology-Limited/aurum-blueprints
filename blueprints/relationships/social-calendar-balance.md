                ## Purpose
                Most people guess their weekends are either all family or all friends, and the guess is usually wrong. Going back through the last eight weekends in your calendar, messages and photos, and sorting each half-day into friends, family, couple time, solo time and chores, gives you a baseline to plan against instead of a vague feeling of being stretched.
