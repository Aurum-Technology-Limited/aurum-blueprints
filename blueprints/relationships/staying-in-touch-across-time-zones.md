                ## Purpose
                Most people abroad drift out of reach not from lack of care but because nobody can remember whether Lisbon is one hour behind or two, or which friend moved to Vancouver last spring. A single list of everyone you want to keep, with their city, time zone and current offset from you, turns vague guilt into something you can plan around.                 4. The list saved somewhere you can open from your phone in under ten seconds.

# Staying in Touch Across Time Zones

This area is for anyone whose close friends live in other countries, whether you moved away, they did, or a group from university scattered across continents. It starts with foundations (a map of friends and time zones, overlap windows, contact tiers and preferences), moves into the weekly and monthly machinery of calls, voice notes, post and timed messages, then the skills of scheduling, voice notes and better catch-up calls, the decisions about apps, costs, shared activities and visits, the occasions worth planning, versions for new emigrants, parents, shift workers and returning expats, and finally specialist work such as restricted apps, funerals from afar and a group newsletter.

What repeats is a weekly voice note and an open weekend hour, monthly calls, a sweep for friends gone quiet, timed messages, photo shares and visit fund transfers, quarterly post, and yearly clock change checks and a review of the whole map.
