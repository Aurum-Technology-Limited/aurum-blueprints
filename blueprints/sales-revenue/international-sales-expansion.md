# Selling Into New Countries

Selling Into New Countries is for founders taking their first overseas customers, managers asked to open or rescue a market, and enterprise teams adding a region. It starts with the foundations (evidence of demand, a country scorecard, a business case, a charter with gates, the legal and tax questions, the entry model and a local price list), then moves through the weekly and monthly machinery that keeps a distant market moving, the skills of selling across cultures and languages, the decisions about hires, partners, contracts and payment, the dated moments from launch week to the six-month review, the situations founders and managers find themselves in, and finally the specialist work of running several countries at once.

What repeats is a Monday support rota and a Wednesday deal call with the country team, weekly one-to-ones across the time difference, monthly partner reviews, scorecards, intelligence notes and reference checks, quarterly price, outreach and competitor refreshes alongside an in-country visit, and a yearly re-score of the shortlist and the buying calendar. The Risk register, Metrics log, Trip, Meeting notes, Purchase decision and 1:1 templates pair with the projects that name them.
