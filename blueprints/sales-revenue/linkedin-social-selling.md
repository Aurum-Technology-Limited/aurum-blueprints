# LinkedIn Social Selling

This area is for founders, freelancers and knowledge workers who want LinkedIn to bring them buyers, not just followers. It starts with the foundations (a profile written for buyers, a goal and time budget, a named target list, connection notes and content pillars), then the weekly machinery of commenting, posting, connection batches and inbox follow-up, the writing and messaging skills behind it, the tests and decisions that improve results, the launches and events worth planning, the situations that change the approach, and finally specialist work on warm paths, trigger alerts and revenue attribution.

What repeats is a weekday commenting and inbox routine, twice-weekly posts, Monday connection batches, monthly numbers and nurture touches, a quarterly list refresh and attribution report, and a yearly strategy review. The Purchase decision, Habit tracker, Content pipeline, Metrics log, Vendor and Marketing campaign templates pair with the projects that name them.
