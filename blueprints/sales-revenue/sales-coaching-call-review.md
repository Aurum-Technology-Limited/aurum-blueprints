              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Recording laws differ by country and state, and some require every party on the call to agree before anything is captured. Before a single call is reviewed, the team needs written rules on how buyers are told, which calls may be recorded, who can listen and how long recordings are kept, checked with whoever owns legal and privacy in your business.

                ## Milestones
                1. The consent requirements for each country or state you sell into listed and checked with your legal or privacy lead.
                2. A standard consent line for the start of calls and matching meeting invite wording agreed.
                3. A retention period and an access list for recordings written down.
                4. The rules shared with every seller and stored beside the recording tool settings.

                ## Notes
                This is organisational, not legal advice. When in doubt, announce the recording on every call and give the buyer an easy way to say no.

            - name: Call review scorecard with behaviour anchors
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                A scorecard turns a 40 minute recording into a few comparable numbers, but only if each score is tied to something a reviewer can point to on the call. Anchoring every line to a heard behaviour stops two managers giving the same call a 2 and a 4, and lets sellers see exactly what moved their score.

                ## Milestones
                1. A scorecard of eight to ten lines drawn from the skills model.
                2. Each line scored on a three-point scale with an example behaviour for each point.
                3. A free-text box for one strength and one change kept at the bottom.
                4. The scorecard tested on three recorded calls and the confusing lines rewritten.

                ## Notes
                Three points beats five: reviewers agree more often and argue less. Score what happened on the call, not what the seller should have known.
              priority: high
              deadlineOffsetDays: 21
              frontmatter:
                mode: building
                output_kind: artifact
                success_criteria: "A scorecard of eight to ten anchored lines, tested on three real calls by two reviewers who reach the same score on most lines."
                cadence: one-shot
                effort_hours_estimate: "4"
              tasks:
                - "Pick eight to ten scorecard lines from the skills model"
                - "Write an example behaviour for each score on a three-point scale"
                - "Score three recorded calls with the draft scorecard"
                - "Rewrite every line two reviewers scored differently"

# Sales Coaching & Call Review

This area is for sales managers, team leads and the sellers they coach, built around recorded calls and the regular conversations that turn them into better selling. It starts with the foundations (recording consent, a skills model, an anchored scorecard, baselines and protected time), then the weekly and monthly review machinery, the coaching skills worth practising, the improvements and decisions that keep the programme honest, the moments worth preparing for, situations such as player-coaches, new managers and remote teams, and finally the specialist work of linking skills to results, call trackers and coaching the coaches.

What repeats is a Tuesday call review block and Thursday skill sessions, sellers' Friday self-reviews and Wednesday peer swaps, monthly trend checks, team listening hours and coaching log read-backs, quarterly rescoring, calibration, focus skills and retrospectives, and a yearly recording audit and programme review. The Skills matrix, 1:1, Metrics log, Development plan and Feedback themes templates pair with the projects that name them.
