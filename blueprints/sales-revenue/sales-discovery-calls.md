            - name: Discovery call exit criteria
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Without a written definition of a finished discovery call, every call ends with a pleasant chat and a vague promise to send something over. Agreeing the five or six facts a call must establish, such as the problem, its cost, why now, budget range, decision process and an agreed next step, gives every later question, note and recap a target to aim at.

                ## Milestones
                1. Five or six exit criteria written as facts a call must establish, each in one line.
                2. Each criterion paired with what a good enough answer looks like.
                3. A rule written for what happens when a call misses two or more criteria.
                4. The criteria pinned where you see them before every call.

                ## Notes
                Keep the list short enough to remember mid-call. A discovery call that meets four criteria and books a second conversation is a good call; perfect information on the first try is rare.
              priority: high
              deadlineOffsetDays: 14
              frontmatter:
                mode: building

                success_criteria: "A one-page list of five or six discovery exit criteria, each with a good enough answer described, used on the next five calls."
                cadence: one-shot
                effort_hours_estimate: "2"
              tasks:

                - "Turn those gaps into five or six exit criteria, one line each"
                - "Describe what a good enough answer looks like for each criterion"

# Sales Discovery Calls

This area is for sellers, founders and freelancers who run first conversations with buyers and want to leave each one knowing the problem, what it costs, why it matters now, the money available and how the decision gets made. It starts with the foundations (exit criteria, a question bank, pre-call research, an opening script, a notes template and the recap email), then the routines that keep calls sharp, the questioning skills worth practising, the improvements and decisions that raise conversion, the high-stakes calls worth preparing for, the situations founders, freelancers and teams each face, and finally the specialist work of executive, technical and pattern-level discovery.

What repeats is a short prep block and same-day notes on every call day, a weekly self-review and buyer language capture, monthly checks on talk time, conversion, next steps and no-shows, a quarterly question bank refresh and pattern review, and a yearly look at your exit criteria and consent wording. The Operational checklist, Meeting notes, Metrics log and Feedback themes templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
