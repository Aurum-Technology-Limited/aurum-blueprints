            - name: Diagnosing where the current sales approach stalls
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Before choosing any methodology, find out where deals actually die: in discovery that never surfaces a real problem, in proposals that go quiet, or in late-stage price fights. Ten recorded calls and the last twelve months of stage-to-stage conversion usually show the pattern within a fortnight. A methodology bought to fix the wrong problem is the most common reason adoption fails.

                ## Milestones
                1. Stage-to-stage conversion rates for the last twelve months pulled from the CRM.
                2. Ten recorded calls from at least four reps listened to with notes on what was missing.
                3. Fifteen lost deals reviewed for the stage and reason they were lost.
                4. A one-page diagnosis naming the two or three stages where deals stall most.

                ## Notes
                Include calls from top performers as well as strugglers. The gap between them is often the clearest description of the method you need.
              priority: high
              deadlineOffsetDays: 30
              frontmatter:
                mode: research
                output_kind: knowledge
                success_criteria: "A one-page diagnosis, built from conversion data, ten call recordings and fifteen lost deals, names the stages where deals stall most."
                cadence: one-shot
                effort_hours_estimate: "12"
              tasks:
                - "Export last year's opportunities with stage history from the CRM"
                - "Pick ten recorded calls across strong and struggling reps"
                - "Note what each call lacked in problem, impact and next step"
                - "Write the one-page diagnosis of where deals stall"

# Sales Methodology Adoption

This area is for sales leaders, front-line managers and enterprise sales teams who want a selling methodology such as Challenger, SPIN, Sandler or a value-selling approach to change how deals are actually run, not just how the training week felt. It starts with the foundations (diagnosing what is broken, comparing methods, a sponsor, a budget, a baseline and a rollout plan), then the operating rhythms that reinforce the method, the specific skills each method asks for, the decisions and fixes that keep adoption on course, the dated moments from launch workshop to the ninety-day checkpoint, the situations new hires, sceptics, account managers and merged teams bring, and finally specialist work such as an in-house trainer bench and behaviour analytics.
