# Sales Objection Handling

This area is for anyone who hears not now, too expensive or we already have someone, and wants a better answer next time: sellers on a team, freelancers quoting their own rates and small business owners who do the selling themselves. It starts with the foundations (an inventory of real objections, a shared log, categories, a ranked top ten and one agreed way of answering), then the routines that keep the log and the responses current, the spoken skills behind a good answer, the decisions that stop objections arising, the events worth preparing for, the situations that change the picture, and finally the analysis an experienced team runs.

What repeats is a two-minute objection note at the end of each selling day, a Friday log catch-up, a weekly team drill, a monthly trend review and a monthly note of themes for product and marketing, a quarterly refresh of the response library and an annual review. The Metrics log, Feedback themes, Training program and Skills matrix templates pair with the projects that name them.
