            - name: Board purpose and role statement
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Many boards cannot say in a sentence what they do that management does not, so meetings slide into either rubber stamping or second-guessing operations. A one-page statement of the board's role, covering direction, oversight of performance and risk, appointing and supporting the chief executive, and accountability to owners or members, gives every later choice about members and agendas a reference point.

                ## Milestones
                1. A list of the board's core responsibilities drawn from the governing documents and applicable law, confirmed with the company secretary or a lawyer.
                2. A one-page role statement separating the board's work from management's.
                3. The statement discussed at a board meeting and any disagreements recorded.
                4. The adopted version filed in the board handbook with the date approved.

                ## Notes
                Keep it to one page. A long statement gets read once; a short one gets quoted in meetings when the board starts drifting into operations.
              priority: high
              deadlineOffsetDays: 30
              frontmatter:
                mode: building
                output_kind: artifact
                success_criteria: "A one-page board role statement is approved at a board meeting and filed in the board handbook with its approval date."
                cadence: one-shot
                effort_hours_estimate: "5"
              tasks:
                - "Pull the board duties clauses from the articles, bylaws or constitution"
                - "Draft a one-page statement of what the board does and does not do"
                - "Ask the chair and chief executive to mark up the draft"
                - "Table the statement for approval at the next board meeting"

              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Before recruiting anyone or changing structure, you need a plain table of who is on the board today, how they got there and when their terms end.                 ## Milestones
                1. A table of every director with appointment date, term end, independence status, committee seats and who appointed them.
                2. Each committee listed with its members, chair, last meeting date and whether written terms of reference exist.
                3. Attendance for the last twelve months recorded for each director.
                4. Lapsed terms, vacancies and inactive committees flagged for the chair.
              priority: high

            - name: Board skills and experience matrix
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Boards recruit people they know and end up with four finance experts and nobody who understands the customer, technology or the regulator. A matrix that scores each director against the eight to twelve capabilities your strategy needs over the next three years shows gaps before a vacancy arises, so recruitment starts from need rather than from whoever is available.

                ## Milestones
                1. Eight to twelve capabilities agreed with the chair, each tied to a strategic priority or risk.
                2. Every director's self-assessment collected and moderated by the chair.
                3. A completed matrix showing depth and gaps for each capability.
                4. The top two gaps named as the brief for the next recruitment.

                ## Notes
                Start from the **Skills matrix** template. Include diversity of background and lived experience as a separate view, not as one more column to tick.
              priority: high
              deadlineOffsetDays: 45

            - name: Governing documents health check
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Articles of association, bylaws, a charity's constitution and any shareholders' agreement decide how directors are appointed, removed and how many must attend for a decision to count. Old documents often contain quorum rules, appointment rights or term limits the board is not following, which can put decisions in question later.

            - name: Chair and chief executive role split

                ## Purpose

                ## Purpose

            - name: Minutes and board action log

                ## Notes

# Board Composition and Effectiveness

This area is for founders forming a first real board, chairs and company secretaries in larger organisations, and nonprofit leaders working with trustees. It starts with foundations (what the board is for, who sits on it, the skills matrix, governing documents and reserved matters), moves to the operating machinery of board packs, minutes, calendars and director pipelines, then the skills of chairing, challenge and reading papers, the decisions about size, terms and recruitment, the dated evaluations and general meetings, situations particular to founders, nonprofits and groups, and finally the specialist work of board dynamics and reporting on effectiveness.
