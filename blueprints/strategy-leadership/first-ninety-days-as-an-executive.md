                ## Purpose
                Executives who arrive having read the annual report, the last three sets of results and the published strategy spend their first fortnight asking better questions instead of basic ones.                 ## Milestones
                1. The last three annual reports or results statements read, with the five numbers that matter most to your remit noted.
                2. A one-page summary of the published strategy, recent leadership changes and press coverage from the past two years.

            - name: Diagnosing the situation you are walking into
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                A business in decline, one starting something new, one that needs realigning and one that is already succeeding each demand a different first ninety days, and leaders who misread which one they are in apply the wrong playbook. Naming the situation for each part of your remit, with evidence, tells you how fast to move, what to change and what to leave alone.

                ## Milestones
                1. Each unit or function in your remit classed as start-up, turnaround, realignment or sustaining success.

# First Ninety Days as an Executive

This area is for anyone starting a senior leadership role, whether hired in from outside, promoted from within or sent into a larger enterprise, and it covers the stretch from accepting the offer to the ninety-day review. It runs in order: preparation and diagnosis before and just after day one, the operating habits that keep the transition honest, the knowledge you need to acquire quickly, the early decisions on priorities, team and wins, the dated checkpoints at thirty, sixty and ninety days, the variants for different starting situations, and finally the advanced work of correcting course and helping other leaders through the same passage.

What repeats is a Friday transition review, a weekly hour with your boss, weekly one-to-ones with each direct report, a weekly note to your team and a Monday calendar audit, with monthly checks on peers, finance figures, front-line contact, your advice network, your commitments ledger, risks and derailment warning signs, each on a different day. The Onboarding 30/60/90, Meeting notes, 1:1, Skills matrix and Risk register templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
