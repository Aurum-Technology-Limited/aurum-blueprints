            - name: Offsite purpose statement and decision list
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Most offsites that end in goodwill and nothing else were planned around a theme rather than a list of decisions. Writing one sentence of purpose and the three to five decisions the team must leave with gives every later choice, from the guest list to the agenda, something to be tested against.

                ## Milestones
                1. A one-sentence purpose that a team member could repeat without notes.
                2. Three to five decisions written as questions with a yes, no or choice answer.
                3. Each decision tagged as decide in the room, shape for later, or inform only.
                4. The sponsor's written agreement to the purpose and the decision list.

                ## Notes
                If you cannot name a single decision, you probably need a team day or a dinner, not an offsite. That is a fine outcome too.
              priority: high
              deadlineOffsetDays: 14
              frontmatter:
                mode: building
                output_kind: artifact
                success_criteria: "A one-page purpose and decision list, with each decision tagged by type, is agreed in writing by the sponsor."
                cadence: one-shot
                effort_hours_estimate: "3"
              tasks:
                - "Write one sentence finishing the phrase: by the end of the offsite we will have"
                - "List every decision the team keeps deferring in normal meetings"
                - "Cut the list to five and phrase each as a question"
                - "Send the draft to the sponsor and book twenty minutes to agree it"

# Leadership Offsite Planning

This area is for managers and leadership teams who run offsites and retreats, from a first half-day away with a team of six to a two-day residential for forty leaders. It starts with the foundations (a written purpose, interviews, the guest list, budget, dates and venue), then the machinery that runs between offsites, the facilitation skills, the design decisions, the dated events themselves, the awkward situations such as an offsite after layoffs, and finally the specialist work of large and confidential sessions.

What repeats is a weekly action status update, a monthly commitments check-in, a monthly sweep of the parking lot and topic pipeline, a quarterly look at cost and value, and a yearly offsite calendar and facilitator review. The Vendor, Operational checklist, Feedback themes, Metrics log and Trip templates pair with the projects that name them.
