            - name: Booking and protecting the strategy day
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Owners who wait for a quiet week to think about the business never find one, because the quiet week never comes. Putting a full day in the diary six to eight weeks out, at a place away from the shop floor or the kitchen table, and telling staff and family it is fixed, is the step that makes everything else in this area happen.

                ## Milestones
                1. A full day six to eight weeks away blocked in your calendar.
                2. A venue chosen away from the premises, such as a library room, a hired meeting room or a friend's office.
                3. Staff, key customers and your household told the date is not available.
                4. A plan written for calls and email on the day: who checks them and when you look.

                ## Notes
                Pick a date after your year-end figures will be ready, and well clear of your busiest trading weeks. A day squeezed between deliveries usually shrinks to half a day.
              priority: high
              deadlineOffsetDays: 14
              frontmatter:
                mode: building
                output_kind: decision

                cadence: one-shot
                effort_hours_estimate: "1"
              tasks:

                - "Book a quiet room away from the business premises"
                - "Tell staff and your household that the date is fixed"
                - "Decide who will handle calls and messages while you are away"

            - name: Year-in-numbers pack for the strategy day
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Strategy days drift into opinion when nobody has the figures in front of them. A short pack of month-by-month sales, gross margin, overheads, net profit, cash in the bank and money owed to and by the business, set beside the previous year, gives the day a factual starting point and usually throws up two or three surprises.

                ## Milestones
                1. Twelve months of sales, gross profit, overheads and net profit laid out by month, with the prior year alongside.
                2. Month-end cash balances, plus amounts owed by customers and to suppliers at year end.
                3. The three biggest changes on last year highlighted, each with a one-line explanation.
                4. The pack checked with your bookkeeper or accountant and printed for the day.

                ## Notes

# Small Business Owner Strategy Day

This area is for the owner of a small business, or the founder of a young company, who keeps meaning to step back from the day-to-day and decide where the business is going. It starts with the preparation (a protected date, cover for the day, the year in numbers, your own goals and what staff and customers say), then the day itself and the one-page plan it produces, the routines that keep that plan alive, the skills that make the numbers speak, the decisions owners usually put off, variants for sole traders, partners, seasonal businesses and early-stage founders, and finally the specialist work of a three-year picture, cash scenarios and a business that runs without you.

What repeats is a weekly hour on the business and a five-number scoreboard every Monday, a monthly numbers review and team check-in, a quarterly half-day and time audit, and the yearly booking of the next strategy day with its accountant and price reviews. The Meeting notes, Metrics log, Purchase decision and Board meeting templates pair with the projects that name them.
