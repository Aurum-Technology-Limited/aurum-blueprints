# Azure Solutions Architect Expert

This area is for engineers who already hold, or are close to holding, the Azure Administrator Associate and want the Expert credential that sits on top of it by passing AZ-305, the design exam. It starts with foundations (the current study guide, the prerequisite on your transcript, a self-assessment, a cold practice score, a dated calendar and a cost-guarded subscription for design proofs), then the weekly study and case study machinery, the design topics from identity and governance through data, continuity, compute, networking and migration, the decisions about practice material, readiness and exam delivery, booking, exam day and the first renewal, plans for administrators, developers, architects arriving from another cloud, partner consultants, working parents and second-language candidates, and specialist work such as a published reference design and running design reviews at work.

The rhythm is three study sessions a week, a daily flashcard set, a weekly case study, a Friday wrong-answer review and a Saturday Architecture Center read, a monthly timed practice assessment with a plan reset, a monthly Azure updates check and twice-monthly whiteboard sessions with a peer, plus a quarterly study guide check and the yearly renewal assessment. The Skills matrix, Reading queue, Metrics log, Portfolio piece and Development plan templates pair with it. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
