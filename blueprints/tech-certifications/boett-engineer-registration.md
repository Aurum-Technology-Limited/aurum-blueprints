            - name: Checking your degree against the Board's academic requirements
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                First the Board looks at at whether your qualification is recognised: an accredited engineering degree is the simplest case, while degrees from unaccredited programmes, foreign universities or part-time routes may need extra assessment. Knowing which case you are early decides whether your application takes the usual path or needs supporting evidence first.

                ## Milestones
                1. Your programme's accreditation status at the year you graduated confirmed from the accrediting body or university.
                2. Whether your degree falls under an international accord the Board recognises, such as the Washington Accord, noted.
                3. The Board's position on your qualification confirmed in writing if it is not straightforward.
                4. Any extra assessment, course or evidence the Board asks for listed with a plan.

                ## Notes
                Accreditation applies by programme and by year. A degree from a university that is accredited today may not have been accredited in the year you graduated.

# BOETT Engineer Registration

This area takes an engineer in Trinidad and Tobago from first reading the Board of Engineering's requirements to holding registration and keeping it in good standing, whether you graduated last year, came home from working abroad or have practised for a decade without getting round to it. It starts with foundations (how the Board works, choosing a category, checking your degree, a dated timeline, documents and referees), moves through the evidence and follow-up routines, the writing and interview skills, the decisions about gaps, mentors and dual registration, the submission itself, then variants for graduates, energy, construction and public sector engineers, returners, foreign-qualified engineers and technologists, and ends with the work of a registered engineer who signs, referees and serves.

What repeats is a monthly evidence note and supervisor sign-off, a monthly CPD entry and mentor meeting, quarterly referee check-ins and readiness reviews, and the yearly renewal, register check and ethics reread. The Skills matrix, Person, Compliance control, Development plan and Remediation plan templates pair with competence mapping, referees, renewal, gap closing and a deferred application.
