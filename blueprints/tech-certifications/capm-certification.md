# CAPM Certification

This area takes you from wondering whether the CAPM is worth it to holding the credential and using it, whether you are a student finishing a degree or someone at work who keeps ending up running projects without the title. It opens with foundations (the fit decision, current PMI rules, membership, the education hours, a diagnostic and a dated plan), then the weekly study machinery, topic reviews across fundamentals, predictive planning, agile and business analysis, the decisions about question banks, delivery and readiness, the booking, mock and exam day, and variants for students, career changers, busy parents and retakes.

The rhythm is protected study blocks twice a week, a short daily question set and glossary review, a weekly wrong-answer review, and a monthly timed exam, accuracy check and plan reset. The Purchase decision, Habit tracker, Metrics log and Resume templates pair with choosing a course, keeping study blocks, tracking scores by domain and updating your CV once you pass. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
