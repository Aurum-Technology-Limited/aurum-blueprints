# CISM Certification

This area is for people who run, or are about to run, information security as a management discipline and want ISACA's Certified Information Security Manager credential to prove it. It opens with foundations (an experience check against the current rules, the exam content outline, the choice between CISM and its neighbours, official study materials, a baseline score and a dated plan), then the weekly study machinery, the four domains of governance, risk management, the security programme and incident management, decisions about courses, delivery mode and readiness, booking, exam day and the certification application, plans for team managers, engineers moving into management, auditors, sponsored cohorts and new security leads, and specialist work such as board reporting, mentoring and the next ISACA credential.

The rhythm is three study sessions a week, a short daily question set and flashcards, a weekly wrong-answer review, a study group check-in and one idea applied at work, a monthly timed mock, score update and plan reset, a monthly entry in the experience log, and quarterly and yearly checks on ISACA changes, CPE hours and the maintenance fee. The Purchase decision, Habit tracker, Metrics log, Risk register, Remediation plan, Course, Onboarding 30/60/90 and Resume templates pair with it.
