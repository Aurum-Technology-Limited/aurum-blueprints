# CompTIA A+ Certification

This area takes you from wondering whether CompTIA A+ is the right first IT credential to holding it and using it to land or grow a support role, whether you are a student, a career changer from retail or hospitality, a parent studying in short evenings, or the person everyone already calls when the laptop stops working. It opens with foundations (the fit decision, the current objectives, a diagnostic, materials, a toolkit, a tracker and a dated plan), then the study machinery, the Core 1 topics from hardware, laptops, printers and networking to virtualisation and troubleshooting, the Core 2 topics from Windows, macOS and Linux to security and operational procedures, the booking and exam-day projects, variants for different lives, and the work an experienced technician moves on to.

The rhythm is two protected study blocks a week, a short daily question set, flashcards twice a week, a weekly wrong-answer review, lab session and performance-based question drill, and a monthly timed practice exam with a re-rating of every objective. The Skills matrix, Purchase decision, Course, Metrics log, Job application and Portfolio piece templates pair with the objectives tracker, choosing materials, the study plan, score tracking, applications and a repair case study. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
