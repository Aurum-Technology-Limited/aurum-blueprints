# CompTIA Network+ Certification

This area takes you from deciding whether Network+ is your next exam to holding the certification and using it, whether you are a student meeting networking for the first time, a help desk technician aiming for a network role, or an engineer from another discipline who keeps getting stuck on DNS and VLANs. It opens with foundations (the fit decision, the current objectives, a baseline score, a dated plan, your course and tools), then the study machinery, topic projects across networking concepts, implementation, operations, security and troubleshooting, the decisions about vouchers, delivery and weak domains, the booking, mock and exam day, variants for students, career changers, sponsored candidates and retakes, and three pieces of specialist work for after the pass.

The rhythm is two protected study blocks a week, a ten-minute subnetting drill and a ports deck every day, a weekly wrong-answer review, lab session and troubleshooting scenario, and a monthly timed practice exam with a domain score check. The Habit tracker, Metrics log, Purchase decision, Remediation plan, Resume and Portfolio piece templates pair with keeping study blocks, tracking scores by domain, choosing a course and voucher, fixing a weak domain, updating your CV and writing up labs. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
