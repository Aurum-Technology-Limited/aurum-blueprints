            - name: Choosing between EngTech and IEng registration
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                EngTech recognises people who apply proven techniques and procedures to solve practical problems, while IEng recognises those who maintain and manage existing and emerging technology, often with design and management responsibility. Applying at the grade your current role actually evidences is the single biggest factor in a first-time success, and aiming one grade too high usually means a deferral and months of rewriting.

                ## Milestones
                1. The EngTech and IEng competence statements read side by side from the current UK-SPEC.
                2. Your last three years of work marked against each grade's five competence areas.
                3. A conversation held with a registered colleague or mentor about which grade fits now.
                4. One grade chosen, with a sentence on why, and a note on when to revisit the other.

                ## Notes
                Many people register as EngTech first and move to IEng once their responsibility grows. That is a normal route, not a lesser one.
              priority: high

# Incorporated Engineer and EngTech

This area takes a practising engineer or technician from wondering whether they qualify to holding Incorporated Engineer or Engineering Technician registration through a UK licensed institution, and then on toward Chartered Engineer if they want it. It starts with foundations (choosing the grade, checking current rules, picking the institution, testing your qualifications and building a dated plan), moves through the evidence machinery and competence-by-competence writing skills, then route decisions, the submission and the review, with variants for apprentices, service leavers, technicians without formal qualifications, overseas engineers and parents, and ends with specialist steps such as IEng to CEng and serving as a reviewer.

The rhythm is a weekly writing block, a monthly evidence capture and mentor conversation, a quarterly competence map refresh and supervisor sign-off, and a yearly fee, ethics and CPD check once you are registered. The Skills matrix, Development plan and 1:1 templates pair with the gap assessment, the registration plan and mentor meetings.
