# ITIL 4 Foundation Certification

This area takes you from first hearing that your organisation "runs on ITIL" to holding the Foundation certificate and using its vocabulary with confidence, whether you work on a service desk, lead an IT team, write code or run a shared service outside IT. It starts with foundations (the current syllabus, a study route, the official book, a baseline paper and a dated plan), then the weekly study machinery, the core concepts, the four dimensions, the guiding principles, the value system and the practices one by one, followed by exam technique decisions, booking and exam day, variants for service desk analysts, team leads, developers, career switchers, busy parents and second-language candidates, and ends with keeping the credential current and choosing the next module.

The rhythm is two study blocks a week, a short daily recall session, a weekly practice question log, a Friday note of ITIL seen at work and two timed mocks a month, plus a yearly certificate check once you have passed. The Purchase decision, Course, Development plan, Metrics log and Resume templates pair with choosing a course, the study plan, the conversation with your manager, tracking mock scores and your CV. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
