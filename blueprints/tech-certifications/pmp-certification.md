# PMP Certification

This area is for people who already run projects, with or without the title, and want the PMI Project Management Professional credential to show for it. It starts with foundations (eligibility, the experience inventory, the 35 contact hours, a baseline score, a dated study calendar and an application that survives an audit), then the weekly study machinery, the exam content from the people, process and business environment domains across predictive, agile and hybrid work, the decisions about question banks, delivery mode and readiness, booking and exam day, plans for accidental project managers, line managers, working parents, second-language candidates and sponsored cohorts, and specialist work such as mentoring others and the first renewal cycle.

The rhythm is three study sessions and a short daily question set, a weekly wrong-answer review, flashcards, a study partner check-in and a note on applying one idea at work, a monthly timed mock, domain score update and plan reset, and quarterly checks for exam outline changes and earned PDUs. The Purchase decision, Course, Metrics log, 1:1, Skills matrix, Reading queue and Development plan templates pair with it. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
