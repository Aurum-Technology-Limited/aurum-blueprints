# PRINCE2 Foundation and Practitioner

This area takes you from first deciding on PRINCE2 to holding Foundation and Practitioner, whether you already run projects or have just been told your organisation expects the method. It starts with foundations (the current scheme and edition, how to study, the official manual, a diagnostic and a dated plan), then the weekly study machinery, the principles, people, practices and processes one by one, tailoring, the exam decisions and both exam days, with variants for project board members, new project managers, busy parents, public-sector teams and second-language candidates, and ends with renewal, PRINCE2 Agile and building a tailored method for your own team.

The rhythm is two study blocks a week, a short daily recall session, a weekly practice question log and manual drill, a monthly timed mock and a yearly renewal check once you hold Practitioner. The Purchase decision, Metrics log, Course, Development plan, Risk register and Resume templates pair with choosing a course, tracking scores, the study plan, the conversation with your manager, risk practice and your CV. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
