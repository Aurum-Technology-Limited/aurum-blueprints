            - name: Choosing between PSPO and CSPO
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Two credentials dominate this field and they work differently: Scrum.org's PSPO I is an online assessment you can sit without a course, while Scrum Alliance's CSPO is earned by attending a course with an approved trainer and is renewed every two years. Hiring managers rarely care which one you hold, but your budget, your employer and how you learn should decide it before you spend anything.

                ## Milestones
                1. A one-page comparison of PSPO I and CSPO covering cost, course requirement, assessment, renewal and recognition in your market.
                2. Five recent product owner job adverts in your area checked for which credential they name, if any.
                3. Your employer's training policy checked for which providers it already pays for.
                4. One credential chosen, with the reason written in two sentences.

                ## Notes
                Other routes exist, such as ICAgile's product ownership certification or the SAFe product owner course. If your organisation runs SAFe, that course belongs in your SAFe planning rather than here.
              priority: high
              deadlineOffsetDays: 14
              frontmatter:
                mode: research
                output_kind: decision
                success_criteria: "A written choice of PSPO I, CSPO or another named credential, backed by a comparison table and five job adverts reviewed."

                - "Read the PSPO I page on Scrum.org and the CSPO page on Scrum Alliance"

                - "Ask HR or your manager which training providers the company already funds"
                - "Write the chosen credential and your reason at the top of your study notes"

# Product Owner Certification

This area is for anyone earning a Product Owner credential, whether that is Scrum.org's PSPO assessments or Scrum Alliance's CSPO course, and then doing the job well enough that the letters mean something. It starts with foundations (choosing the credential, checking the current rules, a close reading of the Scrum Guide and a baseline score), then the weekly study and backlog machinery, the product skills the assessment tests, the course, assessment day and what follows a pass, the improvements that show the skill at work, plans for analysts, project managers, developers and people in agencies or public bodies moving into the role, and advanced credentials and mentoring.

The rhythm is two weekly study blocks, a wrong-answer review and study group, weekly backlog refinement, a monthly backlog health check, value metrics and decision log, and quarterly roadmap and stakeholder reviews. The Development plan, Metrics log, Meeting notes, Feedback themes, Resume and Portfolio piece templates pair with it. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
