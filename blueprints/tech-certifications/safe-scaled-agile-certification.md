              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Scaled Agile ties each certification to a specific course: Leading SAFe leads to SAFe Agilist, SAFe for Teams to SAFe Practitioner, and there are role-based courses for Scrum Masters, Product Owners and Product Managers, Release Train Engineers and others. Picking the one that matches the role you hold now, or the one your organisation is about to ask of you, saves a second course fee and makes the material easier to apply.                 4. One course chosen, with the reason recorded at the top of your study notes.

# SAFe Scaled Agile Certification

This area is for knowledge workers and enterprise teams studying for a Scaled Agile Framework credential, most often SAFe Agilist through the Leading SAFe course, and for the managers who sponsor them. It starts with foundations (which course, the current exam rules, a trainer, funding and a dated study plan), then the study and practice machinery, the framework knowledge the exam tests, honest decisions about whether scaled agile suits your organisation, the course, exam and badge milestones, plans for managers, parents, contractors and training leads, and the specialist routes to RTE, Lean Portfolio Manager, Architect and SPC.

The rhythm is a weekly framework reading block, a weekly error log review and one practice tried at work each iteration, a short daily recall, monthly mentor, community and flow metrics check-ins, and a yearly membership renewal. The Purchase decision, Course, 1:1, Metrics log, Resume and Development plan templates pair with it. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
