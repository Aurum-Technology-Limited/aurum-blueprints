                ## Purpose
                Two credentials dominate Scrum Master hiring: Scrum.org's PSM I, which you can sit without a course, and Scrum Alliance's CSM, which comes with a required live course and a renewal cycle. The right one depends on how you learn, who is paying and what employers in your market ask for, so settle it before spending money on either.

                ## Milestones

                2. The cost, course requirement and renewal terms of PSM I and CSM written side by side from the official websites.

                4. One credential chosen as the first target, with the reason in two sentences.

                ## Notes

# Scrum Master Certification

This area takes you from choosing a Scrum Master credential, usually PSM I or CSM, to passing it and using it well with a real team, whether you are a knowledge worker moving into the role or part of a team adopting Scrum together. It starts with foundations (the choice, current rules, a first read of the Scrum Guide, a baseline score and a dated plan), moves through the study routines and topic reviews of theory, accountabilities, events, artifacts and values, then team improvements, the course and assessment day, situations such as project managers, developers and career changers stepping into the role, and finally advanced credentials and scaling.

The rhythm is ten practice questions a day, a weekly guide reread, wrong-answer review and study group, a monthly timed mock and plan reset, and a yearly renewal check for credentials that need one. The Purchase decision, Habit tracker, Metrics log, Meeting notes and Reading queue templates pair with the course choice, daily practice, mistake tracking, the study group and the reading list. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
