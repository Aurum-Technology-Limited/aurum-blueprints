              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Collectors who buy for love and investors who buy for return make different decisions about what to pay, how to store and when to sell, and muddling the two is how people end up with costly objects they neither enjoy nor can sell well. Writing down which motive drives each category you hold sets the rules for everything else in this area.

                ## Milestones

                4. The note shared with whoever shares the household budget.

                Published long-run studies of art and wine returns generally find them lower and far more uneven than headline indices suggest once buying and selling costs are counted. Treat any gain as a bonus unless you have specific evidence.

Check decisions that matter with a qualified, licensed adviser in your country, and remember that investing and trading can lose money.

# Alternative Collectible Investments

This area is for anyone who owns or wants to buy art, fine wine, watches or classic cars and would like the money side handled as carefully as the pleasure side. It starts with the foundations (why you collect, a cap on how much of your wealth sits in objects, an inventory, the true cost of buying and selling, insurance and paperwork), then the routines that keep a collection insured, stored and valued, the skills of reading catalogues and spotting fakes, the decisions about selling routes, storage and restoration, events such as a first auction or a consignment, situations like inheriting or selling down a collection, and finally specialist work such as lending to exhibitions and building a scholarly catalogue.

What repeats is a quarterly register update, a monthly look at storage conditions and comparable sales, monthly runs for a classic car and bottle choices for the cellar, and yearly jobs such as the valuation refresh, the policy check, the performance review and the allocation check against your cap. The Vendor, Metrics log, Purchase decision, Risk register and Operational checklist templates pair with the adviser shortlist, the comparable sales log, the storage choice, the security review and the collection move.
