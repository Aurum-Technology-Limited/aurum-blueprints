                Plenty of people pay for ongoing advice they do not need, while others go without it at the one moment it would have prevented a costly mistake, such as taking a pension lump sum or giving up a guaranteed pension.                 ## Notes
                Some decisions, such as giving up a guaranteed pension above a set value, may legally require regulated advice in your country. Check this before ruling advice out.

Check decisions that matter with a qualified, licensed adviser in your country, and remember that investing and trading can lose money.

# Choosing a Financial Adviser

Choosing a financial adviser is one of the few money decisions where the person you hire goes on to shape most of the decisions that follow. These projects start with whether you need advice at all and how fees and adviser types work, move through finding, checking and comparing firms, then cover the meetings, the paperwork and the yearly habits of a good client, with variants for retirees, couples, attorneys and anyone facing a windfall, a divorce or a pension transfer.

What repeats is deliberately light: a quarterly read of valuations and scam warnings, a monthly filing session and life change check, and a yearly round of review preparation, register checks and a stay or switch verdict. The Purchase decision, Vendor and Meeting notes templates pair with the comparison, appointment and meeting log projects. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
