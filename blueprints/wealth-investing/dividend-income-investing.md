# Dividend Income Investing

This area is for anyone who wants their investments to pay a growing income, whether that income is decades away or already paying bills in retirement. It starts with the foundations: what the income is for, a target, an inventory of what you already own, the funds or shares choice, reinvestment settings and tax. It then moves through the routines that keep payments reconciled and reinvested, the skills for judging whether a dividend can last, decisions on spread, placement and cuts, yearly events, situations for beginners, retirees, couples and heirs, and finally specialist corners such as withholding tax, registrar holdings and stress tests.

What repeats is a monthly reconciliation, a monthly sweep of idle dividend cash, a monthly corporate actions check and, for retirees, a fixed monthly income transfer; a quarterly look at announcements, position limits and cost records; and a yearly round of tax packs, safety scorecards, allowance checks and the annual income review. The Metrics log, Operational checklist, Purchase decision and Risk register templates pair with the projects that name them.
