              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Ethical investing goes wrong when it starts from a fund brochure instead of from what you actually care about. Spending an evening listing the issues that matter to you, ranking them and noting which ones you would pay or accept lower diversification for gives every later fund decision a test to pass. Most people end up with three to six issues that genuinely drive their choices.

                ## Milestones
                1. A list of every issue you care about in relation to money, from climate and weapons to labour rights and animal welfare.
                2. The list ranked, with the top three to six marked as the ones that will steer decisions.
                3. A sentence for each top issue saying whether you want to avoid harm, reward better companies or fund solutions.
                4. The map reread a week later and adjusted where first instincts did not hold up.

                ## Notes
                Keep it personal. A values map borrowed from a campaign group or a fund provider tends to include issues you do not feel strongly about and miss ones you do.

                Before you start: this plan is general information to help you organise your own money decisions. It is not financial, investment, tax or legal advice. Check decisions that matter with a qualified, licensed adviser in your country, and remember that investing and trading can lose money.

            - name: Red lines and preferences list
              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                A red line is something you will not hold at any price, such as controversial weapons or tobacco makers, while a preference is something you would like less of but can tolerate in a broad fund. Separating the two stops the search collapsing because no fund is perfect, and it gives you a clear threshold to apply, such as no company earning more than 5 or 10 percent of revenue from a given activity.

                ## Milestones
                1. Each priority issue from the values map sorted into red line or preference.
                2. A revenue threshold written beside each red line, or a note that any involvement rules a company out.
                3. Preferences written as directions, such as less fossil fuel exposure than the market.
                4. No more than five red lines, so a diversified portfolio is still possible.

                ## Notes
                Fund screens usually work on revenue thresholds, so write yours the same way and you can compare them directly with fund documents.
              priority: high
              deadlineOffsetDays: 30
              frontmatter:
                mode: building
                output_kind: decision
                success_criteria: "A written list of no more than five red lines with revenue thresholds, plus preferences stated as directions of travel."
                cadence: one-shot
                effort_hours_estimate: "2"
              tasks:
                - "Sort each issue on your values map into red line or preference"
                - "Choose a revenue threshold for each red line, or mark it as zero tolerance"
                - "Rewrite each preference as a direction, such as less than the market average"
                - "Cut the red lines to five or fewer if the list has grown longer"

# Ethical & Impact Investing

Ethical and impact investing is for anyone who would rather their savings did not fund things they object to, and who wants some of it to do measurable good, without ending up in three fashionable theme funds and no diversification. The area starts with the foundations (a values map, written red lines, a look-through of what you already hold, an approach and a fund shortlist), then the routines that keep holdings honest, what is worth learning about ratings, labels, voting and carbon numbers, the decisions on fossil fuels, active versus tracker funds and community investment, a few dated events, variants for new investors, couples, faith-based investors, retirees and heirs, and finally specialist work such as direct engagement, shareholder resolutions and private impact funds.

What repeats is a monthly controversy scan and contribution check, a quarterly look-through of your largest holdings and their labels, spring voting at company meetings, and a yearly round of stewardship reports, impact figures and a values portfolio review. The Purchase decision, Metrics log, Reading queue and Meeting notes templates pair with the platform choice, the impact log, the reading list and your first company meeting.
