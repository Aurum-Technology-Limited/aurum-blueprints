                Before any money moves, it helps to write down what you are actually trying to do: help with a first home, pay for education, reduce a future inheritance tax bill, or simply see the family enjoy the money now.                 ## Purpose
                Generosity that leaves you short at 85 helps nobody, and a gift once made usually cannot be taken back. Testing a proposed gift against a long-life plan, including a period of paid care, shows how much you can give without relying on the family to support you later. The result is a ceiling you can quote to yourself whenever a request arrives.

                5. A maximum total you are willing to give in the next five years recorded.

                ## Notes

# Family Trusts & Lifetime Gifting

This area is for grandparents and parents who want to help the next generation while they are alive, through outright gifts, regular payments from income or a trust, without putting their own retirement at risk or leaving a muddle for the family. It starts with the foundations (goals, an affordability test, a register of past gifts, the rules where you live and a well-prepared first meeting with a specialist), then the machinery that keeps gifts and trusts in order, the knowledge trustees and givers need, the bigger decisions about trust types, trustees and loans, the dated events such as a house deposit or a trust signing, situations like blended families or a disabled beneficiary, and finally specialist work on changing trustees, anniversary charges and winding a trust up.

What repeats is a monthly entry in the gift register, a monthly look for distribution requests, a quarterly trust investment review, and a yearly round of allowance checks, trustee meetings, trust tax filings, beneficiary check-ins and a family gifting review. The Metrics log, Vendor, Meeting notes, Operational checklist, Course and Risk register templates pair with the projects that name them.
