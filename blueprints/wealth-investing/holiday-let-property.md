                ## Purpose
                Many towns now limit short-term letting through registration schemes, annual night caps, planning permission for change of use or licensing, and leases or mortgages can ban it outright.                 ## Milestones

                4. Your mortgage lender's position on short-term letting known, with any consent letter filed.

                ## Notes

# Holiday Let Property

A holiday let is a small hospitality business that happens to be a property, and this area is for homeowners and small business owners buying, converting or already running one. It starts with foundations (viability, local short-let rules, a seasonal income model, mortgages, insurance, fire safety, a separate account and furnishing), moves into the routines that keep bookings, changeovers, pricing, reviews and compliance running, then the tax and platform knowledge worth learning, decisions on agencies, amenities and policies, the dated events of a letting year, variants for part-time second homes, distant owners, shared family houses and annexes, and finally specialist work on a second unit, switching to a long let and selling as a going business.
