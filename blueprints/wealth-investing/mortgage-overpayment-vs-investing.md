# Mortgage Overpayment vs Investing

This area is for homeowners with money left over each month who keep wondering whether it should shrink the mortgage or go into investments, and who want the answer written down rather than revisited every payday. It starts with the foundations (the real terms of your mortgage, your overpayment allowance, a true surplus figure, a cash floor, dearer debts and any employer match), then the routines that make overpaying and investing the difference actually happen, the knowledge that makes the comparison fair, the decisions about offsets, pensions, loan-to-value bands and term length, dated events such as a fixed rate ending, the situations that change the answer, and finally specialist work such as break-even returns and stress tests.

What repeats is a monthly check that the overpayment and the investment contribution both landed, a monthly ledger of the two pots, a quarterly look at rates and your projected mortgage-free date, and a yearly review timed before your mortgage anniversary.
