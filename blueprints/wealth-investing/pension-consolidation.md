              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                A defined benefit pension promises an income based on salary and service, while a defined contribution pot is simply an invested sum. They need entirely different decisions: moving a pot of money is routine, but giving up a promised income is a serious step that many countries surround with compulsory advice. Labelling every pension correctly first stops a guaranteed income being handled like a savings account.

                ## Milestones

                3. Defined benefit schemes set aside on a separate keep-by-default list.
                4. Each part of any hybrid scheme described in one sentence.

                ## Notes
                Older public sector, utility and large-company schemes are often defined benefit. Wording such as final salary, career average or scheme pension usually means a promised income.

                ## Notes

# Pension Consolidation

This area is for anyone holding pensions from several old jobs or past personal plans who wants fewer accounts without giving up something valuable on the way. It starts with the foundations (a full list, written answers from every provider, the type of each pension, guarantees, exit fees, death benefits and the choice of a receiving pension), then the routines that carry transfers through and keep the result tidy, the knowledge that makes transfer paperwork readable, the decisions about method and timing, dated events such as leaving a job, the situations that change the picture, and finally specialist cases such as defined benefit transfers and pots already in drawdown.

What repeats is a weekly look at the transfer tracker while moves are live, monthly filing and contribution checks, a quarterly value log and pension map refresh, and a yearly round of statement reviews, kept-pot checks, contact details and nominations. The Purchase decision, Operational checklist, Metrics log and Meeting notes templates pair with the projects that name them. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
