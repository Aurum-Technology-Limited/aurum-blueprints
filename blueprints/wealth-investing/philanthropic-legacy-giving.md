# Philanthropic Legacy Giving

This area turns charitable giving from a scatter of direct debits and appeal envelopes into a deliberate part of your wealth plan, for anyone who gives regularly and especially for retirees deciding what their money should do after them. It starts with foundations (a record of what you already give, a mission, a budget, a capacity check against your retirement plan and the tax rules where you live), moves through the routines that run giving each month and year, the knowledge behind donor-advised funds, charity accounts and legacy wording, the decisions on shares, pensions and pledges, dated events such as the solicitor meeting and a site visit, situations from a modest income to having no close heirs, and finally specialist work such as a private foundation or an endowed fund.

What repeats is a monthly giving run and receipt filing, a quarterly check of the tracker and any fund grants, and a yearly round of year-end gifts, tax relief claims, charity report reading, a family giving meeting and a legacy register update. The Metrics log, Purchase decision, Reading queue, Meeting notes and Board meeting templates pair with the projects that name them. Everything here is organisational: tax, legal and investment conclusions belong to a qualified adviser where you live. Installing adds all 50 projects as active, so archive the ones that are not for you yet.
