              description: |-
                ## Purpose
                Extra income disappears into everyday spending unless it has a named job before the first payment lands. Deciding now whether it goes to an emergency buffer, paying off a debt, investing for the long term or a specific goal, and in what proportions, turns a side hustle into part of your wealth plan rather than a second way to fund the same lifestyle.

                ## Milestones
                1. A written purpose for the side income, such as investing, clearing a card balance or building a six-month buffer.
                2. A split agreed for each unit of profit after tax, for example half invested, a quarter to debt and a quarter kept.
                3. A monthly profit figure that would make the effort worthwhile written down.
                4. A date set to revisit the purpose after six months.

                ## Notes
                Decide the split on profit after the tax set-aside, not on gross takings, or the tax bill will come out of money already spent.
              priority: high

                ## Purpose
                Earnings from side work above the small tax-free allowances in most countries must be declared, and the registration deadline often arrives months before the first tax bill does. Finding out which rules apply and registering on time avoids late penalties and makes it easier to claim legitimate expenses later. In the UK, for example, the trading allowance and self assessment registration rules both come into play.

                ## Milestones

                3. Registration completed and the reference number saved, where required.

                ## Notes
                Selling personal possessions you no longer want is often treated differently from trading, which is usually taxable. If you are unsure which side of the line you are on, ask a tax adviser before the deadline rather than after.

# Side Income Streams

This area is for anyone earning, or planning to earn, money alongside a main job: knowledge workers selling their expertise, creatives selling work and commissions, and anyone turning a skill or hobby into a second income. It starts with foundations such as the purpose of the money, your employment contract, tax registration, a separate account and a tax pot, then moves through the routines that track hours against profit and sweep profit into investments, the skills of pricing, offers and terms, decisions on rates, platforms and structure, first milestones and launches, situations such as parental leave, benefits and couples, and finally specialist work on concentration risk, sales tax thresholds and knowing when to scale or stop.

What repeats is a weekly hours log, invoice run and session plan; a monthly close, tax pot check, platform reconciliation, profit sweep and energy check; a quarterly keep, grow or cut review; and a yearly round of tax dates, price checks, insurance renewal and the year-end review. The Metrics log, Month-end close, Purchase decision, Marketing campaign and Risk register templates pair with the projects that name them.
